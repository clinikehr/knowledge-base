**A new reagent lot arrives quarantined.** It is received, it is on the shelf, it shows in your stock — and it cannot be used until someone validates it against the lot in service. Labs that do not know this spend a week wondering why good stock is unusable. It is waiting on the **Validation** tab.

## What you're looking at

A toolbar of stock actions — **Receive**, **Dispense**, **Transfer**, **Reports**, **Import**, **Opening stock**, **Export** and **Add item** — over a long row of tabs. A lab uses a handful of them daily and can ignore the rest.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | Every item you stock, with its type and pricing |
| **Stock** | On-hand quantities by **Item**, **Location**, **Qty** and **Reserved** |
| **Ledger** | Every movement in and out, with its reference |
| **Counts** | Physical stock counts, and the variances they find |
| **Validation (\{n\})** | Quarantined reagent lots awaiting lot-to-lot validation |
| **Purchasing** | Reorder suggestions and purchase orders |
| **Recalls** · **Vouchers** · **Locations** · **Reconciliation** | Recalled lots; issued vouchers; your fridges, benches and sites; and reconciling counted stock against the ledger |
| **Alerts (\{n\})** | Expiry and low-stock alerts, the expiry-risk panel and the alert settings |

Only **Validation** and **Alerts** carry a number, and only when there is something in them — a bare **Validation** means the queue is clear. Tabs for work a lab does not do, such as **Fill Rx**, sit alongside these and can be left alone.

**The empty states.** A new catalogue reads **No items yet.** and **Stock** reads **No stock on hand. Receive a lot to get started.** The queue you care about reads **No reagent lots awaiting validation. New reagent lots arrive here quarantined until validated.**, and a clear alerts tab reads **No open alerts. You're all stocked up.**

## Add a reagent to the catalogue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the item">
    Select **Add item** and set its type to **Reagent** — or **Kit**, for something supplied as an assembled kit. Those two sit alongside **Consumable**, **Supply**, **Device** and the clinical types in the same list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on lot-to-lot validation">
    Switch on **Lot-to-lot validation (lab reagent)** — "New lots arrive quarantined until validated." Leave it off only for something that genuinely needs no lot check. Leave **Track by lot / expiry** on, and **Refrigerated** on for anything with a cold chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the stock">
    Use **Receive** to book it in against its lot number and expiry date. Lot and expiry are what every later alert, count and validation hangs off, so never receive a reagent without them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Release a new lot through Validation

The **Validation (\{n\})** tab lists **Reagent**, **Lot**, **On hand** and **Expiry**, with a **Validate** button on each row and a **Search reagents…** box above. A lot that has already failed once carries a **failed** badge beside its lot number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sheet">
    Select **Validate**. **Validate lot** opens, stating its purpose: "Lot-to-lot validation before patient use. Pass releases the lot to active stock." A banner names the lot and what is on hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what you compared it against">
    Choose the lot in service under **Compared against (current in-use lot)**, or **None / first lot** if this is the first lot you have held.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record what you did">
    Enter the **Method** — the placeholder suggests `parallel_testing` — pick a **Reviewer** for sign-off, and summarise the outcome in **Result summary** (for example `Within 2SD of in-use lot`). Put observations and deviations in **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass or fail it">
    **Pass & release** puts the lot into active stock — the toast reads **Lot validated — released to active stock**. **Fail** records the failure and the toast reads **Validation recorded as failed — lot stays quarantined**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Pass & release** puts that lot into patient use. Do it only when you have actually run the comparison and the reviewer has seen the result — a lot released on a guess produces patient results nobody will question until a clinician acts on one.
</Warning>

## Attach reagents to a test

A catalogue test carries a **Reagents consumed** card, which explains itself: "Drawn from inventory each time this test is completed — linked to the order & patient." Each row is a **Reagent**, a **Qty** and an **Opt.** flag for something not always used. Use **Add reagent** for rows and **Save reagents** to save — the toast reads **Reagents saved**. An empty card reads **No reagents yet. Add the items this test consumes.**

Completing that test then draws its reagents down, so counts follow the bench without anyone counting bottles. See [Add your tests and panels](/lims/get-started/tests-and-panels) and [Enter and verify results](/lims/lab/results).

<Note>
  The drawdown rides alongside the status change and can occasionally fall behind, so treat reagent counts as very good rather than perfect. A periodic stock count on the **Counts** tab is what keeps them honest.
</Note>

### Collection consumables are configured separately

The supplies a specimen collection uses — cups, split bottles, seals, temperature strips — are not on the test. They live in the **Collection supplies** sheet, which draws them "each time a specimen is collected … shortfalls warn, never block a collection". Saving it toasts **Collection kit saved**. See [DOT collection](/lims/dot/collection).

## Expiry and low stock

The **Alerts (\{n\})** tab lists each alert as **Type**, **Item**, **Location** and **When**, with an **Acknowledge** button that keeps it quiet for 30 days. Above them the expiry-risk panel projects what is heading for the bin — **Expiry risk — \{amount\} projected to expire unsold**, from "your last 90 days of sales, per lot, first-expiry-first-out".

| You get | On |
| --- | --- |
| The expiry-risk figures — value at risk, lots at risk, which lots | Professional Lab |
| The recommendations — discount-to-clear, supplier returns, and transfers to a site that still uses the item | Business Lab |

Below Professional Lab the panel is replaced entirely by **Expiry risk — predictive**, **Available on Professional and above**. Batch and lot tracking, expiry monitoring, low-stock alerts and purchase orders are all included from **Starter Lab**. See [Plans](/platform/plans/overview).

## Who can do this

This screen is governed by **role**, not by the Diagnostics permission list, which has no slug for stock. A bench scientist therefore reaches it, deliberately — the person who runs out of a reagent should be able to see why.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Reagents & Inventory** | Owner, Manager, **Lab Scientist**, and the business roles (Doctor, Biller, Accountant, Employee) | Role → route |
| Add an item, receive stock, run a count, set a test's **Reagents consumed** | The same people | Role → route |
| **Validate** a quarantined lot | The same people — record the **Reviewer** who signed it off | Role → route |
| See the **Reagents** and **Expiry Risk** reports | Owner, Manager and the business roles | Role → route — see [The report catalogue](/lims/business/analytics) |
| Open it as a Phlebotomist, Receptionist, Radiologist or Radiology Technician | Nobody — Inventory is not on their route list | Role → route |

Nothing here is gated by **Enter results at the bench** or **Verify and authorise results**. Your local procedure, and the **Reviewer** field on the validation sheet, are what enforce who may sign a lot into patient use — not the software.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Somebody never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Where a figure is not yours to see it renders hidden or as a dash — never as a zero, which on a stock screen would read as "we have none".

See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The new lot leaves the **Validation** tab, the count in the tab label drops, and its quantity appears as usable stock.
- The **Reagents consumed** card on the test lists the items and quantities you saved.
- Completing a test moves the reagent's on-hand figure on the **Stock** tab, and a matching row appears on **Ledger**.
- The **Reagents at risk** tile on **Executive** agrees with the expiry-risk panel — see [The owner summary](/lims/business/executive).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stock is on the shelf but the bench cannot use it">
    It is quarantined. Open the **Validation** tab and validate the lot. This is the intended behaviour for anything flagged **Lot-to-lot validation (lab reagent)**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Validation tab">
    Nothing in your catalogue is flagged for lot-to-lot validation yet. Edit a reagent, switch the flag on, and the tab appears.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reagent stock did not move after a test completed">
    Either the test has no **Reagents consumed** rows, or the drawdown fell behind the status change. Check the card first, then reconcile with a stock count on **Counts**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Save reagents refuses with “Pick a reagent for each row”">
    A row was added but left empty. Choose an item on it, or remove the row with its **Remove** button.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expiry risk shows a number but no recommendations">
    The figures are a Professional Lab capability; the recommendations — discount-to-clear, supplier returns and branch transfers — are a Business Lab one. The panel says so itself. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A validated lot shows a “failed” badge">
    It failed a previous validation attempt and stayed quarantined. Investigate the cause, then validate again — the sheet keeps a record of both attempts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Collection supplies are not being drawn down">
    They are configured in the **Collection supplies** sheet, not on the test's **Reagents consumed** card — see [DOT collection](/lims/dot/collection).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do reagent counts update themselves?">
    Yes — completing a test draws down whatever is on its **Reagents consumed** card, and receiving, dispensing or transferring stock writes to the ledger. The drawdown is best-effort alongside the status change, so run a periodic count rather than trusting the figure indefinitely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should every reagent have lot-to-lot validation switched on?">
    Only where a lot change can shift results — most assay reagents and kits. Buffers, tips and general consumables do not need it, and switching it on for them just fills the queue with work nobody will do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to a lot I fail?">
    It stays quarantined and unusable, carries a **failed** badge, and remains in the queue. Nothing is discarded for you — dispose of it or return it to the supplier under your own procedure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I validate the first lot of a brand-new reagent?">
    Yes. Choose **None / first lot** under **Compared against (current in-use lot)** and record what you did in **Method** and **Result summary** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does this screen say “clinic” and show tabs we never use?">
    It is the general inventory screen, shared with the other editions. The vocabulary and the extra tabs come with it; everything a lab needs is on **Catalog**, **Stock**, **Validation**, **Counts** and **Alerts**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
