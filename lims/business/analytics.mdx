**Analytics** is the lab's cross-cutting report catalogue: one card per subject, each opening a workbench with a period, tiles, charts, a data table and an export. It is where you go when the question is "how much / how many / how long", and it covers the whole lab rather than one bench.

## Three screens are called analytics. This is the third

People quote a figure from one while looking at another and the numbers never reconcile. Tell them apart by what they are for.

| Screen | Where | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lab Analytics** | <Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Lab Analytics']} /> | How is the lab trading — trends, mix, revenue by test and by referrer. See [Lab analytics](/lims/business/lab-analytics). |
| **Executive** | <Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Executive']} /> | Today and this week, for the owner. See [The owner summary](/lims/business/executive). |
| **Analytics** — this page | <Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Analytics']} /> | The catalogue. One report per subject, with an export. |

## What you're looking at

The index is headed **Analytics**, with a subtitle counting what you can open — "\{n\} reports available for this clinic. Each one covers a period you choose and can be exported." Cards are grouped under **Clinical**, **Operations** and **Money**, in that order.

Opening one gives you the same workbench every time:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| The period select | **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months** |
| Scope tabs | On the reports that have them — **Laboratory** / **Radiology**, **Invoices** / **Payments** |
| Tiles and charts | The headline figures, then **\{report\} over time** and **\{report\} by category** |
| The data table | Three views — **Over time**, **Breakdown** and **Summary** — with **Export** above them |

A footer note states the arithmetic: "Figures are aggregates for the selected period. Periods longer than 12 months are shortened server-side."

**The empty states.** A period with no activity reads **No data for this period** — "Try a longer period, or check back once there is activity." A report that returns nothing to tabulate reads **Nothing to tabulate**. If the index itself is bare it reads **No reports available**.

## The reports a lab sees

Twelve, in the order the index groups them.

| Report | Scopes | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diagnostics** | **Laboratory** · **Radiology** | — the core report for a lab: volume, turnaround and abnormal rate |
| **Quality Control** | — | The quality-control feature (Professional Lab) |
| **Appointments** | — | — collection slots and bookings |
| **Patients** | — | — |
| **Turnaround Time** | **Laboratory** · **Radiology** | The turnaround feature (Professional Lab) **and** **View turnaround-time analytics** |
| **Staff** | — | — |
| **Workload** | — | — cross-department load and peak hours |
| **Reagents** | — | Valuation and turnover — see [Reagents and consumables](/lims/inventory/reagents) |
| **Predictive Analysis** | — | — demand, revenue and stock forecasts |
| **Revenue** | **Invoices** · **Payments** | — |
| **Referrals** | — | The referral-network feature (Business Lab) **and** **Access the referrer portal** |
| **Expiry Risk** | — | The expiry-risk feature (Professional Lab). It has no period — it reads **As it stands now** |

**Quality Control** is gated on the feature only, not on **Review QC runs** — a manager who cannot open the QC bench can still read the report about it.

### Invoiced or collected — pick one and say which

The **Revenue** report's two scopes are the single commonest reason two people quote different numbers for the same month. **Invoices** is what you billed; **Payments** is what actually arrived. An invoice raised and never paid is a large number under one scope and nothing under the other. Every other money figure in the lab — **Lab Analytics**, the owner summary, referral commissions — is worked out on money **collected**, so **Payments** is the scope that reconciles against them. See [Payments](/lims/business/payments).

## Run a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it from the index">
    Select the card. Reports your plan or your permissions do not cover are not listed at all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    Choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months**. Everything below follows it. **Expiry Risk** has no period — it is a snapshot, labelled **As it stands now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow with a scope, where there is one">
    On **Diagnostics** and **Turnaround Time**, pick **Laboratory** or **Radiology**. On **Revenue**, pick **Invoices** or **Payments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the table, not just the chart">
    Switch between **Over time**, **Breakdown** and **Summary**. The chart shows the shape; the table is what you can defend a decision with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Take a copy away

Select **Export** above the data table and choose **CSV — this view** or **Excel (.xlsx) — this view**. Both take exactly what is on screen — the period, the scope and the table view you have selected — so change the view first, then export.

A CSV confirms with **Exported** and "\{n\} rows · \{period\}. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Sheets." A failed workbook confirms with **Not exported** and the reason.

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Analytics** appears in the sidebar; a feature on your plan decides whether a report exists; a permission decides whether a gated one is offered to you. All three are separate, and the failure looks different in each case.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Analytics** and its ungated reports | Owner, Manager, **Lab Manager**, **Referrer Liaison**, **QC Officer** | Role → route |
| Open **Turnaround Time** | **QC Officer**, **Referrer Liaison**, **Lab Manager**, **All sites** tier | Permission — **View turnaround-time analytics** |
| Open **Referrals** | **Referrer Liaison**, **Lab Manager**, **All sites** tier | Permission — **Access the referrer portal** |
| Open **Quality Control** | Anyone who reaches the catalogue | Plan only — no permission |
| Export any report you can open | The same people | Role → route |
| Open **Lab Analytics** or **Executive** | Owner, Manager and management roles | Role → route |

A **Lab Scientist** is denied **Executive**, **Lab Analytics** and **Referral Commissions** outright — bench roles get the bench, not the lab's revenue. A **QC Officer** reaches quality and turnaround reporting and nothing that alters a record, which is the whole point of the role.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Somebody never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A figure you are not entitled to see renders hidden or as a dash — never as a zero, which would read as real data.

A report you are not permitted to open is not listed on the index, and its address does not resolve — you get a page-not-found rather than a lock card. The bespoke **Lab Analytics** and **Turnaround Time** dashboards behave differently and show **Analytics is hidden** / **Turnaround analytics is hidden** with **Needs authorisation from your clinic owner or manager.** See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The report opens with tiles that change when you change the period.
- The data table has rows under **Breakdown**, not **Nothing to tabulate**.
- **Export** produces a file whose row count matches the **Exported** toast.
- The **Payments** scope of **Revenue** agrees with the **Total Collected** figure on [Payments](/lims/business/payments).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Two people quote different revenue for the same month">
    One is reading the **Invoices** scope and the other **Payments**. Agree which before you argue about the number — every other money figure in the lab is collected money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A report I was told about is not on the index">
    Either the feature is not on your plan, or the permission is not on your account. They have different fixes: [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan) for the first, [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions) for the second.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A report link gives a page-not-found">
    That is what a permission-denied catalogue report looks like — it is not listed and its address does not resolve. Ask an owner or manager for the grant named against it in the table above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="“No data for this period”">
    Nothing happened in the range you picked, or nothing has been completed yet. Try **Last 12 months** before assuming the report is broken.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expiry Risk ignores the period I picked">
    It has no period. It reads **As it stands now** — a snapshot of what is at risk today, not a range. See [Reagents and consumables](/lims/inventory/reagents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Revenue by referring doctor is empty">
    Orders are not being attributed to referrers at intake. The referrer must be picked on the intake or order form — see [Referring doctors](/lims/business/referrers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The export has fewer rows than the chart suggests">
    **Export** takes the table view you are on. Switch to **Breakdown** or **Over time** first, then export again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which of the three analytics screens should I use?">
    **Executive** for the two-minute morning read, **Lab Analytics** for trading trends and mix, and this catalogue when you want one subject in depth with an export. They are not different views of one dataset — they answer different questions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is opening a report logged?">
    Yes. Viewing and exporting a report are both written to your audit log with your name and the time, because reports aggregate patient work. That is a HIPAA requirement, not a setting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I schedule a report to arrive by email?">
    Not from here. Export it yourself, or read the owner summary, which arrives as a morning digest email — see [The owner summary](/lims/business/executive).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can a QC Officer see turnaround time?">
    Both quality and turnaround are oversight of how the lab performs rather than changes to a patient record, so they travel together on that role. See [Turnaround time](/lims/lab/turnaround-time).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I go back more than 12 months?">
    Not on this screen — **Last 12 months** is the longest period, and anything longer is shortened for you. Export successive periods and join them outside the product.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
