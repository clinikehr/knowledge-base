Register the doctors and facilities that send you tests, attribute each order to the person who sent it, and hand them a private link where they follow their own work. The page puts it plainly: "Register the doctors and facilities that send you tests. Each referrer gets a private portal link where they can follow their orders and pick up released results."

## What you're looking at

One table with a toolbar above it. The toolbar holds a search box — **Search referrers…** — and **Add referring doctor**; on the Business Lab plan a **Commission statements** button sits beside them.

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Referrer** | Their name, with their facility beneath it |
| **Contact** | The email and phone you hold |
| **Status** | **Active** or **Paused** |
| **Last portal visit** | The honest answer to "did they ever use the link" |
| **Link generated** | When a portal link was last issued — and therefore when the previous one stopped working |

A date you do not hold renders as **—**, so a fresh referrer is blank under both date columns. The row menu, under **Actions**, offers **Get portal link**, **Edit**, **Pause portal access** / **Re-enable portal access**, and **Remove**.

**The empty state.** A new lab reads **No referring doctors yet. Add one to generate their private results-portal link, then pick them on new orders so their results appear in the portal.** A referrer who exists but has no work yet sees, in their own portal, **No orders have been attributed to you yet. Once the laboratory registers your next referral, it will appear here.**

## Add a referring doctor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Add referring doctor**. It tells you what saving will do: "Register a referring doctor or facility. A private results-portal link is generated when you save."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter who they are">
    Fill **Name** (for example `Dr. Adaeze Okafor`) — the only required field — and, where you have them, **Facility (optional)**, **Email (optional)** and **Phone (optional)**. The email is what lets the lab send their portal link for you rather than you pasting it somewhere. **Notes (optional)** takes anything the next person needs to know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a commission arrangement, if you pay one">
    On the Business Lab plan the form carries a **Commission arrangement** block: "Percent is on the collected amount of lab and imaging orders; the flat amount applies per referred order." Fill **Percent of collected (%)** and **Flat per order**, or leave both at zero if you do not pay this referrer — see [Referral commissions](/lims/business/commissions). Below Business Lab the block is not shown, and editing a referrer there leaves any existing arrangement untouched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Add referrer** (or **Save changes** when editing). The toast reads **Referring doctor added** — or **Referring doctor updated** — and their portal link is offered straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Give them their portal link

The link is shown **only at the moment it is generated**. To obtain one later, open the referrer's row menu and select **Get portal link**; the confirmation asks **Generate a new portal link?** and spells out the cost — "Generating a new link for \{name\} will immediately stop the previous link from working — share the new one with them afterwards." Select **Generate new link** and the **Portal link for \{name\}** dialog opens.

That dialog repeats the rule that governs this whole page: "anyone with the link can see their referred orders, so share it directly with them only." From it you can copy the link — the toast reads **Portal link copied** — select **Email link** to have the lab send it to the address on file, select **Share on WhatsApp**, or select **Done**. **Email link** is offered only when the referrer has an **Email (optional)** on their record; a successful send toasts **Portal link emailed to \{email\}**.

<Warning>
  The portal link is a bearer link and **Get portal link rotates it**. Anyone holding the current link can open that referrer's account and see every order attributed to them, so share it with the doctor directly and never in a group thread. Generating a new link is also the fix if one is ever exposed — but it invalidates the link the doctor already holds, immediately, without telling them.
</Warning>

If the send fails you will see **Could not email the link — copy it and share directly instead**, or **Could not copy the link — select and copy it manually**. Both are recoverable: the link in the field is the real one.

## Attribute work to a referrer

A referrer only sees orders that name them. Pick them on the **lab intake form** or the **lab order form** when the work is registered — see [Register a test request](/lims/lab/test-requests) and [DOT testing](/lims/dot/index). Nothing is attributed retrospectively, so the habit has to start at the front desk.

## What the referrer sees

Their portal opens with your lab's name over **Referring-doctor results portal**, greets them with **Welcome, \{name\}**, and explains its own scope: "Orders you referred and their released results. Contact the laboratory on \{phone\} for anything urgent."

Work is split into tabs by type, each carrying its count — **Laboratory (\{n\})**, **Imaging (\{n\})** and **Drug Testing (\{n\})** — and a tab with nothing in it is not shown at all. A released order is badged **Result ready** and can be taken away with **Download report (PDF)**. Lab results appear as a table of **Parameter**, **Result**, **Reference range** and **Flag**, where an out-of-range value is badged **Critical** or **Abnormal** and an in-range one reads **Normal** in plain text. The footer reminds them whose link it is: "This is a private link intended for \{name\}. All access is logged."

**Only released results appear.** Work in progress, and results that have not been released to them, are not in the portal — which is why an urgent critical value is still a phone call, not a link.

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Referring Doctors** opens. Permission decides what you may do inside it — and releasing a report to a referrer is a separate grant from managing the list.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Referring Doctors** | **Referrer Liaison**, **Lab Manager**, anyone on the **All sites** tier, Owner, Manager | Permission — **Access the referrer portal** |
| Add, edit, pause or remove a referrer | The same people | Permission — **Access the referrer portal** |
| Generate or email a portal link | The same people | Permission — **Access the referrer portal** |
| Release a report into a referrer's portal | **Referrer Liaison**, **Lab Manager**, **All sites** tier | Permission — **Release a report to a referrer** |
| Open **Referral Commissions** from the toolbar | Owner, Manager only | Role → route |
| See the **Commission arrangement** fields on the form | Anyone above, on Business Lab | Plan, not permission |

Without **Access the referrer portal** the screen does not error — it reads **Referring doctors are hidden**, with **Needs authorisation from your clinic owner or manager.** beneath it.

A **Lab Scientist** does not hold these grants — bench roles produce the result, the liaison releases it — and is refused **Referral Commissions** outright with **You don't have permission to manage referral commissions. Please contact your clinic administrator.** A **QC Officer** is review-only by design and holds neither grant.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Somebody never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A referral figure you are not permitted to see renders hidden or as a dash, never as a zero.

See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The referrer appears in the table badged **Active**.
- **Link generated** carries today's date after you generate a link.
- Attribute a test request to them, release the report, and it appears under the matching tab in their portal — see [Produce and release a report](/lims/lab/reports).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The page reads “Referring doctors are hidden”">
    You do not hold **Access the referrer portal**. The card adds **Needs authorisation from your clinic owner or manager** — the wording is shared across editions. An owner or manager grants it; see [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The referrer says their link stopped working">
    Someone selected **Get portal link**, which rotates it. Only the newest link works. Generate once more and send them that link. Check too that their status is **Active** rather than **Paused**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Their portal is empty but you know you ran their work">
    Either the orders were never attributed to them — the referrer has to be picked on the intake or order form — or the results have not been released yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email link is not offered">
    There is no **Email (optional)** address on the referrer. Add one with **Edit**, or copy the link and send it yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Commission statements is missing from the toolbar">
    That button appears only on the Business Lab plan, which is also what reveals the **Commission arrangement** fields on the form — see [Referral commissions](/lims/business/commissions) and [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I removed a referrer by mistake">
    The dialog warned what would happen: "Their portal link stops working immediately. Orders already attributed to them keep their history, but will no longer be linked to a referrer." Re-adding the doctor gives you a fresh record rather than restoring the old attributions.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a referrer see other doctors' patients?">
    No. The portal shows only orders attributed to that referrer, and nothing else in your lab. Two doctors at the same facility each get their own link and each see only their own work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the referrer need an account or a password?">
    No. The link itself is the credential, which is exactly why it must be shared with them directly and rotated if it leaks. There is nothing for them to remember and nothing for you to reset.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I attribute an order to a referrer after it was registered?">
    Attribution is made on the intake or order form when the work is registered. Get it right at the front desk — an order that was never attributed will not appear in the portal or on a commission statement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between pausing and removing?">
    **Pause portal access** stops the link working and keeps everything else. **Remove** deletes the referrer record; past orders keep their history but lose the link to a referrer. Pause when a doctor is on leave, remove when the arrangement has ended.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does releasing a report to a referrer also send it to the patient?">
    No — they are separate acts on separate surfaces. Releasing to a referrer puts the report in that doctor's portal only; see [Produce and release a report](/lims/lab/reports).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
