The **Radiology Station** is the imaging side's control room. **Imaging Requests** is the queue you work; the station is where you see whether the department is keeping up — how much came in, what is stuck, how long it takes, and what needs a phone call rather than a report.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Radiology Station']} />

## What you're looking at

Four counters sit across the top of the station, and they are the whole department in one line.

| Card | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Radiology Tests** | Everything ordered in the period |
| **Pending Tests** | Ordered but not yet completed |
| **Completed Tests** | Acquired and finished |
| **Critical Findings** | Studies flagged as needing immediate attention |

**Critical Findings** is not a statistic to review at the end of the week. A critical finding is a phone call to the referrer, and the counter exists so nobody has to notice it by reading a report.

Below the counters are four tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Analytics** | The counters and every chart below |
| **Test Catalog** | The imaging studies your centre offers, and their prices |
| **Reports** | A placeholder. It says the reporting workspace is **under development** here — do your reporting on [Radiology Reports](/lims/imaging/reports) instead |
| **Modalities** | Registering imaging machines — see [Connect your imaging machines](/lims/imaging/modalities) |

**Modalities** appears only when the imaging bridge is switched on for your workspace, which is a **Professional Lab** feature. Below that plan the tab is simply not there.

**The empty state.** A diagnostic centre that has not yet raised an imaging request sees every counter at zero and each chart drawn empty rather than missing. Nothing is wrong — the station has nothing to measure yet. Raise a study on [Imaging Requests](/lims/imaging/requests) and the counters move the same day.

## Reading the charts

Seven charts sit on the **Analytics** tab, and each answers a different question about the department.

| Chart | Its own caption | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Test Volume Trends** | "Daily radiology ordering and completion patterns" | See whether completions are keeping pace with orders |
| **Most Requested Tests** | "Most frequently ordered radiology tests" | Decide what to staff and stock for |
| **Turnaround Time** | "Average hours to process tests" | Spot a backlog before referrers do |
| **Staff Performance** | "Radiologist and technician activity metrics" | See where the reporting load actually sits |
| **Modality Distribution** | "Breakdown by imaging type" | Check machine utilisation |
| **Patient Demographics** | "Age and gender distribution of radiology patients" | Understand who your imaging serves |
| **Urgency Distribution** | "Test priority breakdown" | Notice when everything has become **STAT** |

Turnaround time is the number a referring practice judges you by, and it is measured across the lab as a whole under [Turnaround time](/lims/lab/turnaround-time).

<Note>
  A figure of zero and a figure that could not be calculated are different things, and the station distinguishes them. Where a number cannot be worked out for your role or period you will see a dash rather than a nought — do not read a dash as "nothing happened".
</Note>

## Work the queue from here

The station reports; it does not dispatch. Use its counters as a to-do list and act on the queue itself:

- **Critical Findings** above zero → open **Critical Findings** on [Imaging Requests](/lims/imaging/requests), which filters the queue to exactly those studies, and ring the referrer.
- **Pending Tests** climbing while **Completed Tests** flattens → you have a bench or machine problem, not a demand problem.
- **Turnaround Time** rising → check **Staff Performance** for where the reporting load has landed.

## Who can do this

Role decides whether the station opens at all. Permission decides what you may do once it is open.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the station and read the counters | Owner, Manager, **Radiologist**, **Radiology Technician** | Role → route |
| Read the turnaround figures | **Lab Manager**, **Referrer Liaison**, **QC Officer**, Owner, Manager | Permission — **View turnaround-time analytics** |
| Open **Modalities** | Anyone who reaches the station | Plan only — the modality-integration feature, no permission |
| Report a study from the queue | Radiologists with reporting access | Role → route |
| Open **Executive** or **Lab Analytics** | Owner, Manager | Role → route — a **Lab Scientist** is denied both |

Imaging and the bench are deliberately separate worlds. A **Radiologist** or **Radiology Technician** reaches the imaging screens and patient records and nothing else; a **Lab Scientist** does not reach imaging at all; a **Receptionist** reaches imaging requests but not the station. Access tiers — **Own bench**, **Whole lab**, **All sites** — decide how much of the lab a person sees; **All sites** is the only one that carries lab-wide reporting.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding the station from somebody's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

Where a figure is not yours to see, the card renders hidden or as a dash — never as a zero. See [Roles](/platform/team/roles) and [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The four counters carry numbers that match the queue on **Imaging Requests** for the same period.
- **Completed Tests** rises within minutes of a radiographer finishing a study.
- **Critical Findings** drops as flagged studies are dealt with.
- Selecting **Critical Findings** on the queue returns exactly the studies the counter is counting.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Modalities tab">
    The imaging bridge is a **Professional Lab** feature — the tab is hidden below that plan, and the page it leads to says **Modality integration is a Professional feature**. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Reports tab says it is under development">
    That tab is a placeholder and always has been. The working reporting screen is [Radiology Reports](/lims/imaging/reports) in the sidebar — nothing is broken.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Critical Findings is higher than I expect">
    Every study flagged critical counts until it is dealt with. Work them from the **Critical Findings** button on [Imaging Requests](/lims/imaging/requests), which filters the queue to exactly those studies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Turnaround time looks impossible">
    Turnaround runs from the time a study was ordered, not from when someone got to it. A study raised on Friday and acquired on Monday carries the weekend.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Staff Performance is missing names I expect">
    Someone whose work is attributed to another account, or who is not an imaging role, will not appear. Check their role rather than their output.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Some cards show a dash where I expect a number">
    Those figures are permission-gated. A dash means "not yours to see", not "nothing happened" — ask an owner or manager about **View turnaround-time analytics**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot open the station at all">
    You are on a bench role. Imaging and the bench are deliberately separate — ask an owner or manager if your role is wrong.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the station's Test Catalog the same as the lab's test list?">
    They are the same catalogue seen from the imaging side — the studies your centre offers and their prices. The commercial price a patient is invoiced comes from [Tests and prices](/lims/business/billing); the study definitions come from [Add your tests and panels](/lims/get-started/tests-and-panels).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does the station show different numbers from Lab Analytics?">
    The station counts imaging studies only, over its own period. [Lab analytics](/lims/business/analytics) counts drug testing, lab and imaging together and reports money **collected** rather than volume. Different questions, different answers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a referring doctor see these figures?">
    No. The referrer portal shows only the orders that doctor sent you and their released results — never your volumes, turnaround or staff performance. See [Referring doctors and their portal](/lims/business/referrers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the station update live?">
    It reads the queue each time you open it. Reopen the station, or move off the tab and back, to pull the latest figures after a busy hour.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export what is on this screen?">
    Not from the station. The exportable versions of these figures are in the report catalogue under [Reports and analytics](/lims/business/analytics), which has an **Export** on each report's data table.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
