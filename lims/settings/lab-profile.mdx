Two tabs, one job: making the lab identifiable and keeping the right people informed. **General Settings** holds the details printed on every report and invoice you release. **Notifications** decides which of those events reach your team by email and in the app.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'General Settings']} />

<TaskHeader before="Your registered lab name and address, a phone number and inbox someone monitors, and a logo file" time="About 5 minutes for both tabs" after="Reports that carry your lab's identity, and alerts going only where you want them" />

<Note>
  This screen is shared across every edition, so its fields are labelled **Clinic Name**, **Clinic Logo**, **Clinic Branding** and **Clinic Description** even on a lab. They mean your lab — enter your lab's details into them.
</Note>

## Fill in the lab profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General Settings">
    Under the **LABORATORY** section, open **Profile › General Settings** — "Clinic profile and basic info".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name people should see">
    **Clinic Name** is the name that appears on every report and invoice. Use the registered lab name, not the abbreviation the bench uses in conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the address, phone and email">
    Fill in the address, phone number and email address. A referring doctor chasing a critical result calls the number printed on the report, so use a line that is answered during opening hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Under **Clinic Branding**, upload the **Clinic Logo**. Use the file your letterhead uses, at a size that stays readable when a report is printed small.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timezone">
    **Timezone** must be where the lab physically sits. It decides how received times, collection slots and every turnaround figure are measured, and which business day a piece of work falls into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the optional detail">
    **Website** and the description are optional. Write the description for a patient booking a collection slot, not for a regulator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Timezone is the field worth double-checking. A lab left on the wrong one reports turnaround times that are hours out and buckets work into yesterday — see [Turnaround time](/lims/lab/turnaround-time). For the setup-checklist version of this task, see [Complete your lab profile](/lims/get-started/lab-profile).

## Choose which alerts go out

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Notifications']} />

The **Notifications** tab — "Alerts and communication" — sits in the **Laboratory notifications** group. It has one master switch and a list of categories under it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the master switch">
    **Email notifications** is described as the "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." Off means the lab sends no non-essential email at all, whatever the categories below say.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn categories on or off">
    Each category has its own switch and governs both the email and the in-app alert for that kind of event. The list adapts to your edition, so a lab sees fewer categories than a clinic — you will not find appointment-heavy categories a lab never generates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave System alone">
    **System** cannot be switched off: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic." That is the channel outages, plan changes and security notices arrive on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the tab. Changes apply to alerts raised from that moment; anything already sent has been sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning a category off silences the alert, not the event. The result is still verified, the invoice is still raised — nobody is simply emailed about it.

<Note>
  Transactional email is never affected by these switches. Password resets, invitations, signing links and anything a patient must receive to complete something they started go out regardless.
</Note>

## Check it worked

- Reopen **General Settings** after a refresh and your saved values are still there.
- Produce a report from a completed test: it carries the lab name, logo, address and phone you entered. See [Produce and release a report](/lims/lab/reports).
- Trigger an event in a category you left on and confirm the alert arrives; trigger one you switched off and confirm it does not.
- A recently received sample shows a received time that matches the clock on the wall.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An old report still shows the previous details">
    A report carries the details it was built with. Produce it again after saving — documents generated earlier are historical records and are not rewritten.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Times across the product are hours out">
    That is the **Timezone** field, not the individual records. Set it to the lab's own timezone, save, then check a recent received time. See [Turnaround time](/lims/lab/turnaround-time).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The logo prints blurry or crops oddly">
    Upload a larger, squarer version. A wide letterhead strip loses its edges when it is scaled into a report header.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My team still gets email after I turned a category off">
    Check whether the message is transactional — invitations, resets and signing links ignore these switches by design. If it is genuinely a category alert, confirm you saved the tab and that the person is not receiving it via a second workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot switch System notifications off">
    You cannot, and that is deliberate. They carry updates about your workspace that you would not want missed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The fields are greyed out for me">
    Settings needs an owner, manager or accountant, and the profile fields are owner and manager territory. See [Roles](/platform/team/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
