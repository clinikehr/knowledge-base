The **BILLING** section covers two separate things people often confuse: what your patients and referring practices pay **you**, and what you pay ClinikEHR. Three tabs — **Billing & Plans**, **Online Payments** and **Payment Details** — sit under it.

## Billing & Plans

This tab holds your ClinikEHR subscription and four lab-wide money settings:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Currency** | The currency every price, invoice and receipt is shown in |
| **Tax Rate (%)** | The rate applied to invoices |
| **Free Clinic Mode** | Turns off charging for services entirely |
| **Flat Rate Fee** | A single fee applied in place of per-item pricing |

A Diagnostics workspace defaults to Naira. Currency does more than change a symbol: it also selects which payment provider handles a card payment — Naira routes to Paystack, everything else routes to Stripe. Set it once, at the start.

<Warning>
  Changing the currency does not re-price anything. Existing invoices, receipts and price lists keep the numbers they already hold and are simply displayed under the new symbol, so a switch mid-trading turns ₦15,000 into $15,000 on screen. Set the currency before you price your tests, and if you must change it later, review your service prices and any unpaid invoices immediately afterwards.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Currency**, **Tax Rate (%)** and the free-clinic switch are **owner-only**. A manager or accountant sees them on the screen but cannot change them — the controls render disabled rather than hidden, so you can see what is set without being able to set it.
</Note>

For how these figures land on an invoice, see [Invoicing and payments](/lims/business/billing). For the currency list and how amounts are formatted across the product, see [Currency](/platform/settings/currency).

## Online Payments

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Online Payments']} />

"Stripe and Paystack setup" — this is how patients and referring practices pay **you** by card, and it is entirely separate from how you pay for your ClinikEHR plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Connect through **Stripe Connect** or **Paystack Connect**, matching the currency you set on **Billing & Plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the connect flow">
    For Paystack in Nigeria you supply the business name, choose your bank from the picker, and enter the account number — the field prompts **Enter 10-digit NUBAN**. Stripe runs its own onboarding in a new window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back and confirm the status">
    The tab shows the connection state when the provider hands you back. An incomplete connection takes no payments, so check rather than assume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Details

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Payment Details']} />

"Bank, Cash App, Venmo & more on invoices" — the channels you want a payer to see **printed on the invoice itself**, for anyone who is not paying by card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a channel">
    Select **Add payment details**, choose a **Type**, and give it a label a payer will recognise — "GTBank Business Account" rather than "Account 2".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the fields">
    Add as many **Fields** as the channel needs, each a **Label** and a **Value** — account name, account number, sort code, a handle, a reference to quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Each channel has its own toggle. Only the ones switched on are printed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Enabled channels appear on invoice PDFs and invoice emails while a balance is still due, and drop off once the invoice is paid. That makes this tab particularly useful for employer-billed drug testing, where the payer is an accounts department that will pay by transfer and needs your bank details in front of them. See [Invoicing and payments](/lims/business/billing).

## Check it worked

- A new invoice shows the currency symbol you chose and the tax rate you set.
- The **Online Payments** tab reports a completed connection for your provider.
- A test invoice PDF carries every payment channel you switched on, with the labels and values you entered.
- A paid invoice no longer shows the payment channels.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Currency and tax rate are greyed out">
    Those are owner-only, even for a manager or accountant. Ask the workspace owner to change them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My prices look wrong after changing currency">
    Amounts are not converted. Review your service prices and re-price them in the new currency, then check any invoice still awaiting payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A card payment will not go through">
    Confirm the provider connection is complete on **Online Payments**, and that the provider matches your currency — Naira goes through Paystack, other currencies through Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My bank details are not on the invoice">
    Check the channel's own toggle is on, and that the invoice still has a balance due. Paid invoices do not print payment channels.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am being asked for a NUBAN and do not know it">
    It is the ten-digit account number on your Nigerian bank account. Your bank's app or a statement shows it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to change the ClinikEHR plan, not my prices">
    That is the subscription part of this same tab. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
