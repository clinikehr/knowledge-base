## What this tab controls

The card is headed **Notification Preferences** — "Choose which in-app and email notifications your clinic receives. Essential emails (receipts, security, and billing alerts) always send."

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** | The master switch. "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." | On |
| **Appointments** | "New bookings, cancellations, and reminders" — collection and imaging slots. | On |
| **Patients** | "New patient registrations and updates" | On |
| **Staff** | "Staff invitations accepted and role changes" | On |
| **Billing** | "Payment received, invoices, and overdue alerts" | On |
| **Subscription** | "Plan changes, trial expiry, and payment status" | On |
| **Documents** | "Document signed, viewed, or expired" | On |
| **System** | "System updates and maintenance notices" | On, and **cannot be turned off** |
| **Achievement emails** | "Milestone badges, tier-ups, and your clinic's trophy case" | On |
| **Daily operational digests** | "Morning owner summaries for inventory, pharmacy, and lab" | On |

Each switch saves the moment you flip it — there is no save button. You get "Notifications enabled" or "Notifications muted" with the category named.

<Note>
  This card is where the settings screen speaks your edition's vocabulary correctly: the group in the sidebar is **Laboratory notifications** and the category here is **Patients**. **Insurance** is not offered at all — a lab has no insurance module, and a switch for mail that can never be sent would be a false promise.
</Note>

## Your notification bell, and what is yours alone

The bell in the header keeps **your own** copy of every notice, in three views: **Unread**, **All** and **Archived**. What you do with a notice is yours: a notice sent to the whole clinic that a colleague has read stays unread for you. On each notice you can **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Archive** or **Restore**; **Mark all read** clears everything unread that does not need you. A notice you have read is archived automatically 30 days later, and an archived notice is kept for 90 days.

**Notices that need you.** A patient assigned to you, a countersignature waiting for you, a note returned to you and a change of supervisor are marked **Needs action** and carry an **Acknowledge** button. They stay in **Unread** — reading them is not enough — until you acknowledge them or the thing they ask of you is done (the visit is checked out, the note is countersigned), when they clear themselves. **Mark all read** leaves them where they are and tells you how many. A notice that needs you cannot be archived until you have acknowledged it. Acknowledging is recorded against your name.

**Pop-ups.** When you are signed in, a new notice also appears as a card in the corner of the screen, with **Acknowledge** or **Open** where they apply. Notices that were already waiting when you opened the page do not pop up. While the bell is open nothing pops up over it, because a new notice joins the list you are looking at (an emergency still does).

**New lab order.** When a lab order is placed, the lab scientists and phlebotomists on shift now are told (everyone in those roles if your rota names no one), with a pop-up that says **New lab order** and opens the lab. It never names the patient, and several at once show as one pop-up. A clinic that has muted **Patient** does not send it.

**Your own choices.** Under **Settings → Notifications**, the **My notifications** card has three groups, saved the moment you change a switch: **Clinical activity** (**Show in my bell** and **Pop up** for **New lab order**), **Assigned to you** (**No pop-up for notices that need me**, and **Email me when a patient or shift is assigned to me**, a short email that names no one) and **Everything else** (**Show in my bell** and **Pop up** per category). These sit inside your clinic's switches above, and are kept per clinic.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide on the master switch first">
    Leave **Email notifications** on unless you want to pause everything non-essential at once. Turning it off gives you "All non-essential clinic emails are paused. Receipts, security, and billing alerts still send."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off the categories you do not want">
    Work down the list and switch off anything nobody reads. Each saves immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on the two email-only preferences">
    **Achievement emails** are milestone and badge mail. **Daily operational digests** are the owner's morning roll-up, and that is where expiring reagents and low stock surface — think before switching it off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

| Setting | What you'll notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** off | Non-essential lab email stops. In-app notifications carry on — this switch is about email only. |
| A category switched off | Both the email and the in-app notification for that category stop. Turning it back on resumes future notices; it does not deliver the missed ones. |
| **Daily operational digests** off | The morning inventory summary stops arriving. Expiring reagents still appear on the inventory **Alerts** tab — you just have to go and look. |
| **System** | Never changes. The tab states it plainly: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic." |

**Transactional email is never affected by anything on this tab.** Receipts, password resets, invitation emails, security alerts and billing warnings send regardless of every switch here. If you have turned everything off and still receive mail, that is why — and it is correct.

**Nor is a released report.** Sending a report to a patient or a referring clinic is an action someone takes, not a notification preference; it is unaffected by these switches. See [Releasing reports](/lims/lab/reports).

These preferences belong to the lab, not to you personally. Turning off **Billing** here turns it off for every colleague. Your own account-level email preferences are on [your account's notification settings](/platform/account/notifications).

## Check it worked

Reload the tab; every switch should have kept its position. Then cause a notice you left on — accept a pending staff invitation, for example — and confirm it arrives, while a category you switched off stays quiet.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I turned everything off and still get email">
    You are receiving transactional mail — receipts, security alerts, billing and password resets. Those are deliberately outside these switches.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The System switch will not move">
    It is fixed on by design. See the line at the foot of the card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no Insurance switch">
    A lab has no insurance module, so the category is not shown. A colleague on the clinical edition sees it; you will not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A referring clinic stopped receiving reports">
    That is not this tab. Report release is an action, not a notification — check the referrer's own record and [Referring clinics](/lims/business/referrers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A switch flips back after a moment">
    The save failed and the switch rolled back, with an error toast. Check your connection and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I stopped seeing expiring-reagent warnings">
    **Daily operational digests** is off. Turn it back on, or read them on the inventory **Alerts** tab — see [Inventory settings](/lims/settings/inventory).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
