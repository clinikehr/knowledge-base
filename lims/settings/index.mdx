Settings is where you set the things that apply to the whole lab: the name and address printed on your reports, who works here and what they may do, which laboratory workflow your bench runs, how patients pay you, and how long your data is kept and exported. Everything on this page is lab-wide — nothing here changes a single order.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings']} />

## Getting in, and who can

**Settings** sits at the foot of the sidebar, under the modules. Opening it needs an **owner**, a **manager** or an **accountant**. Anyone else — including a bench scientist with full result-entry rights — sees "You don't have permission to access the settings module. Please contact your clinic administrator." instead of the screen.

On a desktop the screen opens with a left rail titled **Lab Settings**, strapline "Configure your professional workspace", holding collapsible groups of tabs. On a phone that rail collapses into a single dropdown. Tabs deep-link with `?tab=`, so a link someone sends you opens the right one — and a link to a tab this edition does not have quietly opens **General** rather than showing an error.

A manager can open every tab a lab has, but four money controls are **owner-only**: **Currency**, **Tax Rate (%)** and the free-clinic switch render for a manager but will not take an edit. See [Billing, plans and taking payment](/lims/settings/money).

<Note>
  The settings screen says **Client** where the rest of your lab says patient. Its third section is headed **CLIENT EXPERIENCE** and the portal tab is labelled **Client Portal**, while the sidebar title reads **Lab Settings** and its first section reads **LABORATORY**. Same people, two words. We use "patients" throughout these pages and quote the screen's own wording whenever we name a control, so you can match what is in front of you.
</Note>

## The four sections a lab has

| Section | Groups | Tabs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LABORATORY** | Profile · Team · Inventory · Lab workflow · Laboratory notifications | **General Settings**, **Staff & Teams**, **Inventory**, **Lab Workflow**, **Notifications** |
| **BILLING** | Laboratory Billing · Payment processing | **Billing & Plans**, **Online Payments**, **Payment Details** |
| **CLIENT EXPERIENCE** | Client portal · Documents | **Client Portal**, **Sharable Documents** |
| **SYSTEM** | Data & Security | **Security**, **Data Export** |

**Inventory** appears when the Inventory care area is switched on for your workspace. **Lab Workflow** exists only on Diagnostics — no other edition has it.

A lab has no **AUTOMATION & AI** section, because both of the tabs that section holds belong to the clinical edition.

## Looking for something that isn't here?

Settings changes by edition, so several tabs people ask for genuinely do not exist on a Diagnostics workspace:

| Tab | Where it lives |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto Pay**, **Memberships**, **Store Page** | Clinic & Hospital edition only |
| **Appointment Services**, **Booking Settings**, **Booking Page Designer**, **Contact Form** | Clinic & Hospital edition only |
| **Communications**, **AI Settings**, **Calendar Integrations**, **Insurance Settings** | Clinic & Hospital edition only |
| **Loyalty Program** | Pharmacy edition only |
| **Facility**, **Theatre**, **Ward Management**, **Departments**, **Role Navigation** | Enterprise plan of the Clinic & Hospital edition |

If you followed a link or a bookmark to one of those, you will land on **General Settings** rather than an error page — that is the fallback working, not a fault. To compare what each edition and plan includes, see [Plans and editions](/platform/plans/overview).
