The **CLIENT EXPERIENCE** section is what your patients see of the lab from outside it: a portal where they can collect their own reports, and the documents that go out with an appointment. Two tabs — **Client Portal** and **Sharable Documents**.

<Note>
  This section is headed **CLIENT EXPERIENCE** and the tab is labelled **Client Portal**, while the rest of your lab calls the same people patients. There is no second group of people — the settings screen simply uses a different noun here.
</Note>

## Set up the portal

**Client Portal** — "Client access settings" — is organised into seven tabs:

| Tab | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Branding** | How the portal looks and what it is called |
| **Appointments** | Whether patients can book and manage appointments |
| **Messaging** | Secure messages between patients and your team |
| **Telehealth** | Video consultations from inside the portal |
| **Documents** | Which documents patients can see and download |
| **Notifications** | What the portal emails patients about |
| **Privacy** | What is visible and what stays internal |

Work through them in order the first time. For the full setup walkthrough, see [Turn on the patient portal](/portal/setup/enable); for what each feature does once it is live, see [Portal features](/portal/setup/features).

<Warning>
  Once the portal is published, the publish button becomes a green pulsing **Live** badge — and selecting it **unpublishes** the portal. It is a toggle, not a status light. A patient who follows their link to an unpublished portal cannot sign in and cannot collect a report, and nothing tells you it happened except the badge changing. Leave it alone once it is live.
</Warning>

## Grant a patient access

Publishing the portal does not put anybody in it. The tab's settings decide what the portal **can** do; access is still granted to one patient at a time from their own record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish the portal">
    Configure the seven tabs, then publish. The button becomes the green **Live** badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the patient's record">
    Find the patient and open their profile from the patients list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant portal access">
    Turn on portal access for that person. They receive their invitation and can sign in from then on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for anyone else who needs it">
    Portal access is per patient. A patient nobody has granted it to has no way in, however complete your settings are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This catches people out regularly: the clinic-wide switch is a capability, not an invitation list.

## Sharable Documents

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Sharable Documents']} />

**Sharable Documents** — "Default docs for appointments" — does exactly what its own instruction says: "Select which documents are automatically sent to clients during appointments."

Select the documents you want to go out every time, and they are attached to appointments without anyone remembering to do it. Typical choices for a lab are your preparation instructions, a consent form, and a privacy notice. Anything you do not select can still be sent by hand from the patient's record when it is actually needed.

Keep the automatic set small. A patient who receives five documents for a routine blood draw reads none of them, including the fasting instructions that would have saved you a repeat sample.

## Check it worked

- The portal shows the green **Live** badge, and opening its address in a private browser window loads your branding rather than an error.
- A patient you granted access to receives their invitation and can sign in.
- A released report appears in that patient's portal — see [Produce and release a report](/lims/lab/reports).
- Booking an appointment sends exactly the documents you selected, and no others.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A patient says the portal link does not work">
    Check the **Live** badge first — the portal may have been unpublished by someone selecting it. Then check that the patient has been granted access individually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient signs in but sees nothing">
    Their records are attached but nothing has been released to them yet, or the **Documents** tab is not sharing that document type. Release the report, then check what the **Documents** and **Privacy** tabs allow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I turned on messaging but nobody can message us">
    Messaging is a portal feature and a per-patient access matter both. Confirm the feature is on in **Messaging**, then confirm the patient has portal access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Documents are not going out with appointments">
    Only documents selected on **Sharable Documents** are sent automatically. Anything else has to be sent from the patient's record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Patients are getting too many portal emails">
    Trim them on the portal's **Notifications** tab. That is separate from your team's alerts, which live on the **Notifications** settings tab — see [Your lab profile and notifications](/lims/settings/lab-profile).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find these tabs">
    They are under the **CLIENT EXPERIENCE** heading, which is the third section in the settings rail. On a phone, open the settings dropdown and scroll to it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
