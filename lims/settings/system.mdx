Three tabs that protect the lab rather than run it: **Security** and **Data Export** under the **SYSTEM** section, and **Inventory** under **LABORATORY**, which is where the rules governing your reagents and consumables live.

## Security

"Clinic-wide security rules" — the settings that apply to everyone who signs in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide on two-factor">
    **Require Two-Factor Authentication** makes every member of your team confirm a second factor. On a lab holding patient results this is worth the small friction; a shared bench computer is exactly the machine a password alone does not protect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let people register passkeys">
    Passkeys let staff sign in with the device's own fingerprint, face or PIN rather than typing a password on a gloved hand. See [Two-factor authentication and passkeys](/platform/security/mfa).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave security alerts on">
    Security alerts tell you about new sign-ins and changes to the account. They are the only warning you get that someone else is using a colleague's credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning the requirement on takes effect at the next sign-in, so warn the bench first — a scientist who arrives at 6am to a two-factor prompt they were not expecting will call you rather than set it up.

## Data Export

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Data Export']} />

"Export clinic data as CSV or ZIP. Large exports run in the background…" — your own data, in a format you can open elsewhere.

Choose what to export, start it, and leave the page — a large export keeps running after you navigate away and tells you when it is ready. Use it for an accountant who wants figures in a spreadsheet, a regulator asking for records, or your own archive.

An export is a copy of patient data. Store the file where you would store the records themselves, and delete it when you are done. See [Export your data](/platform/security/data-export).

## Inventory

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Inventory']} />

"Alerts, dispensing rules & reset" — for a lab, this governs **reagents and consumables**. The tab appears when the Inventory care area is switched on for your workspace.

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Expiry alerts** | How far ahead of an expiry date you want warning |
| **Dispensing enforcement** | Whether the product blocks a draw against stock that is expired, quarantined or absent |
| **Danger Zone** | Resetting your inventory entirely |

Set the expiry window to your real lead time. A reagent you can reorder in three days needs more than three days' warning; one that ships from abroad needs weeks. Dispensing enforcement is what stops a run consuming stock it should not — leave it on unless you have a specific reason not to.

<Note>
  A newly received reagent lot arrives **quarantined** when it is set to require lot-to-lot validation. It shows in your stock and cannot be used until somebody validates it — that is intended, and it is why a delivery can sit on the shelf looking unusable. Release it from the validation queue. See [Reagents and consumables](/lims/inventory/reagents).
</Note>

<Warning>
  The **Danger Zone** reset wipes your inventory. It asks you to type `RESET` to confirm, because there is no undo and no restore — your reagent catalogue, stock levels, lot numbers and expiry dates all go, and the validation history with them. Use it only on a workspace you are deliberately starting over, never to tidy up a mistaken receipt.
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- With the two-factor requirement on, sign out and back in: you are asked for the second factor.
- A colleague who has registered a passkey can sign in with it.
- A small export finishes and downloads; a large one keeps running while you work elsewhere and notifies you when it is ready.
- A reagent approaching your expiry window raises an alert with enough notice to reorder.
- A quarantined lot cannot be drawn against until it is validated.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Someone is locked out after I required two-factor">
    They need to complete the second-factor setup at sign-in rather than being let past it. Walk them through it on the phone — see [Two-factor authentication and passkeys](/platform/security/mfa).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My export never arrives">
    Large exports run in the background and finish out of sight. Check for the notification before starting another one — a second run of the same export does not make the first any faster.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find the Inventory tab">
    It appears only when the Inventory care area is switched on for your workspace. Ask the owner to enable it — see [Care areas](/platform/team/care-areas).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stock is on the shelf but cannot be used">
    The lot is quarantined awaiting lot-to-lot validation. Release it from the validation queue — see [Reagents and consumables](/lims/inventory/reagents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expiry alerts arrive too late to act on">
    Widen the alert window to more than your reorder lead time, not less.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I typed RESET and lost my catalogue">
    There is no undo. Rebuild the catalogue from your last export, which is the reason to take one before any large change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
