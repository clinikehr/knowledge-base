Some people only want one test or one scan and will never be your patient. Choose **Walk-in** instead of searching for a patient, record who they are, and the request goes to the bench like any other. A walk-in is **not added to your patients** — they do not appear in Patient Management, your patient counts or your patient limit, and they never receive reminders or marketing messages.

## Request a test or scan for a walk-in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Walk-in">
    On the lab ordering form ([Lab Reports](/lims/lab/reports)) or the imaging ordering form ([Imaging requests](/lims/imaging/requests)), the first card has a **Patient** / **Walk-in** switch. Select **Walk-in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record who they are">
    Enter **Full name**, **Sex** and either **Age (years)** or **Date of birth (optional)**. **Phone (optional)** and **Email (optional)** may be left empty. If you give a date of birth the age is worked out from it. There is no "unidentified" option: a result is a clinical record, and the report has to say who it is for. If the client gives a phone or email **and agrees**, tick **OK to message me when my results are ready** so you can tell them later that their results are ready to collect (see [Tell them their results are ready](#tell-them-their-results-are-ready)). Never tick it for them. A local number such as 0803... is read using your clinic's country, set in **Settings → General**, and saved with its country code (+234803...). If no country is set, enter the country code yourself, for example +234..., or text messages cannot be sent to that number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the tests and take payment">
    Add the tests or studies exactly as you would for a patient. Tick **Collect Payment Now** to take payment at the desk — walk-in requests are meant to be paid at the counter. If a request is left unpaid, it waits in **Pending payments** (see below). Then select **Create Lab Orders** (or **Create &amp; Pay**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the slip">
    The receipt names the client and prints their number in the form **Walk-in W-000123**. Each clinic numbers its walk-ins from **W-000001**. That number is what the client quotes when they come back for the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each walk-in request gets its own walk-in number. If the same person has a lab test and a scan on one visit, put the tests on one request where you can; if they register later, link each of their walk-in records to the one patient.

## Find a walk-in client

On **Lab History** and **Radiology History** there is a **Walk-in clients** tab beside **By Patient** and **All Records**. It lists one row per client — their number, name, sex and age, how many requests, the latest status, and what has been paid of what is due — with a search box for name, number or phone. Select **Open** for the client's record: their details, every lab test and imaging order with its result, and its payment state.

The list on [Test Requests](/lims/lab/test-requests) shows a walk-in's request with the client's name, their walk-in number in the ID column, and **Walk-in client** as the type. Results are entered, reported and downloaded exactly like any other request; the report carries the walk-in number where a patient's report carries their patient ID.

## Take payment later

If a request was captured offline, or payment was skipped, open the client from **Walk-in clients**. Under **Take payment**, choose the payment method and select **Record payment**. You can also take it from **Payments → New Payment → Walk-in clients**. Walk-in clients who owe are listed in the **Walk-in clients** tab of **Pending**.

## When a client has not paid yet

An unpaid walk-in order is listed on the **Walk-in clients** tab of **Pending** on the Payments page, with a **Walk-in** badge, their walk-in number and what they owe. Select **Process payment** to open **New Payment** on the **Walk-in clients** tab for that client.

## Pay before the sample is collected, and hold the result until paid

Two switches under **Settings → Billing**, in the card **Walk-in clients: payment**, decide how strict your front desk is. Only an owner or manager can change them.

- **Pay before the sample is collected.** While it is on, an unpaid walk-in test or scan cannot be started: moving it on from its first status is refused with "This walk-in client has not paid yet. Take payment before the sample is collected or the scan is done." Cancelling is never blocked, and an order with nothing to pay is not held up. Owners and managers can still let an unpaid order through, and so can anyone you give **Let walk-in tests go ahead before payment** in the permissions sheet.
- **Hold the result until it is paid.** The result is still entered as usual. On the report screen the **Download** and **Report** buttons are greyed out and read **Held until paid**, with a **Collect payment** link that opens **New Payment** for that client. If a report is requested anyway it is refused: "This result will be available once the walk-in client's payment is complete." Paying lifts the hold at once.

Clinics created from October 2026 start with both switches **on**. A clinic that existed before keeps working exactly as it did, with both **off**, until its owner turns them on.

## Tell them their results are ready

If the client agreed to be messaged at the counter, open them from **Walk-in clients**. The **Tell them their results are ready** card offers **Email notice** and **Text notice** once at least one test or scan is completed. You confirm before it goes. The message says only that their results are ready to collect at your clinic and quotes their walk-in number; it contains no results, test names or amounts. A text uses your clinic's text credits.

- The link in the message opens a page that shows your clinic's name and phone and the walk-in number, and nothing else. It expires after 14 days.
- Each client can be sent at most three notices a day to the same address or number.
- A client who did not agree cannot be messaged; the card says so.
- On a test workspace such as Staging a notice is recorded but nothing is delivered, and the card marks it "test, not delivered".

## When a walk-in registers as a patient

When you register a new patient and a walk-in has the same name and the same phone number, a message asks **Link walk-in W-000123?** and says how many tests and scans would move. Choose **Link walk-in** to attach their tests, results and payments to the new patient, or **Not now** to leave things as they are. Nothing is linked unless you confirm. The link is recorded in the [Audit log](/platform/security/audit-log). An owner or manager can still link later from the client's record.

## Link a walk-in to a patient

When someone you tested at the counter registers as a patient, an owner or manager can attach their history to their record. Open the client, under **Link to patient** search for the patient and select them, then confirm **Link to patient**.

- Their tests, scans and the payments for them move onto the patient's record.
- The walk-in record is kept, with who linked it and when.
- It cannot be undone, and a client can be linked only once.

## Who can do this

| Action | Who by default | The governing permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request a lab test or scan for a walk-in | Anyone who can order lab or imaging | The lab and radiology ordering permissions |
| Take payment | Owners, managers, and staff with the permission | **Collect payments** |
| Link a client to a patient from their record | Owners and managers | Role |
| Accept the link offered when a matching patient is registered | Anyone who registers patients | Role |
| Let an unpaid walk-in order go ahead | Owners and managers; others when you switch it on for them | **Let walk-in tests go ahead before payment** |
| Turn pay-first and result hold on or off | Owners and managers | Role |
| Send a results-ready notice | Anyone who can open the walk-in's record | Role; the client must have agreed |
| Open the client's record | Lab and imaging staff | Role |

Opening a walk-in's record is recorded in the [Audit log](/platform/security/audit-log) against the walk-in's number, never their name.

## Check it worked

After you create the request, leave the form and open **Lab History** → **Walk-in clients**: the client should be there with their number. Search for them in **Patients**: they should not be.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“This walk-in client has not paid yet…”">
    Your clinic requires walk-in clients to pay before the sample is collected. Take payment from **Pending payments** or the client's record, then try again. An owner or manager can let it through, or give you the **Let walk-in tests go ahead before payment** permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“This result will be available once the walk-in client's payment is complete.”">
    Your clinic holds results until they are paid. The result is saved; take the payment and the report can be downloaded and printed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Walk-in switch is missing">
    It appears when you start a **new** request. On a request that already exists the card shows the client's details instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“Enter the client's name, sex and age or date of birth.”">
    One of the required fields is empty. Name, sex and age (or date of birth) are all needed; phone is not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Send is missing on a walk-in's report">
    A walk-in has no patient record to send to, so a report cannot be sent securely. Download or print it, or link the client to a patient first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment failed after the request was created">
    The request is saved and waits as unpaid. Open the client under **Walk-in clients** and use **Take payment**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I register them as a patient straight away instead?">
    Yes — choose **Patient** and use **Select Patient**. Walk-in is for people you do not want to register.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is a walk-in's record kept?">
    As long as any lab record: seven years. It is included in your data export under **Walk-in Lab &amp; Imaging Clients**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as a walk-in test in Lab History?">
    No. A **Walk-in** test in **All Records** is a registered patient tested without a visit or ward stay. A **walk-in client** is someone who was never registered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work offline?">
    Yes for the request: it is held on the device and sent when the connection returns, and the walk-in number is assigned then. Payment is never taken offline, so settle it under **Take payment** once you are back online.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
