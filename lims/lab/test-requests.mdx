A test request is the record everything else hangs off: the specimen you accession against it, the results you enter, the report you release and the invoice you raise. **Test Requests** is the plain working list of them — today's requests, in one table.

## What you're looking at

The page opens with a greeting — "Good Morning, \{your name\}!" — over the line **Here's a list of all lab requests for today!**. Below that is the table and nothing else. This screen is deliberately simple.

The toolbar holds **Search for patient**, **New Test Report**, a refresh button (**Refresh table**) and **View Columns**, whose menu is headed **Toggle columns** and offers **Patient ID**, **Patient Name**, **Status**, **Conducted By**, **Requested By**, **Date**, **Patient Type**, **Test Name** and **Result**.

| Column | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **Pending** or **Completed** — those two, and no others |
| **Conducted By** | The scientist who ran it, or **Not conducted yet** |
| **Requested By** | The clinician who ordered it |
| **Result** | The recorded value, once there is one |

<Note>
  **Everything richer lives on [Lab Reports](/lims/lab/reports), not here.** The four-status ladder (**Pending**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**), the **Critical** badge, the **Paid** / **Partial** / **Pending** payment badges, **Select Lab Test**, **Order Summary** and the **\{n\} / \{n\} parameters filled** counter are all on that page. If you are looking for one of them, you are on the wrong screen.
</Note>

**The empty state.** A new lab sees **No lab test data found.** — correct until the first request is raised. **No recent lab test data available for this clinic.** means the same thing.

## Raise a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order form">
    Select **New Test Report**. It takes you to the full ordering form on [Lab Reports](/lims/lab/reports), where the price and the parameters are in front of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the patient and the tests">
    Fill in the patient, then add each test from the catalogue. If the patient has no record yet, create one first — see [Register your first patient](/lims/get-started/first-patient).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submit the order. It appears back here as **Pending**, ready for the bench.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work a request from the table

Each row's menu offers **View Lab Results** and, for a lab scientist only, **Conduct Lab Test** on a pending request or **Update Lab Test** on a completed one. On a phone the same menu is shorter: **View Results** and **Conduct Test**.

Selecting one opens a full page rather than a panel, headed **Conduct Lab Test**, **View Lab Results** or **Update Lab Results** to match what you are doing. It carries a **Patient Information** card and a **Laboratory Tests** card, and under **Test Parameters** a field per analyte — **Enter test result**, **Enter \{parameterName\}** or **Select value** for a list — each showing **Reference: \{range\}**. Finish with **Submit Lab Results** or **Update Lab Results**; **Cancel** leaves it untouched.

<Note>
  Only lab scientists may conduct or update a test through this path. Everyone else who opens a request gets read-only view mode and the message **Only lab scientists can conduct or update lab tests**. That is a role limit, not a fault — see [Roles](/platform/team/roles).
</Note>

## Raising a request with no connection

Order capture works offline. Raise the request as normal and it queues on the device, then syncs when the connection returns. The offline bar reads **Offline · reference data \{staleness\} — new lab orders queue and sync on reconnect** and carries a **Sync now** button.

Two things change offline. Patient search is reduced to name and patient number and returns at most 25 matches, so search precisely. And payment is never taken offline — a replayed order arrives unpaid and settles at the desk. Accessioning, screening, result entry and verification all stay online, because they are status-guarded steps whose conflicts need a person at the bench. See [Working offline](/platform/apps/offline).

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Test Requests** opens, and whether the working actions appear on a row. Permission decides what happens on the form those actions open.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Test Requests** and read the list | Owner, Manager, **Lab Manager**, **Accessioning Clerk**, lab scientists, phlebotomists, receptionists | Role → route |
| Raise a request with **New Test Report** | The same people | Role → route |
| **Conduct Lab Test** / **Update Lab Test** on a row | Lab scientists only | Role — everyone else gets view mode |
| Record readings on the form | Lab scientists — everyone else is in view mode | Role → route — no permission on this form |
| Move the test to **Completed** | **Whole lab**, **All sites** | Permission — **Verify and authorise results** |
| Change a result after completion | **All sites**, or a named verifying scientist | Permission — **Amend a verified result** |

**A Lab Manager does not hold verify by default.** Managing the lab and signing a result out are different jobs, and verification arrives with the *access tier* — **Whole lab** or **All sites** — not with the administrative role. A **phlebotomist** reaches this page, the intake queue and patients, and nothing else in the lab; a **lab scientist** reaches the whole bench but is denied **Executive**, **Lab Analytics** and **Referral Commissions**.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Somebody never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"** — from then on they can only do what is switched on there.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A figure you are not entitled to see is hidden or rendered as a dash, never as a zero.

See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The request appears in **Test Requests** with the status **Pending**.
- The **Lab Station** **Analytics** tab counts it under **Total Lab Tests** and **Pending Tests** ("Awaiting processing").
- The patient's record lists the request.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The test I want is not in the search">
    The search only offers tests in your catalogue. Add it under **Lab Station › Test List** — see [Add your tests and panels](/lims/get-started/tests-and-panels).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The order has no price">
    Price lives on the catalogue test, not the request. Open the test in **Test List** and set a price on the **Basic Information** tab; new orders then price themselves.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Conduct Lab Test is missing from the row menu">
    You are not signed in as a lab scientist, or the request is already **Completed**, where the action becomes **Update Lab Test**. Opening it anyway gives **Only lab scientists can conduct or update lab tests**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find the Critical badge or the payment status">
    They are not on this table. Both live on [Lab Reports](/lims/lab/reports), along with the **In Progress** and **Cancelled** statuses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My offline order has not appeared">
    Open the offline bar and select **Sync now**. If the bar shows a rejected count, the order needs a person to look at it — rejected items are never retried silently. See [Working offline](/platform/apps/offline).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This is a drug test, not a clinical one">
    Regulated collections go through the DOT workflow, which carries its own chain of custody and forms. See [DOT testing](/lims/dot/index) and [Lab intake mode](/lims/dot/lab-intake-mode).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does this list only show Pending and Completed?">
    Because it is the day's working list, not the reporting surface. A request is either waiting for the bench or done. The fuller status ladder — including **In Progress** and **Cancelled** — is on [Lab Reports](/lims/lab/reports).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does New Test Report add a test to my catalogue?">
    No. It orders a test for a patient. To define a new test and its analytes, use **New Test Record** on **Lab Station › Test List** — see [Add your tests and panels](/lims/get-started/tests-and-panels).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a receptionist raise a request?">
    Yes. Raising is not a bench act, so anyone who reaches the page can order a test. Conducting and updating it are limited to lab scientists.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I amend a result after it has been reported?">
    Only with **Amend a verified result**, and the change is recorded alongside the original. Tell a verifier rather than working around it — see [Enter and verify results](/lims/lab/results).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do drug-testing requests appear?">
    Not here. They populate the [Worklist](/lims/lab/worklist), the drug-testing bench queue, which is empty for a lab doing only clinical work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
