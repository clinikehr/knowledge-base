A report is the document that leaves the building. It is produced from a completed test, so the work of getting it right happens on [Enter and verify results](/lims/lab/results) — this page covers producing it, finding it later, and getting it to whoever asked for the test.

## What you're looking at

The heading is **Lab Reports** — "Manage and track laboratory test results" — with a **New Report** button and a period select ("Select period") offering **Today**, **Last 7 Days**, **This Month** and **All Time**. The period scopes everything below it.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The four stat cards, plus **Test Volume Trends**, **Status Distribution** and **Recent Lab Reports** |
| **Lab Reports** | The **Lab Test Reports** table — "Complete list of all laboratory tests" |
| **Analytics** | **Test Type Distribution**, **Completion Rate Trend** and **Performance Summary** |
| **Reports** | Document generation — **Daily Summary**, **Monthly Report**, **Critical Results**, **Pending Tests**, **Performance Report** and **Custom Report** |

<Note>
  **Two of the four tabs are called Reports.** The second reads **Lab Reports** on a wide screen and shortens to **Reports** on a phone; the fourth is always **Reports**. The one you want for a patient's result is the table — **Lab Test Reports** — not the document-generation tab at the end.
</Note>

The stat cards read **Total Tests** ("\{n\}% from last week"), **Pending Tests** ("Awaiting processing"), **Completed** ("\{n\}% completion rate") and **Critical Results** ("Requires attention").

The table carries a **Search reports...** box and a status filter of **All Status**, **Pending**, **In Progress**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**.

**The empty state.** When the chosen period holds nothing you get **No lab reports found for this period** — widen the period before you conclude a report is missing.

## Produce the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the completed test">
    From **Lab Test Reports**, use the row menu's **View Details**. A test that is not yet **Completed** has nothing final to print.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Select **Download report**. The button reads **Preparing…** while the document is built, then the file arrives. The report is assembled from the saved record — your lab's details, the patient, each analyte with its reference range, and the verifying scientist's credentials. A second **Download Report (PDF)** button in the right-hand column does the same thing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print or send it">
    From the row menu, **Print Report** prints the clinical report and **Print Receipt** prints the money slip. They are different documents — do not hand one out in place of the other.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A report produced before the test is signed out is stamped as preliminary, and a preliminary report must never leave the building looking final. Do not remove the stamp, screenshot around it, or pass a preliminary result on as a final one. Verify the test first, then produce the report.
</Warning>

## Releasing to a referring doctor

A doctor who sent you the patient sees results through the referrer portal, and there is **no separate release button**. The portal shows a clinical lab test the moment its status reaches **Completed** — signing the test out *is* the release, which is why the verify grant is the one that matters here. A test still pending or in progress is listed to the referrer without its result.

So the thing to get right is the sign-out, not a hand-over: verify the test, and the referring doctor has it. Set the doctor up first — see [Referring doctors](/lims/business/referrers) — and, if you pay for referrals, [Referral commissions](/lims/business/commissions).

<Note>
  The permissions sheet offers **Release a report to a referrer**, and the **Referrer Liaison** role holds it by default. Nothing reads it — no screen in the product performs a per-report release. It is **Verify and authorise results** that decides whether a referrer can see a result.
</Note>

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Lab Reports** opens. Permission decides whether a report may leave the lab.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Lab Reports** and read the tiles | Owner, Manager, **Lab Manager**, lab scientists | Role → route |
| Download or print a report for a test you can see | The same people | Role → route |
| Sign the test out so the report is not preliminary | **Whole lab**, **All sites** | Permission — **Verify and authorise results** |
| Correct a report after it was authorised | **All sites**, or a named verifying scientist | Permission — **Amend a verified result** |
| Open a referrer's account and statements | **Referrer Liaison**, **Lab Manager**, **All sites** | Permission — **Access the referrer portal** |
| Put a result in front of the referring doctor | **Whole lab**, **All sites** | Permission — **Verify and authorise results**; there is no separate release act |

**A Lab Manager does not hold verify by default** — authorising a result arrives with the *access tier* (**Whole lab** or **All sites**), not with the administrative role. That matters more than it looks: since signing out is what publishes the result to the referrer, the tier is what decides whether a referring doctor ever sees it. A **QC Officer** is review-only by design.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Somebody never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"** — from then on they can only do what is switched on there.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A figure you are not entitled to see is hidden or rendered as a dash, never as a zero.

See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The downloaded file opens as a report, not a short error file. If it is tiny and unreadable, the download did not complete — try again.
- The report carries no preliminary stamp.
- The test appears under **Recent Lab Reports** on the **Overview** tab.
- The referring doctor can see it in their portal.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The report says preliminary">
    The test is not signed out. Complete and verify it — see [Enter and verify results](/lims/lab/results) — then produce the report again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No credentials appear under the signature">
    The verifying scientist has no licence recorded on their profile. Ask them to add it under their account, then reproduce the report — see [Your profile](/platform/account/profile).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The download produced a tiny unreadable file">
    That is a failed download rather than a report. Retry it; if it keeps happening on one test, note the test and [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No lab reports found for this period">
    The period select filters everything on the page. Widen it to **All Time**, and clear the status filter, before assuming the report is gone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The referring doctor cannot see the result">
    There is no release button to press. The referrer portal shows a clinical lab test only once its status is **Completed**, so the test is still pending or in progress — or the doctor is not set up as a referrer on the order. Sign the test out (needs **Verify and authorise results**) and check the referrer on [Referring doctors](/lims/business/referrers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This is a DOT report">
    Regulated drug-testing reports have their own release path, including the MRO and employer steps, and their own rule about what may be called **Positive**. See [DOT reports](/lims/dot/reports).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a result before it is verified?">
    No, and the product will not help you pretend otherwise — an unsigned test produces a report stamped preliminary. If a clinician needs the number urgently, telephone it and say it is preliminary, then send the final report once it is signed out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Print Report and Print Receipt?">
    **Print Report** is the clinical document — analytes, reference ranges, the verifying scientist's credentials. **Print Receipt** is the money slip on 80mm till roll. They are produced by different parts of the product and are never interchangeable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does downloading a report tell the patient or the referrer?">
    No. Downloading gives you a file and nothing more. A referrer only sees a report once it is released into their portal, which is a separate, separately-granted act.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do the tiles here differ from the Lab Station ones?">
    They count the same work with slightly different labels — this page says **Total Tests** and **Completed** where the **Lab Station** says **Total Lab Tests** and **Completed Tests** — and each obeys its own period or date range. Match the ranges before comparing them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reissue a report after correcting a result?">
    Yes. Amend the result with **Amend a verified result**, then produce the report again; it is rebuilt from the saved record. The original result and the correction both remain in the record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
