## What you're looking at

The toolbar carries **Receive**, **Dispense**, **Transfer**, **Reports**, **Import**, **Opening stock**, **Export** and **Add item**. The tabs split the module by question:

| Tab | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | What do we stock at all? |
| **Stock** | How much is on hand, and where? |
| **Ledger** | Every movement in and out |
| **Counts** | Physical counts and what they corrected |
| **Fill Rx** | Prescriptions waiting to be filled |
| **Purchasing** | What is on order from a supplier |
| **Vouchers** | Stock issued against a funding source |
| **Recalls** | Batches pulled from use |
| **Locations** | The rooms, cabinets and fridges stock lives in |
| **Alerts (\{n\})** | Low stock and approaching expiry, counted in the tab, and a **Ledger reconciliation** card for records whose stock was never taken off the shelf — open a row to take it off, or mark it reconciled with a reason |
| **Reconciliation** | Where the ledger and the shelf disagree |

Three more appear only when your own stock calls for them, not because of your plan: **Validation** for reagent lots, **Vaccines** once you hold a vaccine-type item, and **Ward MAR** if you run wards. A banner sits above the tabs when something needs attention: "\{n\} open inventory alerts — see the Alerts tab."

A brand-new module shows empty states, not an error: "No items yet. Add your first product (e.g. Botox 100U vial).", "No stock on hand. Receive a lot to get started." and "No open alerts. You're all stocked up."

On the **Free plan** a strip across the top counts you against each limit — **20 items**, **5 locations**, **2 vendors**, **5 purchase orders** — as badges like "12/20 items", with **Upgrade for unlimited** beside them.

## Add a catalogue item

An item is the *thing*; stock is the quantity of it: "A catalog entry — e.g. 'Botox 100U vial'. Stock is tracked per lot."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add item and name it">
    **Name** is required — write it the way your team says it out loud, not as a supplier's code. **Barcode / UPC (optional)** lets a scanner find it later, and **Look up drug (NDC or name)** pre-fills a medication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classify it">
    Set the **Type** — Injectable, Skincare / Cosmetic, Medication, Vaccine, Consumable, Supply, Device, Reagent or Kit — the **Unit** you count it in (required), and a **Category (optional)**. The unit is the one decision that hurts to change later: vials and millilitres are not the same thing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seed the shelf if you already hold some">
    **Opening stock (optional)** with **Into location** puts what is already there into the ledger — "Received at the cost price above." Give it an **Expiry date** (required when **Track by lot / expiry** is on) and, optionally, a **Lot / batch number (optional)**. **SKU (optional)** is your own reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the reorder rules">
    **Low-stock threshold** — "Alerts at/below this on-hand." — is what makes the **Alerts** tab useful. **Reorder qty**, **Unit cost** and **Sale price** feed purchasing, valuation and the store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the handling switches">
    **Refrigerated**, **Track by lot / expiry** for anything with a shelf life, and **Witness required (controlled)** for anything a second person must countersign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receive stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Receive">
    The **Receive stock** sheet — "Add a delivered lot to a location." Choose the **Item** and the **Location**, and enter the **Quantity**, labelled with the item's own unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the funding source">
    **Funding source** records who paid — **Private stock**, **VFC (Vaccines for Children)**, **Section 317** or **State-supplied**. It is what makes the **Vouchers** tab meaningful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the lot and expiry">
    **Lot number** and **Expiry** identify the batch. **Scan package (GS1)** reads both off the supplier's barcode instead — "Auto-fills item, lot & expiry from the package barcode."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    On-hand rises on the **Stock** tab and a receipt line appears in the **Ledger**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stock leaves the same way it arrives: **Dispense** draws it for a client, **Transfer** moves it between locations, and both say how they pick — "(earliest-expiring lot first)".

<Warning>
  Picking is always **earliest-expiry-first**, and a lot with no expiry date has nothing to sort on — so it is invisible to the picking order *and* to every expiry alert, and sits on the shelf until somebody finds it by hand. Turn on **Require expiry date on new lots** in [Inventory settings](/practice/settings/inventory).
</Warning>

For lots and expiry in depth see [Batches and expiry](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry); for supplier ordering see [Purchase orders](/pharmacy/inventory/purchase-orders).

## Who can do this

A solo or team practice has 21 staff permission toggles, and none of them is about stock. Nor does this edition have a role→route layer. So **Inventory** is open to everybody in the practice, and what limits it is your **plan**.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Inventory** | Any staff member | Nothing — the page is not role- or permission-gated |
| Add an item, receive, dispense or transfer | Any staff member | Plan — Free caps you at 20 items, 5 locations, 2 vendors and 5 purchase orders |
| Run a count and post its adjustment | Any staff member | Nothing |
| See cost, valuation and margin | Any staff member | Nothing |
| Change how expiry and receiving behave | Owner, manager or accountant | Role — it is a settings tab |
| Use the Danger Zone on that tab | Owner | Role — owner-only |

Every paid plan lifts the capacity limits to unlimited; a lapsed or cancelled subscription drops you back to the Free caps.

Fencing colleagues out of stock screens by role is an Enterprise feature — it needs the Enterprise plan plus role-scoped navigation switched on. See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Filter the Catalog and Stock tabs

Both tabs have a filter bar above the list. Every filter is applied to your whole inventory, not just the page on screen, so the count above the list — "12 items", or "3 stock lines match your filters" — is the true total and the page buttons follow it. Search still works and narrows within whatever filters you have set.

**On a computer** the main filters sit in a row: **Type**, **Category**, **Stock level**, **Status** and **Expiry** on **Catalog**; **Location**, **Lot status**, **Expiry**, **Quantity** and **Type** on **Stock**. **More filters** opens the rest. **On a phone or tablet** a single **Filters** button, with a badge showing how many filters are on, opens them all in a panel. Choices apply as you make them; **Done** closes the panel.

**Catalog** can be filtered by:

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | One or more item types, such as medication, supply or vaccine |
| **Category** | One or more categories, including **Uncategorised** |
| **Stock level** | **In stock**, **Low (at or below par)** and **Out of stock**. Low means you hold some, and no more than the par level set on the item |
| **Status** | **Active items** (the default, so archived items stay out of your way), **Archived** or **All items**. Archived items show **Inactive** in the list |
| **Expiry** | Items with expired stock, or stock expiring within 30, 60 or 90 days |
| **Properties** | **Controlled**, **Refrigerated**, **Hazardous** and **Sold at the till**. Pick several and an item must have all of them |
| **Barcode** | **Has a barcode** or **No barcode** — handy for finding what a scanner cannot read |
| **Medicine index** | Items **Linked to the medicine index** or **Not linked** |
| **Manufacturer**, **Default supplier** | Items from the makers and suppliers you choose |
| **Has stock at** | Items you hold in the locations you choose |
| **Sale price**, **Cost** | A minimum and a maximum. **Cost** appears only if you are allowed to see cost prices |

**Stock** can be filtered by **Location**, **Lot status** (active, quarantined, recalled, expired, depleted), **Expiry** (**Expired**, **Within 30 days**, **Within 60 days**, **Within 90 days**, **No expiry date**), an **Expires between** date range, **Quantity** (**Zero quantity**, **Has reserved units**, **Fully reserved**, **Item below par**), **Type**, **Category**, **Funding source** and **Item**. **Item** searches as you type, by name, barcode or SKU, so it finds any product however large your catalogue is.

Each filter you set appears as a chip under the bar. Select a chip to take that one off, or **Clear all** to start again (on **Catalog**, back to **Active items**). **Sort** orders the list — by name, on hand, price or recently updated on **Catalog**; by item, expiry, quantity or location on **Stock**.

Your filters are kept in the page address, so a refresh keeps them, the back button returns to them, and you can send the link to a colleague — it opens the same tab with the same filters. If the filters hide everything, the list says **No items match these filters.** (or **No stock lines match these filters.**) with a **Clear filters** button.

<Note>
  Cost filters follow the same permission as cost figures. If you are not allowed to see cost prices, there is no **Cost** filter or cost sort, and the list never uses cost to narrow what you see.
</Note>

## Check it worked

- The item is on the **Catalog** tab and can be found by name or barcode.
- After receiving, the **Stock** tab shows the quantity in the right location, with its lot and expiry.
- The **Ledger** has one line per movement, and the **Alerts (\{n\})** count reacts when you set a threshold above current stock.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Some of this stock is past its expiry date and cannot be transferred.'">
    The shelf holds the quantity you asked for, but part of it has passed its expiry date, and **Transfer** only moves stock that can still be used. Transfer a smaller quantity, or remove the expired stock first with **Waste** or **Return to vendor**. Your count is not wrong — expired stock is also written off automatically overnight.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transfer says some of this stock is close to expiry">
    This is a warning, not a refusal — **Transfer** never blocks stock that is merely close to its date, only stock already past it (see above). Once you enter a quantity, the form lists which lot it would come from, how much, and how many days remain, soonest first, right under **Quantity**. If the whole quantity would come from short-dated stock it says so plainly. The **Transfer** button stays enabled and nothing extra needs confirming — moving short-dated stock to a busier location is often the right call. "Close to expiry" is your practice's own expiry-alert horizon under **Settings** › **Inventory** › **Expiry alert horizons**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot add another item">
    "Your Free plan includes up to 20 items. Upgrade to a paid plan for unlimited inventory." The same caps apply to locations, vendors and purchase orders — see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An expiring lot never appeared in Alerts">
    Its lot has no expiry date, so nothing can compare it to a horizon. Correct the lot, then turn on **Require expiry date on new lots** in [Inventory settings](/practice/settings/inventory) so it cannot recur.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The shelf and the Stock tab disagree">
    Run a count on the **Counts** tab; the adjustment it posts is recorded in the **Ledger** with a reason. See [Stock counts](/pharmacy/inventory/stock-counts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dispense will not let me save">
    Either there is no stock in the location you chose, or the item is **Witness required (controlled)** and needs a second person to countersign.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inventory disappeared from my sidebar">
    Not something a solo or team practice can do to itself: the sidebar entry here carries no care-area switch, and there is no per-role navigation editor on these plans. Reload; if it is still missing, note the time so support can check it. (Care areas *do* hide Inventory on Enterprise — see [Care areas](/platform/team/care-areas).)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I load my existing stock list rather than typing it?">
    Yes — **Import** takes a file of catalogue items, and **Opening stock** seeds what is already on the shelf. See [Import and export](/pharmacy/inventory/import-export).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the online store draw from these numbers?">
    Yes. Stock and cost are read from here and are not editable in the store — see [Run an online store](/practice/money/store).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a location, exactly?">
    Any place stock physically sits — a consulting room, a cabinet, a fridge. Locations are what make **Transfer** and a per-room count meaningful.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to track lots?">
    No. Turn **Track by lot / expiry** off for things with no shelf life, such as instruments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I see what my stock is worth?">
    The **Inventory** report — valuation, turnover and expiry risk. See [Reports and analytics](/practice/insights/analytics).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
