## What you're looking at

**Telehealth** — "Manage video consultations with your patients" — opens on four counters: **Total Calls**, **Total Minutes**, **Avg Duration** and **Completion Rate**. **New Call** sits at the top right, and below that are four tabs.

| Tab | Holds | When it is empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Scheduled calls, badged **Scheduled**, **Waiting** or **In Progress** | "No upcoming calls — Schedule a telehealth call to get started" |
| **Recent** | Finished calls, badged **Completed**, **No Show** or **Cancelled** | "No recent calls — Completed calls will appear here" |
| **Recordings** | Finished recordings | "No recordings yet" |
| **AI Notes** | Notes drafted from recordings | "No AI notes generated yet" |

## Schedule a call

Select **New Call**. This opens a full page, **Schedule Telehealth Call** — "Create a new video consultation with a patient" — not a pop-up, so **Back** is how you leave without saving.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the client">
    Under **Patient Information**, open **Select Patient** and search ("Search patients..."). **New Patient** registers somebody who is not on your list yet.

    A client with no email address triggers a warning in place: "This patient doesn't have an email address. Please add one to send the invite, or disable 'Send Invite Email' below."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time">
    Under **Schedule Details**: **Date**, **Time** in 15-minute slots, **Duration** (15 minutes up to 2 hours) and **Room Type** — **Consultation**, **Group Therapy**, **Team Meeting** or **Webinar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what the room can do">
    **Room Settings** has five switches: **Waiting Room** ("Patients wait until you admit them", on by default), **Recording** (off), **Screen Sharing**, **Chat** and **Send Invite Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context, then create it">
    **Room Notes** under **Additional Notes** holds anything the session needs. Select **Create Room**: "Telehealth Room Created — Invite email sent to patient". The call appears under **Upcoming**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each **Upcoming** row has a **Join** button and a **⋯** menu offering **Send Invite**, **Copy Link**, **Mark as No Show** and **Cancel**.

## Host the call

Select **Join** and you go straight into the room — there is no lobby for the host. The client gets one: **Ready to join?**, where they check camera and microphone before selecting **Join Call**. Your own start screen shows a microphone level bar and a **Play test sound** button, so you can check them before you join; you can ignore both and join.

The top bar carries the room code, a red **REC** badge whenever recording is running, **Notes** (host only), **Copy Link** and **Settings**. Along the bottom: mute, camera, **Background** effects, **Screen Share**, **Raise Hand**, **Chat & Participants**, and **Leave call** for everyone. **Leave call** drops only you: the call stays open and you can rejoin with the same link, from the **Rejoin call** button on the screen you land on. Beside it, the arrow opens a menu with **End call**, which only the host sees (not a co-host, and not the client). Choosing it asks **End this call for everyone?** first; **End call** then removes everyone right away, the link stops working and a recording that is running stops. Nobody can rejoin after that. **Leave call** keeps the call open; **End call** finishes it for everyone. If everyone has left and nobody comes back within 30 minutes, the call finishes on its own, as if the host had ended it, and the link stops working.

With a waiting room on, an amber card tells you "\{n\} people are waiting to join", with **Admit** and **Deny** per person and **Admit everyone** when there is more than one.

## How many people can join

The plan sets how many people can be in one call: **Free** 2, **Essential** 12, **Team** 20, and **Enterprise** has no plan limit. A group session holds the booked group plus its clinicians, never more than the plan allows. Everyone connected counts, staff and clients alike.

When the call is full, the next person waits in the lobby and sees "This session is full" instead of being turned away. You see them in the waiting list marked **Session full - waiting**, with a note saying which plan allows more. Nobody is removed automatically; remove someone, or ask someone to leave, and then admit the person waiting.

A call keeps the limit it was booked with. If you move to a smaller plan, calls you already booked keep their size, and new calls use the new plan. The plan change screen lists the upcoming calls this affects. The 30-day paid Essential trial has the full Essential limit.

## Breakout rooms in a group session

During a **Group Therapy** call, select **Rooms and settings** to split the group (up to 8 rooms, at least 2 people in each). Place people yourself or split them at random, and choose whether they may pick their own room. Rooms use first names or the names you give them, so never put a client's name or details in a room name.

A room with no clinician in it shows **Not monitored** to the people in it. You confirm that once before opening such a room. A room with anyone under 18 always needs a clinician or co-host in it. Anyone in a room can select **Ask for help**; **I need the clinician now** sounds an alert for you and brings that person straight back to the main room, and you are reminded to document it. If someone drops out of a room, you are told, and when they return they join the main room. You can visit any room; everyone in it is told you are there. After 60 seconds of countdown, everyone returns to the main room. Each client's time in the main room and in rooms is added up for their own attendance record at the end of the call. Rooms are not recorded unless everyone in the room has agreed, and a room with a minor also needs a guardian's agreement.

In **Rooms and settings** you also choose who can share a screen and whether private messages between clients are allowed. Private messages start off whenever anyone under 18 is in the call. Chat is not kept after the call.

### Plan the rooms ahead

Before the call, open **Upcoming**, select **⋯** on the group session and choose **Plan breakout rooms**. Name the rooms and drag each person into one (or pick the room from the list beside their name). The list also shows the practice staff booked on the session, marked **(staff)**, so you can put a co-facilitator in each room, which keeps rooms with anyone under 18 staffed. For a supervision meeting it shows the colleagues invited to it. Tick **Use this plan for every session in this recurring series** to reuse it each week. When the call is open, select **Rooms and settings**, then **Use the saved plan**: people who have not joined are left out, and you can still change anything before you select **Open the rooms**. On a phone you can split people at random; placing people one by one is for a tablet or computer.

### Private messages

Select **Private messages** to write to one person in the call. Messages are shown only to that person and are not saved anywhere. The list of people you can write to follows your **Rooms and settings**: when private messages are off, clients can still write to the clinician or co-host.

### Remove someone from the call

The host can remove anyone but themself. A co-host can remove guests (clients, or outside guests in a supervision meeting) but not the host, another co-host or practice staff. Select the **⋯** on their tile, then **Remove from call**. Each removal is recorded.

### Your session scratchpad

Select **Scratchpad** to jot notes while you visit rooms. In a chat, select **Keep this chat** to save what is in it. The scratchpad is private to you and any co-host, nothing in it is part of a client's record, and it is deleted after 7 days. When you write a client's note afterwards, select **Insert from session scratchpad**, tick only the pieces that belong in that client's note, and **Add to the note**. Nothing is added unless you choose it. Use initials or first names in it.

### Bill a group session

When a group session has ended, open **Recent** and select **Bill group session**. Each client who attended gets their own charge for the group service, for their own minutes in the main room and the breakout rooms together. If the payer needs a minimum time, pick the payer under **Payer (optional)** and the minimum saved for it fills in; or type a number under **Payer minimum minutes (optional)**. You can change it for this one run. Clients under it show a warning and are not billed unless you tick them. An owner or manager saves each payer's minimum once: under **Group session minimum minutes** on the payer (US clinics, in **Settings → Insurance → Payers**) or on the insurer (clinics outside the US, **Edit insurer**). Then select the button that reads **Create** and the number of **charges**. Running it again never bills anyone twice, and the client the appointment was booked for is not billed a second time if the appointment already has its invoice. On a US clinic's account the service's code goes on each charge so it carries into that client's claim.

## Live captions

Each person can switch on captions for themselves with the **Captions** button along the bottom of the call. Captions appear as short lines over the video, with the speaker's name, and fade a few seconds after they stop speaking. They are off whenever you join a session, and switching them on changes nothing for anyone else. In a breakout room you see captions only from the people in your room.

- **Nothing is saved.** Captions are shown on your screen and gone when the line fades. They are not added to the recording, the chat, the scratchpad or any note.
- **Everyone is told how many people have captions on**, never who, so anyone speaking knows their words are being turned into text live.
- **Where they work.** Captions are for group sessions, supervision meetings and any session of five or more people. A one-to-one client session does not have captions yet: the button is greyed out and says so.
- **Everyone must be able to see the notice.** Captions are refused for the whole session while anyone in it is using a version of ClinikEHR or the client portal that cannot yet show the "captions are on" notice. Nobody is captioned without being told. The button then explains, and captions come back by themselves once everyone has updated.
- **They use AI minutes.** Captions are charged to the clinic like dictation, once for the whole room however many people are speaking: 5 minutes at a time, from the clinic's agent credits, for as long as at least one person has captions on. The unused minutes of the last block are returned when the last person switches captions off or leaves. If the clinic is out of credits or its plan has no AI, the captions switch off and tell you why. A clinic owner or manager can add credits in Settings.
- **English only for now.** Captions are in English. A language setting for your clinic can follow on request.

## Whiteboard

A whiteboard lets you draw, write and point together during a session, a breakout room or a supervision meeting. Open it with the **Whiteboard** button along the bottom of the call, then select **Turn on the whiteboard**. Everyone in the call is told, and the board opens for them. It needs the Essential plan or above. Nothing is kept unless you save it: when the session ends, the board is gone.

- **Tools.** Pen, highlighter, eraser, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text and sticky notes, in six colours and three sizes. A stylus draws with pressure, and a resting palm or finger is ignored while a pen is on the screen. It works with a mouse, a finger and a stylus at any screen size.
- **Pages.** A board has up to 20 pages. Select **Page** to add one, and the numbers to move between them. Everyone who is following you moves with you.
- **Who can draw.** Select **Rules** to choose **Host and co-host only** or **Everyone**. You can change it during the session, and **Lock the board** makes it look-only for everyone except you and any co-host. Anyone you have not allowed sees **View only** and can still follow along.
- **Undo is personal.** **Undo my last change** takes back only what that person drew. You, as host, can remove anyone's drawing, or select **Clear page** to empty the page for everyone.
- **Laser pointer.** The laser pointer is for the host and co-host: drag it over the page and everyone sees a red dot that fades when you stop.
- **Templates.** Select **Templates** to load a CBT thought record, an ABC model, a 0-10 mood scale or a timeline onto the current page. You can save the page you have prepared as your own template, or share it with the clinic so everyone can load it. Use a blank or template page: do not save a page with a client's details on it as a template. Owners and managers can remove a clinic template.
- **Breakout rooms.** Each room has its own board, and the people in a room see that room's board only.
- **Save to notes.** Select **Save to notes**, choose the pages and select **Save**. Each page is kept in the client's record as a picture, and the editable board is kept with it. For a supervision meeting the same button saves the board to the meeting, with a reminder to make sure nothing identifies a client. The board is not part of any recording, and the AI note taker never sees it.
- **Download.** In **Rules**, choose whether participants may download a copy: **Not participants** or **Everyone**. You and any co-host can always download. Whoever is allowed sees **Download** and can take a PDF of the pages they choose, or the written content as text. Each download is recorded on the session. In a client session people are shown by their display names only.
- **Pictures.** Select **Add a picture** to put a PNG or JPG on the board, or one page of a PDF. Each picture can be up to 10 MB and the pictures on one board up to 50 MB. Every picture is checked for viruses first: you see **Checking picture...**, and nobody else sees it until it passes. One that fails the check, or cannot be checked, is not added. In a call, anyone you allow to draw can add pictures. The button is only there once pictures are switched on for your installation. A picture lives only as long as the session unless you save the board.
- **Share to the client portal.** Open a saved board from the client's record and select **Share to the client portal**, with an optional note. The client sees the saved pages in their portal, can add text, sticky notes and pen drawing to a copy of their own and send it back. Your board does not change, you can **Withdraw** it until it comes back, and every share is recorded. The client gets an email, unless they switched off **Email me when I have new homework or documents** (on by default; you can see and change it for them under the client's contact and notification settings when you edit the client), saying "You have new homework from" your clinic, with nothing about what is on the board, when your client portal and its Documents area are on, email notifications are on for your clinic, and the client has an email address. The sheet tells you if no email was sent and why. A returned copy appears in the client's record as "Returned by the client" with **Open their copy**.
- **Signed records.** Once a note, consultation or psychotherapy note is signed, a board saved to it is added as an addendum: the signed record itself never changes.
- **Psychotherapy notes.** A board saved from a psychotherapy note is protected like the note. Only the person who holds the note sees it. It is not on the client's Files tab, in an export or in the client portal, and it leaves only when the note itself is released: the released note's PDF carries its boards, and the owner's psychotherapy export lists them and gives their pictures in a separate ZIP.
- **Where it shows.** Boards saved to a note are on the note, on the client's Files tab under **Whiteboard**, in the note's PDF as pictures, and in the record export. Boards saved from a video session also show on that session's note, and on the client's blank notes.
- **In person.** On a note, a consultation, a treatment plan or a psychotherapy note, select **New whiteboard** under **Whiteboard** to draw with a client in the room, on a tablet or any screen. It has the same tools and templates, nothing is shared, and it is discarded when you close it unless you select **Save to notes**. Saved boards appear as thumbnails on the note and under **Whiteboard** on the client's Files tab. Open one and select **Edit as a new version** to add to it: the earlier versions stay. Once a note is signed its boards are locked with it, and the save says so.
- **For screen readers and keyboards.** Select **Text list** to read everything written on the page as a list, add a sticky note without a pointer, or select **Export as text**. Press Ctrl+Z (Command+Z on a Mac) to undo and Shift with it to redo.

## Record the session

Select the record button to open the **Start Recording?** sheet:

> Records BOTH sides of the call — every participant's video and audio, plus any shared screen, composited into one file. Everyone in the call is asked to agree first, and recording begins only when they have.

Below it sits an **AI Note Taker** switch with a **Note template** picker when your plan includes it. The switch starts **off** for every recording: the recording goes to an outside AI service, so you turn it on for the recordings you want notes for. The sheet ends with the consent line: "**Consent:** each person in the call sees a request to agree to being recorded. If anyone says no, nothing is recorded. Each answer is saved with the recording."

Confirm with **Start Recording**. Recording does not begin straight away. Each other person in the call sees **Record this session?** with **Yes, I agree** and **No, do not record**, and cannot carry on without choosing. You see who is still to answer ("Waiting for … to accept the recording"), and if the recording does not start you are told whether someone said no or did not answer in time. A short loss of your own connection while recording does not stop it; if you are not back within about 15 seconds it does. When everyone has agreed, recording starts and everyone sees the red **REC** badge. If anyone says no, or does not answer within about 90 seconds, nothing is recorded and the call carries on. Someone who joins while a recording is running sees the badge at once and is asked the same question; until they answer they are kept out of it (their video and voice are left out of the file) until they answer; if they say no, or do not answer, the recording stops, the part already recorded is kept and can be played, and no AI notes are made from it. In **Recordings** that recording carries the note **Stopped — a participant declined recording**. If you leave the call while you are the one recording, the recording stops (leaving while someone else is recording does not stop it), the part already recorded is kept and can be played, and **Recordings** says **Stopped — the host left the call**.

If your clinic has turned off **Require explicit consent to record** in **Settings → Client Portal**, people are still told with the **REC** badge, but they are not asked to agree first. Leave that setting on unless your clinic's policy says otherwise.

**Stop Recording?** ends it and begins processing; the file lands on **Recordings** once that finishes. Each person's answer, with the time and the wording they saw, is kept with the recording and is included in your data export as **Recording Consents**.

<Warning>
  **Say what you are recording and why before you press Start.** The recording captures every participant's video and audio and becomes part of the client's clinical record. ClinikEHR asks each person to agree and keeps their answer, but a clear word from you first is still the right way to start. If anyone objects, do not record.
</Warning>

## Write the note

**Notes** opens a host-only panel headed **Clinical Notes** beside the call, with a template picker defaulting to **Medical clerkship**. It saves as you type — the chip reads **Unsaved**, then **Saved just now** — and the footer states the rule: "Autosaves as you type · saved to the client's chart as a draft".

A call with more than one client gains a **Documenting** switcher, and each chart gets its own note. Before anyone is attached you see "No client linked yet". Finish with **Save Notes**.

Drafts produced by the in-call AI Note Taker arrive on the **AI Notes** tab instead; read them before filing — see [Generate a note with AI](/practice/clinical/ai-notes).

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Telehealth** opens. The plan decides what the room can do, and permission decides who you may document against.

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule and host a call | Any clinician | Role — any plan; Free is capped at 5 calls a month. Once you save a person's permissions: **Host video visits** |
| Record a session | The host | Plan — **Session Recordings**, Essential and above. Once you save a person's permissions: **Record video visits** |
| Open the whiteboard, change who draws or downloads, clear it, add pages, load a template and save it to the record | The host and any co-host | Plan — Essential and above |
| Play, download or list recordings | Any clinician | **View video visit recordings** |
| Delete a recording | Owners and managers | **Delete video visit recordings** — off for everyone else until you switch it on for a person |
| Generate or read AI consult notes from a recorded session | The host | Plan — Essential and above; the upgrade prompt names Team. Once you save a person's permissions: **AI notes from video visits** |
| Document against a client who is not yours | Owner, manager, and staff set to **Full client list** or **Entire practice** | The access tier chosen when they were invited |

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted; the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding **Telehealth** from a sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

The call moves from **Upcoming** to **Recent**, badged **Completed**. A recording appears on **Recordings** a few minutes later, an AI draft on **AI Notes**, and anything you typed in **Clinical Notes** is on the client's chart in [Notes](/practice/clinical/notes). The client's side is described in [Join a video visit](/portal/patients/telehealth).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Telehealth Consultations Limit Reached">
    Every plan includes telehealth links each month: Free 10, Essential 250, and Team and Enterprise unlimited. Links reset on the 1st of each month. A link for a visit that is cancelled before the call starts does not count. When you have used them, the message names your plan, says when the allowance resets and offers an **Upgrade** button to the next plan. **Recordings** and the **AI Notes** tab need Essential and above. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A call is still shown as in progress after everyone left">
    A call nobody ended is closed automatically once it has been idle for 24 hours. It moves to **Completed** and anyone still shown in it is marked as having left. This is done quietly: no follow-up message or workflow is triggered by it, and the client's join link stops working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Patient email required">
    "Please add an email address for this patient to send the invite." Add one to their chart, or switch **Send Invite Email** off and share the link yourself with **⋯** → **Copy Link**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The client cannot get in">
    Resend with **⋯** → **Send Invite** — an old email may carry a stale link. If the room has a waiting room they are already there, waiting for you to **Admit** them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The client never showed up">
    Use **⋯** → **Mark as No Show** so the session closes honestly rather than sitting in **Upcoming**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nobody can see or hear me">
    Check the mute and camera buttons and your devices under **Settings**, close any other application using the camera, then rejoin — an in-progress call offers **Rejoin** on **Recent**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording is not on the Recordings tab">
    A recording is composited after the call ends, so give it a few minutes. If the **REC** badge never appeared during the call, recording never started.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This browser can't play this recording's format">
    Safari cannot play the video format most session recordings use. Select **Download recording** to watch it in another video player, or open **Recordings** in Chrome, Firefox or Edge instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording has expired / is no longer available">
    Recordings are kept for 90 days. Past that, the badge reads **Expired**, and the row shows "This recording expired on [date] and is no longer available" — Play and Download are gone, but you can still delete the row. If a recording that should still be within its 90 days shows "This recording's file is no longer available" instead, its file was lost; deleting the row is the only remaining action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the client need an account or an app?">
    No. They open the emailed link in a browser and select **Join Call**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a call after it has finished?">
    No. **Cancel** and **Mark as No Show** are offered on scheduled and in-progress calls only. A completed call has no row actions; a cancelled one can be deleted from **Recent**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to the note if I switch templates mid-call?">
    Sections that exist in both are kept. Anything you had completed that the new template does not have is cleared, and you are told how many — so switch early rather than late.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the room reusable?">
    Each scheduled call gets its own room and its own link. Schedule a new call rather than reusing an old one. The **Notes** panel is host-only throughout — clients never see what you are writing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
