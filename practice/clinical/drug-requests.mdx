A partner you've connected to — for example a health plan that also runs pharmacies — can send you
a **request** for medicines for one of its enrollees, once that person is linked as your own
customer. You decide what happens to every request; nothing is prepared or set aside until you say
so, and no price ever appears on a request or moves through it. Dispensing always happens at your
own till, the same way as any other sale.

## What you can do with a request

| Action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | You confirm you will prepare exactly what was asked for. Still nothing is set aside. |
| **Accept some lines, decline others** | The request becomes **Partly accepted** — the partner sees which lines you agreed to. |
| **Propose a substitute** | You offer a different medicine for a line, from your own catalogue. The partner has to confirm it before you go any further. |
| **Decline** | You tell the partner you won't fill this request, with a reason they can see. |
| **Mark ready** | You've decided what you'll hand over. This still reserves nothing — it only tells the partner the request is prepared. |
| **Cancel** | You withdraw from a request you'd already accepted or marked ready — for example, if the stock turned out to be needed elsewhere. |
| **Start a sale from a ready request** | Opens a sale at your till with this request in mind. You still confirm every line yourself before completing the sale, exactly like any other sale. |

A request the partner doesn't hear back on by its needed-by date **expires** on its own — nothing is
sent to anyone when that happens.

## Money never moves through a request

A request never carries a price, and accepting one never charges anyone. Payment happens the normal
way, at your till, when the sale is completed — the request is simply linked to that sale afterward
so you and the partner both know it was fulfilled.

## Matching a line to your catalogue

Most lines arrive with a code your system can already match automatically. When one doesn't match —
or matches more than one item — you'll see **Not found in your catalogue**, and you can match or
substitute it by hand when you decide on the request. This never guesses on your behalf.

## While you're offline

Requests can't be accepted, declined, or updated while your connection is down — you'll see your
last-known list, clearly marked, until you're back online. If you start a sale from a ready request
while offline, the sale itself is captured the same way any offline sale is, and it links back to the
request automatically once your till reconnects.
