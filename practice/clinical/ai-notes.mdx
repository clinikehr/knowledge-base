The **AI Note Taker** listens to a session, or reads a file you upload, and drafts a structured note in the format you choose. You then read it, correct it and sign it. It drafts; you remain the author.

For a quicker draft from text you already have — dictation, a transcript, or your own rough notes, with no recording or upload — use the **Draft with EvidenceMD** button inside the note editor instead. It appears only when your clinic has chosen and connected EvidenceMD; on ClinikEHR's own assistant, the AI Note Taker is the way in. See [Choose a note assistant](/practice/clinical/note-assistant).

**The AI Note Taker uses whichever assistant your clinic has chosen** — see [Choose a note assistant](/practice/clinical/note-assistant). When an outside assistant is chosen, your recording is still transcribed here at ClinikEHR; only the resulting text is sent onward to draft the note. Before you record, the sheet tells you which assistant will write the note. If the outside assistant can't answer, nothing is lost — you can draft with ClinikEHR from the same recording instead, without recording again.

## What you're looking at

The sheet opens on **AI Note Taker** — "Generate structured clinical notes from recordings or documents" — with a **How it works** list and a **Consent reminder:** "Always obtain verbal or written consent from all session participants before recording." Select **Get Started** and it walks you through in order: **Select a Template**, **Customize Output**, **Select Method**, then **Generating Notes ✨** and **Notes Ready!**. **Back** and **Next** move between steps.

## Generate the note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    "Choose the note format for your documentation." Search by name, category or keyword. **Blank Note** is an option if you want the AI to write without a structure, and templates your practice built carry a **Custom** chip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise the output">
    | Setting | Options |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Note Tone** | **Professional** (precise clinical terminology), **Empathetic** (person-centered language), **Concise** (brief, focused notes), **Detailed** (thorough documentation) |
    | **Speaker Mode** | **Multi-Party** for a conversation, **Single Speaker** for dictating alone |
    | **Custom AI Instructions (Optional)** | Free text, for example "Focus on medication changes and mental status" |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record, or upload a file">
    **Live Recording** needs microphone permission and tells you "Speak naturally — all participants will be captured". You can pause, resume and stop; the status pill moves through **Recording**, **Paused** and **Complete**.

    **Upload File** takes audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG, WEBM), video (MP4, MOV) and documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD), up to **500 MB**. Drop a file on the box, or select it to choose one. On a phone you can choose a voice memo or a video from your camera roll. Use it for audio recorded elsewhere, or a referral letter you want folded into the note.

    Once a file is accepted, its name and size appear with **Change** beside it. A file the note taker cannot read is refused straight away with the reason under the box, for example "This file type is not supported", and **Generate Notes** stays off. An older Word file (.doc) must be saved as DOCX or PDF first.

    A recording over **500 MB** is refused as soon as you select **Generate Notes**, before anything uploads, and tells you its size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to process">
    Select **Generate Notes**. The recording uploads in small parts, with a progress bar: if your connection drops, it carries on from where it stopped. Keep the tab open until the upload finishes. If it cannot finish, you see **Try again**; it sends the same recording to the same session, so nothing is recorded twice.

    A short recording takes 15–60 seconds. A long one is drafted in the background and can take several minutes: the sheet shows **Drafting your note...** and the note appears when it is ready. Your recording is already saved by then, so you can close the sheet; the session stays in **AI Notes** and the note is there when it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, then apply">
    **Notes Ready!** shows the draft. Read it against what actually happened, then select **Apply to Form** to drop the text into the note form, where you edit and sign as normal — see [Write a clinical note](/practice/clinical/notes). **Re-record / Re-upload** starts again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Closing the sheet asks first while a recording is only on your screen.** If you are recording, paused, have a finished recording that has not been sent, or an upload is still running, closing shows **Discard this recording?** Select **Keep recording** to carry on, or **Discard recording** to lose it. Once the recording is uploaded and the note is being drafted, closing the sheet loses nothing.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Read every line before you sign.** A generated note is written from audio, and audio mishears drug names, doses and negations. A clinician must review and approve it — your signature says the words are yours, not the model's.
</Warning>

## When your included minutes run out

Your plan includes AI Note Taker minutes each month, shared by the whole clinic: 3,000 on Essential (600 during the 30-day paid trial), 5,000 on Team and 10,000 on Enterprise. The meter in the sheet reads **Included minutes used: X of Y**.

- **Beyond the included minutes, each minute costs 2 agent credits.** The meter then reads "Now using agent credits — 2 credits a minute · balance" followed by your balance, and the owner or a manager sees **Buy credits**.
- **The clinic can switch this off.** **Settings** → **AI Settings** → **Use agent credits when AI Note Taker minutes run out**. With it off, AI Note Taker stops when the included minutes run out.
- **You are told before you record.** If the included minutes are used up, or fewer than 15 are left, and the switch is off or the balance cannot cover the rest of a session, the sheet says so before recording starts. You can **Buy credits** (owner and managers), or **Write the note by hand**. If your own place is beyond the plan's included AI Note Taker seats and you have no add-on, the sheet says that too, and the owner or a manager sees **Add the AI Note Taker add-on**. If the sheet cannot check, it says "We could not check whether an AI note can be made for this recording. You can still record." Nothing fails silently.
- **Regenerating a note you already generated costs nothing extra.**

See [AI credits](/platform/plans/credits) for rates, packs and automatic top-up.

## Manage your AI sessions

**AI Notes** in the left rail opens a panel over the page — "Manage your Ambient AI recordings & generated notes". Search by template, file or status, and filter with **All**, **Completed**, **Processing**, **Failed** and **Incomplete**, each showing a count.

Opening a session gives you the recording, the generated note, and a **Regenerate with** template picker:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Regenerate note** / **Retry generation** | Runs the same recording again, optionally against a different template. You will see "Note regenerated" |
| **Stop** | Ends a session that is still running — "Generation stopped. You can retry it or delete it." |
| **Delete** | Removes the recording and its tracking record. A note already saved to **Notes** is kept |
| **Open in Notes** | Takes you to the note it produced |

A session still processing after 15 minutes gets an amber **Stalled** badge and a banner: "This note has been processing longer than expected and was likely interrupted. Stop it, or retry from the recording below." If the sheet is open when that happens, it stops the session for you, with "This note took too long and was stopped. Your recording is safe; select Generate again." The minutes the session had reserved are given back when a session is stopped, so a stopped note is never charged.

### When the note could not be drafted

Sometimes the recording is transcribed but the assistant cannot finish writing the note, for example because it took too long. The sheet then says **Your recording and its transcript are saved. Only the note drafting failed.** and gives the reason in plain words, such as the assistant taking too long or not being reachable. Nothing needs to be recorded again.

- **Draft again from the saved transcript** writes the note from the transcript that was already made. The transcription is not charged a second time.
- **Draft with ClinikEHR instead** appears when your clinic uses EvidenceMD and EvidenceMD could not draft the note.
- From **AI Notes**, **Retry generation** on a failed session does the same: it uses the saved transcript when there is one, and it works even when only the transcript was kept.
- If your clinic uses EvidenceMD and a session has a saved transcript, **AI Notes** also asks **Write the note with**: your clinic's assistant (EvidenceMD) or **ClinikEHR AI**. Your clinic's assistant is selected to begin with. When EvidenceMD was the one that could not write the note, ClinikEHR AI is marked as suggested. Choosing ClinikEHR AI asks you to confirm first, and tells you the cost: the recording is not transcribed again, so no minutes are used; the first time ClinikEHR AI re-drafts a session it is free, and after that it uses 2 agent credits. If your clinic uses only ClinikEHR, there is nothing to choose and the question does not appear.
- A failed session in the list shows the reason in plain words, for example that the assistant took too long or could not be reached.
- Either way the new note is an AI draft. Read and correct it before you sign it.
- If both assistants failed, the sheet lists each reason so you can see what happened with each.

An empty panel reads "No AI notes yet — Generate a note with the AI Note Taker and it'll show up here to manage."

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Notes** opens. Your plan decides whether the AI Note Taker runs: it is included for every clinician on Essential, Team and Enterprise, with no per-clinician charge. The clinic's shared monthly minutes are the only limit, and past them each minute uses agent credits. It is not part of Free. A practice that still holds an AI Note Taker add-on from before keeps it working until it ends. It isn't available on Free.

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the AI Note Taker | Any clinician on Essential, Team or Enterprise | Every clinician on Essential, Team or Enterprise |
| Regenerate or stop a session | The clinician who owns it | The same entitlement — "Regenerating requires an active AI Note Taker add-on" appears when it's missing |
| Apply the draft to a client's note | Any clinical staff member | The access tier chosen when they were invited |
| Sign the finished note | The signing clinician | Role, plus the Essential plan for signing |

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted; the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding **Notes** from a sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions) and [Plans and add-ons](/platform/plans/overview).

## Check it worked

The session shows **Completed** in the **AI Notes** panel, the drafted text is in the note form under the template you chose, and it is attached to the right client. Nothing reaches the chart until you save or sign it — a draft left in the sheet is not documentation.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I am asked to Manage Add-ons">
    "You need an active AI Note Taker add-on to use this feature." You see this on Free: the AI Note Taker is part of Essential, Team and Enterprise. Upgrade the plan — see [Plans and add-ons](/platform/plans/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI Note Taker minutes used up">
    Your clinic has used its included minutes for the month. If agent credits are switched on and the balance is at least 2, recording carries on and uses credits. Otherwise you will see a message before recording, with **Buy credits** for the owner and managers. The same message appears when fewer than 15 included minutes are left and credits could not cover the rest of a session. The allowance resets on the 1st of next month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No client selected">
    Choose the client in the **Quick Note** card before opening the sheet. The note taker files its output against a chart, so it needs to know whose.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The session is stuck on Processing">
    A long recording is drafted in the background and can take several minutes. After 15 minutes the session is marked **Stalled** and, with the sheet open, stopped for you. Select **Stop** if you are not waiting on the sheet, then **Retry generation** from the recording. Stopping gives back the minutes the session had reserved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The upload did not finish">
    Keep the tab open and select **Try again**: it resumes with the same recording and the same session, so you do not record again and no second session is created. If you close the tab while it is uploading, your browser asks first, because the recording would be lost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nothing happens when I choose a file">
    An accepted file shows its name and size under **Upload File**; a refused one shows the reason in red under the box. If you see neither, choose the file again. If the file is a type the note taker does not take (a photo, or a format your phone's recorder uses such as 3GP or AMR), export it as MP3 or M4A, or share it from the recorder app as audio, and upload that.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="File too large">
    The cap is 500 MB — "Try splitting the session into shorter recordings." A long lossless recording will exceed it; export it as MP3 first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The note came back in the wrong format">
    Open the session in **AI Notes**, choose another template under **Regenerate with**, and select **Regenerate**. The recording is reused, so you do not have to see the client again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording stopped playing, or would not play after a pause">
    Select **Load player** on the session in **AI Notes** to listen. The link to a recording lasts a few minutes, to protect it. While the session is open, the player renews it by itself, including after a long pause or when you jump to another point, and carries on from where you were. **Download** always gets a new link when you select it. If the player says "The recording could not be loaded. Check your connection, then select Try again.", select **Try again**: it starts from where you stopped. If it says "This recording is no longer stored, so it cannot be played or downloaded.", the file is no longer there, for example because the recording was deleted. A note already saved from it is not affected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording missed the second speaker">
    Set **Speaker Mode** to **Multi-Party** for a conversation. **Single Speaker** is for dictating on your own after the client has left.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need consent to record?">
    Yes, and the sheet says so twice. Ask before you start, wait for the answer, and do not record if anyone objects. Consent is yours to obtain — the product reminds you, it does not collect it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is anything saved to the chart automatically?">
    No. The draft lives in the session until you **Apply to Form**, and it only becomes documentation when you save or sign the note.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to the recording?">
    It stays with the session so you can replay, download or regenerate from it. **Delete** removes the recording and the session but leaves any note you already saved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use it during a video call?">
    Telehealth has its own note taker built into the call — see [Run a video session](/practice/clinical/telehealth).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
