You do not have to retype the same patient, referring provider or payer on every claim. Once a claim has been confirmed, its patient, providers and payer are available to fill the next one, and a whole claim can be copied for a patient you bill regularly.

## Fill from an earlier claim

On an open claim, the **Fill from an earlier claim** card lets you find a patient by member ID, or pick a provider or payer you have billed before.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the patient">
    Under **Member ID (box 1a)**, type at least three characters of the member ID and select **Find patient**. Matches show the name and "Member ID ending" the last four characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the patient">
    Select **Use this patient**. The patient's details are filled into the claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a provider or payer">
    The list shows **Referring provider (box 17)**, **Rendering provider (box 24J)** and **Payer (box 11c)** from your earlier claims. Choose one to fill it in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what was filled">
    The boxes are filled **for you to check**. They join **To check**, and nothing counts until you confirm it. See [Review and confirm a claim](/practice/claims/review-a-claim).
  </Step>
</Steps>

"No earlier confirmed claim has that member ID." means nothing matched. The lists are empty until the clinic has a confirmed claim: "Providers and payers appear here once the clinic has a confirmed claim."

## Copy a claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a claim">
    Any claim, in any status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Copy this claim">
    You see "Claim copied" and "Set the date of service on each line, then check it." A new draft opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the dates and check it">
    The patient, provider, diagnoses and service lines are kept. The dates of service, the claim number and any original claim number are cleared, and the copy is a normal original claim, never a correction. Confirm every box, then mark it ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To correct or cancel a claim Medi-Cal already has, use [Corrected claims and voids](/practice/claims/corrected-claims-and-voids) instead.

## Check it worked

- The filled boxes appear in **To check** with the source of the value.
- A copied claim opens as a **Draft** with empty dates.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Type a little more of the member ID'">
    Enter at least three characters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Fill from an earlier claim card has nothing in it">
    Nothing has been confirmed yet. Confirm a claim first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'The claim could not be copied'">
    Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support with the time it happened.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing a saved patient confirm the fields?">
    No. They are filled in as your own typing, unconfirmed. You still confirm each one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the saved patients stored?">
    They are not a separate list. They come from the claims your clinic has already confirmed, so you manage them by managing claims.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
