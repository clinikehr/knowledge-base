Approval is the second pair of eyes. A biller prepares a claim and marks it ready; someone allowed to approve says it can go into a **production** file. A **test file** takes claims that are ready or approved, so approval is not needed to test.

## Approve claims

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the claims">
    For one claim, select the **⋯** menu at the end of its row and choose **Approve**. It shows only on a claim whose status is **Ready**, and only if you are allowed to approve. A claim with problems to fix shows it greyed out, with "Fix N problems first". For several claims, tick them, or **Select all on this page**, then open the menu beside **Generate** and choose **Approve** with the number you ticked, for example **Approve (3)**. Claims with problems to fix are left out, and the confirmation says how many.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog says (for one claim it reads "this claim" and "it") "Approving says you have checked these claims and they can go into a production file. Only claims marked ready can be approved, and changing an approved claim later withdraws its approval." Select **Approve 3 claims**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see how many were approved, for example "3 claims approved. 1 was skipped.", and a second message listing why any were skipped:

| Reason shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "is not marked ready" | Open it, confirm every field and mark it ready |
| "still has a field nobody has confirmed" | Open it and confirm the remaining boxes |
| "was confirmed by you, so someone else must approve it (an owner, a manager or another approver)" | Ask someone else to approve it |
| "could not be found, or you do not have access to it" | Reload the list |

## Who can do this

| Action | Who by default | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approve claims | Owners and managers | **Approve claims for a file** |
| Make a production file | Owners and managers | **Make production Medi-Cal files** |
| Make a test file | Anyone whose role opens Medi-Cal Claims | None |

Other staff do not have either permission until an owner or manager gives it. Give them from **Staff & Teams**, in a person's **Permissions**, under **Claims**. See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions). Enforcement starts for a person when you save their permissions.

**A biller cannot approve a claim they confirmed themselves.** The claim is skipped, and another approver, an owner or a manager has to do it. An owner or manager can approve their own claims, so a solo practice can still work alone.

## Check it worked

- The toast reads "N claims approved."
- The claims show the status **Approved**. The step bar moves to **Generate**.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Approve in the menu">
    Your role does not have **Approve claims for a file**. Ask an owner or manager to give it to you, or to approve the claims.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'You cannot approve claims'">
    "Approving a claim needs permission at this clinic. Ask an owner or manager."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'No claim was approved'">
    "A claim must be marked ready, with every field confirmed, before it can be approved." Open the claims and finish the review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An approved claim went back to Ready">
    Changing an approved claim withdraws its approval. Approve it again after the change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does approving send anything to Medi-Cal?">
    No. Approving only says the claim may go into a production file. Nothing leaves ClinikEHR until you download a file and upload it yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to approve claims before a test file?">
    No. A test file takes claims that are marked ready.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
