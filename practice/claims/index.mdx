ClinikEHR Claims makes the electronic file Medi-Cal takes from the claims you already have on CMS-1500 forms. You add the claims, check every field, approve them, make the file, and **upload it to Medi-Cal yourself** in the Medi-Cal Provider Portal.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Medi-Cal Claims']} />

## What it does, and what it does not

| ClinikEHR Claims does | You still do |
| --- | --- |
| Reads fillable CMS-1500 PDFs and CSV exports, and gives you a typed form beside a scan | Check every field against the original |
| Checks procedure codes, modifiers, units and diagnoses and tells you what looks wrong | Decide what to do about each finding. The checks are advice |
| Makes a **test file** (free) and a **production file** | Upload the file in the Medi-Cal Provider Portal |
| Keeps your claims, your files and a count of what this month has used | Download Medi-Cal's reports from the Provider Portal and add them under [Reports](/practice/claims/reports) |

There is no clearinghouse in the middle. ClinikEHR does not send anything to Medi-Cal for you, and it has no way to see your Provider Portal account. ClinikEHR is not certified or approved by Medi-Cal; **Medi-Cal decides** whether your Submitter ID is active, whether your test passes and whether each claim is accepted.

## The four steps

The bar at the top of **Medi-Cal claims** shows where you are: **Add claims**, **Review**, **Generate**, **Upload to Medi-Cal**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get set up with Medi-Cal, once">
    Ask Medi-Cal for a Submitter ID, register in the Provider Portal and send the test claims. See [Get set up with Medi-Cal](/practice/claims/get-set-up).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add claims">
    Drop in PDFs, pictures or a CSV, or type a claim. See [Add claims](/practice/claims/add-claims).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Open each claim, compare it with the page, confirm every field and run the code checks. See [Review and confirm a claim](/practice/claims/review-a-claim) and [Approve claims](/practice/claims/approve-claims).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the file and upload it">
    Make a test or production file, download it and upload it in the Provider Portal. See [Make a Medi-Cal file](/practice/claims/make-a-file).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

Test files are free. A claim in a production file uses clinical credits the first time it goes into one: **25** on Free, **15** on Essential, **10** on Team, included on Enterprise. See [Make a Medi-Cal file](/practice/claims/make-a-file#what-a-file-costs) and [Clinical credits](/platform/plans/clinical-credits).
