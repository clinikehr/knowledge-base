## Start on Free, or try Essential

Most new clinics land on a setup screen offering two paths: **Start on the Free plan**, with no card and no time limit, or **Try Essential — \$29.90 for 30 days**, which needs a card and converts automatically to the full Essential price afterwards unless you cancel. See [What Free includes](/platform/plans/overview#what-free-includes) and [Try Essential — the paid trial](/platform/plans/overview#try-essential-the-paid-trial).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Start on the Free plan, or Try Essential">
    Free needs no payment details. Try Essential takes you to checkout for the \$29.90 (or ₦29,900) trial charge; the card you enter there is the one billed \$99.90/mo from day 30.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or start it later from the plans page">
    If you are already past that screen, open the plans page and use **Try Essential** there instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The trial is once per clinic, and a clinic that has ever had a paid subscription is not offered it again. Once used, you see a message to that effect and the only route onward is a paid plan.
</Warning>

## Compare and choose a paid plan

Select **Or compare plans** from the welcome screen, or open the plan step from the checklist. The page is headed **Choose Your Plan** (or **Manage Your Subscription** if you already have one).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the billing period">
    The **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle sits above the cards, with the yearly discount badge next to it. The cards restate it — "Billed annually at \{total\}/yr" or "Billed monthly • Cancel anytime".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the paid plans">
    **Essential** and **Team** are on the page; **Essential** carries the **Most Popular** badge. Your current plan's card shows the **Current Plan** badge and its button is disabled. A clinic still on the legacy Starter plan sees its own card marked **Legacy plan** — see [Legacy plans](/platform/plans/legacy-plans).

    The differences that matter most day to day are how many staff seats are included before extra seats are charged, how many client records you may hold, and whether the AI features are included. Full breakdown in [Plans overview](/platform/plans/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the plan">
    The button reads **Upgrade to \{Plan\}** if you are on the free plan, or **Select \{Plan\}** otherwise, then **Processing...** while the payment session opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    You are handed to the payment provider — "Redirecting to Payment / Setting up secure payment...". Complete it there and you return to your clinic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Upgrading to Team while the Essential trial is running ends the trial immediately and charges the Team price in full — the trial fee is not credited.
</Note>

## What happens when the trial ends

Nothing is deleted. If you cancelled during the 30 days, your clinic moves to Free at the end of them — see [What Free includes](/platform/plans/overview#what-free-includes). If you did not cancel, the trial converts automatically to a full Essential subscription at \$99.90/mo (₦129,900/mo) and billing continues from there.

## Check it worked

- The banner at the top of the plans page reads **Active \{Plan\} Plan** with an **Active** or **Trial** badge, and either **Next billing: \{date\}** or **Trial active until \{date\}**.
- The **Get Started** checklist ticks **Choose a paid subscription plan**, and the step's own line changes to "You are currently on the \{Plan\} plan" — with **(Trial Mode)** appended while the trial runs.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Trial temporarily unavailable'">
    Payments are not configured for the environment you are on. This is on our side, not yours — [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid but the plan has not changed">
    Payment confirmations arrive within a minute or two. Reload the plans page once; if the banner still shows the old plan after five minutes, [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) with the time of payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My plan shows '(Past Due)'">
    A renewal payment failed, and staff capacity is restricted until it clears. Use **Fix Payment** to update the card — "Your subscription payment is past due."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need more staff seats, not more features">
    Essential includes 3 seats and Team includes 5; extra seats beyond that are charged monthly, up to Essential's cap of 5. Some roles — supervisor, biller and scheduler — neither count toward the cap nor cost anything. See [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone other than me needs to pay">
    Only the owner can change the plan. A manager sees the plans page but not a working checkout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
