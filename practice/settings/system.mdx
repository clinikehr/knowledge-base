## Security

**Security** ("Clinic-wide security rules") sets the rules everyone in your practice signs in under.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your own second factor first">
    Turn on two-factor for your own account before you require it practice-wide. Enforcing a rule you have not satisfied yourself is how an owner locks themselves out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Require Two-Factor Authentication">
    Everyone in your practice must then complete a second step at sign-in. Staff who have not set one up are asked to do so before they can continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review passkeys">
    Passkeys let a person sign in with a device rather than a password. They are convenient, but a passkey on its own does not satisfy the two-factor requirement — someone using one still completes a second step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your security alerts">
    Alerts tell you about sign-ins and changes worth noticing. These are transactional and are sent regardless of what you have muted under **Notifications**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full walkthrough, including recovery, is in [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa).

## Data Export

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Data Export']} />

**Data Export** ("Export clinic data as CSV or ZIP") is how you get your records out — for an accountant, for a migration, or before you close an account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to export and in which format">
    CSV for one dataset you intend to open in a spreadsheet, ZIP for everything together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export and leave it running">
    The tab says it plainly: "Export clinic data as CSV or ZIP. Large exports run in the background…" You do not have to sit on the page while it works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file when it is ready">
    Download links do not last indefinitely. Save the file somewhere durable as soon as it is available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Export before you close an account.** Closing a practice account removes access to its records, and an export requested afterwards is not something support can produce for you. Take the export first, confirm you can open it, and only then close.
</Note>

Details are in [Export your data](/platform/security/data-export).

## Inventory

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Inventory']} />

**Inventory** ("Alerts, dispensing rules & reset") only appears when the Inventory care area is switched on. It covers "Expiry alerts, dispensing enforcement and inventory data controls".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your expiry alerts">
    Decide how far ahead you want warning that stock is going out of date. Too tight and you find out the week it expires; too loose and the alerts become noise you stop reading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how strictly dispensing is enforced">
    Enforcement decides what happens when someone tries to dispense stock the system does not think you have. Stricter settings stop the action; looser ones let it through and leave you to reconcile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the Danger Zone alone unless you mean it">
    The **Danger Zone** at the bottom holds a full inventory reset, and it requires you to type `RESET` to confirm. That confirmation exists because the action does not ask twice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The inventory reset deletes your inventory data and cannot be undone. There is no restore, and an export taken afterwards will not contain what the reset removed. Take a **Data Export** first, and only reset a practice you are genuinely starting over.
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- Sign out and back in. With **Require Two-Factor Authentication** on, you are asked for the second step.
- A colleague signing in is prompted to set up their own second factor if they have not already.
- Your export downloads and opens — check the file has content rather than assuming it did.
- After changing expiry alerts, stock inside your new window shows as expiring.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I turned on two-factor and now cannot get in">
    Use the recovery route described in [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa). Set up your own second factor before enforcing the rule to avoid this entirely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A staff member says two-factor is blocking them">
    They have not finished setting up a second factor. They are prompted to at sign-in; they must complete it before reaching their work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Signing in with a passkey still asks for another step">
    Expected where two-factor is required. A passkey does not on its own satisfy the requirement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My export has been running for a long time">
    Large exports run in the background and can take a while. Leave the page — it does not need to stay open. If it never completes, start a smaller export of one dataset to narrow down where it stalls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The download link no longer works">
    Links expire. Start the export again and download it promptly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Inventory tab is not in my sidebar">
    It appears only when the Inventory care area is switched on for your practice. If you do not stock anything, there is nothing to configure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The reset button will not activate">
    You must type `RESET` exactly into the confirmation field. Anything else leaves the button disabled — deliberately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
