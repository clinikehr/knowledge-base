Two tabs in the **PRACTICE** section: **General Settings**, which holds the details clients actually see, and **Notifications**, which decides what lands in your inbox. Get both right early — the first prints on documents, the second is what people mute in frustration six months later.

## Fill in your practice details

**General Settings** ("Clinic profile and basic info") carries your name, address, phone, email, website, description, logo and regional preferences.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the four details that matter most">
    **Clinic Name**, **Address**, **Phone Number** and **Email Address**. These four print on invoices, receipts and your public pages, and filling all four is what ticks the setup step. **Address** is one free-text box — write it as you would on an envelope, postcode included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    A square image with a white or transparent background works best. It is scaled to fit a document header, so a wide banner ends up small.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your Timezone">
    This is the one to get right before you start operating. **Timezone** decides what time an appointment means, where the boundary of a business day falls, and when scheduled doses are due. Changing it later does not move anything already booked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the optional extras">
    **Website** and a description. The description can appear on your public booking page, so write it for a client, not for a colleague.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save Changes**. A toast confirms the update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Currency is not on this tab — it lives on **Billing & Plans**, along with tax. See [Billing, payments and insurance](/practice/settings/billing-and-payments).

## Choose which notifications you get

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Notifications']} />

**Notifications** ("Alerts and communication") opens with a line stating its job: "Choose which in-app and email notifications your clinic receives."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide on the master switch first">
    **Email notifications** is described as the "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." Leave it on unless you genuinely want everything paused — turning it off is a blunt instrument, and the per-category switches below are the better tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the categories">
    Eight switches, one per kind of event: **Appointment**, **Patient**, **Staff**, **Billing**, **Subscription**, **Document**, **Insurance** and **System**. Each one governs both the email and the in-app alert for that category, so muting **Billing** quietens the bell as well as the inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on the two extras">
    **Achievement emails** cover milestones your practice hits. **Daily operational digests** are the once-a-day summary of what is outstanding. Both are optional and neither carries anything time-critical.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**System cannot be switched off.** The tab says why: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic."

Transactional email is never affected by any of this. Security alerts, password resets and payment receipts go out regardless of what you mute — you cannot accidentally silence the message telling you someone signed in from a new device.

## Check it worked

- Reload **General Settings**. All four core fields still hold what you entered.
- Issue or preview an invoice — your practice name, address, phone and email appear on it.
- Open your [booking page](/practice/daily/booking-page) and confirm the contact details match. That page keeps its own copies in the **Booking Page Designer**, so if it disagrees, edit it there.
- Book a test appointment and check the time reads as you expect. If it is out by hours, the timezone is wrong.
- On **Notifications**, reload and confirm the switches held. **System** should still be on and unmovable.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A field is greyed out and Save does nothing">
    Some fields are owner-only even for a manager. The control renders but stays disabled rather than showing an error. Ask the owner to make the change, or see [Roles](/platform/team/roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Appointment times are an hour or two out">
    The timezone is wrong, or was changed after the appointments were booked. Set the correct **Timezone** and check the affected bookings individually — an existing appointment is not moved when the timezone changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The booking page still shows the old address">
    The booking page holds its own contact block, edited under **Settings › Booking Page Designer** and republished from there. See [Your booking page](/practice/daily/booking-page).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The logo looks stretched or cropped on documents">
    Upload a square image. The document header scales to fit, so a wide logo comes out small and a tall one gets clipped.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Emails stopped arriving after I changed one switch">
    Check the master **Email notifications** switch first — with that off, non-essential email pauses for the whole practice no matter what the categories say. Then check the individual category the missing email belongs to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I muted a category but still get emails about it">
    Some messages are transactional — receipts, password resets, security alerts — and are sent regardless of preference. If the email is genuinely promotional or a digest, check the **Achievement emails** and **Daily operational digests** switches, which are separate from the eight categories.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to stop emails to one person, not the whole practice">
    These switches govern email directed at the practice. A person's own account email preferences live in their user settings, not here.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
