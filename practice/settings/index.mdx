Everything you configure once and then forget about lives here: your practice details, your team, how money moves, what clients see, and how your data is protected. This page is the map — one row per section, and a link to the guide that covers it.

## Getting in, and who can

**Settings** sits at the bottom of the sidebar, in the footer. Opening it needs one of three roles: **owner**, **manager**, or **accountant**. Anyone else gets a single line instead of the screen — "You don't have permission to access the settings module. Please contact your clinic administrator."

Getting in is not the same as being able to change everything. A handful of controls are **owner-only** even for a manager — currency, tax rate, free clinic mode, and most of the publish buttons on public pages. The control still renders; it is simply disabled. If a switch will not move for you and there is no error, that is why.

On a desktop you get a left sidebar headed **Clinic Settings**, with the strapline "Configure your professional workspace". On a phone that sidebar collapses into a single dropdown at the top of the screen. The tabs are identical either way.

<Note>
  The settings screen says **Patient** in a few places where the rest of your workspace says **Clients** — the **PATIENT EXPERIENCE** section heading and the **Patient Portal** tab, for instance. It is the same people. This help centre bolds what is on your screen, so those two read "Patient" here too.
</Note>

## A tab you were sent to is not there

Settings links carry a `?tab=` on the end, and a tab your plan or edition does not include **silently falls back to General** rather than showing an error. So a link that lands you on **General Settings** when you expected something else is almost never broken — it is telling you that tab does not apply to your practice.

## The five sections

| Section | Tabs | Covered in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PRACTICE** | General Settings, Staff & Teams, Inventory, Notifications | [Your practice profile and notifications](/practice/settings/practice-profile) and [Staff and teams](/practice/settings/team) |
| **BILLING** | Billing & Plans, Insurance Settings, Online Payments, Payment Details, Auto Pay, Memberships, Appointment Services, Store Page | [Billing, payments and insurance](/practice/settings/billing-and-payments) |
| **PATIENT EXPERIENCE** | Calendar Integrations, Booking Settings, Booking Page Designer, Contact Form, Patient Portal, Sharable Documents | [Booking, portal and documents](/practice/settings/client-experience) |
| **AUTOMATION & AI** | Communications, AI Settings | [Communications and AI](/practice/settings/automation-and-ai) |
| **SYSTEM** | Security, Data Export | [Security, data export and inventory](/practice/settings/system) |

Two tabs move around. **Inventory** ("Alerts, dispensing rules & reset") only appears when the Inventory care area is switched on, and it is documented with the system settings because of the reset it carries. **Calendar Integrations** ("Sync external .ics calendars") is visible to owners and managers only — an accountant will not see it.

## Not here? It belongs to another edition

People are sent looking for these by a colleague on a different plan. They are not missing — they do not exist for a solo or team practice.

| Tab | Where it lives | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Facility** | Hospital & Enterprise | [Hospital settings](/hospital/settings/facility) |
| **Theatre** | Hospital & Enterprise | [Departments, wards and theatre](/hospital/settings/departments-wards-theatre) |
| **Ward Management** | Hospital & Enterprise | [Departments, wards and theatre](/hospital/settings/departments-wards-theatre) |
| **Departments** | Hospital & Enterprise | [Departments, wards and theatre](/hospital/settings/departments-wards-theatre) |
| **Role Navigation** | Hospital & Enterprise | [Team and roles](/hospital/settings/team-and-roles) |
| **Loyalty Program** | Pharmacy | [Loyalty settings](/pharmacy/settings/loyalty) |
| **Lab Workflow** | Diagnostics | [Lab workflow settings](/lims/settings/lab-workflow) |

The reverse is true of one tab. **Insurance Settings** exists **only** on Solo and Team, and disappears on Enterprise — the two plans run different insurance systems, so an enterprise colleague genuinely cannot see the tab you are describing to them.

To move between plans, see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
