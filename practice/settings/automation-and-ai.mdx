The **AUTOMATION & AI** section has one group, **Communications & AI**, and two tabs under it. The first decides how messages leave your practice. The second decides what your AI agents can reach and what sets them running.

## Communications

**Communications** ("SMS, voice, fax & email channels") is where you turn each channel on and connect the account behind it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review which channels are enabled">
    The tab lists the channels your practice can use. A channel that is off simply does not appear as an option elsewhere in the product — a reminder cannot go by text if text is not enabled here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up messaging and voice">
    Connect the account each one runs on. Until that is done the channel shows as available rather than active, and nothing sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a mailbox">
    You can connect a **Gmail** or **Outlook** mailbox so email from your practice comes from your own address rather than a generic one. You are sent to that provider to sign in and approve access, then returned here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send yourself a test">
    Every channel is worth testing to your own address or number before a client is on the receiving end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Which categories of email go out at all is a separate decision, made under **Notifications** — see [Your practice profile and notifications](/practice/settings/practice-profile). This tab decides *how* a message leaves; that one decides *whether* it is sent.

## AI Settings

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'AI Settings']} />

**AI Settings** ("Agents, credits & MCP tools") covers two things: the external tools your agents may use, and the webhook trigger that lets something outside ClinikEHR start an agent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the external tools you want agents to reach">
    Each connector is added deliberately and can be removed the same way. An agent can only use a tool that has been connected here, so a workflow that depends on one will do nothing until it exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the agent webhook trigger">
    A webhook-triggered agent **will not fire until the trigger is enabled on this tab**. This is the single most common reason an agent that looks correctly configured never runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your credit position">
    Agent runs consume credits. The tab shows where you stand, and an agent stops rather than running unfunded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Agent runs consume AI credits from your balance, and a webhook trigger can be fired by anything that holds the address — including in a loop. Enable a trigger only when you know what will call it, and watch your credit balance for the first day after you do.
</Warning>

Building and running the agents themselves is covered in [AI agents](/platform/ai/agents).

## Check it worked

- Send a test message on each enabled channel and confirm it arrives.
- Send a test email after connecting a mailbox, and check the sender address is yours rather than a generic one.
- On **AI Settings**, a connected tool appears in the list and is selectable when you build an agent.
- Fire the webhook once by hand. The agent run appears in its history. If nothing happens, the trigger is still off.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A channel is listed but nothing sends">
    Being listed is not being connected. Open the channel and complete its account setup — an unconnected channel shows as available while silently sending nothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email still arrives from a generic address">
    The mailbox connection did not complete, or it was approved for a different account than you expected. Reconnect and watch which account you sign in with at the provider's screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The mailbox connection keeps dropping">
    Access granted to an outside provider expires and can be revoked from that provider's own security settings. Reconnect from this tab; if it drops repeatedly, check whether an administrator on that mail account is revoking it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reminders are not going out even though the channel works">
    Channels and preferences are separate. Check the category switch under **Notifications**, and the master **Email notifications** switch above it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My webhook-triggered agent never runs">
    The trigger is not enabled on **AI Settings**. Enable it, then fire the webhook again. A correct address into a disabled trigger produces no error and no run.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An agent stops part-way through">
    Either it needs a tool that is not connected on this tab, or your credit balance ran out mid-run. Check the connector list first, then your credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credits are draining faster than expected">
    Check what is calling your webhook. A trigger wired into something that retries can start the same agent repeatedly. Disable the trigger while you investigate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
