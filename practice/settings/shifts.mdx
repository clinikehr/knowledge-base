This is where your staff rota lives: who is working, when, and what happens when someone needs cover. It sits inside Settings rather than its own page, next to **Staff & Teams**.

<Path steps={['Settings', 'Team', 'Shifts']} />

## What this tab controls

| Section | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rota** | The week or month grid, by person or by department, ward or unit. Click a slot to assign a shift, or a shift to edit it. On a phone this becomes a day-by-day list instead of a squeezed grid. | Everyone (edit needs a permission — see below) |
| **Shift types** | Reusable templates like "Day 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM", including overnight shifts. Repeating patterns generate real shifts from a template on a schedule you set (e.g. every Monday, Wednesday and Friday). | Managing needs a permission |
| **Cover & swaps** | Requests to swap or cover a shift: a colleague accepts, then a manager approves before the shift actually changes hands. | Everyone with a stake in the request; approving needs a permission |
| **Coverage rules** | A minimum headcount for a department, ward or role during a time window — the rota flags any window that falls short, and highlights a person booked onto two overlapping shifts. | Managing needs a permission |

A staff member who cannot manage the rota sees a simple **My shifts** view instead: their own upcoming shifts, a **Request swap or cover** button on each, and the requests they are part of.

## Assign a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Rota tab">
    Choose **Week** or **Month**, and **By person** or **By department, ward or unit**. The filter next to it lists the places your shifts use in separate groups. Use the arrows to move between periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a slot or a shift type">
    Click an empty slot for that day and person, or **New shift** to pick anyone. Choosing a shift type fills in the hours for you; you can still adjust them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and save">
    Pick who it belongs to — or leave it **Open (unassigned)** for someone to claim later — and save. The shift appears on the rota immediately, and the person gets a notification. The email and calendar invite that come with it, and the reminder before the shift starts, name the shift's department, ward, unit and any place you typed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Say where a shift is

A new shift needs a place, under **Where is this shift?**: a **Department**, a **Ward** (if you set up wards), a **Unit**, or an **Other place** you type (up to 80 characters, for example **Front entrance** — a place, never a client's name). Choose at least one; with none, the panel says **Choose where this shift is — a department, ward, unit, or type a place.** and nothing is saved. Shift types and coverage rules have the same four fields, all optional, and choosing a shift type fills them in for you. The rota, **My shifts** and the lists show every place a shift names.

## Build a repeating pattern

Instead of assigning the same shift week after week, create a shift type once, then a pattern that says which days it repeats on and how far into the future to generate it. The tab shows how many shifts a pattern will add before you confirm. Running it again later only adds what's missing — it never duplicates a shift already on the rota.

**Copy week** duplicates a whole week's rota to another week in one step, for the whole team or one person.

## Upload a rota from a spreadsheet

If your rota already lives in a spreadsheet, **Upload rota** adds many shifts at once instead of entering them one by one.

1. Open **Upload rota** and choose **Download template**. It is a CSV with one row per shift: **Staff email**, **Staff name**, **Department or ward**, **Date**, **Start time**, **End time**, and optional **Title** and **Notes**. Times are your clinic's local time, and an end time earlier than the start means the shift ends the next day.
2. Fill it in and choose your file. CSV and Excel (XLSX) files both work, up to 500 shifts at a time.
3. Check the preview. Every row is listed with what will happen to it. Nothing is saved until you choose **Import**.

How people and places are matched:

- A person is found by **Staff email** first. Only if the email is blank is the full name used, and it must match one active staff member exactly. A name shared by two people is not guessed; use the email.
- A department or ward is found by its name or its code, as set up in your departments. An inactive department is not used.
- Dates are read day first (02/11/2026) or as 2026-11-02.

Rows that cannot be used are listed with the reason and left out, never guessed: staff or department not found, a date or time that is not readable, a row repeated in the file, or a shift that overlaps another shift the same person already has. Fix them in your file and upload it again. Uploading the same file twice is safe: shifts that are already on the rota are recognised and not added again.

If a person is marked **Out of Office** at that time, that row is reported as not saved so you can confirm it from the rota yourself. Each person gets one notice that shifts were added, not an email for every shift. You need to be an owner or manager, or have the Shifts permission, to upload a rota.

## Request and approve cover

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for cover">
    From your own shift, or a colleague's if you manage the rota, select **Request swap or cover** and add a reason, then **Ask for cover**. The panel closes and **Request sent** appears; the request is under **Cover & swaps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A colleague accepts">
    Anyone the request was sent to — or anyone at all, for an open request — can accept it from **Cover & swaps**. Accepting does not move the shift yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A manager approves">
    Only once a manager approves does the shift actually reassign. Approving or declining notifies both people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **A shift fills in a day that has NO working hours set at all — it does not add to a day that already has some.** If a person has no [Availability](/practice/settings/booking-settings) schedule covering a given weekday (a day off in their regular hours, for example), a shift you assign them on that date makes them bookable for exactly the shift's hours. But on a day they DO already have a working-hours schedule — even a narrow one — an extra shift on that same day changes nothing about what clients can book; their regular hours are what's offered. To make someone bookable for LONGER on a day they already work, widen their Availability schedule for that day rather than adding a shift. If your clinic never uses shifts, nothing about booking changes.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Cancelling a shift removes it from the rota permanently**, along with any open swap or cover request for it. This cannot be undone — assign a replacement shift afterward if the work still needs covering.
</Warning>
