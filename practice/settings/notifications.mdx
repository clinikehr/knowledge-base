## What this tab controls

Every switch saves on its own, straight away. There is no **Save** button.

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** | "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." | On |
| **Appointment** | "New bookings, cancellations, and reminders" | On |
| **Patient** | "New patient registrations and updates" — also gates the provider-arrival email when someone is checked in | On |
| **Staff** | "Staff invitations accepted and role changes" | On |
| **Billing** | "Payment received, invoices, and overdue alerts" | On |
| **Subscription** | "Plan changes, trial expiry, and payment status" | On |
| **Document** | "Document signed, viewed, or expired" | On |
| **Insurance** | "Claim status changes and approvals" | On |
| **System** | "System updates and maintenance notices" | On, **and cannot be switched off** |
| **Achievement emails** | "Milestone badges, tier-ups, and your clinic's trophy case" | On |
| **Daily operational digests** | "Morning owner summaries for inventory, pharmacy, and lab" | On |

The **Document** switch also covers forms: a notification when someone mentions you in a comment on a form, the **Every response** notification, the **Daily digest** email that team members can choose for a form they build, and the **Prescreen follow-up** notification a screening form raises for a team. Turning it off stops all four for everyone in your practice.

The **System** switch is drawn but disabled, and the line under the card says why: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic." The category list also adapts to your edition, so you only see categories your practice can produce.

## Your notification bell, and what is yours alone

The bell in the header keeps **your own** copy of every notice, in three views: **Unread**, **All** and **Archived**. What you do with a notice is yours: a notice sent to the whole clinic that a colleague has read stays unread for you. On each notice you can **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Archive** or **Restore**; **Mark all read** clears everything unread that does not need you. A notice you have read is archived automatically 30 days later, and an archived notice is kept for 90 days.

**Notices that need you.** A patient assigned to you, a countersignature waiting for you, a note returned to you and a change of supervisor are marked **Needs action** and carry an **Acknowledge** button. They stay in **Unread** — reading them is not enough — until you acknowledge them or the thing they ask of you is done (the visit is checked out, the note is countersigned), when they clear themselves. **Mark all read** leaves them where they are and tells you how many. A notice that needs you cannot be archived until you have acknowledged it. Acknowledging is recorded against your name.

**Pop-ups.** When you are signed in, a new notice also appears as a card in the corner of the screen, with **Acknowledge** or **Open** where they apply. Notices that were already waiting when you opened the page do not pop up. While the bell is open nothing pops up over it, because a new notice joins the list you are looking at (an emergency still does).

**Clinical activity.** The people whose work a record starts are told when it is created: the nurses on shift when a patient joins the triage queue (**New triage**), the lab when a lab order is placed (**New lab order**), and the pharmacy when a prescription is written (**New prescription**). Each says only what happened and opens the queue, never who the patient is. If your rota names who is on shift now, those people are told; if not, everyone in that role is. You are never told about a record you made yourself. Several arriving together show as one pop-up, such as **3 new lab orders**. A clinic that has muted the **Patient** category does not send these three.

**Emergency.** When a patient is marked as an emergency, the nurse of record and the doctors and nurses on shift see **Emergency in triage** in red. It always reaches you, always pops up, stays on screen until you dismiss it, and waits in your bell as **Needs action** until you acknowledge it or the patient is seen. Neither you nor the clinic can switch it off, and not even **No pop-up for notices that need me** silences it.

**Your own choices.** Under **Settings → Notifications**, the **My notifications** card has three groups, saved the moment you change a switch (you get **Notification settings saved**):

- **Clinical activity**: **Show in my bell** and **Pop up** for **New triage**, **New lab order** and **New prescription**.
- **Sounds**: **Play a sound for emergencies** (on by default) plays a short two-tone alert when an emergency arrives, and once more ten seconds later if you have not acknowledged it. **Play a sound for other new notices** (off by default) adds a soft chime for everything else. Your browser allows sound only after you have clicked or pressed a key on the page; until then the emergency pop-up shows **Sound blocked — click to enable**.
- **Assigned to you**: **No pop-up for notices that need me**, and **Email me when a patient or shift is assigned to me**. The email says that something was assigned and names no patient; it follows the clinic's email switches and your account's **Product & account emails** switch. A changed or cancelled shift still emails you so your calendar stays right.
- **Everything else**: **Show in my bell** and **Pop up** for each category.

**New patient added.** When someone registers a patient, the owner, managers, receptionists and other administrative and front-desk roles get a pop-up by default. Everyone else gets the entry in the bell without a pop-up, and can switch the pop-up on under **My notifications → Clinical activity → New patient added**, which says which default applies to your role.

These sit inside your clinic's switches above: a category your clinic has muted reaches nobody. Your choices are kept per clinic, because your role differs from clinic to clinic. You can also set them from **Profile and Settings → Notifications**, under **My notifications in a clinic**, with a clinic picker if you belong to more than one.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    From the sidebar footer select **Settings**, then **Practice notifications** → **Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on email as a whole">
    Leave **Email notifications** on unless you want to pause practice email entirely. Turning it off confirms with "All non-essential clinic emails are paused. Receipts, security, and billing alerts still send."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mute the categories you don't act on">
    Flip any category switch off. You get "Notifications muted" and the name of what you turned off — for example "Appointment notifications turned off."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal with the two extras">
    **Achievement emails** and **Daily operational digests** sit below the categories, separated by a line. The digests are a morning summary for the owner; turn them off if nobody reads them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

There is no save — each switch takes effect as you flip it, and the confirmation toast tells you it landed.

| What you changed | What you'll notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** off | Non-essential practice email stops. **Transactional email keeps sending** — receipts, security alerts, password resets and billing alerts are never suppressed by this switch. In-app notifications are unaffected. |
| A category off | Both the in-app notice **and** the email for that category stop. The bell stops showing them, and nothing about that category reaches the practice inbox. |
| A category back on | Notifications resume from that moment. Nothing that was suppressed while it was off is delivered late. |
| **Achievement emails** off | Badge and tier-up emails stop. Your trophy case is still there to look at. |
| **Daily operational digests** off | The morning inventory, pharmacy and lab summary stops arriving. |

**This store governs email directed at the practice.** It is not the same thing as your own account settings, and it is not the same thing as what a client has asked for. If you are trying to stop a specific message, work out which store owns it first:

| Store | Where you set it | What it governs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** master switch | This tab | Practice-directed email, on or off, as a whole |
| The category switches | This tab | Practice email **and** in-app notices, per category |
| Your personal account settings | [Account notifications](/platform/account/notifications) | Email addressed to you as a person — account, security and marketing |
| The individual client's record | The client's intake sheet | That client's own appointment reminders and confirmations |

So a client complaining about reminders is fixed on their record, not here; an owner tired of milestone emails turns off **Achievement emails** here.

## Rebooking reminders

A second card on this tab, **Rebooking reminders**, is about your **clients**, not your practice. With **Suggest the next visit** on, a client whose usual time until their next visit has come round — and who has nothing booked with you since — gets one email suggesting they book that service again, with a button that opens your booking page with the service already chosen. The same suggestion appears as a **Book your next visit** card in their client portal.

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Suggest the next visit** | Turns rebooking emails and the portal card on or off for the whole practice | Off |

- **Only the clinic owner can change it.** Anyone else sees the switch, and the line under it says so.
- **Nothing is sent until a service has a usual gap.** Set **Usual time until the next visit** on each service in [Appointment services](/practice/settings/appointment-services). A service without one never sends a rebooking email.
- **One email per visit, at most.** It goes out the day the gap is reached (or within a week of it). If the client books anything in the meantime — or has already been back — nothing is sent.
- **The email says as little as possible.** It names the service and your practice, and nothing about the client or the visit.
- **Clients stay in control.** A client whose record has appointment emails turned off gets no rebooking email, and every email carries **Stop these reminders**. Using it stops rebooking emails and the portal card from your practice for that client; their appointment confirmations and reminders carry on.
- **Your booking page must be published** and the service bookable online by existing clients, because the button takes them there.

Turning the switch on does not send anything for old visits: only visits whose gap is reached from now on (or in the last week) are suggested.

## Check it worked

Flip a switch and watch for the confirmation — "Notifications muted" or "Notifications enabled", naming the category. Reload the tab; the switch should hold its new position. Then trigger something in that category (book a test appointment, say) and confirm nothing arrives in the practice inbox and nothing appears under the bell.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The System switch will not move">
    It is deliberately fixed on. System notices carry service and maintenance information every practice needs, so there is no way to mute them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I turned everything off and email still arrives">
    Essential email is out of scope for these switches. Receipts, security alerts, password resets and billing alerts always send. If what you are getting is an appointment reminder to a *client*, that is set on the client's record, not here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A category I was told about isn't in my list">
    The list adapts to your edition and only offers categories your practice can produce. A missing switch means that category never fires for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Failed to update preferences"'>
    The switch snaps back to where it was and nothing was saved. Check your connection and flip it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rebooking reminders are on but no client got one">
    Check three things. The service needs a **Usual time until the next visit**; the client's last visit must be **Completed**, with nothing booked since; and your booking page must be published. A client whose record has appointment emails turned off, or who used **Stop these reminders**, is skipped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A colleague still gets emails I muted">
    Muting here stops practice-directed email. Email addressed to them as a person — account and security notices, marketing — is governed by their own [account notifications](/platform/account/notifications).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For how these preferences are honoured across the platform, see [Notification settings](/platform/settings/notifications).
