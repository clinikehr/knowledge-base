Communications is where your practice gets a dedicated number, watches its message credit balance, and turns on the channels you use to reach clients — texts, calls, fax and email. You never type in a provider's numbers or connection details here: the platform buys and registers your number for you, and the database — not this form — is what proves a number belongs to your practice.

## What this tab controls

| Card | Control | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your number** | **Get a number** | Opens checkout for a dedicated number ($15/month plus a one-time $25 registration fee). The number appears once payment is confirmed |
| | **Release number** | Stops the monthly charge and releases the number. Reminders fall back to email until you get a new one |
| **Fax Inbox** | **Open Fax Inbox** | Faxes your practice has received, waiting to be filed into a client's documents |
| **Communication credits** | Balance, this month's usage | One shared balance for texts, calls and fax pages |
| | **Buy a pack** | A one-time top-up, billed to your card on file |
| | **Auto top-up** | Automatically buys a pack when your balance runs out. Off by default |
| **Channels** | **SMS**, **Voice**, **Email** switches | Turn each on or off for your practice |
| | **Fax** | Always available once your practice has a fax-capable number — sent from any signed document, no switch needed |
| | **WhatsApp** | Not available yet |
| **Email** | **Send from (verified address)** | The address email is sent from, e.g. `clinic@yourdomain.com` |
| | **Reply-to (optional)** | Where replies land if that is a different address, e.g. `frontdesk@yourdomain.com` |
| | **Send from your own mailbox** → **Google** / **Outlook** | Not available yet. The **Connect** buttons are visible but inactive |

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a number">
    In **Your number**, select **Get a number**. You are taken to checkout for the recurring number fee plus a one-time registration fee. Once payment is confirmed, you land back on this tab and the number appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the channels you use">
    In **Channels**, switch on **SMS**, **Voice** and/or **Email**. Fax needs no switch — see [Faxing](#faxing-a-document) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your sending address">
    In **Email**, enter your **Send from (verified address)** — an address on your own domain that has been verified. Add a **Reply-to** if replies should reach a different inbox, such as your front desk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your credit balance">
    Texts, calls and fax pages all draw from one balance in **Communication credits**. Buy a pack, or turn on **Auto top-up** so you never run dry mid-month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Faxing a document

Fax is not a switch you turn on — it is gated by having a number and by the **Send Fax** permission (owners and managers always have it; anyone else needs it granted in Settings → Team → Permissions). To send a fax, open any signed document — a clinical note, a lab or radiology report, an invoice, or a signed patient document — and choose **Send by fax**. You'll see the page count and credit cost before you confirm.

Faxes your practice **receives** land in the **Fax Inbox**, reachable from this tab or from the sidebar. Nothing is matched to a client automatically — a fax tells you who sent it, never who it's about — so a person reviews each one, opens it to see what it says, and either files it into a client's documents or marks it junk. An unfiled fax is removed automatically 30 days after it arrives, and the inbox flags anything close to that date.

## What changes once you save

Turning on a channel means anything already written to use it — reminders, confirmations, automated messages — starts actually going out over it, from the number and address on this tab. Until a channel is on, whatever depends on it **stays silent rather than failing**: no error appears, the message simply never leaves.

Getting a number is a real, recurring charge from the moment checkout confirms — releasing it stops that charge immediately. Running out of communication credits does not fail silently either: sends fall back to email so nothing is ever lost, just delivered a different way.

## Check it worked

**Getting a number:** after checkout, return to this tab — you should see your number listed as "Registered and active" (a fresh number briefly shows a provisioning status first).

**A channel:** save, leave the tab, and come back — the switches you set should have persisted. Then trigger something real: book a test appointment for yourself and confirm the confirmation arrives at the right address or number.

**Fax:** send a document to a real fax number you control, or check the Fax Inbox after asking someone to fax your practice's number.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An upgrade panel covers the whole tab">
    Communications starts on the Essential plan: "Get a number for texts and calls, and let your clinic send appointment reminders and messages. Available on any paid plan." See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A message says only clinic owners and managers can configure communications">
    An accountant can reach settings but not this tab's controls. Ask an owner or manager to make the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Get a number does nothing, or names a payment problem">
    Select it again — it should open a payment page for the number's monthly fee and one-time registration fee. If it says numbers aren't available for your practice's currency yet, that currency isn't priced yet (today: US and Canada only) — reminders still send by email in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I turned on SMS or Voice and nothing sends">
    Confirm your practice has a number in **Your number** — a channel switch with no number behind it has nowhere to send from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Fax Inbox is empty">
    That's expected until someone actually faxes your practice's number — nothing is seeded or simulated. Once a fax arrives, it appears here for filing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Connect buttons under my own mailbox do nothing">
    Google and Outlook mailbox sending is not available yet. Email sends from your **Send from (verified address)** in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Emails arrive but replies go nowhere useful">
    Set **Reply-to (optional)** to a monitored inbox. Without it, replies go back to the verified sending address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some clients get reminders and others do not">
    This tab opens the channel; each client's own notification preferences decide whether they are contacted on it. Check the client's record, and the appointment's own notification setting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
