Communications is where your practice gets a dedicated number, watches its message credit balance, and turns on the channels you use to reach clients — texts, calls, fax and email. You never type in a provider's numbers or connection details here: the platform buys and registers your number for you, and the database — not this form — is what proves a number belongs to your practice.

## What this tab controls

| | **Bring your own number** | Keep a number you already have with another carrier. With no number on the card yet: $49 setup and $15 a month, like a new number. Replacing a number you already have with us: $25 once. See [Bring your own number](#bring-your-own-number) |

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a number">
    In **Number for texts and calls**, select **Get a number** and follow [Pick your number and change it once](#pick-your-number-and-change-it-once). Once payment is confirmed, you land back on this tab and the number appears. Do the same in **Fax number** if you want a fax line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the channels you use">
    In **Channels**, switch on **SMS**, **Voice** and/or **Email**. Fax needs no switch — see [Faxing](#faxing-a-document) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your sending address">
    In **Email**, enter your **Send from (verified address)** — an address on your own domain that has been verified. Add a **Reply-to** if replies should reach a different inbox, such as your front desk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your credit balance">
    Texts, calls and fax pages all draw from one balance in **Communication credits**. Buy a pack, or ask the owner to set up **Auto top-up** (see [Auto top-up](#auto-top-up)) so the balance does not run dry mid-month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick your number and change it once

Settings → Communications has two numbers, each bought and changed on its own: a **Number for texts and calls** and a separate **Fax number**. Each costs $49 to set up and $15 a month. A toll-free number adds $10 a month.

**Getting a number.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the kind">
    Select **Get a number**, then choose **Local** or **Toll-free**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    For a local number, enter an area code. Select **Search** to see a list of available numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the exact number">
    Select the number you want from the list. The price summary shows the setup fee and the monthly price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Select **Continue to payment** and complete checkout. Your number appears once payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the number you picked was taken by someone else before your payment finished, you are asked to pick another. Select **Pick a new number** and choose again. You are not charged anything extra.

**Changing a number.** Each number can be changed once, to a local or a toll-free number. Select **Change number**, choose **Local** or **Toll-free**, search, and pick the new number.

- **No setup fee** for a change.
- **Switching to toll-free adds $10 a month.** Switching back to local takes the $10 off.
- **Your old number keeps working** until the new one is ready, then it is released.
- **Only once.** After a number has been changed, **Change number** is unavailable. To change it again, contact support.
- If the change cannot be completed, your current number stays as it is and you can try again.

**The $49 setup fee is not refunded** if you cancel a number later. Only a number that fails to set up refunds the first payment.

## Bring your own number

If your practice already has a number with another carrier, you can keep it instead of buying a new one. It works on either card, **Number for texts and calls** or **Fax number**, and it is US and Canada numbers only.

**What it costs.** It depends on whether the card already has a number:

- **No number on the card yet:** the same as a new number, a **$49 setup fee** plus the first month ($15 a month; a toll-free number adds $10 a month), paid together. There is no separate transfer fee.
- **The card already has a number you pay for:** **$25 once** to move your own number in. Your monthly price carries on, and you do not pay the setup fee again.

Your current carrier may charge its own fee to release the number, and a contract may have an early-termination fee. Those are between you and that carrier.

**What to have ready.**

- Your latest bill from the current carrier, as a PDF, PNG or JPEG file of up to 10 MB.
- The account number and the PIN or passcode for that account.
- The name of the person authorised on the account, the billing telephone number and the service address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    On the card, select **Bring your own number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your number">
    Enter the number in full, starting with + and the country code, and select **Check this number**. Checking is free. If the number cannot be moved, you are told why and nothing is charged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account details">
    Enter the current carrier, the account number, the **PIN or passcode**, the authorised person, the billing telephone number and the service address, then select **Save and continue**. The account number and PIN are kept private and are never shown again; to change either, type it again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your latest bill">
    Choose the file and select **Upload bill**. It shows your carrier that you own the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the letter">
    Read the letter of authorisation. Type the authorised person's full name exactly as it is on the account, tick **I am authorised to move this number.** and select **Sign and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Check the price, then select **Continue to payment** and complete checkout. If you have already paid, the button reads **Submit** and sends the request to your carrier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**While it moves.** Your number keeps working with your current carrier until it moves. The carrier sets the date; when it is known, the card shows it. When the number arrives it replaces the number you bought on that card, if there was one, and that card's one free change is not used up. Calls and faxes work as soon as the number arrives. Texting needs the texting registration after the move, which starts on its own for the main number; see [Check it worked](#check-it-worked).

**If your carrier needs something fixed.** The card reads **Your carrier needs something from you** and shows what to fix. Select **Fix and resubmit**, correct the details or the bill, and select **Send to my carrier again**.

**Cancelling.** Select **Cancel port** on the card.

- Before the request is sent to your carrier, everything you paid is refunded.
- After it is sent, the one-time fee (the setup or the transfer fee) and any month already started are not refunded.
- If your carrier cancels the transfer, you are refunded in full.

**While a transfer is open**, that card's **Get a number**, **Change number** and **Cancel number** are paused, and the card says "A number transfer is in progress."

## Credits your plan includes each month

Every paid plan adds message credits to the shared balance on the first of each month, and the credits you buy never expire.

| Plan | Included each month |
| --- | --- |
| Essential | 200 |
| Team and Enterprise | 1,000 |
| Pharmacy Starter and Professional, Diagnostics Starter Lab and Professional Lab | 200 |
| Pharmacy Business and Enterprise, Pharmacy Listing, Diagnostics Business Lab and Enterprise | 1,000 |

A paid trial gets 200 a month, the same as Essential. Free has none. More credits are $15 per 500.

## What uses credits

One balance pays for texts, calls and fax pages, and it is shared with the texts and calls made by AI agents.

- **A text uses one credit for each text part.** A long text, or one with an emoji or curly quotes (the kind a phone keyboard types), is sent in more than one part and uses more than one credit.
- **Caribbean +1 numbers cost more than the US and Canada.** A text or call to a number such as Jamaica or the Bahamas uses more credits than one to the US or Canada, even though they also start with +1.
- **Premium-rate numbers are blocked.** Nothing is sent to them and no credits are used.
- **AI agents use credits too.** Texts and calls an agent makes are charged to the same balance. Agents can no longer send faxes; staff send faxes from **Documents**.
- **With no credits, reminders go by email only.** If the balance is zero or below, reminders are sent by email — no text and no call — until you add credits.
- **This month's usage lists every charge** in plain words, so you can see what each one was for.

## Auto top-up

Auto top-up buys a pack for you when the balance runs low, so reminders keep going by text and call. It is **off by default**, and only the **owner** can turn it on or change it; a manager can see its status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the setup">
    In **Communication credits**, select **Set up** next to **Auto top-up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the level, the pack and the limit">
    Choose the balance it should top up at (**Top up when my balance falls to**), the **Pack to buy**, and the **Most automatic purchases per month**. Switch on **Buy packs automatically** and select **Save and turn on**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **It charges the card on your ClinikEHR subscription**, not a new checkout. A one-time **Buy a pack** is the one that opens a payment page.
- **The monthly limit is a ceiling.** Once it is reached you are told, and nothing more is bought until next month. You can still buy a pack yourself.
- **One at a time.** It never buys a second pack while one is being paid for.
- **If the charge fails it pauses**, says why on this tab, and emails the owner. It never retries a declined card. Check the card on the subscription, then select **Review and turn on**.
- **You are told each time**: the owner gets a receipt by email and a notice in the app for every automatic purchase. The list under **Automatic purchases this month** shows what was bought.
- Clinics billed in naira can use it once credit packs are available for naira.

## Faxing a document

Fax is not a switch — it is gated by having a number and by the **Send and triage fax** permission (owners and managers always have it; anyone else needs it granted in Settings → Team → Permissions). Without the permission, **Send by fax** is not shown.

**What you can fax.** A signed clinical note, a final lab report, a visit's lab results once every test is completed, a reported radiology report, an issued invoice, a dispensed prescription (a prescription for a medicine marked **Controlled** can't be faxed), or a signed client document. Open it and choose **Send by fax** next to the download button. Only a signed or reported document can be faxed, so on a draft the button is greyed out and says so. A fax cannot be recalled once it is sent, which is why drafts are held back.

**The steps.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the number">
    Type the fax number with its country code, for example +14155551234, and choose **Get cost**. Under the field you also see your **Recent numbers** — the last ten you faxed to — and choosing one fills it in. You see the page count and the communication credits it will use before anything is sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Choose **Send fax**. A last check shows the number and pages, for example "Fax 3 pages to +1 (415) 555-1234?", and reminds you a fax cannot be recalled. If you have never faxed that number before, the check also says **New number — check it carefully before sending.** Credits are taken only after you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pressing **Send fax** again after a slow connection never sends the document twice or charges twice — if the first attempt did go out, you are told "This fax was already sent". If it was refused or failed, you are told why, and a fresh attempt is a new fax.

If a prescription's medicine isn't in your inventory, the fax screen asks **Is this a controlled medicine?** once, and you must answer before you can send. Answering **Yes** means it can't be faxed.

**Follow it on the Sent tab.** Open **Communications** → **Fax** and choose **Sent**. Each row shows when it was sent, the kind of document, the number it went to, the pages, the credits used and one of four statuses:

- **Queued** — accepted and waiting to go out.
- **Sending** — on its way to the receiving machine.
- **Delivered** — the receiving machine answered.
- **Failed** — it did not get through. The reason is shown on the row, the credits are refunded automatically (the **Refunded** column shows how many), and the person who sent it gets a notification. A failed fax is not retried on its own: open the document and choose **Send by fax** again.

**Limits.** To protect against mistakes and misuse, each practice can send up to 200 faxes a day, and up to 10 a day to any one number. If you reach a limit, the message tells you when it resets. Some premium-rate and international destinations can't be faxed, and you are told before any credits are used.

Faxes your practice **receives** land in the **Fax Inbox**, reachable from this tab or from **Communications** → **Fax** in the sidebar. Nothing is matched to a client automatically — a fax tells you who sent it, never who it's about — so a person reviews each one, opens it to see what it says, and either files it into a client's documents or marks it junk. An unfiled fax is removed automatically 30 days after it arrives, and the inbox flags anything close to that date.

To read a fax, select **View**; it downloads as a PDF. To file it, select **Assign**, search for the client and select them, then check the name and select **File fax**. Filing cannot be undone from the inbox, so nothing is filed until you confirm.

**Where a filed fax goes.** Once filed, a fax is in that client's chart: open the client, go to the **Documents & forms** tab, and it is listed under **Faxes**. Select **View** to open it on screen. **Download** and **Print** each ask you to confirm first, and both are logged. Filed faxes also stay listed under **Filed** at the bottom of the **Fax Inbox**, with **Open client** to jump to the client's chart.

## What changes once you save

Turning on a channel means anything already written to use it — reminders, confirmations, automated messages — starts actually going out over it, from the number and address on this tab. Until a channel is on, whatever depends on it **stays silent rather than failing**: no error appears, the message simply never leaves.

Getting a number is a real, recurring charge from the moment checkout confirms — cancelling it stops the next charge straight away, and the number works until the period you paid for ends. Each number is cancelled and resumed on its own, from its own card or from **Billing → Other subscriptions**, which lists the number for texts and calls and the fax number as separate items. Running out of communication credits does not fail silently either: see [What uses credits](#what-uses-credits).

## Check it worked

**Getting a number:** after checkout, return to this tab. A new number first reads **Setting up your number** with "This usually takes a few minutes", then **Active**. If it reads **We couldn't set up this number**, your payment for it will be refunded — contact support if you don't see it within a few days — and you can get a number again. The message stays on this tab for 30 days.

**Texting:** a number can take calls as soon as it is **Active**, but texting needs a separate registration to be approved. Under the number you see **Texting registration in review** while that happens, then **Texting active**. If it reads **Texting registration needs attention — contact support**, get in touch. Until texting is active, the **SMS** option on an appointment stays off with the note "Texting turns on once your number's texting registration is approved", and reminders go by email.

**A channel:** save, leave the tab, and come back — the switches you set should have persisted. Then trigger something real: book a test appointment for yourself and confirm the confirmation arrives at the right address or number.

**Fax:** send a document to a real fax number you control, or check the Fax Inbox after asking someone to fax your practice's number.

    Set **Reply-to (optional)** to a monitored inbox. Without it, replies go back to the verified sending address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some clients get reminders and others do not">

  </Accordion>
