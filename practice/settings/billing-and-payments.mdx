The **BILLING** section holds two different things that are easy to confuse: how you pay ClinikEHR, and how your clients pay you. **Billing & Plans** is the first. Everything under **Payment processing** is the second.

## Set currency and tax first

Do this before you price a service or issue an invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your Currency">
    **Currency** — "Currency for all transactions. Search by name or code." Every amount in your practice displays in it. Currency also decides your payment processor: Naira routes to Paystack, everything else to Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Tax Rate (%)">
    **Tax Rate (%)** — "Applied to invoices and receipts." Leave it at zero if you do not charge tax.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Free Clinic Mode only if it applies">
    **Free Clinic Mode** — "Enable this if your clinic provides free services. All charges will be disabled." Your practice then carries a **Free Clinic** badge. This disables charging across the product, so do not use it as a temporary pause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Flat Rate Fee if you use one">
    The **Flat Rate Fee** section applies a single standing charge rather than per-service pricing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Currency, **Tax Rate** and **Free Clinic Mode** are **owner-only**. A manager sees them disabled.

<Warning>
  Changing currency does not re-price anything already recorded. Existing services, invoices and payments keep the numbers they were saved with — only the symbol in front of them changes. Set your currency once, at the start.
</Warning>

## Take card payments

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Online Payments']} />

**Online Payments** ("Stripe and Paystack setup") connects the account that receives client money.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the connector your currency routes to">
    **Stripe Connect** for most currencies, **Paystack Connect** for Naira.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the connector's own onboarding">
    For Paystack that means your business name, choosing your bank from the picker, and your account number — the field prompts "Enter 10-digit NUBAN". Stripe runs its own identity and bank verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return and confirm the account shows as connected">
    Until it does, the payment options that depend on it stay unavailable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disconnecting a processor stops clients paying you; it does not cancel your ClinikEHR subscription, which is charged on **Billing & Plans**.

## List the other ways clients can pay

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Payment Details']} />

**Payment Details** ("Bank, Cash App, Venmo & more on invoices") is the manual counterpart — the account numbers and handles you want printed on an invoice. As the tab puts it: "How patients can pay you — bank transfer, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal and more."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Add payment details">
    Choose a **Type**, then give it a label you would recognise on a statement — "GTBank Business Account".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Fields">
    **Fields** are repeatable **Label** and **Value** pairs, so one entry can carry an account name, a number and a sort code together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide where it appears">
    Each entry has its own toggles for showing on invoice **PDFs** and in invoice **emails**, while a balance is due. An entry with both off is stored but never shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn on Auto Pay

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Auto Pay']} />

**Auto Pay** ("Auto-charge saved cards after visits") charges a client's saved card automatically once their appointment is confirmed or completed, so nobody has to chase it.

It needs the **Essential** plan or above **and** a connected Stripe account — without one the tab says "Connect Stripe to use Auto Pay" and the switch stays off. It carries a **1.9%** platform fee on each transaction.

## Insurance, services and public pages

**Insurance Settings** ("Payers and claims setup") has three sub-tabs: **Insurance Profile**, **Payers** and **Enrollment**. Set those up here; the day-to-day of submitting and tracking a claim is in [Insurance claims](/practice/money/insurance-claims).

The rest of this section configures things documented in full elsewhere:

| Tab | What it does | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appointment Services** | Your bookable service catalogue | [Add the services you offer](/practice/get-started/services) |
| **Memberships** | Public membership signup page and embed | [Memberships](/clinic/revenue/memberships) |
| **Store Page** | Public storefront branding and embed | [Store](/clinic/revenue/store) |

Each of those public pages works the same way: a slug that forms its web address, a switch to take it live, branding, and an embed snippet for your own website.

## Check it worked

- A new invoice shows the right currency symbol and applies your tax rate.
- Your processor shows as connected on **Online Payments**, and a test payment reaches it.
- An invoice PDF and an invoice email both show the payment details you marked to appear.
- With Auto Pay on, completing an appointment for a client with a saved card produces a payment without you recording one.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Currency, tax or Free Clinic Mode will not change">
    Those three are owner-only. A manager or accountant sees them disabled rather than getting an error. Ask the owner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Old invoices still show the previous currency amounts">
    Expected. Changing currency does not convert recorded figures. Reissue anything that must be correct in the new currency.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Paystack option is missing">
    Paystack appears for Naira. Check your currency on **Billing & Plans** first — everything else routes to Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment details are saved but do not appear on the invoice">
    Each entry has separate toggles for the PDF and the email, and they only show while a balance is due. A fully paid invoice will not display them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Auto Pay is on but a client was not charged">
    It needs Essential or above and a connected Stripe account — the tab says "Connect Stripe to use Auto Pay" when that is the missing piece. Beyond that, it fires only when the client has a saved card; without one the invoice stays outstanding for you to collect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Insurance Settings is missing for my colleague">
    That tab exists only on Solo and Team. On Enterprise it disappears, because that plan uses a different insurance system.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
