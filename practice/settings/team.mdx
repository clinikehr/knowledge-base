**Staff & Teams** ("Manage practitioners and roles") is where you add people, see who has accepted, and chase the ones who have not. On a solo or team plan it is also where seat charges become visible, so read the billing section before you invite in bulk.

## What is on the tab

Three lists, in order:

- **The staff list** — everyone who has accepted, with their role.
- **Pending invitations** — sent, not yet accepted.
- **Invites awaiting seat payment**, on this plan only: "Staff invites awaiting seat payment via Stripe Checkout. Use Retry to generate a new payment link or Cancel to discard." An invite sitting here has not been sent to the person yet — it is waiting on you.

## Invite a colleague

**Add team member** opens a five-step wizard. The steps are numbered on screen: **1 Profile → 2 Specialty → 3 Supervisor → 4 Permissions → 5 Review**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Profile">
    Name, email address and the role they will hold. The email is the identity — the invitation goes there and that address becomes their login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specialty">
    Their clinical specialty and any licence or registration details you want recorded. This is what prints as credentials on documents they sign, so an incomplete entry shows up later on a report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supervisor">
    Who they report to, where your practice works that way. A supervisor relationship is what lets a senior clinician co-sign or oversee their work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Permissions">
    What this person may actually do, beyond what their role implies. Two people on the same role can be given different permissions here — this is the layer that decides who can act, not just who can look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    A summary of all four steps before anything is sent. Check the email address here: an invitation to a typo'd address is not recoverable, you simply cancel it and send another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After **Review**, the invitation is either sent straight away or lands in the awaiting-payment list, depending on whether the seat is billable.

## How seats are charged

<Warning>
  Seats are billed on this plan. Your plan sets a maximum number of billable seats, and **every billable seat is charged monthly from the first one** — there is no free allowance to use up before charges begin. Adding a colleague adds to your monthly bill.
</Warning>

Three roles are free and count toward nothing: **supervisor**, **biller** and **scheduler**. If someone only needs to see the diary or chase invoices, giving them one of those roles costs nothing and stays out of your seat count.

When you hit your plan's maximum, the invite stops rather than silently over-billing you. Raise the ceiling by moving plan — see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).

## Chase a pending invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check which list it is in">
    **Pending invitations** means the email went out and they have not accepted. **Awaiting seat payment** means it never went out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Retry on an unpaid seat">
    **Retry** generates a fresh payment link for that seat. Use it when the original link expired or was closed part-way through.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Cancel to discard">
    **Cancel** discards the invite entirely. Nothing is charged and the person is never emailed. Send a new invite if you still want them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it worked

- The person appears under **Pending invitations** immediately after you send.
- They receive an email and, once they accept and set a password, move into the staff list with the role you chose.
- Their permissions match what you set at step 4 — open their record and confirm rather than assuming the role covered it.
- Your seat count on **Billing & Plans** reflects the new person if their role is billable.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The invitation never arrived">
    Check it is not sitting in **awaiting seat payment** — that list has not emailed anyone yet. If it is genuinely pending, have them check their spam folder, then cancel and re-send to confirm the address is right.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="They got the email but the link says it is no longer valid">
    Invitations expire. Cancel the pending invite and send a fresh one from **Add team member**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Add team member is disabled">
    You are at your plan's seat maximum, or you are signed in as an accountant rather than an owner or manager. Check the seat count first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="They accepted but cannot see the screens they need">
    Role decides which pages open; permissions decide what they can do on them. Re-open their permissions and grant the specific action. See [Roles](/platform/team/roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to remove someone without losing their records">
    Removing a person removes their access, not the work they did. Notes, appointments and invoices they created stay attributed to them — that attribution is a compliance requirement and is not editable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I invited the wrong person and they have already accepted">
    Remove them from the staff list. If the seat was billable, check your next invoice reflects the reduced count.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
