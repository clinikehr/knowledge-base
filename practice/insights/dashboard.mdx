**Executive** is your practice at a glance — six headline figures, and a card for every report your practice can open. Each figure is a link to the report that explains it, so a number you do not believe is one select away from its workings.

## What you're looking at

Six tiles across the top, then the **Reports** list underneath.

| Tile | Counts | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appointments today** | Today's booked appointments | The Appointments report |
| **Appointments this week** | This week's booked appointments | The Appointments report |
| **Collected this month** | Money actually received this month | The Revenue report |
| **Outstanding** | Invoiced and not yet paid | The Revenue report |
| **Patients** | Everyone on your client list | The Patients report |
| **New this month** | Clients added this month | The Patients report |

Money is shown in your practice's own currency, taken from your billing settings — see [Currency](/platform/settings/currency). The two money tiles answer different questions, and mixing them up is the commonest misreading of this page: **Collected this month** is what has arrived, **Outstanding** is what has been billed and is still owed.

<Note>
  The count of people is labelled **Patients** here, and its report is called **Patients**, even though the rest of your workspace says **Clients**. It is the same list either way.
</Note>

### Em dash, not zero

A tile shows an em dash while its figure is still loading, and a number only once the figure has arrived. So a **0** on this screen is a real zero — no appointments, no money — and a dash is "not known yet". A brand-new practice therefore sees zeroes rather than a blank page, and the **Reports** list below is short until you have used the modules the reports describe.

## Open the report behind a figure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the tile">
    Selecting **Outstanding**, for example, opens the Revenue report rather than expanding anything in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    Choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months**. Snapshot reports have no period select and read **As it stands now** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rows, not just the chart">
    The data table at the bottom holds the rows behind the headline, and **Export** takes exactly what is on screen. See [Reports and analytics](/practice/insights/analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Reports** list below the tiles is the same set as the Analytics page, filtered the same way — by your plan, your edition, and the care areas your practice has switched on.

## Who can do this

A solo or team practice has no role→route layer: everybody invited into it can open **Executive**, and every tile resolves for all of them.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the Executive page | Any staff member | Nothing — the page is not role- or permission-gated |
| See the money tiles | Any staff member | Nothing |
| Open any report from a tile | Any staff member | The report catalogue — edition, plan and care area |

- **The invite sheet's View financial dashboard and View Payment Reports toggles are recorded, not enforced here.** They are stored against the staff member; nothing on this page reads them. If you need money figures hidden from a colleague, this page is not where that happens today.
- **Fencing people out of pages by role is an Enterprise feature.** It needs the Enterprise plan plus role-scoped navigation switched on for the practice.

A tile that shows an em dash is a figure that has not arrived, never a permission refusal — it never renders as a zero, because a zero would be a lie. See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- All six tiles show either a number or a zero, not a dash, within a few seconds of the page loading.
- Selecting a tile lands you on the matching report with data in it.
- **Collected this month** moves after you record a payment and reload — see [Record and collect payments](/practice/money/payments).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The executive overview could not be loaded.">
    A temporary read failure. Reload the page. If it keeps happening, note the time — support can match it against your audit log.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A tile has shown an em dash for a while">
    The figure has not arrived. Reload rather than assuming a zero; the dash exists precisely so an unknown is never mistaken for none.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Every money figure is a dash but the appointment counts are fine">
    The money reads failed while the rest succeeded. Reload; if it persists, note the time so support can match it against your audit log.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A report I expected is not in the list">
    Reports follow your plan, your edition and the care areas you have on. [Reports and analytics](/practice/insights/analytics) lists exactly what a practice gets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Collected this month does not match my bank">
    It counts payments recorded in ClinikEHR, not settlements landing in your account. Open the Revenue report and compare its **Payments** scope against your provider's payouts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change which tiles appear?">
    No. The six are fixed. Anything more specific lives in a report, which you can filter, scope and export.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often do the figures refresh?">
    They are read when the page loads. Reload to refresh — nothing on this page updates itself while you watch it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Outstanding include cancelled invoices?">
    No. Cancelled invoices are withdrawn, so nothing is owed against them. Overdue invoices are still outstanding — see [Billing overview](/practice/money/billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see client names from here?">
    No. This page is aggregates only — no names, no clinical detail, nothing to act on. Go to the module for that: [Appointments](/practice/work/appointments) for the diary, [Billing](/practice/money/billing) for money owed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is this the same screen a hospital sees?">
    The layout is, but a hospital's report list includes ward, theatre and diagnostics reports that a solo practice has no data for and therefore never sees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
