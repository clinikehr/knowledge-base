## What you're looking at

The page counts itself at the top: "\{n\} reports available for this clinic. Each one covers a period you choose and can be exported." The cards are grouped under **Clinical**, **Operations** and **Money**, each naming a report and what it covers.

| Report | Covers | Scopes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | Invoices, payments and what is still outstanding | **Invoices** · **Payments** |
| **Appointments** | Volume, completion and no-show rate | — |
| **Patients** | New versus returning, and how the list is growing | — |
| **Consultation** | Encounter volume, by status | **Consultations** · **Telehealth** · **Notes** |
| **Staff** | Headcount by role and activity | — |
| **Workload** | How the load falls across your team, and your peak hours | — |
| **Inventory** | Valuation, turnover and expiry risk | — |
| **Predictive Analysis** | Demand, revenue and stock forecasts | — |
| **Memberships** | Sign-ups, cancellations and recurring revenue | — |
| **Store** | Storefront orders and takings | — |
| **Insurance Claims** | Electronic claims: volume, status and what has been paid | — |
| **e-Prescribing** | Prescriptions transmitted, and how many landed | — |

Three things filter that list: your plan, your edition, and the care areas your practice has switched on. A report only appears once the module behind it is in use, so a new practice sees a short list. If the page says **No reports available**, nothing is broken — there is nothing yet for a report to describe.

The last two rows are specific to solo and team plans, because the [electronic claims](/practice/money/insurance-claims) and [e-prescribing](/platform/integrations/eprescribe) workflows they report on are too.

<Note>
  A shorter list than you expected is deliberate, not a missing feature. A report is a promise that the data behind it exists, so one you have never generated any data for is not published at all. Start using the module and its report appears.
</Note>

## Read a report

Every report opens in the same workbench, so learning one teaches you all of them.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The report's name |
| Period select | **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months** |
| Scope tabs | Only on reports that have them, e.g. **Invoices** / **Payments** |
| Summary tiles | The headline figures for the chosen period |
| Chart over time | The same figures plotted across the period |
| Breakdown chart | The composition — by category, status or staff member |
| Data table | The rows behind the charts, with **Export** |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Select its card. Reports also open from the tiles on [the executive dashboard](/practice/insights/dashboard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the period">
    Set the period select first. Every figure and chart on the page redraws for that range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch scope if the report has tabs">
    On **Revenue**, **Invoices** answers "what did we bill" and **Payments** answers "what did we collect". They are not the same number and are not meant to be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the table, not only the chart">
    The chart shows the shape; the table shows the rows you can reconcile against.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Money is shown in your practice's own currency — see [Currency](/platform/settings/currency).

Some reports are snapshots rather than periods. Those have no period select, say **As it stands now** in its place, and their footer says the same. Stock at risk of expiry has no meaningful "last 90 days"; it is either at risk today or it is not.

## Export the rows

**Export** on the data table takes the rows **currently shown** — the period and the scope you selected, not the whole dataset. Set both before exporting, or you will reconcile the wrong numbers. For a full copy of your practice's records rather than one report, use [Data export](/practice/settings/data-export).

## Who can do this

On a solo or team practice there is no role→route layer, so **Analytics** is reachable by anybody in the practice. What narrows the catalogue is the practice's edition, plan and care areas — not the person opening it.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Analytics | Any staff member | Nothing — the page is not role- or permission-gated |
| Open any report on the list | Any staff member | The catalogue, which filters by edition, plan and care area |
| Export a report's rows | Same as opening it | Whatever governs that report |

- **The invite sheet's money toggles — View financial dashboard, View Payment Reports — are recorded, not enforced here.** They are stored against the staff member; no gate on this page reads them.
- **A shorter list is the catalogue, not a permission.** A report you cannot see is one your practice has no data or no module for.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The report card count at the top of the page matches the number of cards below it.
- Changing the period changes the summary tiles, not just the chart.
- The exported file opens and its row count matches what the table showed.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="This report could not be loaded.">
    A temporary read failure. Reload the page, or choose a shorter period and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A report vanished from the list">
    The usual cause is a lapsed subscription: every gate falls back to Free. The other is a care area being switched off. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no period select on this report">
    That report is a snapshot and shows **As it stands now**. There is no range to choose.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The export has fewer rows than I expected">
    It exports what is on screen for the selected period and scope. Widen the period, or switch the scope tab, then export again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Revenue does not match my bank statement">
    The **Invoices** scope is what you billed; the **Payments** scope is what you recorded as received. Neither is a settlement report — your provider pays out on its own schedule.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Selecting Analytics opens a page of cards, not a menu">
    That page is the menu. Each card opens a report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I build my own report?">
    No. The catalogue is fixed and grows with the product. Export the closest report and pivot the rows yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I schedule a report by email?">
    Not from this page. What arrives by email is governed by your [notification settings](/practice/settings/notifications).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do reports contain client names?">
    Some tables list the record a row came from. Treat an export as clinical data: store it somewhere access-controlled, and delete it when you are done.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a report show a period I did not choose?">
    Each report opens on its default period. The select always shows which range is in force.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the Inventory report include what the store sold?">
    Yes — the store sells against the same stock. Takings appear in the **Store** report; the stock movement appears in **Inventory**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
