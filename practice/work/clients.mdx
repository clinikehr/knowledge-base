The client list is the single place a person is created, found and edited. Every other part of the product — appointments, notes, billing, documents — reads from it.

## What you're looking at

The page is headed **Clients Management**, with four figures across the top:

| Card | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Clients** | "Records you can open in this clinic" — a client restricted to assigned staff counts only for the people who can open them |
| **New Clients** | "Last 30 days" |
| **Active Clients** | "With recent activity" |
| **Consultations** | "Last 30 days" |

The toolbar carries **Import**, **Export** and **Create client**. When an outside system has sent something for you to review, a **Review outside data** button with a count appears at the start of the toolbar. It is not there when nothing is waiting. Selecting it opens a panel listing each item, with **Accept** and **Decline** (a reason is required to decline); nothing reaches a chart until you accept it. See [Records](/hospital/records/overview#outside-submissions--confirm-what-other-systems-send). The row under it holds **Search clients...**, the **Client status** and **Billing type** filters, a **Waitlist** button — a toggle, not a menu, which turns amber while it is on — and a **Sort** control offering **Sort by last name**, **Sort by first name** and **Sort by date added**. A filter that is applied shows a **1** badge and gains a **Clear filter** item.

The table has five columns:

| Column | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Name, client type, and the status as an editable dropdown |
| **Contact** | Email and phone |
| **Billing** | **Self pay** or **Insurance** |
| **Waitlist** | A **Waitlist** badge, or **N/A** |
| **Action** | The **⋯** row menu |

Status is changed in place from the **Name** column — **Active**, **Inactive**, **Prospective** or **Archived** — and confirms with **Client status updated**.

A practice with no records yet gets **Your client registry is empty** instead of a table, offering **Explore with sample clients**, **Import clients** and **Add your first client**. The sample tour is for clicking around in: "About 60 seconds · nothing is saved to your clinic". When a search matches nobody you get **No clients found matching "…"** instead.

## Add one client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Create client">
    The intake sheet opens over the list. It runs to three steps and shows its position as **Step 1 of 3 • Basic information**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter who they are">
    **Legal first name** and **Legal last name** are required; name they go by, gender and date of birth are worth filling but optional. Enter what you have — the record is editable forever and a booking does not wait on a complete profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how they pay and their status">
    **Billing type** is **Self pay** or **Insurance**, set separately for individual and group appointments. **Status** is required: **Active** or **Prospective**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create client**. The toast reads **Client created successfully!** and they appear in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The full intake form, field by field, is walked through in [Add your first client](/practice/get-started/add-clients).

## Open or edit a record

The **⋯** menu at the end of a row offers **View details** — the full chart, see [The client chart](/practice/work/client-chart) — **Edit**, **Add sticky note**, and **Add status note** for owners, managers and clinical staff.

## Work the self-registration waitlist

If people can register themselves, their requests arrive as a waitlist rather than as live records. Anyone waiting carries a **Waitlist** badge, and the **Waitlist** toggle shows only them. Then use the row menu:

- **Approve request** opens **Approve client request** — "They'll become an active client in your clinic." Confirm with **Approve**.
- **Decline request** — "The request will be archived and removed from the waitlist. This does not notify the requester."

<Warning>
  The **Enable client portal access** checkbox on **Approve client request** — "Lets them sign in via magic link to view records, book appointments, and message your team." — **arrives already ticked**. Approve without clearing it and the toast reads **Client approved and portal access enabled**: that person now has a working login to their own records. Clear the checkbox if you are not ready for that. See [Turn on the patient portal](/portal/setup/enable).
</Warning>

## Import and export a list

**Import** opens **Bulk-import patients** — "Upload a CSV to register multiple patients in this clinic at once." Take **Download sample CSV** first so your columns match, upload the file (".csv only, max 5MB, up to 500 rows"), read the **Preview**, then run it. The **Result** panel reports **Submitted**, **Created** and **Failed**, with **Download failed rows with error column** for anything that did not land.

**Export** opens the export sheet and downloads the registry as a single file.

Import works on every plan, and it respects your plan's client limit. On **Free**, which holds up to 50 clients, the sheet shows how many you have used before you run it — for example "42 of 50 clients used on the Free plan · this file adds 8" — and a file that would take you past the limit is refused as a whole, before anything is added, with the number of rows to remove. Nothing is imported part-way. Essential and above have no client limit.

The limit covers every way a client arrives, not only imports. When a Free practice is full, people booking online, joining a membership or registering as a new client from your pages are told "This clinic isn't accepting new clients online right now. Please contact the clinic directly." — your plan is never mentioned to them — and existing clients keep booking as normal. Registering a newborn from the maternity chart is never blocked.

Import in one pass rather than in fragments — a half-imported list is harder to reconcile than a missing one.

## Items accepted automatically

If an owner or manager has turned on [automatic acceptance](/platform/integrations/auto-accept-outside-data) for an app, its new allergies, conditions, reported medicines and referrals reach the record without waiting. Each one shows **Accepted automatically from** and the app's name, who turned it on and when, and "not reviewed by a clinician". That is different from an item a clinician confirmed, which shows no such line.

A clinician, manager or owner can select **Mark reviewed** on it. The line then reads "reviewed by" their name and the date. To take one out of use, choose **Entered in error**; the confirmation says it was accepted automatically. **Review outside data** also lists **Accepted automatically (last 7 days)** beneath the waiting items.

## Allergies a connected app sent, and the detailed allergy list

A connected app can send an allergy for a client. It waits in **Review outside data**, marked **From an outside system**, and nothing reaches the chart until a clinician decides. An allergy shows its substance, code, category, criticality, type, status, reactions (for example "hives, swelling (severe)"), onset date and note.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read it">
    Open **Review outside data** from the toolbar, or the **Outside submissions** card on the client's record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or decline">
    Select **Accept**, then confirm. Only a clinician, a manager or the owner can accept an allergy. For anyone else **Accept** is switched off and the item says why. If the same allergy is already on the chart, the app can still send it: it waits here, accepting is refused and the item stays waiting, so decline it with a reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it on the record">
    An accepted allergy appears under **Allergies (detailed)** on the client's record, with a quiet **From** line naming the sending app. The substance is also added to the client's plain allergy list, so medicine checks see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Allergies (detailed)** lists the allergies a clinician has confirmed, with their criticality, reactions and onset. It is a separate list from the allergies you type in **Edit client**. A clinician can select **Change** on a row to mark it **resolved**, **inactive**, **ruled out** or **entered in error**. This does not remove the name from the allergy list on the client record: edit it there too. Someone who is not a clinician sees the list but cannot change it. If the list cannot be loaded you see a message and **Try again**, never an empty list.

## Medications a connected app reported

A connected app can send a medicine a client says they take. It waits in **Review outside data**, marked **From an outside system**, and nothing reaches the chart until a clinician decides. A reported medicine shows the medicine, its code, dosage, route, frequency, status, dates, reason and note.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read it">
    Open **Review outside data** from the toolbar, or the **Outside submissions** card on the client's record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or decline">
    Select **Accept**, then confirm. Only a clinician, a manager or the owner can accept a reported medicine. For anyone else **Accept** is switched off and the item says why. Accepting records that the client reports taking the medicine. It does not prescribe or dispense anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it on the record">
    An accepted medicine appears under **Medications reported** on the client's record, with a quiet **From** line naming the sending app. Select **Details** to read the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Medications reported** is a separate list from prescriptions and from **Current Medications**. Nothing in it was ordered, dispensed or billed. A clinician can select **Change** on a row to mark it **stopped**, **completed** or **entered in error**. This does not cancel any prescription. Someone who is not a clinician sees the list but cannot change it. If the list cannot be loaded you see a message and **Try again**, never an empty list.

### How a reported medicine is used when you dispense

When a pharmacist dispenses to this client, [drug interaction screening](/pharmacy/dispensing/interaction-screening) also looks at the medicines the client reports taking, while they are active:

- A reported medicine that has a recognised medicine code is checked for interactions, like a prescription the client already has.
- A reported medicine without one (typed in words, or with a code we do not recognise) is **never guessed at**. The dispensing form lists it as **Reported medicine not checked**, in the client's own words, so the pharmacist knows it was not checked.
- A stopped, completed, on hold or entered-in-error medicine is not used.

On the client's record, each active medicine says whether it is **Used in pharmacy interaction checks** or **Not checked by pharmacy interaction checks**.

## Outside care team

**Outside care team** on a client's record lists the doctors and practices outside your clinic who look after the client: their primary care doctor, the clinician who referred them, a specialist. It is a contact list. Nobody on it is given access to anything. It is not the same as the staff you assign to a client, which is managed elsewhere.

Each contact shows its type (**Primary care**, **Referring**, **Specialist** or **Other**), name, practice, specialty, NPI, phone, fax and address, and a **Primary** badge on the client's main contact of that type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a contact">
    Select **Add contact**, enter a name, choose a type, fill in whatever you know and select **Add contact**. An NPI is 10 digits; leave it blank if you do not have it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it">
    Select **Edit** on a contact. Turn on **Primary for this type** to make it the main contact of that type. Only one contact of each type is primary, so this clears it on the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it">
    Select **Remove** and confirm. A removed contact stays in the audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Notes** on a contact are only for your team. Any staff member can add, edit or remove a contact.

A connected app can add a contact too. Its contacts say **From** and the app's name, and the app that added one can still change or remove it. Every contact says whether connected apps can change it: contacts your team or an intake form added cannot be changed by an app. You can edit or remove any contact yourself, including one an app added. If the list cannot be loaded you see a message and **Try again**, never an empty list. When a client's intake form names a primary care doctor and you accept it, it joins this list as the **Primary care** contact.

## Referrals another practice sent

A connected app can also send a referral asking your clinic to see a client. It waits in **Review outside data** like any outside item. Any member of staff can add it to your list, and the client's record then shows a **Referrals** section with its status. Work it from the **Referrals to action** tab or from that section. See [Work referrals sent to your clinic](/practice/work/referrals).

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Clients** opens. Permission decides what you may do once it is open.

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Clients** and search it | Any staff member | Role |
| Create, edit or archive a client | Any staff member | Role |
| See clients who are not their own | Owner, manager, and anyone on **Full client list** or **Entire practice** | The access tier chosen when they were invited |
| Add a **status note** | Owner, manager, clinical roles | Role — the item is simply absent from the **⋯** menu for anyone else |
| Approve or decline a waitlist request | Anyone who can open the list | Role |

On Solo and Team the real levers are the **role** you gave someone and the **access tier** you picked when you invited them — **Basic**, **Billing**, **Full client list** or **Entire practice**. There is no per-role navigation editor on these plans, and no per-action permission covers the client list itself: the **Permissions** sheet on a team member ("What they may do on the pages their role already opens") carries only clinical-authority acts such as signing and countersigning.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding a module from somebody's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The client appears in the list and can be found by searching their name.
- Opening **View details** shows the details you entered.
- They can be selected when booking an appointment and when writing a note.
- An approved waitlist request no longer carries a **Waitlist** badge.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A client I just created is not in the list">
    A **Client status**, **Billing type** or **Waitlist** filter is still applied from earlier. Each filter carries a **Clear filter** item at the bottom of its menu; the **Waitlist** toggle is cleared by selecting it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create client is greyed out">
    You have reached your plan's record limit. Hovering the disabled button shows the upgrade prompt. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An approved client cannot sign in to the portal">
    The **Enable client portal access** checkbox was cleared at approval. Open the record and grant access from there — see [Turn on the patient portal](/portal/setup/enable).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Some rows failed on import">
    The sheet gives you the failures back with **Download failed rows with error column**. Correct those rows and upload that file — do not re-upload the whole list, or you will duplicate the rows that already worked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The import says my plan has no room">
    Your plan's client limit would be passed. The message names your plan, its limit, how many clients you already have and how many rows to remove — trim the file to that, or upgrade to **Essential** for unlimited clients, then run it again. Nothing from the refused file was added. Archived clients still count toward the limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sample clients appeared that nobody created">
    That is **Explore with sample clients**. It is a preview of the screen, separate from your real list, and "nothing is saved to your clinic".
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Inactive and Archived?">
    Both take somebody out of your working list. **Inactive** is for a client you expect back; **Archived** is for one you do not. Neither deletes anything — the chart, its notes and its invoices stay exactly as they were.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete a client?">
    No. Clinical records are kept for their full retention period, so a client is retired by status rather than removed. Set them to **Archived**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does declining a waitlist request tell the person?">
    No — "This does not notify the requester." If they should hear from you, contact them yourself before declining.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can two staff members edit the same client at once?">
    Yes, and the last save wins. For anything the whole team needs to see, use a sticky note on the chart rather than a field somebody else may overwrite.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does importing send anybody an email?">
    No. An import creates records and nothing else — no portal invitation, no welcome message. Portal access is granted per person afterwards.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
