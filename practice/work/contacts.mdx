**Contacts** is where people who are not yet clients live: enquiries from your website, referrals, callbacks and anyone you are still following up. They move across a board of stages until they become a client or drop out.

## What you're looking at

Five tabs, of which the last two appear only for people who may manage the CRM:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Board** | Contacts as cards in stage columns — drag between stages |
| **Contacts (\{n\})** | The same people as a searchable table. The count is what your filters currently match, and disappears when nothing does |
| **Follow-ups** | What is due and who owes it |
| **Campaigns** | Marketing sends against a saved segment |
| **Insights** | Lead scoring and pipeline analysis |

The header carries **Custom fields**, **Import**, **Export**, **Share contact page** (or **Set up contact page** if you have not published one), a chip showing contacts used against your plan limit, and **New contact**. Everything on that row except the chip and **New contact** is manager-only.

Under it: **Search by name, email, phone, or company…**, a pipeline picker when you have more than one, a **Segments** menu, a sort control offering **Recent activity** and **Lead score**, and **Clear** when filters are on. The **Board** tab adds **Manage stages** and a **Board** / **List** toggle.

The **Contacts** table has seven columns — **Name**, **Contact**, **Stage**, **Score**, **Source**, **Owner** and **Last activity**. **Stage** shows one of **Lead**, **Inquiry**, **Prospect**, **Waitlist**, **Customer** or **Lost**; **Score** shows a number, or an em dash at zero; **Source** names where they came from (**Booking page**, **Website**, **Contact form**, **Referral**, **Manual**, **Import**, **Walk-in**, **Phone**, **Social**). A contact whose priority is not normal carries a **High** or **Low** badge beside their name.

A brand-new practice sees **No pipeline stages yet.** before a pipeline exists, **Drop contacts here** in each empty column once one does, and **No contacts match your filters.** on the table. **Follow-ups** reads "You're all caught up — no open follow-ups."

## Work the board

Drag a contact's card from one column to the next to move it a stage. Save the current filters from the **Segments** menu with **Save current as segment…** for reuse in a campaign, and bring an existing list in with **Import** or take yours out with **Export**.

**Manage stages** opens the stage manager, where you can rename, recolour, reorder, add and remove stages, and mark which one counts as won and which as lost. A pipeline must keep at least one stage.

<Note>
  Customising the board needs the Essential plan or above — the notice reads "Custom pipelines & stages are an Essential feature". Below that you get the default pipeline and its default stages, which is a complete working board. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
</Note>

## Work the follow-ups

**Follow-ups** lists what is open, newest problem first, with a **My follow-ups** / **All** toggle. Each row is badged **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** or **No due date**, with the date appended, and shows the task over the contact it belongs to. **Mark done** clears it.

## Publish the public enquiry form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form settings">
    Go to **Settings → Contact Form** — "Where people fill out the form." Everything about the public form is configured here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields">
    The field editor explains itself: "Drag to reorder. The first six are built-in fields you can rename or hide; add your own custom fields below." Custom fields are a Team feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the hosted link">
    **Share contact page** copies the address of the hosted form. This works on every plan — put it on your website, in an email or on a printed card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed it, if your plan allows">
    "Copy this snippet into your site's HTML, just before `</body>`." There are two levels: a widget snippet that drops a floating button onto your site, and a raw iframe for full control over placement. The iframe is a Team feature.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Submissions arrive on the board in your first stage, ready to work.

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Contacts** opens, and whether you may manage it. There is no third layer here: managing the CRM is a single yes-or-no that owners and managers hold.

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Contacts**, search it, add and move contacts | Any staff member | Role |
| Complete a follow-up | Any staff member | Role |
| Open **Campaigns** and **Insights** | Owner, manager | Role — the tabs are absent for everyone else, never greyed out |
| **Import**, **Export**, **Custom fields**, **Manage stages**, save a segment | Owner, manager | Role — "Only clinic owners and managers can do this" |
| Run more than one pipeline, or customise stages | Owner, manager, on Essential or above | Plan |

Two things worth separating. **Plan never hides a tab** — it caps how many contacts, segments, campaigns and pipelines you may have, and those caps report themselves when you hit them. **Permission does hide tabs**: **Campaigns** and **Insights** are simply not in the strip if you may not manage the CRM, so an absent tab is the answer rather than a fault.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding a module from somebody's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Campaigns or Insights tab">
    You may not manage the CRM — that is owners and managers. They are hidden rather than disabled, so an absent tab is the answer, not a fault. Your plan does not affect this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Manage stages will not let me add a stage">
    "Custom pipelines & stages are an Essential feature." Below Essential the default pipeline is fixed. The button also stops at your plan's pipeline count: "You've reached your plan's limit of \{n\} pipelines."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New contacts are refused">
    You are at your plan's contact limit. The message names your plan, the limit and the next plan up, with an **Upgrade** action on the toast.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Contacts tab shows no count">
    The count is what your filters currently match, not your total. Nothing matching means no number — use **Clear** to drop the filters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The embed snippet does nothing on my site">
    It must go inside the page's HTML just before `</body>`, not inside a text block or a page builder's plain-text field. If your plan only includes the hosted link, use **Share contact page** instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An enquiry has become a real client — what now?">
    Register them under **Clients** — see [Manage your client list](/practice/work/clients) — and move their card to the stage you have marked as won. The contact and the client record are separate things, and both are worth keeping.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a contact count against my client limit?">
    No. Contacts and clients are counted separately, on separate limits. Someone only starts using a client record once you register them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will a campaign email somebody who has opted out?">
    No. "Consent + unsubscribe are always enforced" — a campaign reaches opted-in, non-unsubscribed contacts only, whatever the segment says.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Score column?">
    A lead score the product calculates from a contact's activity and source. It is a sorting aid, not a judgement — a score of zero shows as an em dash rather than a nought.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to contacts if I drop to a lower plan?">
    Nothing is deleted. You fall back to that plan's limits, so you keep what you have but cannot add beyond the new cap until you upgrade again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
