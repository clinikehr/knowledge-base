Mail has no storage limit of its own. Every message and every attachment your practice keeps counts toward the **one Drive storage amount** your plan includes, alongside your files, patient files, Team chat and forms. See [Drive storage and limits](/platform/drive/storage-and-limits) for the amount on each plan.

## The one thing to know first

<Note>
  **Incoming mail never stops.** Everything addressed to you still arrives when Drive is full, and nothing is bounced or refused. **Messages without attachments still send.** Only a new message *with attachments* is held back.
</Note>

This is deliberate. A message from a patient — a referral, a discharge summary, a photograph of an injury — often has nowhere else to go. Refusing it because storage is full would lose clinical information permanently, so Mail does not do that.

## What you see, and when

| Drive storage used | What Mail shows | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 80% | Nothing | Nothing |
| 80% or more | A banner above your mail | Nothing yet — this is notice. Drive also sends its own notice |
| 95% or more | A stronger banner | Nothing yet |
| Full | A red banner | You cannot attach files to a new message. Plain messages still send. Receiving continues. |

Two figures are involved. A message's size includes its attachments, and the copy of an attachment saved as a file in Drive is counted separately, because both are stored. Mail in Spam counts too; deleted mail stops counting.

The banner shows the amount used and the amount in your plan, and links to **Drive storage**. The figure is the same one Drive shows. Mail sends no separate storage warning of its own: you are told once, in Drive.

## When Drive is full

- **A new message with attachments cannot be sent** (and a file cannot be attached) until you free space or add storage. You are told why rather than the message being silently queued.
- **A message without attachments sends as usual.**
- **Everything addressed to your practice still arrives**, is filed in **Inbox** as usual, and is fully readable.

## Freeing space

Attachments are almost always the reason a practice runs out — a message is a few kilobytes, a scan is several megabytes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the conversations with files">
    In Mail, open the filters and select **Has attachment**. Sort by date and work backwards from the oldest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete what you no longer need in Mail">
    Anything already filed on a client's chart does not need a second copy in a mailbox. Deleting the message in Mail does not touch the chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Empty Spam and Trash">
    Both clean themselves out after 30 days, but clearing them by hand releases the space now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Deleted mail stops counting toward your storage, but it is not destroyed.** Health records must be retained for seven years, so a deleted message leaves every view and stops using your allowance — we keep the retention copy at our own cost.
</Warning>

## Getting more room

On a paid plan the owner can add a monthly storage pack in Drive, or change the plan. See [Buy more storage](/platform/drive/storage-and-limits#buy-more-storage).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will a patient be told their message bounced?">
    No. Nothing your practice does with storage causes a bounce.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does Mail no longer warn me at 90%?">
    Mail's own storage limit and its own warnings were retired. Drive tells you at 80% and 95%, so you are never told twice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the client portal count toward this?">
    Attachments on secure portal messages are Drive files and count; the portal itself does not use Mail.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
