The **Appointment** workspace is where the day is booked and read. It holds the calendar, the services clients can book, the availability behind every free slot, and the settings that decide how bookings arrive.

## What you're looking at

The sidebar row is **Appointment**, singular. Its own rail, headed **Appointments**, has four entries — and only the first stays in the workspace:

| Entry | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The calendar |
| **Services** | The **Appointment Services** tab in Settings |
| **Booking Page** | The **Booking Page Designer** tab in Settings |
| **Settings** | The **Booking Settings** tab in Settings |

Changing a service or a working pattern is therefore the same edit wherever you start from.

The calendar's top row carries **Today**, back and forward arrows, the month, a **Live** or **Syncing** connection pill, the view switcher — **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, plus **Providers** and **Resources** swimlanes — and a colour control offering **Color by Status** and **Color by Service**.

The row beneath it holds **Search patients, services...**, **More filters** with a count badge, **Refresh**, **Current Schedule** and **Create**.

**More filters** opens **Calendar Filters** — "Refine your view by status, service, or staff." — with sections for **Appointment Status**, **Urgency Level**, **Event Types**, **Task Status**, **Task Priority**, **Venue Type**, **Services** and **Staff Members**, and **Reset all filters** at the bottom.

A booking carries one of seven statuses, which is what **Color by Status** colours: **Scheduled**, **Confirmed**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, **No Show** and **Rescheduled**.

A brand-new practice sees the empty hour grid, not a message — the calendar always renders the day. The swimlanes do explain themselves: **Resources** reads "No rooms or equipment yet. Add them in Appointment Settings → Resources."

<Note>
  Only **Overview**, **Services**, **Booking Page** and **Settings** are part of the workspace today. Anything else you reach by editing the address bar is unreleased.
</Note>

## Book an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a slot, or select Create">
    Selecting an empty slot opens the sheet with that time already filled in. **Create** opens the same sheet — titled **Initial Entry** — with no time imposed, which is what you want when the client is standing in front of you. Its three tabs are **Appointment**, **Event** and **Task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it is for">
    Leave the type on **Individual or couple**, or choose **Group** for a group session — that needs the Essential plan, and the gate reads **Upgrade for group sessions**. Then find the person with **Search Client**; **Create client** beside it registers somebody who is not on file yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the services and the provider">
    Add one or more under **Services**. Under **Select Provider**, choose a staff member or leave it on **Any available provider** and let the slot decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time and place">
    Set the start, end and **Duration** under **Schedule**. Choose **Location**: **Physical Office**, which asks for an address or room number, or **Video Office**, which generates its own joining link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Book appointment">
    The toast reads **Appointment created successfully** and the booking appears on the calendar. Confirmation and reminder emails go out from your notification settings — you do not send them by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Booking one for the first time, field by field, is walked through in [Schedule your first appointment](/practice/get-started/first-appointment).

## Set availability

Availability is not on the calendar — it is in **Settings → Booking Settings**, and four things there decide every free slot:

| Setting | Decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Clinic Availability** | The regular working pattern, per provider |
| **Special Dates** | One-off closures, holidays and extra sessions |
| **Resources** | Rooms and equipment a booking has to reserve |
| Per-provider booking policies | Notice, buffers and what each provider accepts online |

A slot only appears when the schedule is open, no special date closes it, and any resource the service needs is free. Which reminders go out is set under **Notifications**; the channels they travel on are set under **Settings → Communications**, and a reminder with no channel is never sent. See [Notification settings](/platform/settings/notifications).

## Charge the card automatically with Auto Pay

**Auto Pay** charges a client's saved card when their appointment is **Confirmed** or **Completed** — you choose which — so a routine visit never needs a manual invoice. Turn it on in **Settings → Auto Pay**.

<Warning>
  **Auto Pay** takes real money without anyone pressing a button. It needs the Essential plan or above and a connected Stripe account, only charges people who already have a card on file, and carries a **1.9%** platform fee on every charge. Only the practice owner can change these settings. Turn on **Email a receipt to the patient** so nobody is charged silently.
</Warning>

Card details are held by Stripe and never reach ClinikEHR. Connect the account first under **Settings → Online Payments**; until you do, the **Auto Pay** card shows **Connect Stripe to use Auto Pay** and stays inert.

## Who can do this

Role decides whether **Appointment** opens. Permission decides what you may do once it is open.

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the calendar and book | Any staff member | Role |
| See bookings for clients who are not their own | Owner, manager, and anyone on **Full client list** or **Entire practice** | The access tier chosen when they were invited |
| Change a service, a schedule or a resource | Owner, manager | Role — these live in Settings |
| Turn **Auto Pay** on, or change what it charges | Owner | Role — the card is inert for everyone else |

On Solo and Team the real levers are the **role** somebody holds and the **access tier** you picked when you invited them. There is no per-role navigation editor on these plans — that is an Enterprise feature — and no per-action permission covers booking: the **Permissions** sheet on a team member ("What they may do on the pages their role already opens") carries only clinical-authority acts such as signing and countersigning.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding a module from somebody's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The new appointment is on the calendar at the right time, in the right provider's column.
- Opening it shows the client, the service and the venue you entered.
- With **Auto Pay** on, a confirmed appointment for someone with a saved card produces a paid invoice rather than an outstanding one.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No free slots show for a provider">
    "No bookable slots for this date in your configured schedule." The provider has no schedule, a special date is closing the day, or a resource the service requires is already booked. **CONFIGURE AVAILABILITY** in that warning takes you straight to **Booking Settings**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The person I want to book is not in the search">
    They have no client record yet. Use **Create client** inside the booking sheet, or add them from **Clients** first — see [Manage your client list](/practice/work/clients).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Group is flagged and will not open">
    Group sessions need the Essential plan — the gate reads **Upgrade for group sessions**. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This appointment would run past midnight">
    A start time plus a duration that crosses into the next day is refused: "Pick an earlier start time or a shorter duration." Split it into two bookings instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Auto Pay is on but nothing is being charged">
    Three things stop it: the plan is below Essential, Stripe is not connected, or the client has no saved card. The screen says so itself — "Only patients with a saved card are charged." Everyone else is invoiced as normal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reminders are not arriving">
    Check the category is enabled under **Notifications**, then check the channel exists under **Settings → Communications**. A reminder with no channel is not queued and not retried.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does cancelling an appointment refund the client?">
    No. Setting a booking to **Cancelled** frees the slot and stops its reminders; money already taken stays taken. Refund it from the invoice — see [Invoices](/practice/money/invoices).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Cancelled and No Show?">
    **Cancelled** means the appointment was called off. **No Show** means it stood and the client did not arrive. Keep them apart: they are the two numbers you will want when you review a month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does an online booking say Unassigned?">
    A client who booked without choosing a provider lands under **Unassigned / Online** in the **Staff Members** filter. Open the booking and set a provider.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I book somebody into two places at once?">
    The calendar will let you; the booking page will not offer the slot to a client, because online availability checks the provider and any resource the service needs. Double-booking is a deliberate act, never an accident of the public page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should the team use Color by Status or Color by Service?">
    Pick one and keep to it. The setting is held per person and per browser, so a room full of people reading two different colour schemes is the failure it causes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
