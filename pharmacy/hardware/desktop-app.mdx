The desktop app is the same pharmacy workspace you use in a browser, installed on the till. It adds the three things a browser cannot do: print a receipt without a dialog, keep queued sales somewhere durable, and reach a serial count terminal.

It contains **no app code of its own** — it loads your hosted workspace, exactly as a browser does. That is deliberate: it can never fall behind the web version, and there is no second product to keep in step.

## Pick the right build

| Build | Loads | Install it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ClinikEHR Pharmacy POS** | Your pharmacy workspace, straight to the till | Counter terminals |
| **ClinikEHR Desktop** | The full clinic workspace | Not a pharmacy install |
| **ClinikEHR LIMS Desktop** | The diagnostics workspace | Not a pharmacy install |

For a pharmacy the answer is **ClinikEHR Pharmacy POS** on every machine, front and back office. The other two point at different workspaces entirely and will not show you your pharmacy.

Each build reopens on whatever page you were last on, so a counter terminal comes back up on the till after a restart. Neither pharmacy build signs you out on a timer: a session ends when someone ends it. The **ClinikEHR Desktop** build additionally blanks its own window in screen-capture software, because the medical records it shows are confidential in a way a till is not.

Installers are published per operating system:

- **Windows** — an installer `.exe`. It installs for the current user and lets you choose the folder.
- **macOS** — a `.dmg` or a `.zip`, for both Intel and Apple silicon machines.
- **Linux** — an AppImage or a `.deb`.

Get the current links from [Desktop app downloads](/platform/apps/desktop).

## Install and set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the installer for this machine">
    Take the **Pharmacy POS** build and the file that matches the operating system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the installer">
    On Windows, read the SmartScreen entry under **Common issues** before you start — the warning is expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it while the machine is online">
    This step is not optional. The app has to load your workspace once with a working connection before anything offline will work — until it has, there is nothing cached to fall back to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in exactly as you do in a browser">
    Same email, same password, same two-factor. Nothing privileged ships inside the app: it signs in as your own user, so what you can see and do is identical to the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the receipt paper form on this machine">
    Do it now, once, rather than discovering it mid-sale. See [Set up your receipt printer](/pharmacy/hardware/thermal-printer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you're looking at

A normal desktop window with your pharmacy inside it — the same sidebar, the same till, the same colours. There is no extra chrome to learn and no settings screen of its own. Three differences give it away:

- The window title carries the build name rather than a browser's tab bar.
- A receipt printed from a sale goes straight to the printer with a **"Receipt sent to printer"** toast, instead of opening a print dialog.
- The receipt dialog on the [sales list](/pharmacy/money/sales) carries a small **Printer settings** control that does not exist in a browser.

Selecting a link to somewhere outside your workspace — a supplier, a support article — opens it in your normal browser. The app is locked to your workspace's own address and will not navigate anywhere else.

## Who can do this

**Nothing here is permission-gated.** Installing the app is a decision about a device, not about a person: anyone who can sign in to your pharmacy can sign in to the app, and they see exactly what they see in a browser — same role, same permissions, same figures withheld.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Install the app on a machine | Whoever administers that computer | The machine, not ClinikEHR |
| Sign in to it | Any staff member of your pharmacy | Their normal sign-in |
| What they can then see and do | Exactly as in a browser | Their role and permissions, unchanged |
| Set the receipt paper form | Anyone using that machine | Nothing — it is a per-computer setting |

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted in the app for the same reason they are unrestricted in a browser, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Installing the app changes neither — it is the same workspace, audited the same way.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The app opens on your pharmacy workspace, signed in, with the same sidebar you see in a browser.
- It lands on the till, or on whatever page you were last on.
- Opening the receipt dialog on a recent sale shows a **Printer settings** control — that control appears **only** inside the desktop app, so seeing it confirms you are running the app rather than a browser window.
- A test reprint prints without a dialog and toasts **"Receipt sent to printer"**.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Can't reach ClinikEHR' with a Retry button">
    A cold start with nothing cached cannot show your workspace at all, so you get an offline page and a **Retry** button. It also reassures you: "Sales already saved on this device are safe and will sync automatically." Get the machine online and select **Retry**. Opening the app once online **before** you need it offline is the single most common setup mistake.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Windows shows an 'unrecognised app' warning">
    Current builds are unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen shows a click-through on first run. Choose **More info** and then **Run anyway**. Download installers only from the links your workspace gives you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The macOS app is not updating itself">
    Automatic updates need a signed build, and current macOS builds are unsigned. Download the newer `.dmg` and install it over the old one. Windows and Linux update themselves.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A link opened in my browser instead of the app">
    Expected. The app is locked to your workspace's own address, so anything external opens in your default browser. That lock is a safety feature, not a fault.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I installed it but I am looking at the wrong product">
    You have **ClinikEHR Desktop** or **ClinikEHR LIMS Desktop**. Those point at different workspaces. Uninstall and take the **Pharmacy POS** build.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Receipts still open a print dialog">
    Either you are in a browser window rather than the app, or the app could not reach the printer and fell back — it says so: "Direct printing unavailable — using the print dialog". See [Set up your receipt printer](/pharmacy/hardware/thermal-printer).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the app need updating when ClinikEHR changes?">
    No. The app holds no product code — it loads your live workspace, so a change we ship reaches it the moment it reaches the browser. Shell updates are rare and are about the window itself, not the pharmacy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need the app to sell offline?">
    No — a browser queues offline sales too. The app stores that queue outside the browser profile, so clearing browsing data cannot wipe queued sales, and that is the reason to prefer it on a till. See [Selling when the internet is down](/pharmacy/pos/offline).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is anything stored on the machine I should worry about?">
    Only your sign-in session and the offline sale queue, both of which the app needs to work. Nothing privileged is built into the app, and signing out clears the session exactly as it would in a browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can two people share one installed app?">
    Yes. It is one workspace with normal sign-ins, so a shift change is a sign-out and a sign-in. Neither pharmacy build locks the screen on a timer, so sign out deliberately when you leave a shared machine.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does not work offline in the app?">
    The same things that do not work offline in a browser: payment, returns, and workflow steps on records that already exist. Sale capture queues and replays; everything else waits. See [Working offline](/platform/apps/offline).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
