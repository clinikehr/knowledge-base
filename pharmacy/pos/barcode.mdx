Scanning is the fastest way to build a cart, and it is the only practical way to work a catalogue bigger than the 100 tiles the grid shows. This page covers the three ways to scan a product, and the separate trick of scanning a customer's receipt.

## What you're looking at

Scanning has no screen of its own — it is the till and the sales list, listening.

On **POS**, the search card reads "Search or scan (name, generic, brand, barcode)…" with a camera button beside it whose tooltip is **Scan barcode with camera**. The camera opens the **Scan medication barcode** dialog — "Point the camera at the product barcode to add it to the cart." — with **Choose camera** and a torch button. Nothing on the page indicates a handheld scanner is connected, because nothing needs to: a wedge scanner is picked up at page level whether or not a field has focus.

On **Sales** the equivalent box reads "Search or scan receipt..." and its camera button is **Scan a receipt barcode with the camera**, opening **Scan receipt barcode**.

**A brand-new pharmacy** can scan, but nothing will match: a barcode only resolves once it is saved on a catalogue entry, so a fresh workspace answers every scan with **No medication matches that barcode**.

## Three ways to scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="A handheld or countertop scanner">
    A keyboard-wedge scanner — the ordinary kind that behaves like a very fast typist — is picked up **anywhere on the till page**. You do not have to click into the search box first, and you should not: just point and pull the trigger.

    The till recognises a scan by the speed of the burst, so a code typed by hand is never mistaken for one. See [Barcode scanners](/pharmacy/hardware/barcode-scanner) for setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The search box">
    Scan or type into "Search or scan (name, generic, brand, barcode)…" and press **Enter**. A value that looks like a code is treated as a scan; a value that looks like a name is treated as a search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The camera">
    Select the camera button — **Scan barcode with camera** — beside the search box. The **Scan medication barcode** dialog opens: "Point the camera at the product barcode to add it to the cart."

    Use **Choose camera** to pick a lens on a device with more than one, and the torch button to light a dark shelf.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make a product scannable

A product is only scannable once its barcode is saved on its catalogue entry. The inventory **Add item** sheet has a **Barcode / UPC (optional)** field with two helpers: **Scan pack**, which reads the code straight off the box, and **Generate…**, which mints an internal code for anything unbarcoded — a repackaged item, a local product, a bulk line you split yourself.

Print shelf and pack labels from the inventory row action **Print barcode label**, which takes a **Copies** count. See [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index).

## Scan a receipt to find a sale

On the [Sales page](/pharmacy/money/sales), the same scanner pulls up a transaction. Scan the barcode at the bottom of a customer's receipt into "Search or scan receipt...", or use the camera button — **Scan a receipt barcode with the camera** — which opens **Scan receipt barcode**: "Point the camera at the barcode at the bottom of the receipt."

- A completed sale opens straight into its amendment sheet, ready for a return or a discount — see [Returns, voids and amendments](/pharmacy/pos/returns).
- A sale in any other state opens its receipt instead, with a toast naming the state: **Sale \{number\} is \{status\} — opening its receipt.**
- Anything that is not one of our receipts is refused with **That barcode is not a ClinikEHR receipt.**

<Note>
  An offline sale's provisional slip carries **no barcode**, because its number does not exist on the server yet. Scan the reprinted receipt after the sale has synced. See [Print and reprint receipts](/pharmacy/pos/receipts).
</Note>

## Who can do this

**Role** decides where you can scan. A **Cashier** reaches **POS** and **Sales**, so both scan boxes are available to them; saving a barcode onto a product is not, because **Inventory** is not in their sidebar.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan a product into the cart | Cashier, Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | **Dispense and sell at the till** |
| Scan a receipt on the Sales page | Anyone who can open **Sales** | Role |
| Open the amended sale a receipt scan lands on | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | **Refund or void a sale** |
| Save a barcode onto a product, or **Generate…** one | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **Adjust stock counts** |
| Print barcode labels from an inventory row | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **Adjust stock counts** |

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Scanning a receipt is never refused; what the scan *opens* can be. Without **Refund or void a sale** the amendment sheet reads "Needs authorisation — amending a completed sale is reserved for your Pharmacist-in-Charge."

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- A toast reads **Scanned: \{product name\}**, and the line is in the cart. The toast appears only when the product was actually added, so a refusal never looks like a success.
- Scanning the same code again increases the quantity rather than adding a second line.
- After a scan, focus returns to the search box ready for the next one.

If nothing matches, the toast reads **No medication matches that barcode** and the code is dropped into the search box so you can look for the product by name without re-typing it.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The scanner types into the box but nothing is added">
    The code is not on any catalogue entry — you will have seen **No medication matches that barcode**. Find the product by name, open it in Inventory, and save the barcode on it with **Scan pack**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Scanning does nothing while a dialog is open">
    That is deliberate. Wedge capture stands down while any dialog, menu or picker is open, and while you are typing in a field other than the search box, so a scan can never fire behind a screen you are reading. Close the dialog and scan again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Camera access requires HTTPS. Open this page over a secure connection to scan.'">
    The webcam scanner only runs on a secure connection. Use your normal ClinikEHR address rather than a plain-HTTP or IP-address shortcut.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Camera permission denied. Allow camera access in your browser settings to scan.'">
    The browser is blocking the camera for this site. Allow it in the site permissions, then select **Retry**. On a shared counter machine, check the permission is not blocked at the operating-system level too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'No camera device was found on this machine.'">
    There is no webcam attached, or another application has it open. Close the other application, or use a handheld scanner instead — it is faster at a counter anyway.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Every scan lands in the wrong field">
    Something else on the page has focus and is swallowing the keystrokes. Select an empty part of the till once, then scan; the till is listening at the page level and does not need a field to be focused.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a special scanner?">
    No. Any keyboard-wedge scanner — the ordinary kind that behaves like a very fast typist — works, handheld or countertop, corded or wireless. There is nothing to install. See [Barcode scanners](/pharmacy/hardware/barcode-scanner).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the till tell a scan from typing?">
    By the speed of the burst. A scanner delivers a whole code in a fraction of the time a person takes, so a code you type by hand is treated as a search, not a scan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What about products with no barcode on the box?">
    Use **Generate…** on the product's **Barcode / UPC (optional)** field to mint an internal code, then print labels with the inventory row action **Print barcode label**. Repackaged items, local products and bulk lines you split yourself all want this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I scan while offline?">
    Yes. Scanning and product search run against the cached catalogue — the whole sellable list, not just the 100 tiles on screen. See [Selling when the internet is down](/pharmacy/pos/offline).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will scanning work on a tablet with no scanner?">
    Yes, through the camera button. It is slower than a handheld at a busy counter, but it needs no hardware and works anywhere the page is served over a secure connection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
