Two things that keep a queue moving: parking a customer who has gone back to the shelf, and taking money off without leaving the till.

## What you're looking at

Both jobs live on the **Cart** card in the right-hand column of the till.

| Control | Where it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hold** | The **Cart** card header | Parks the current sale and clears the till |
| **Held** | Beside it, with a count when carts are parked | Opens the **Held carts** sheet |
| **Clear All** | The **Cart** card header | Empties the cart — see the warning below |
| **Discount** | A dotted link under each cart line | Opens **Discount this line** |
| **%** and your currency symbol | Small toggles above **Subtotal** | Discount the whole cart by percentage or flat amount |

The **Held carts** sheet is headed "Parked sales waiting for the customer to complete payment. Resume to continue, or discard." Each card shows its label, **\{n\} items · \{amount\}**, how long ago it was parked, and **Resume** and **Discard**.

**With nothing parked**, the **Held** button carries no count and the sheet is empty — there is no error, just an empty list. A discounted line, by contrast, is always visible: the original price is struck through and a **−\{n\}%** badge sits on the row.

## Hold a cart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the cart as normal">
    Add the products, and attach the customer if you have one — the hold is labelled with their name, which is what makes it findable later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Hold">
    The **Cart** card's **Hold** button parks the sale and clears the till for the next person. A toast confirms it: **"\{label\}" held — attend the next customer**.

    The label is the customer's name if one is attached; otherwise the first product and a count, such as "Paracetamol \+2"; otherwise **Walk-in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Serve the next customer">
    The till is yours again. The **Held** button now carries a count of parked carts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the cart back up">
    Select **Held** to open the **Held carts** sheet — "Parked sales waiting for the customer to complete payment. Resume to continue, or discard."

    Each card shows its label, **\{n\} items · \{amount\}** and how long ago it was parked. Select **Resume** to load it back into the till, or **Discard** to throw it away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the hold, or complete the sale">
    Once a cart is resumed, the hold button reads **Update hold** — use it to park the same cart again with your changes. Or take payment as usual and the hold is done with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Clear All** does not just empty the cart — it also drops the link to the cart you resumed. Press **Hold** afterwards and you park a _new_ cart, leaving the original one still sitting in the list. Use **Discard** in the **Held carts** sheet to get rid of a parked cart, not **Clear All**.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Held carts are stored on the device, so they survive a refresh and work during an outage — a card may show **unsynced** while offline. They belong to the terminal they were parked on: a cart held on the front till is not on the back one.
</Note>

## Discount one line

Use this when the customer keeps everything but one item is damaged, short-dated or being matched to a competitor's price.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the line's discount menu">
    Select the dotted **Discount** link under the cart line. The menu is headed **Discount this line**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the percentage">
    Five presets — 5, 10, 15, 20 and 50 — each showing what it takes off, such as "5% · save \{amount\}". The line total is then struck through and the reduced price shown beneath it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it if you change your mind">
    The line carries a badge such as **−10%**. Select it to take the discount off — its tooltip reads "Remove this line's discount".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discount the whole sale

Above **Subtotal** sit two small toggle buttons: **%** for a percentage off the whole cart, and your currency symbol for a flat amount off. Select one, enter the value, and the **Discount** row in the totals updates.

A whole-cart discount stacks with line discounts, so check the **Total** rather than assuming — it is easy to give twice as much away as you meant to.

<Note>
  Both kinds of discount need the **Discount beyond the set limit** permission. A cashier does not have it by default, which is deliberate: the person taking the money is not usually the person who decides the price. See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
</Note>

## Who can do this

The two jobs on this page sit on opposite sides of a deliberate line. **Holding** a cart is part of serving a queue and every till operator does it. **Discounting** is a pricing decision and is not a cashier's to make.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold a cart, resume it, discard it | Anyone whose role reaches **POS**, a Cashier included | Role — holding is not permission-gated |
| Discount one line | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor — **not** a Cashier | **Discount beyond the set limit** |
| Discount the whole cart | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | **Discount beyond the set limit** |
| Change an item's price outright | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | **Override the selling price** |
| Reverse a sale you should have discounted | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | **Refund or void a sale** |

**Role** governs the sidebar: a Cashier reaches **POS** and **Sales** only, so both controls are on their screen — the discount one simply refuses.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Someone never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted and *can* discount, regardless of their role bundle. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A discount refused at the till leaves a line in the audit log; one you were entitled to give does not.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- **Held** shows a count, and the parked cart is in the **Held carts** sheet with its label, item count and value.
- Resuming restores the cart exactly as it was, customer included.
- A discounted line shows a struck-through original and a **−\{n\}%** badge, and **Discount** appears as a negative in the totals.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Add items before holding the cart'">
    There is nothing to park. Add at least one line first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The held cart is not on this terminal">
    Held carts live on the device where they were parked. Go back to that till, or rebuild the cart.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I resumed a cart, pressed Clear All, then Hold — now there are two">
    That is the behaviour in the warning above. Open **Held carts** and **Discard** the one you no longer want.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Discount link does nothing for me">
    You do not have **Discount beyond the set limit**. Ask an owner or manager to grant it, or have them apply the discount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The total is lower than I expected">
    A line discount and a cart discount are both applied. Remove the line badge or clear the cart-level value and check the **Total** again before completing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A held card is marked 'unsynced'">
    You are offline, or were when it was parked. The cart is safe on the device and resumes normally; the badge is telling you its state has not left this terminal yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens to a held cart overnight?">
    It is still there in the morning, on the same terminal, because it is stored on the device rather than in the browser session. It survives a refresh, a browser restart and an outage. Discard the ones nobody came back for — a stale hold is the easiest thing in the shop to resume by accident.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does holding a cart reserve the stock?">
    No. The stock is not touched until the sale completes, so another till can sell the last box of something sitting in your held cart. Resuming a cart whose stock has gone behaves like any other short line.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many carts can I hold at once?">
    There is no number you need to watch. The **Held** button counts them, and each is labelled with the customer's name, or the first product and a count such as "Paracetamol \+2", or **Walk-in** — attach the customer if you want it findable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a colleague resume a cart I parked?">
    Only at the same terminal. Held carts belong to the device they were parked on — a cart held on the front till is not on the back one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a whole-cart discount the same as an override?">
    No. A discount takes an amount or a percentage off the total and is governed by **Discount beyond the set limit**. Changing what a single item sells for is **Override the selling price**, a separate permission.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
