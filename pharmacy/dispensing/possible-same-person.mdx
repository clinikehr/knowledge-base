Sometimes two records at your pharmacy or practice turn out to describe the same person —
registered twice, under slightly different details. ClinikEHR cannot know this for certain, so it
never decides for you: it shows you a **possible match**, with the reason it noticed, and you look
and decide. **This is a prompt, never a finding** — nothing about it changes a sale, a dispensing
warning, or anything a check evaluates, and nothing is ever merged automatically.

## What counts as a possible match

Two records are shown together only when their **date of birth is the same**, and at least one of
the following also matches:

- The **same phone number** (formatting and country code ignored — "0803 555 0101" and
  "+234 803 555 0101" are treated as the same number).
- The **same full name** (capitalisation, spacing, accents, and the order names are written in are
  all ignored).

A shared phone number **alone** is never enough — households share one number. A shared name
**alone** is never enough — common names repeat. And a missing date of birth on either record
means no comparison is made at all.

## The twins example — a known, expected case

Twins share a date of birth, a surname, and very often the household phone number. Because the
phone rule doesn't need the given name to agree, **twins will show up as a possible match** — the
same phone, the same date of birth, different first names. This is expected, not a mistake: look
at the two names, and mark them **Different people** in one click.

## What each button does

- **Different people** — records that you checked and they are not the same person. This pair
  will not be shown to you again.
- **Same person — to be combined by hand** — records that you believe they ARE the same person.
  **Nothing is merged automatically** — ClinikEHR has no merge feature. Combine the two records'
  information yourself, and make sure your team knows which one to keep using.
- Either button opens a small panel where you can add an optional reason.

## What this is not

This is not the same thing as the **External access** card you may see on the same profile — that
one is about outside organisations reaching this person's record through the API, a completely
different question. It is also not "Possible duplicates" — the review for a record created twice
by an import or an API connection. This feature is about two *separately registered* people who
might be the same human being.
