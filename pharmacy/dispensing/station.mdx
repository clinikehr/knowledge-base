The **Pharmacy Station** is where a script is written, screened and drawn from stock. It is one screen with three tabs, and it is the only prescription surface in the Pharmacy edition — there is no separate prescription list or prescription history in the sidebar.

## What you're looking at

The header reads **Pharmacy Station** and carries a date-range picker plus **New Prescription**. Everything below it sits under three tabs.

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Analytics** | Volume, value and stock pressure over the chosen date range |
| **Inventory** | The full stock screen — the same one the **Inventory** sidebar entry opens |
| **Reports** | **Pharmacy AI Reports** — written summaries you generate on demand |

**Analytics** opens on four tiles — **Total Medications**, **Active Prescriptions**, **Dispensed Prescriptions** and **Low Stock Alert** — followed by Prescription Trends, Top Medications, Inventory Status, Staff Performance and Predictive Analytics.

<Note>
  If the figures cannot be loaded you get **"Could not load pharmacy analytics — refresh to try again."** rather than a screen of zeros. A zero on this screen always means zero.
</Note>

The **Inventory** tab is not a summary. It is the stock screen itself — one place, two doors — so anything in [Stock control](/pharmacy/inventory/index) can be done without leaving the station. Its own tab strip runs **Catalog**, **Stock**, **Ledger**, **Counts**, **Fill Rx**, **Purchasing**, **Vouchers**, **Recalls**, **Locations**, **Alerts** and **Reconciliation**; **Fill Rx** is the one this page cares about, and **Alerts** carries a count badge when anything is open.

On **Reports**, pick an entry from the **Report Types** list and select **Generate Report**. An existing report offers **Regenerate** and **Export**.

**A brand-new pharmacy** sees all four **Analytics** tiles at zero, empty charts, and a **Fill Rx** tab with nothing to fill. That is the correct starting state — nothing is broken. Add a stock item first, then write your first script.

## Write a prescription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **New Prescription** in the station header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who it is for">
    The **Select Patient** card searches your customer records — "Search by name, ID, phone, or email...". The card keeps the product's wording; the records behind it are the ones you manage under [Customers](/pharmacy/customers/index).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date">
    **Prescription Date** defaults to today. Change it only if you are recording a script written earlier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each medication">
    Use the medication search — "Search medications…" — to add a line. Every line asks for **Mode**, **Quantity**, **Dosage**, **Frequency** and **Duration**, each marked required with an asterisk. **Instructions (Optional)** is what the customer reads on the label, so fill it for anything that is not obvious.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    **Additional Notes** covers anything that belongs to the script rather than a line. The **Prescription Summary** and **Estimated Total** update as you go, so the price is settled before the customer is told it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    A toast confirms **"Prescription created successfully"** and the **Prescription Created** dialog appears with a **Reference:** you can quote. Printing from that dialog goes straight to the roll on the desktop app — **"Prescription sent to printer"** — and to the browser print dialog otherwise. See [Thermal printers](/pharmacy/hardware/thermal-printer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dispense it against stock

Writing a script does not move stock. Drawing it down is a separate, deliberate act on the **Fill Rx** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fill Rx">
    Go to the **Inventory** tab, then the **Fill Rx** tab, and open the row for the script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the lot and where it comes from">
    The **Prescription dispense** sheet — "Draw from stock against this script — FEFO, patient-linked, audited." — asks for **Stock item**, **Location** and **Quantity**. The oldest-expiring lot is used first; see [Batches, expiry and FEFO](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a witness for a controlled item">
    A **Witness** field appears when the item is flagged as controlled. Pick the colleague who watched the count. The dispense will not go through without one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Interaction alerts appear against the script exactly as they do at the till — see [Drug interaction screening](/pharmacy/dispensing/interaction-screening).

## Who can do this

Two separate gates. **Role** decides whether **Pharmacy Station** opens at all: a **Cashier** reaches only **POS** and **Sales**, so the station is not in their sidebar. A **Pharmacy Technician** gets the station, **Inventory** and **Customers**, but not the money screens. Owners, managers and pharmacists are unrestricted by role.

**Permission** decides what you may do once it is open.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the station and read **Analytics** | Any role except **Cashier** | Role |
| Write a prescription | Any role that reaches the station | Role |
| Dispense against stock on **Fill Rx** | Anyone whose role reaches the station | Role — unless a controlled countersignature is recorded |
| Witness a controlled-drug dispense | Dispensing Supervisor, and anyone named as Pharmacist-in-Charge | **Witness a controlled-drug dispense** |
| See cost and margin on the stock screens | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **View cost prices and margin** |
| Receive a delivery into stock | Anyone whose role reaches **Inventory** | Role — the toggle exists but nothing reads it yet |

A **Pharmacy Manager** deliberately does **not** get the controlled-drug witness by default — that countersignature is a pharmacist's act, not a manager's, and the role bundle leaves it off on purpose. It is also not the only one they lack: **View per-cashier takings** is a default for no pharmacy role or tier and must be granted per person.

**Three of the stock toggles are not wired to anything yet.** **Receive deliveries into stock**, **Adjust stock counts** and **Transfer stock between branches** are offered on a person's permissions sheet, but nothing in the product checks them today — reaching **Inventory** is the gate on all three. The stock permissions that *are* enforced on the server are **Manage suppliers and purchase orders** and, whenever a countersignature is actually recorded, **Witness a controlled-drug dispense**.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted — no checks apply to them at all. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"** before you touch a switch.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A missing sidebar entry is a convenience, not a security boundary — the real check runs when the action is submitted.

Where a figure is gated it never shows a zero. Cost and margin read **Hidden — needs authorisation**, or the column disappears entirely. See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The **Prescription Created** dialog showed a **Reference:**.
- After dispensing, the lot's on-hand quantity has dropped on the **Stock** tab and a movement appears on the **Ledger** tab.
- **Dispensed Prescriptions** on **Analytics** counts the script once the date range covers it.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Save button will not enable">
    Every line needs **Mode**, **Quantity**, **Dosage**, **Frequency** and **Duration** — they carry an asterisk. **Instructions** and **Additional Notes** never block saving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The medication search finds nothing">
    The search reads your catalogue, not a drug reference. If the product is not in **Inventory** yet, add it first — see [Stock control](/pharmacy/inventory/index).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot pick a lot on the dispense sheet">
    There is no stock on hand for that item at that location. Receive a delivery first, or change **Location**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The witness list is empty">
    Only other staff accounts can witness. Invite the colleague first — see [Invite your team](/pharmacy/get-started/invite-team).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot see the Pharmacy Station in the sidebar">
    You are on the **Cashier** role, which reaches **POS** and **Sales** and nothing else. Ask an owner or manager to change your role — see [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Could not load pharmacy analytics — refresh to try again.'">
    The figures could not be fetched — usually a dropped connection. Refresh. Nothing on the screen is wrong; it is simply absent rather than guessed at.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cost or margin reads 'Hidden — needs authorisation'">
    You do not hold **View cost prices and margin**. The rest of the stock screen still works. An owner or manager grants it in your permissions sheet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does writing a prescription move stock?">
    No. The script is paperwork; **Fill Rx** is what draws the stock down. That separation is deliberate — a script can be written today and collected on Friday, and the shelf should only change when the box actually leaves it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell a prescription item through the till instead?">
    For most items, yes. A product flagged as needing a witnessed dispense is refused at POS — "… requires a witnessed dispense — use the inventory Fill Rx flow instead of POS." — because the till has nowhere to capture a countersignature.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which lot does a dispense come out of?">
    The one that expires soonest, automatically. You choose the **Stock item** and the **Location**; the lot is picked for you so short-dated stock moves first. See [Batches, expiry and FEFO](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reprint a prescription later?">
    Yes — reopen the script and print it again. The **Prescription Created** dialog is a convenience at the moment of saving, not the only chance to print.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the customer picker labelled 'Select Patient'?">
    That card is shared with the other ClinikEHR editions and keeps their wording. The records behind it are the ones you manage under [Customers](/pharmacy/customers/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
