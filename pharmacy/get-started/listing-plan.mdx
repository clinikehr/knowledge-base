The **Listing** plan is for a pharmacy that already sells through another till and only wants to be found by medicine-finder apps. It costs **$9.50 a month ($95 a year)**, or **₦15,000 a month (₦150,000 a year)**, and it is a pharmacy plan like the others: you can move up to a full plan whenever you want.

<Path steps={['Your avatar', 'Profile and Settings', 'Billing']} />

## What you get

- Your public pharmacy profile and opening hours.
- **Get listed** with medicine-finder partners, and the coverage view that shows how much of your catalogue can be listed.
- Catalogue import and medicine matching, on the **Inventory** screen's **Catalog** tab.
- Settings, including the listing profile and your audit log.
- Sending your stock report from another system, with a key that has the **Stock report** permission (see [Stock source](/pharmacy/settings/stock-source)).
- Three staff accounts and one branch.

## What it does not include

The Listing plan does not include the till, sales history, branch transfers, requests, customers, forms, billing, expenses, the executive dashboard, analytics, stock control, or the online store. Your stock numbers come from the stock report you send, not from our till. Those entries are not in the menu, and typing their address takes you back to your catalogue. If you try to sell, the till says "Your plan covers listing only."

## Move to a full plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan chooser">
    Go to **Billing** as shown above and choose **View plans**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a full plan">
    Choose **Starter** or above. The change is an upgrade on your existing subscription, so nothing is re-entered, and the till and the rest of the menu appear straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your Listing plan lapses, the plan's limits stay in place until you choose a full plan.
