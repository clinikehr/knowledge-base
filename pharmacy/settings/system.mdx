## Require two-factor sign-in

The **Security** tab — "Clinic-wide security rules" — carries one switch that applies to everybody: **Require Two-Factor Authentication**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    **Settings** → **Data & Security** → **Security**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on Require Two-Factor Authentication">
    The description is explicit: "Require all staff members to set up two-factor authentication for their accounts. When enabled, every staff member is prompted to set up an authenticator app on their next sign-in before they can access any clinic data."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Save Changes">
    Only an owner sees this button. The rule takes effect on each person's next sign-in, not immediately — nobody is thrown out mid-sale.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell your team before they arrive">
    A cashier who turns up for an early shift and meets an enrolment screen with no phone in their pocket cannot open the till. Give people a day's notice and remind them to bring the phone that holds their authenticator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Set it up on your own account first so you know what your team is about to see. Full detail, including recovery codes and what to do when someone loses their phone, is in [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa).

Passkeys are a separate sign-in method that belongs to a person rather than a pharmacy, and they are managed from that person's own account security page — see [Account security](/platform/account/security). A passkey is not a substitute for the second factor this switch requires.

Security alerts reach you when something notable happens to an account: emergency access being granted or revoked, a recovery code being used, or a two-factor reset. They are not something you configure here — they arrive because they matter.

## Export your data

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Data & Security', 'Data Export']} />

**Data Export** — "Export clinic data as CSV or ZIP. Large exports run in the background — you can navigate away and check back."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Data Export tab">
    The datasets available to a pharmacy are listed and grouped.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to export">
    Select the datasets you want. Exporting everything at once is slower and rarely what you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the export">
    A single dataset comes back as CSV; a multi-dataset export is packaged as a **ZIP**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave it running">
    A large export runs in the background. Carry on serving customers and check back — it does not need the tab left open.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the file">
    The finished export appears in the list with a download link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Take an export before anything irreversible. The obvious case is the inventory reset described in [Inventory rules and alerts](/pharmacy/settings/inventory) — that reset has no export step of its own, so the copy has to be yours. More detail on formats and what each dataset contains is in [Export your data](/platform/security/data-export).

An export file is a plain, unprotected copy of your records. Once it leaves the product, keeping it safe is on you: store it somewhere access-controlled, and delete it when the job it was pulled for is done.

## Check it worked

- Sign out and back in. With the requirement on, you are prompted to enrol before you reach anything.
- Ask one member of staff to sign in on their own device and confirm they meet the same prompt.
- Open your export file. It contains the datasets you ticked, and a multi-dataset export opens as a ZIP holding one file per dataset.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see Save Changes on the Security tab">
    The two-factor requirement is owner-only. Managers and accountants can read the setting but not change it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A cashier is locked out at the enrolment screen">
    They need the phone with their authenticator app. Until they enrol they cannot reach the till, which is the point of the rule — the fix is the phone, not switching the requirement off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone lost the phone holding their authenticator">
    Recovery is covered in [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa). A reset is itself a security event and generates an alert.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My export has not finished">
    Large exports run in the background and may take a while. You do not need to stay on the tab; come back and check the list. If it is still not there after a long wait, try fewer datasets in one go.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A dataset a colleague mentioned is not in my list">
    The list is filtered to what your workspace holds. Datasets belonging to appointments, wards or telehealth never appear for a pharmacy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I opened a ZIP and expected one spreadsheet">
    A multi-dataset export packages one CSV per dataset inside the ZIP. Select a single dataset if you want a single file.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
