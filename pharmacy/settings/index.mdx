Everything you configure once and then stop thinking about lives here: your pharmacy's details, your team, how money moves, your loyalty programme, and the stock rules the till obeys. This page is the map — one row per section, and a link to the guide that covers it.

## Getting in, and who can

**Settings** sits at the bottom of the sidebar, in the footer. Opening it needs one of three roles: **owner**, **manager**, or **accountant**. Anyone else gets a single line instead of the screen — "You don't have permission to access the settings module. Please contact your clinic administrator." A cashier cannot open Settings at all, which is deliberate: nobody working a till should be able to change your tax rate mid-shift.

Getting in is not the same as being able to change everything. A few controls are **owner-only** even for a manager — **Currency**, **Tax Rate (%)** and the free-clinic switch. The control still renders; it is simply disabled. If a field will not accept a change and no error appears, that is why.

On a desktop you get a left sidebar headed **Pharmacy Settings**, with the strapline "Configure your professional workspace". On a phone that sidebar collapses into a single dropdown at the top of the screen. The tabs are identical either way.

## A tab you were sent to is not there

Settings links carry a `?tab=` on the end, and a tab this edition does not include **silently falls back to General** rather than showing an error. A link that lands you on **General Settings** when you expected something else is almost never broken — it is telling you that tab does not exist for a pharmacy.

## The three sections

A pharmacy has far fewer settings than a clinic does, and that is the point: three sections, ten tabs, nothing about appointments or wards.

| Section | Tabs | Covered in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PHARMACY** | General Settings, Staff & Teams, Inventory, Notifications | [Your pharmacy profile and notifications](/pharmacy/settings/pharmacy-profile), [Staff and permissions](/pharmacy/settings/team) and [Inventory rules and alerts](/pharmacy/settings/inventory) |
| **BILLING** | Billing & Plans, Online Payments, Payment Details, Loyalty Program | [Billing, plans and taking payment](/pharmacy/settings/money) and [Loyalty programme and customer alerts](/pharmacy/settings/loyalty) |
| **SYSTEM** | Security, Data Export | [Security and data export](/pharmacy/settings/system) |

Two notes on that table. **Inventory** ("Alerts, dispensing rules & reset") only appears when the Inventory care area is switched on — for a retail pharmacy it always is. And there is **no AUTOMATION & AI section** for a pharmacy at all: both of the tabs that would fill it belong to the clinical editions, so the sidebar drops the heading rather than showing an empty band.
