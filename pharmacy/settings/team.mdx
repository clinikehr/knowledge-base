## Seats are included — this is the thing people get wrong

On the Pharmacy edition, **seats are included at no extra cost** up to your plan's cap. Nothing is ever billed per seat, so adding a Saturday cashier does not change your invoice.

Two consequences follow:

- **Every account counts towards the cap.** There are no free roles — an accountant and a stock controller each take a seat exactly like a cashier does.
- **The invitation that would exceed the cap is refused**, and you are offered the next plan up rather than being charged for the overflow.

This is the opposite of the clinical edition, where every seat is billed — so a colleague who tells you an extra login costs money is right about their workspace and wrong about yours. See [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats).

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Staff & Teams">
    **Settings** → **Team** → **Staff & Teams**. The staff list and any pending invitations are on this tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Add team member">
    The five-step wizard opens: **1 Profile → 2 Specialty → 3 Supervisor → 4 Permissions → 5 Review**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Profile">
    Name and email address. The invitation goes to this address, so check it — a typo means a person who never arrives and a seat that looks used.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Specialty and Supervisor">
    Pick the role bundle that matches the job, and name who they report to.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Permissions">
    Set the access tier and tick the individual permissions. What you save here is enforced immediately — read the next section first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and send">
    Check the summary and send. The person sits under pending invitations until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The four pharmacy role bundles

| Bundle | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Pharmacy Manager** | Runs the branch — customer records, pricing, reports and staff accounts |
| **Cashier / Till Operator** | Serves at the till — rings up sales, takes payment, issues receipts |
| **Inventory & Purchasing** | Owns stock — deliveries, counts, suppliers and purchase orders |
| **Dispensing Supervisor** | Oversees dispensing — checks work, authorises refunds and voids, reviews takings |

Alongside the bundle you set an **access tier**, which decides how much of the business a person can see:

- **Own till** — only the sales they rang up themselves
- **Whole branch** — every sale, customer and stock movement at this branch
- **All branches** — everything, across the whole business

## What you can grant, group by group

The permission sheet groups the individual permissions the way the job does:

- **Till** — Dispense and sell at the till · Override the selling price · Discount beyond the set limit · Refund or void a sale · Open the cash drawer · Cash up and close the day
- **Stock** — Receive deliveries into stock · Adjust stock counts · Transfer stock between branches · Manage suppliers and purchase orders
- **Controlled drugs** — Witness a controlled-drug dispense
- **Money** — View cost prices and margin · View the net position
- **Oversight** — View per-cashier takings

A default **Cashier** gets exactly two: **Dispense and sell at the till** and **Open the cash drawer**. Everything else is off until you turn it on — which is what stops a busy counter discounting its way through your margin.

## Enforcement is opt-in, per person

The permission sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**, and it means it literally. Until you open the sheet for a particular person and save it, that person is **unrestricted** — the tick boxes describe an intention nobody is checking.

The first save is the moment the rules become real, and it is per person: saving your Saturday cashier's sheet changes nothing for anybody else. If someone can still do what you thought you had blocked, the usual cause is a sheet that was never saved. Owners and managers are not restricted by these permissions. For the full model, see [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The invited person appears in the staff list, or under pending invitations until they accept.
- Reopen their permission sheet. The boxes hold what you saved.
- Ask them to sign in and try one thing you did **not** grant — a price override, say. It is refused at the till, not merely hidden.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The invitation was refused and I was offered a bigger plan">
    You are at your plan's seat cap. Remove someone who has left, or move up a plan — see [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats) and [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A cashier can still discount beyond the limit">
    Their sheet has never been saved, so nothing is being enforced for them. Open **Staff & Teams**, open their permissions, and save — even with no boxes changed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The invitation never arrived">
    Check the address on the pending invitation and ask them to look in spam. Invitation email is transactional and ignores your notification switches.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone sees sales that are not theirs">
    Their access tier is **Whole branch** or **All branches**. Set it to **Own till** and save.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find Add team member">
    You are signed in as an accountant. Managing staff needs the owner or a manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
