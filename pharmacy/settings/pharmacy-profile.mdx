Two tabs, both under **PHARMACY**. **General Settings** holds the details a customer sees — your name, address, phone number and email — plus the timezone that decides which business day a sale lands on. **Notifications** decides which emails and in-app alerts reach you.

<Note>
  **General Settings** is a screen shared with the clinical editions, so a few of its field labels still say **Clinic** — **Clinic Name**, **Clinic Logo** and so on. They are your pharmacy's name and your pharmacy's logo. Nothing else on the screen behaves differently.

## Fill in your pharmacy details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General Settings">
    In the sidebar footer select **Settings**, then **Profile** → **General Settings**. On a phone, choose **General Settings** from the dropdown at the top.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the four that matter">
    **Clinic Name**, address, phone number and email. These four are the ones that reach your customers — they print on receipts and appear on invoices — and filling all four is what ticks the profile step of your setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the optional detail">
    Website, description and **Clinic Logo** are optional. The logo appears on invoice PDFs, so upload it if you send invoices to account customers or institutional buyers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your Timezone">
    Under regional preferences, choose the timezone your counter actually operates in. This is not cosmetic — see below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Select the save button at the foot of the card. A confirmation toast appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why the timezone matters more than it looks

Your timezone sets the **business-day boundary** that sales are bucketed into. A sale rung at 11 pm belongs to today or tomorrow depending on that one setting, so a wrong timezone quietly puts a night shift's takings on the wrong day and your daily totals never reconcile against the cash drawer.

Set it before your first busy day. Changing it later does not move sales that were already recorded. See [Sales and reporting](/pharmacy/money/sales) for how the day is totalled.

## Choose which notifications you get

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Pharmacy notifications', 'Notifications']} />

The **Notifications** tab — "Choose which in-app and email notifications your clinic receives" — has one master switch and a list of categories beneath it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the master switch">
    **Email notifications** is described as "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." Turning it off pauses the lot without you having to work through the categories one at a time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn categories on or off">
    Each category has its own switch and governs both the email and the in-app alert for that kind of event.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave System alone">
    **System** cannot be switched off: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic." Its switch is fixed on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Your choices apply from the next event onwards. Anything already queued still arrives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The category list adapts to your edition, so you only see categories that can actually happen in a pharmacy. If a colleague on a clinical edition describes a category you cannot find, that is why.

**Transactional email is never affected by any of these switches.** Security alerts, password resets and a receipt a customer asked you to email still go out with every category turned off. You cannot mute those, and you should not want to.

## Check it worked

- Ring up a test sale and print or preview the receipt. Your pharmacy name, address and phone number appear in the header.
- Reopen **General Settings**. The fields hold what you typed, and the timezone shows your own.
- Reopen **Notifications**. Every switch is where you left it, and **System** is still on.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The fields say Clinic Name and Clinic Logo">
    Expected. **General Settings** is shared across editions and keeps those labels. Treat them as your pharmacy's name and logo — the values print on your receipts exactly as you enter them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My receipt header is blank or missing the address">
    The receipt prints what the profile holds. Fill in address, phone and email in **General Settings**, save, then print a fresh receipt — an already-printed one does not change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Yesterday's sales are showing under today">
    The timezone is wrong. Correct it in **General Settings**. Sales recorded before the change keep the day they were filed under, so compare against the till only from the correction onwards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I turned everything off and still get email">
    Check what arrived. Security alerts, password resets and receipts are transactional and ignore these switches by design. If it is genuinely a category alert, confirm the master **Email notifications** switch is off and that you saved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A notification category a colleague mentions is not in my list">
    The list is filtered to your edition. Categories that only apply to appointments, wards or insurance never appear for a pharmacy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The fields are greyed out">
    You are signed in as an accountant, or as a manager on an owner-only control. Ask the owner to make the change — see [Settings](/pharmacy/settings/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
