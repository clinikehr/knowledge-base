**Loyalty Program** — "Reward repeat customers with automatic rebates" — is the one settings tab that exists **only** for the Pharmacy edition. Set a spend threshold, and a customer who reaches it earns a reward that the till offers on their next sale without anyone having to remember it.

## Set up the programme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the programme on">
    **Program active** — "Turn the loyalty program on or off." Nothing accrues until this is on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it">
    **Program name** is what your staff see. "Loyalty rewards" is fine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the Spend threshold">
    The amount a customer must spend to earn one reward. It is in your own currency — see [Currency](/platform/settings/currency).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the Reward type and value">
    **Reward type** is either **Percent off** or **Fixed amount off**. **Reward value** is the number that goes with it — 5 for five percent, or a cash amount for a fixed rebate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the Accrual window">
    How far back spend counts towards the threshold. A rolling window rewards steady custom; a lifetime window rewards a customer once and keeps rewarding them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cap it if you want to">
    **Max rewards per customer** is optional. Leave it empty for unlimited.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the programme">
    It applies to sales recorded from now on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a cashier sees

The programme surfaces at the till, so nobody has to look anything up.

- When a reward is due, a banner reads **"\{n\}% loyalty reward available"** with an **Apply** button.
- Once applied, it becomes **"Loyalty reward applied to this sale"**.
- When the customer is part-way there, it shows **"\{amount\} more to the next reward"** — which is the line that turns a browsing customer into a slightly larger basket.

A walk-in with no customer record cannot accrue anything, because there is nobody to credit. If you want loyalty to work, the cashier has to attach the customer before taking payment — see [Ring up a sale](/pharmacy/pos/index).

With no programme running, a customer's profile reads "No loyalty programme running — Set one up in Settings → Loyalty to reward repeat customers." See [Customers](/pharmacy/customers/index).

## Customer alerts live on the same tab

Beneath the programme sits **Customer alerts & reminders** — "A daily sweep. Everything here is off until you turn it on, and customers can opt out of their own reminders from their profile."

These two cards share a tab deliberately: both are about reaching a customer **between visits**, and one of the four is the loyalty email itself. Setting up a reward scheme and then never telling anyone they have earned something is the commonest way to waste it.

| Control | Who it emails |
| --- | --- |
| **Remind customers after (days)** | The customer, once their oldest unpaid balance is older than this |
| **Alert me above (amount)** | **You**, when one person owes more than this |
| **Tell customers when a reward is ready** | The customer, at most once a month, and only while a programme is running |
| **Remind me about overdue prospect follow-ups** | You, with a list of prospects whose follow-up date has passed |

Both numeric fields are off when left empty, and both switches start off. Save with **Save alert settings**.

## Check it worked

- Reopen the tab. **Program active** is on and your threshold, type and value are as you set them.
- Record a sale against a real customer for at least the threshold amount, then start a second sale for the same customer. The reward banner appears.
- Select **Apply**. The banner changes to "Loyalty reward applied to this sale" and the total drops by the reward.
- Open that customer's profile. The programme is named there instead of the "No loyalty programme running" line.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Loyalty Program tab is not in my sidebar">
    It needs the Professional plan or above. On a lower plan the card explains "Available on Professional and above" — see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No reward appears even though the customer has spent enough">
    Check three things: the sales were attached to that customer rather than rung as walk-ins, the spend falls inside your **Accrual window**, and the customer has not hit **Max rewards per customer**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The reward is far bigger than I meant">
    **Reward value** is read against **Reward type**. A value of 20 means twenty percent under **Percent off**, and twenty in cash under **Fixed amount off**. Correct the type, save, and check the till banner again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customers are not being told about their rewards">
    Turn on **Tell customers when a reward is ready**. It only sends while a programme is active, and at most once a month per customer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A customer asked to stop receiving reminders">
    They can opt out from their own profile, and their choice overrides these switches. You do not need to turn the whole programme off for one person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Changing the threshold reset someone's progress">
    Accrual is measured against the threshold currently saved and the window currently chosen. Raising the threshold mid-window moves the finish line for everyone — change it between promotions rather than during one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
