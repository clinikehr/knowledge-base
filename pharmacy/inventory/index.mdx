**Inventory** is the ledger everything else draws from: the till, the dispensing form, purchase orders and branch transfers all move stock through this one screen. Its header reads **Inventory** — "Stock, lots, and expiry across your rooms and devices."

The same screen opens from **Pharmacy Station** › **Inventory**. One place, two doors.

## What you're looking at

The toolbar carries **Receive**, **Dispense**, **Transfer**, **Reports**, **Import**, **Opening stock**, **Export** and **Add item**.

| Tab | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | Every product you stock, whether or not you hold any |
| **Stock** | On-hand quantity per lot, per location |
| **Ledger** | Every movement in and out, with who and when |
| **Counts** | Cycle counts and physical counts — [Run a stock count](/pharmacy/inventory/stock-counts) |
| **Fill Rx** | Scripts waiting to be drawn from stock — [The pharmacy station](/pharmacy/dispensing/station) |
| **Validation (\{n\})** | Reagent lots awaiting validation — **shown only where you hold reagent stock** |
| **Purchasing** | Purchase orders and vendors — [Purchase orders and suppliers](/pharmacy/inventory/purchase-orders) |
| **Vouchers** | Goods-received vouchers behind each delivery |
| **Recalls** | Quarantining affected lots |
| **Locations** | Your rooms, shelves and fridges |
| **Alerts (\{n\})** | Expiry, low stock, temperature and count alerts, counted in the tab itself — [Batches, expiry and FEFO](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry) |
| **Reconciliation** | Clearing sales made beyond recorded stock |

**Validation** is conditional, and so is a **Vaccines** tab where you hold vaccine stock. Most pharmacies see neither, and their absence is not a fault. **Alerts** carries its count only when something is actually open — a bare **Alerts** means nothing is. When alerts are open, a line above the tabs says so: "\{n\} open inventory alerts — see the Alerts tab."

A brand-new pharmacy lands on **Catalog** with a single line — **"No items yet. Add your first product (e.g. Botox 100U vial)."** — and everything else empty behind it. **Stock** reads "No stock on hand. Receive a lot to get started." until your first delivery, which is the honest order of work: catalogue first, stock second.

<Note>
  Two labels on this screen still say "clinic" where they mean your pharmacy — the count scope **Whole clinic** is the one you meet most. They are bolded in these pages exactly as the product renders them.
</Note>

## Add an item

A catalog entry is the product, not the stock. As the sheet puts it: "A catalog entry — e.g. 'Botox 100U vial'. Stock is tracked per lot."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sheet">
    Select **Add item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look the product up, or name it yourself">
    **Look up drug (NDC or name)** fills the details for you. **Barcode / UPC (optional)** takes a scan through **Scan pack**, or **Generate…** if the pack carries no barcode you can use. Otherwise enter the **Name** by hand. **Product image (optional)** is what the till shows on the tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what kind of thing it is">
    **Type**, **Unit** and **Category** decide how it is counted and grouped. **NDC** and **DEA schedule** — **None (not controlled)** through **CII** to **CV** — decide whether it is treated as controlled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the drug details">
    **Generic name**, **Brand name**, **Strength**, **Dosage form**, **Manufacturer** and **NAFDAC/FDA No** are what a customer or an inspector asks for. **Storage instructions** is marked "Shown at the till", so write it for the person selling, not for a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price it">
    **Unit cost** is what you paid; **Sale price** is what you charge, and **Markup %** derives one from the other. **Low-stock threshold** — "Alerts at/below this on-hand." — and **Reorder qty** are what make the reorder suggestions work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add opening stock, if you have some in the room now">
    **Opening stock (optional)** with **Into location** and **SKU** saves you a separate receive for a product already on the shelf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the switches">
    **Refrigerated**, **Track by lot / expiry** and **Witness required (controlled)**. Leave **Track by lot / expiry** on for anything with an expiry date — it is what makes oldest-first picking possible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receive a delivery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Receive">
    Select **Receive** in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the pack, if it carries a GS1 code">
    **Scan package (GS1)** — "Scan or paste GS1 DataMatrix…" — fills in the item, the lot number and the expiry from the pack itself. This is faster and it does not mistype an expiry date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or enter the delivery by hand">
    **Item**, **Location**, **Quantity**, **Funding source**, **Lot number** and **Expiry**. Every delivery of the same product with a different expiry is a different lot — do not merge them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Receiving against a purchase order is done per line from the **Purchasing** tab instead; see [Purchase orders and suppliers](/pharmacy/inventory/purchase-orders).

## Adjust or waste stock

**Dispense** in the toolbar is how stock leaves for any reason that is not a sale or a script: breakage, spillage, expiry.

It asks for a **Reason** — "Broken vial, spill, expired…" — and, on a controlled item, a **Witness (controlled item)**. Both land in the **Ledger** tab against your name.

<Note>
  The toolbar's **Transfer** moves stock between rooms **inside this branch**. Moving stock to another pharmacy branch is a different screen with the same word on it — see [Transfer stock between branches](/pharmacy/inventory/transfers).
</Note>

## Locations

The **Locations** tab is your physical geography: **Add location** creates a room, shelf or fridge; **Par levels** sets what should be held there; **Log temp** and **Sensors** cover cold-chain records. Locations carry badges for controlled, hazardous-rated and temperature zone.

A row action, **Print barcode label**, prints shelf labels — set **Copies** before printing.

## Check it worked

Each empty state tells you exactly what is still missing:

- **"No items yet. Add your first product (e.g. Botox 100U vial)."** — the catalogue is empty.
- **"No stock on hand. Receive a lot to get started."** — you have products but nothing physically held.
- **"No counts yet. Start a cycle count to reconcile physical stock against the system."**
- **"No open alerts. You're all stocked up."**

After adding an item it appears on **Catalog**; after receiving, it appears on **Stock** with its lot and expiry, and the movement appears on **Ledger**.

## Who can do this

**Role** decides whether **Inventory** opens at all. A **Cashier / Till Operator** reaches only **POS** and **Sales** and never sees this screen. **Permission** decides what you may do once it is open, and the switches live in groups named **Till**, **Stock**, **Controlled drugs**, **Money** and **Oversight** on each person's permissions sheet.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Inventory** and read the catalogue | Everyone except a cashier | Role |
| Receive a delivery | Anyone whose role reaches **Inventory** | Role — the toggle exists but nothing reads it yet |
| Adjust or waste stock with **Dispense** | Anyone whose role reaches **Inventory** | Role — the toggle exists but nothing reads it yet |
| Move stock to another branch | Anyone whose role reaches **Transfers** | Role — the toggle exists but nothing reads it yet |
| Raise a PO or edit a supplier | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **Manage suppliers and purchase orders** |
| Countersign a controlled dispense | Dispensing Supervisor only | **Witness a controlled-drug dispense** |
| See the valuation and margin columns | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **View cost prices and margin** |
| Reset the inventory from the **Danger Zone** | The owner of record, nobody else | Ownership — not a grantable permission |

**Three of the stock toggles are not wired to anything yet.** **Receive deliveries into stock**, **Adjust stock counts** and **Transfer stock between branches** are offered on a person's permissions sheet, but nothing in the product checks them today — reaching **Inventory** is the gate on all three. The stock permissions that *are* enforced on the server are **Manage suppliers and purchase orders** and, whenever a countersignature is actually recorded, **Witness a controlled-drug dispense**.

On top of the role bundle, each person carries an access tier — **Own till**, **Whole branch** or **All branches** — which is what decides how much of the business their figures cover.

Three gates change what the screen *shows*, not just what it does:

- **View cost prices and margin** turns the valuation column into **"Hidden — needs authorisation from your clinic owner."** It is never rendered as a zero, because a zero reads as an empty shelf rather than as a permission boundary.
- **Manage suppliers and purchase orders** also redacts negotiated unit costs and supplier account numbers, so somebody who can count your stock does not automatically learn what you pay for it.
- The **Danger Zone** under **Settings** › **Inventory** is hidden from the page entirely for managers and accountants. It is not greyed out; there is nothing there to select.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"** before that changes.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** Hiding **Inventory** from somebody's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

The caps belong to the **free** plan, not to Starter: every paid pharmacy plan — Starter included — is uncapped while the subscription is active or trialing. A pharmacy with no active subscription (typically after the 7-day trial lapses) gets a **Free plan** strip showing usage against the limit for each countable kind — 20 items, 5 locations, 2 vendors and 5 purchase orders — with **Upgrade for unlimited**. At the limit, creation is refused rather than silently dropped. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).

## Inventory settings

**Settings** › **Inventory** holds the defaults for new items, **Selling without stock** (whether the till may sell past recorded stock), **Receiving quality** (including **Require expiry date on new lots**), and a **Danger Zone**.

<Warning>
  The **Danger Zone** reset deletes your entire inventory — catalogue, lots, stock and history. It asks you to type `RESET` because there is no undo and no export that puts it back. Take an [export](/pharmacy/inventory/import-export) first if you are resetting a workspace you might want to look at again.
</Warning>

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The item saved but the till cannot find it">
    A catalogue entry with no stock is not sellable unless **Selling without stock** is on. Receive a lot, or add **Opening stock** on the item.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Receive will not accept the delivery without an expiry">
    **Require expiry date on new lots** is on under **Settings** › **Inventory** › **Receiving quality**. That is deliberate — see [Batches, expiry and FEFO](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Your Free plan includes up to {n} items. Upgrade to a paid plan for unlimited inventory.'">
    Your pharmacy has no active paid subscription, so it is on the free tier's 20-item cap and **Add item** was refused rather than silently dropped. The **Free plan** strip shows your usage per kind — items, locations, vendors and purchase orders — and **Upgrade for unlimited** removes the caps. Any paid pharmacy plan, Starter upward, is uncapped.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Hidden — needs authorisation from your clinic owner.'">
    You do not hold **View cost prices and margin**, so the valuation is withheld rather than shown as zero. An owner or manager grants it on your permissions sheet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Needs authorisation — buying on the clinic's account is purchasing authority.'">
    **New PO** and **Draft PO from these** are disabled without **Manage suppliers and purchase orders**. The tooltip names the switch to ask for. **Vendors** stays open to everyone and simply opens read-only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I moved stock and it went to the wrong place">
    Check whether you used the toolbar's **Transfer** (room to room, this branch) or the **Transfers** screen (branch to branch). They are different moves with the same name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'This inventory cannot be reset'">
    Clinical administration records reference these lots, so the reset is blocked to protect that traceability. Adjust quantities with a [stock count](/pharmacy/inventory/stock-counts) instead — resetting is only for an inventory with no history behind it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a catalogue item and stock?">
    A catalogue item is the product — "Botox 100U vial". Stock is how many you physically hold, tracked per lot with its own expiry and location. Adding an item creates no stock at all; the quantity arrives through **Receive** or **Opening stock**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do some labels say 'clinic' on a pharmacy screen?">
    A handful of strings are shared with the other editions and have not been relabelled — the count scope **Whole clinic** is the one you meet most. It means your whole pharmacy. We quote them exactly as the product renders them so you can find them on your screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I see the Validation or Vaccines tab?">
    Both are conditional. **Validation** appears only where you hold reagent stock and **Vaccines** only where you hold vaccine stock. A pharmacy that stocks neither is not missing anything.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete a product I no longer stock?">
    Yes, from the **Catalog** tab — but a product with movements behind it is better left in place and simply not reordered, because deleting it takes the name off every historic line that referred to it. Freeing a slot against a plan limit is the one good reason to delete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my margin column blank?">
    Two different reasons look identical. Either you do not hold **View cost prices and margin** — in which case the column reads **"Hidden — needs authorisation from your clinic owner."** — or the stock was received with no **Unit cost**, in which case there is genuinely no margin to compute. Record costs at receiving so the second reason stops happening.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does resetting the inventory delete my sales history?">
    No. The **Danger Zone** removes the catalogue, lots, stock levels, ledger, purchase orders, vendors and locations. Your medication list, store history and clinical records are kept.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
