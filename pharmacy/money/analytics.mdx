**Analytics** is the report workbench: pick a report, pick a period, read the tiles and charts, export the table. If you want a glance at today rather than a report over a period, that is the [executive dashboard](/pharmacy/money/executive) instead.

## What you're looking at

**Analytics** opens on an index of report cards, grouped **Clinical**, **Operations** and **Money**. The list is generated from what your pharmacy can actually populate, so nothing clinical appears — a pharmacy has no wards or consultations to report on, and those reports do not exist for you rather than sitting empty.

| Report | What it covers | Scope tabs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | Invoiced and paid money | **Invoices** · **Payments** |
| **POS Sales** | Till takings and payment mix, net of refunds | **Takings** · **Transactions** · **By cashier** |
| **Customers** | Who you serve, and how often | — |
| **Dispensing** | Dispensing volume and stock-outs | — |
| **Staff** | Headcount by role and activity | — |
| **Workload** | Load across the business and peak hours | — |
| **Inventory** | Valuation, turnover and expiry risk | — |
| **Expenses** | Operating spend by day and category | — |
| **Expiry Risk** | Stock value at risk of expiring, **as it stands now** | — |
| **Expiry & Waste** | Stock actually written off in the period | — |
| **Predictive Analysis** | Demand, revenue and stock forecasts | — |

Open one and every report has the same shape, which is the point of one workbench: a period select, scope tabs where the report has them, summary tiles, a chart over time, a breakdown chart, and a data table with **Over time**, **Breakdown** and **Summary** views plus an **Export** button.

**A report with nothing in it says so rather than drawing an empty chart** — "Nothing to tabulate", with "This report returned no rows for \{period\}." underneath. On a new pharmacy that is the normal answer, not a fault.

## Read a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a report from the index">
    Select its card. The workbench opens on the **Last 30 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the period">
    The select offers **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** and **Last 12 months**. There are three, deliberately — a free-form date range on an aggregate invites comparisons between windows of different lengths.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a scope, if the report has tabs">
    **Revenue** splits **Invoices** (what you billed) from **Payments** (what actually arrived). **POS Sales** splits **Takings**, **Transactions** and **By cashier**. A report with one scope shows no tabs at all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the tiles first, then the charts">
    The tiles are the period's totals; the time chart is the shape; the breakdown chart is the split. The footer states the rule everyone eventually trips over: "Figures are aggregates for the selected period. Periods longer than 12 months are shortened server-side."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the table view if you need the numbers">
    **Over time**, **Breakdown** and **Summary** are three tabulations of the same report — pick the one you want to export before you export it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export it

Select **Export** above the data table:

- **CSV — this view** — exactly the rows on screen. The toast reads **Exported** — "\{n\} rows · \{period\}. Opens in Excel, Numbers or Sheets."
- **Excel (.xlsx) — this view** — a formatted workbook, built for you and downloaded. Its toast reads **Exported** — "Excel workbook · \{period\}."

"This view" is literal: you get the table tab you are looking at, over the period you have selected, and nothing else. If the workbook cannot be built you get **Not exported** with the reason.

## Expiry Risk and Expiry & Waste are not the same report

These two are confused constantly, so read this once.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Expiry Risk — the future" icon="/images/icons/triangle-exclamation.svg">
    A **snapshot**. What is on your shelves right now that will not sell through before it expires. It has **no period select** at all — in its place the header reads **As it stands now**, because a period control that changed nothing would be a lie you could not see. Act on it: discount, transfer, return.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Expiry & Waste — the past" icon="/images/icons/trash-can.svg">
    Period-shaped. What was actually written off in the window you selected, by day and by kind. Learn from it: which lines you keep over-ordering.
  </Card>
</Columns>

**Expiry Risk needs the Professional plan.** Below that the report is not offered on the index — see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan). The recommendations that go with it — discount-to-clear targets, supplier returns, transfers to the branch where an item still sells — are a **Business** feature and appear on the expiry-risk panel in [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/batches-and-expiry), not in this report.

## Who can do this

**Role** decides whether **Analytics** opens at all. A **Cashier / Till Operator** reaches only **POS** and **Sales**, and a pharmacy technician's sidebar stops before the money screens — neither of them has this entry. **Permission** decides one scope tab inside it.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Analytics** and any report | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing, Dispensing Supervisor | Role — not a grantable permission |
| Change the period, switch scopes, read the charts | Everyone who can open the report | Role |
| **Export** to CSV or Excel | Everyone who can open the report | Role — there is no separate export permission |
| The **By cashier** scope on **POS Sales** | Nobody by default — granted per person, plus owners and managers | **View per-cashier takings** |
| See **Expiry Risk** at all | Everyone, on **Professional** and above | Plan, not a permission |

On top of the role bundle each person carries an access tier — **Own till**, **Whole branch** or **All branches** — which decides how much of the business their figures cover. Someone on **Own till** reads the same reports about a smaller slice of the pharmacy.

**View per-cashier takings** is the one worth understanding, because it is granted to nobody by default. The till total and *who took it* are different questions, and only the second is colleague-performance visibility — so **POS Sales** stays open to everyone and just that tab is gated. **If the scope is not yours the workbench silently opens the first one you can see.** You are not told which tab is missing; if you expected **By cashier** and there is no tab strip at all, that is why.

Cost and margin figures are gated separately by **View cost prices and margin**, and that gate has no reference at all in the reports workbench — it governs [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index) and the [sales dashboard](/pharmacy/money/sales), where it withholds the figure as **"Hidden — needs authorisation from your clinic owner."** rather than showing a zero. Its defaults are the **Pharmacy Manager** and **Inventory & Purchasing** bundles, not the Dispensing Supervisor.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** A colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not.** A scope quietly falling back is not a denied action and is not logged.

See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The tiles, both charts and the table all describe the same period as the select.
- Switching scope changes the figures — **Invoices** and **Payments** on **Revenue** should rarely match exactly.
- An export downloads a file whose row count matches the toast.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Nothing to tabulate'">
    The report returned no rows for that period. Widen the period, or check you are on the scope you meant — **Payments** is empty until money is actually recorded, even when **Invoices** is not.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expiry Risk has no date picker">
    Correct, and deliberate. It is a snapshot of your shelves as they stand now. For a period-shaped view of what was written off, open **Expiry & Waste**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A report I expected is not listed">
    The index is generated from what your pharmacy can populate, so a report you cannot fill is not offered. **Expiry Risk** additionally needs **Professional** — see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no By cashier tab">
    You do not hold **View per-cashier takings**, so the workbench opened the first scope you can see instead. Ask an owner or manager to grant it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="'Not exported'">
    The Excel workbook could not be built; the toast carries the reason. Take **CSV — this view** instead — it is produced in your browser and does not depend on the same step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The exported file will not open properly">
    Take the Excel option rather than CSV if your spreadsheet mangles accents or currency symbols on import.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot see Analytics in the sidebar">
    Your role does not reach the reporting screens. A cashier's sidebar is **POS** and **Sales** only. Ask an owner or manager to change your role — see [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Expiry Risk missing a date picker?">
    Because it does not report on a period. It answers "what on my shelves is going to expire before it sells", which is true of this moment and nothing else. A period control that silently changed no figure would be worse than none, so the header says **As it stands now** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pick my own date range?">
    No — the periods are **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** and **Last 12 months**. Anything longer is shortened, and the footer says so. For a specific window, export the view and filter it in your spreadsheet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do Revenue and POS Sales disagree?">
    They count different money. **POS Sales** is what the till took, net of refunds. **Revenue** is invoiced and paid money — the account and institutional side. A pharmacy that rarely invoices will see a large **POS Sales** figure and a small **Revenue** one, and that is correct.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does exporting a report share it with anyone?">
    No. The file downloads to the machine you are on. It does leave a record that you opened and exported the report, with your name and the time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did the executive dashboard go?">
    It has its own screen — **Executive** in the sidebar. Reports answer "how did the last 90 days go"; the executive dashboard answers "how is today going". See [the executive dashboard](/pharmacy/money/executive).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
