The online store sells what your inventory already holds, at a public address customers can reach without signing in. Stock and cost stay in **Inventory**; the store is the shop window. Owners, managers, pharmacists, storekeepers, cashiers and pharmacy technicians can open it; the setup assistant cannot.

## Open it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect payments">
    The store can publish products only once your pharmacy can take payment online. The screen tells you what is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products from your stock">
    Create a product and link each variant to the inventory item it sells. The link is what lets customers' orders draw from your stock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Switch the store page on and set the products to **active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Links from listings

When a medicine-finder partner shows your pharmacy, it can also show a link to your store. A product link appears only when:

- your store is switched on and can take payment;
- your pharmacy is not on the Listing plan and keeps its stock in ClinikEHR (see [Stock source](/pharmacy/settings/stock-source));
- the item is listed and not hidden by you, and an active product in your store is linked to that exact item.

If you have connected your own web address to the store, listings use it.
