**Sales** is the record of what your till has done. It is where you look up a receipt, check the day's takings, and see which products are actually moving.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Sales']} />

## What you're looking at

The header reads **Sales Dashboard** — "Track and analyze your pharmacy sales performance" — with a period select (**Today**, **Last 7 Days**, **This Month**, **All Time**) and two buttons: **Debtors** and **New Sale**.

Everything below the period select obeys it. Change the period and every tile, chart and table on the page re-reads.

| Tab | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Four takings tiles, the **Net position** card, and the charts |
| **Recent Sales** | The **Sales History** table, with search, scan and a status filter |
| **Products** | **Product sales** — units, line revenue and margin per product |
| **Analytics** | **Daily Sales Comparison**, **Revenue Trend** and a **Performance Summary** |

**A brand-new pharmacy** sees the tiles at zero, **No sales found for this period** on the Overview list, **No sales found — Try adjusting your search or filter criteria** on **Recent Sales**, and **No products sold in this period.** on **Products**. Nothing is broken; there is simply nothing yet.

<Note>
  **There is no export button on this page**, and people hunt for one. Exports live in two other places on purpose: the **Reports** card on the **Executive** dashboard, and every report in [Analytics](/pharmacy/money/analytics), which offers **Export** as CSV or Excel for the view you are looking at.
</Note>

## Read the overview

Four tiles across the top — **Total Revenue**, **Total Sales**, **Avg Sale Value** and **Items Sold** — then the **Net position** card, then the charts.

### The Net position card

"Revenue less cost of goods and expenses, for the selected period." Five figures that make one number honest:

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | What you took in over the period |
| **Cost of goods** | What the stock you sold cost you |
| **Expenses** | Running costs recorded over the same period |
| **Loss (expired/damaged)** | Stock written off, valued at what it cost |
| **Net** | The four above, resolved — green when positive, red when not |

Revenue on its own is not profit, and a pharmacy that reads only the top tile will believe a good week happened when it did not. Expenses are the line you control — the card is only as truthful as what you record on the [Expenses page](/pharmacy/money/expenses).

Where stock was received without a unit cost, the card says so rather than quietly counting it as free: **Partial — \{n\} units sold with no cost recorded. Add a unit cost when receiving stock.**

### The charts

**Sales Trend** plots takings over the period. **Payment Methods** breaks the same money down by tender. **Recent Sales** underneath is a quick list of the latest transactions.

## Find a past sale

**Recent Sales** holds the **Sales History** table — **Sale #**, **Date & Time**, **Customer**, **Items**, **Payment**, **Amount** and **Status** — with **Search or scan receipt...** and a status filter (**All Status**, **Completed**, **Pending**, **Cancelled**, **Refunded**).

The search box takes a scan as well as typing: point a scanner at the barcode on a customer's receipt and the sale comes straight up. The small barcode button inside the box scans with the camera instead, for a till with no hardware scanner. See [Set up a barcode scanner](/pharmacy/hardware/barcode-scanner).

A sale captured while the till was offline carries an **Offline** badge. It is a real, completed sale that reached the server later — the badge tells you the receipt the customer holds shows a provisional number rather than the one in this table. See [Selling when the internet is down](/pharmacy/pos/offline).

Refunds, voids and amendments are worked from each row's **⋯** menu and are covered in [Returns, voids and amendments](/pharmacy/pos/returns).

## See what sold

The **Products** tab holds the **Product sales** card, filterable by product type, with totals for **Products**, **Units sold**, **Line revenue** and **Margin** above the table. The totals follow the filter, so what you read always matches what is on screen.

**Line revenue** is the value of the lines themselves — it excludes sale-level tax, surcharges and whole-sale discounts, so it will not add up to **Total Revenue** and is not meant to.

**Margin** appears only where a cost was recorded when the stock was received. No lot cost, no margin — the column will not guess.

## Who can do this

Anyone who can work the till can open **Sales** — a cashier needs to look up a receipt. What differs is the *money* on the page: cost, margin and the bottom line are separate grants, and where you are not entitled to a figure the product removes it rather than showing you a zero.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Sales**, read the takings tiles and charts | Cashier, Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor | Reaching the module at all |
| Find a sale, scan a receipt, reprint it | The same people | **Dispense and sell at the till** |
| See the **Cost** and **Margin** columns | Pharmacy Manager, Inventory & Purchasing | **View cost prices and margin** |
| See the **Net position** card | Pharmacy Manager | **View the net position** |
| See the **By cashier** scope on the POS Sales report in Analytics | Nobody by default — granted per person | **View per-cashier takings** |
| Refund, void or amend a sale from here | Pharmacy Manager, Dispensing Supervisor — **not** a Cashier | **Refund or void a sale** |

How a denied figure behaves is deliberate, and different in each case:

- **Without View cost prices and margin**, the **Margin** total reads **—** with **Hidden — needs authorisation** beneath it, and the **Cost** and **Margin** columns are not in the table. The rest of the Products tab works normally.
- **Without View the net position**, the whole card is gone — not zeroed, not blanked. A card showing 0 would be read as "we made nothing", which is a lie. Absent says "not yours to see".
- **View per-cashier takings** is off for every role until someone grants it, because the till total and *who* took it are different questions and only the second is colleague-performance visibility.

Two behaviours surprise people:

- **Enforcement is opt-in, per person.** Someone never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted and sees every figure, whatever their role bundle. The sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**.
- **A denied action is recorded; an allowed one is not** — but a *hidden figure* is not an action, so quietly not seeing the net position leaves no line in the audit trail.

Both are set per person by an owner or manager — see [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- Change the period select and every tile, chart and table re-reads together — if one figure looks stuck, it is the period you are reading, not the page.
- **Total Revenue** for **Today** matches what the till has taken, with refunds already netted out.
- **Debtors** opens the list of customers with an unsettled balance from a part-paid sale, headed **Total outstanding**.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'That barcode is not a ClinikEHR receipt.'">
    You scanned something that is not a sale number — an item barcode, or a return or adjustment reference. Receipt numbers look like **POS-20260816-59E46A**. Type the number or find the sale by date and customer instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A sale I know we took is not in the list">
    Widen the period select — it defaults to a recent window — and set the status filter back to **All Status**. If it still is not there, it was captured offline and has not synced yet; check the till's status button reads **Synced**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Net position card is missing entirely">
    You do not have **View the net position**. It is hidden rather than zeroed, on purpose. Ask an owner or manager to grant it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Margin reads 'Hidden — needs authorisation'">
    You do not have **View cost prices and margin**. Everything else on the **Products** tab still works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Line revenue does not add up to Total Revenue">
    It is not meant to. **Line revenue** is the value of the product lines only, so it excludes sale-level tax, surcharges and whole-sale discounts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A product sold, but its margin is blank">
    No unit cost was recorded when that stock was received, so there is nothing to subtract. Add the cost on the next delivery and future sales will carry a margin.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no export button on Sales?">
    Because exporting was deliberately moved to the two places built for it: the **Executive** dashboard's **Reports** card, which exports top products, sales, customers and payment methods, and the report workbench in [Analytics](/pharmacy/money/analytics), where every report offers **Export** as CSV or Excel. Keeping one copy of that logic is why the figures agree wherever you take them from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do refunds reduce the figures on this page?">
    Yes. A reversal is netted out of the sales reports for the period the sale belongs to, so **Total Revenue** is money you actually kept. The reversed sale stays in **Sales History** with a **Refunded** status rather than disappearing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do sales taken offline appear here?">
    They do, once the terminal has synced — with a real receipt number and an **Offline** badge. They are stamped with the time they were captured, not the time they synced, so they count towards the right trading day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Total Sales and Items Sold?">
    **Total Sales** counts transactions — how many customers you served. **Items Sold** counts units across all of them. A quiet day with two large orders can beat a busy one on **Items Sold** and lose on **Total Sales**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see which cashier took which sale?">
    Only with **View per-cashier takings**, and only on the **By cashier** scope of the POS Sales report in Analytics — not on this page. The till total and who took it are treated as separate questions, so seeing the takings never implies seeing your colleagues' individual performance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
