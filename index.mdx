## Start with your product

ClinikEHR comes in three editions. They share an account, a login and a security model, but each has its own screens and its own vocabulary — so pick yours and stay in that section.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Clinic & Hospital" icon="/images/icons/stethoscope.svg" horizontal={false} href="/clinic/get-started">
    Solo practices, group clinics and multi-department hospitals. Appointments, consultations, clinical notes, wards, theatre, billing and insurance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pharmacy" icon="/images/icons/prescription-bottle-medical.svg" horizontal={false} href="/pharmacy/get-started">
    Retail and hospital pharmacies. Point of sale that keeps working offline, batch and expiry control, dispensing, and customer wallets.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Diagnostics" icon="/images/icons/microscope.svg" horizontal={false} href="/lims/get-started">
    Standalone labs, imaging centres and DOT collection sites. Sample lifecycle, worklists, results, imaging and quality control.
  </Card>
</Columns>
