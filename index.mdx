## Start with your product

Pick the one that matches your screen and stay in that section. Each has its own guides, its own steps, and its own words for the people you serve — a pharmacy has **customers**, a solo practice has **clients**, a hospital has **patients**.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Solo & Team" icon="/images/icons/user.svg" horizontal={false} href="/practice/get-started">
    A solo practitioner or a small team. One flat menu, clients rather than patients, and a straight line from a session to getting paid.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hospital & Enterprise" icon="/images/icons/hospital.svg" horizontal={false} href="/hospital/get-started">
    A multi-department facility. Front desk, triage and consultation, wards, theatre, diagnostics, pharmacy and insurance — grouped by service line.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pharmacy" icon="/images/icons/prescription-bottle-medical.svg" horizontal={false} href="/pharmacy/get-started">
    Retail and hospital pharmacies. Point of sale that keeps working offline, batch and expiry control, dispensing, and customer wallets.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Diagnostics" icon="/images/icons/microscope.svg" horizontal={false} href="/lims/get-started">
    Standalone labs, imaging centres and DOT collection sites. Sample lifecycle, worklists, results, imaging and quality control.
  </Card>
</Columns>
