## Quiet hours

Under **Quiet hours** choose **Add quiet hours**, pick the days and the times, and select **Save quiet hours**. Sounds, notifications and the email reminder pause in those hours; badges still update. Urgent alerts and critical results still get through. A rule that crosses midnight belongs to the day it starts on.

## Announcements

Owners and managers write one message under **Announcement**, with an optional link and end time, and select **Post announcement**. It shows across the top of Team chat for everyone until they dismiss it or it ends, and it is also posted in #general. It never shows over a point-of-sale screen. **End this announcement** removes it.

## The policy

Everyone accepts the **Team chat policy** the first time. Under **Team chat policy** an owner selects **Edit the policy**, and can turn on **This is a material change** so that everyone has to accept it again before they can post.

## Direct messages, roles and holds (owners)

- **Allow direct messages between staff** turns direct messages on or off for the clinic. Existing ones stay readable.
- **Roles with Team chat turned off** keeps a role out of Team chat.
- **Legal holds** stop retention deleting a person's messages, a channel or a patient's conversations while the hold is on.
- **Welcome message** is the first direct message a new colleague receives.
