## Send an emergency code

If your clinic has set up codes you see a red **Codes** button at the top of the list, on a phone too. Select it, choose the code, say where (if it asks), and select **Send** to confirm. Everyone the code reaches gets a banner and the code's own sound, and it stays until they confirm. A code can be sent only a few times in a short while, so a slip of the finger can't flood anyone.

## Set up codes (owners)

In **Team chat settings**, **Emergency codes** lists them. Select **New code**, then give it a name, a colour, a sound, who it reaches (everyone on shift, one role, or one site) and whether it asks where.

## Start a huddle

Select **Start a huddle** in a channel's header. The huddle opens in its own window, where your browser asks for the microphone and the controls live. A huddle is audio only and is never recorded. A bar above the conversation shows who is in it, with **Join**; the person who started it, owners, managers and channel admins also see **End huddle**. The conversation gets a card with the same **Join** and **End huddle**, and the card reads **Huddle ended** with how many people were in it and for how long once it is over. A huddle nobody joins closes by itself after a few minutes. If it is full you see **Call is full**. Calls and huddles are part of Essential and up; on the Free plan you see an upgrade prompt.

When a call ends, the chat from the call is posted back as one reply that is folded; select it to show the lines.

## Since your last shift

At the start of a shift you may see **Since your last shift**. Select **Summarise (AI)** on a conversation to read a private summary. It costs 1 credit per conversation, and only when you ask.

## Reply to a code

A code shows **Responding** and **Can't respond**. Either one stops the alarm on your screen, but only **Responding** counts as answering; if you can't, it carries on to others. The person who sent the code, and owners and managers, can see who is responding, who can't and who hasn't answered, live.

## Drills

An owner can turn on **This is a drill** when sending a code. It is marked **DRILL, practice only**, uses a softer sound and is left out of the clinic's figures. Afterwards the **Drill report** shows who answered and how fast.

## Calls from a direct message

Starting a call or huddle in a direct message or group message rings the other people. On a phone it fills the screen with **Accept** and **Decline**; on a computer it is a card in the corner. It stops by itself after about 30 seconds, then the conversation shows **Missed call from Ada** with **Call back**. The ring stays quiet during Do not disturb and quiet hours. A huddle in a channel shows a bar and a card you can join, and never rings.

## Calls about a patient

In a patient conversation you are first told that the call is listed on the patient's access trail: who joined, when and for how long, never what was said. It is not recorded.

## Captions

In a call or huddle, **Turn captions on** shows live captions for you. They are English only and are not saved. While anyone has them on, everyone sees a notice that captions are on (not who). Captions are part of Essential and up.

## Hand over a consult

An accepted consult has **Hand over**: choose a person and add a note. If two people tap **Accept** together, the first one owns it and the other sees who has it.
