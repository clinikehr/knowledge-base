Ozni reads the same **Inventory** screens you do, with the same rules. It can only tell you about stock your role can see, and it can only prepare changes your role can make.

## Asking what is on the shelf

Ask what is low, what expires soon, what moved, what a count found, which purchase orders are open, which recalls are open, what is waiting to be filled, or what the fridge readings were.

- Every stock figure shows when it was read. If you ask again later, Ozni reads it again. It does not reuse an old number.
- A fridge reading that is more than a day old is called old, not current.
- Costs show only to people allowed to see them. For everyone else a cost is a dash, and Ozni does not work it out from other figures.
- A recall can list the clients who received the recalled stock, only as the **Recalls** screen would show them to you.

## Changes Ozni can prepare

Ozni prepares a Confirm card for each of these. Nothing changes until you confirm.

- Add or change an item, archive or restore it, or change reorder levels or prices for up to 50 items at once. A change to more than 10 items is a larger request, and Ozni tells you the price first. See [Credits](/platform/ozni/credits).
- Add or change a storage location.
- Receive a delivery and move stock between locations. Receiving cannot be undone here. A mistake is corrected with an adjustment or a count.
- Start a purchase order as a **draft**, submit a draft or cancel one. Ozni never emails a purchase order to a supplier.
- Open a stock count, save counted quantities, and approve it. The approval card lists every difference with its value before you confirm. A count of more than 500 lines is not approved here: Ozni tells you, and you approve it on the **Inventory** screen.
- Change which stock items a service uses. This replaces the whole list, and the card says so.
- **Reorder what is low.** Ozni builds one draft purchase order for each supplier from the items under their reorder level. You review and submit them yourself.

## What stays on the Inventory screen

Ozni never dispenses, fills a prescription from stock, wastes or writes off stock, deletes an item, validates a lot, rings up a sale, files a vaccine report or uses stock for an appointment. Counts and transfers of controlled medicines, and anything that needs a witness, are done on the **Inventory** screen with the witness signing in.
