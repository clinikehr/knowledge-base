The **ClinikEHR API** lets another system — your own website, a partner's software, an internal report — read straight out of your ClinikEHR workspace, without anyone exporting a file by hand. You create the key yourself, from inside your own workspace, and choose exactly what it can see.

## What it opens today

By default, a key you create reads your workspace's own **inventory**: locations, catalogue items, availability, and exact stock levels. The **API add-on** is what opens everything else your own keys can reach — patients, appointments, clinical notes, and your CRM — still only with keys you create yourself, for your own systems. See [API access](/platform/settings/api-access) for the full walkthrough, including the add-on and the API data agreement your patient records need before they're reachable.

You can also let another organization — a marketplace, a billing partner — connect to your workspace directly, item by item, under **Connected organizations** on the same tab. Nothing is ever shared without you approving it.

## Accepting outside items without review

Items such as allergies or referrals that an app sends wait for a clinician by default. An owner or manager can choose to accept new items from a key or a connected organization without review. See [Accept outside data automatically](/platform/integrations/auto-accept-outside-data).

## Where the documentation is

Full endpoint reference, authentication, error codes, permissions and guides are at **[docs.clinikehr.com](https://docs.clinikehr.com)**. If you're the one writing the integration (rather than the one creating the key), that's where to send your developer.

## Not to be confused with ClinikAPI

**ClinikAPI** is a **separate product** — a FHIR R4 developer platform for building your own healthcare software from scratch, licensed on its own, apart from any ClinikEHR subscription. It does not read a ClinikEHR clinic's records; if you're trying to get data out of your own ClinikEHR workspace, the API access above is what you want, not ClinikAPI. If you're building an independent product and want FHIR, SDKs and embeddable components, see [docs.clinikapi.com](https://docs.clinikapi.com).
