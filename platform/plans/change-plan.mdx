Moving between plans is not a support ticket. Pick a plan card, read the summary of what you will pay and when, and confirm. If you already pay for a plan, the change is made to the subscription you have — you are never billed for two plans at once.

## The two kinds of change

Every change is one of two kinds, and the summary you confirm tells you which:

| You are… | When it happens | What you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moving **up** a tier, or switching **monthly → yearly** | **Immediately** | The new price, less credit for the unused part of the period you already paid for. Your billing date resets to today. |
| Moving **down** a tier, or switching **yearly → monthly** | **At the end of your current period** | Nothing today. You keep everything you paid for until then; no refund is issued for the remainder. |

The credit is worked out to the second on your current billing period. If an Essential plan billed monthly moved to Team billed yearly five days into a thirty-day month, you would be charged the Team annual price less twenty-five thirtieths of what you paid for Essential that month.

A workspace on legacy pricing (a Starter plan, or an older Essential or Team price) moves onto our **current** pricing when it changes plan or billing period — a downgrade moves at the end of the period, with everything else. The summary says so before you confirm; see [If your workspace is on legacy pricing](#if-your-workspace-is-on-legacy-pricing) below and [Legacy plans](/platform/plans/legacy-plans).

## Upgrade, or switch to yearly billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan page">
    The heading reads **Choose Your Plan** if you are on Free, or **Manage Your Subscription** if you already pay. Anywhere you hit a cap you also get a **View plans** or **Upgrade** button that lands in the same place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Monthly or Yearly">
    The toggle at the top switches between **Monthly** and **Yearly**. Yearly cards show the effective monthly price and the amount billed annually, so compare on the same footing before you decide. The banner at the top tells you which you are on today — **Active · monthly** or **Active · annual**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the plan card">
    The button says what the change is: **Upgrade to Team**, **Downgrade to Free**, **Switch to annual billing**, **Switch to monthly billing**. Your current plan on your current billing period is badged **Current Plan** and cannot be selected again; the same plan on the other billing period can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary, then confirm">
    If you already pay for a plan, a panel opens (a bottom sheet on a phone): the new price, the credit for the unused part of your current period, the line **Charged today**, and **Takes effect**. The button reads **Pay … and switch** with the exact amount, kept at the bottom of the panel. Nothing is charged until you select it, and **Keep current plan** closes the panel with nothing changed.

    If your card is declined, you get **Payment declined** — "Your card was not charged and your plan is unchanged" — and that is exactly what happened: nothing moved.

    If your workspace bills through Paystack, the summary instead takes you to a Paystack page to pay the difference; your new plan starts the moment that payment succeeds and you return to **Payment received — your new plan is being applied now.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You get **Plan changed** and the banner reads **Active Team Plan**, or whichever you chose, with the new **Next billing** date — today's date next month or next year.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **On Free?** There is nothing to change yet, so choosing Essential or Team is a checkout instead — see [Payment methods](/platform/plans/payment-methods). You can go straight to the full plan, or start with **Try Essential** — \$29.90/₦29,900 for 30 days of Essential, converting automatically to the full price unless you cancel. The trial is offered only during a workspace's first 30 days, and never on yearly billing; see [Plans and what they include](/platform/plans/overview#try-essential-the-paid-trial). **Already on the trial?** Upgrading to Team ends the trial immediately and charges the Team price in full — the trial fee is not credited. On success you land on **Payment Successful!** and are returned to your dashboard; if you back out you get **Payment Cancelled**: "Your payment was cancelled. No charges were made to your account."
</Note>

## If your workspace is on legacy pricing

A workspace still on Starter, or on an older Essential or Team price, sees one more block at the top of the summary:

> **Changing plan moves you to our current pricing.**
>
> Your Essential price of \$59.90/month is a legacy rate. It can't be kept, and you can't return to it later — if you change back, you'll pay today's price for that plan.
>
> Your 6 clinicians: 5 included, 1 extra at \$35/month.
> **New monthly total:** \$285 (was \$259.90).
> **Takes effect:** today

What to know before you confirm:

- **It applies to every change except cancelling** — upgrading, downgrading, and switching between monthly and yearly on Essential or Team. A downgrade shows **Takes effect: on** the date, **(end of period)**, and moves to current pricing then.
- **The total is for your own team.** Legacy pricing charged every clinician after the owner; current pricing includes some clinicians in the plan price (Essential 3, Team 5) and charges each extra one monthly. The summary counts your clinicians the way the plan will, so a small team can pay less in seats and more for the plan, or the other way round. Compare **New monthly total** with **was** before you confirm. If you are billed yearly, the summary lists the yearly plan price and the monthly clinician charge separately instead of a single monthly total.
- **Moving to Essential has a five-person ceiling, and the owner counts.** If your team is larger, the panel tells you how many people to deactivate or re-role first, for example "Essential allows 5 people. Deactivate or re-role 2 clinicians first." Nothing is charged and nothing changes until you do.
- **Yearly billing and extra clinicians.** A yearly plan cannot carry a monthly charge for extra clinicians on the same subscription, so they are billed on a **separate monthly subscription** on the same card, and you see two charges. The panel says so: "New recurring charges: \$960/year, plus 2 extra clinicians at \$40/month billed separately." Today's total includes the first month of the extra clinicians. You need a default card on file; if you have none the panel asks you to add one first, and nothing is changed or charged. Moving back to monthly billing folds the extra clinicians back onto your main subscription and ends the separate one.
- **Earlier seat charges on the same invoice.** If you added clinicians earlier in the billing period, their prorated charges are collected on the invoice a plan change creates. The summary lists them as **Earlier seat adjustments on this invoice**, so every line adds up to **Charged today**.
- **Seats you already pay for.** Clinicians who become included stop being charged. On an immediate change you receive Stripe's proration for the unused part of the old seat charge; on a scheduled change nothing is charged for them after the date. There is no separate credit or card refund. If you pay through Paystack, the old seat subscriptions are ended and the clinician charges you still need are started again at the current fee on the date the change applies.
- **Starter switching between monthly and yearly keeps Starter's price.** Starter is no longer sold, so this is your current plan's own price on the other billing period. It is the one change that does not move you.
- **Pharmacy and Diagnostics** workspaces are unaffected: they have no new pricing to move to.
- **You get one email.** "Your plan and price" arrives when the change is confirmed (or scheduled), repeating the figures above. It is a notice about your account, so it cannot be switched off. It comes from Bettar Platforms, Inc., the company behind ClinikEHR.

## Downgrade, or switch to monthly billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the lower plan's card">
    The button reads **Downgrade to Free** (or **Switch to monthly billing** for the same plan). The summary opens with **Charged today** showing nothing due, and the date the change will take effect — the end of your current period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Schedule change">
    You get **Change scheduled**. The banner at the top now carries an amber line: **Scheduled: Free · monthly starts on** the date, **You keep your current plan until then.** Every plan card is held until that date passes or you withdraw the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your mind before that date">
    Select **Withdraw change** on the banner and confirm. Your subscription simply continues as it bills today; nothing was charged for the change, so there is nothing to refund.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **A downgrade takes features away the moment it applies**, and the caps on the lower plan are enforced from then on. Nothing you already created is deleted — but where you are over a cap, the surface stops accepting **new** entries. Over the CRM contact cap, you cannot add a contact. Over the store product cap, you cannot publish another product. Over the published-agent cap, you cannot publish another agent. Existing records stay exactly where they are and stay readable; you simply cannot add to them until you are back under the limit or back on the higher plan.
</Warning>

Before you drop a tier, check what sits above the new one: telehealth, insurance, the store, memberships and AI features are the usual casualties. Staff seats are capped by plan too, and on Clinic & Hospital each seat beyond the plan's included ones is charged individually — see [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats).

## Cancel, and change your mind

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Cancel Subscription">
    On the workspace's card under **Billing** → **Plan Subscriptions**. The dialog spells out **What you'll lose:** and confirms the date access runs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You get **Subscription Cancelled** — "Your subscription will be cancelled at the end of the billing period." The card then shows **Ending** with the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your mind before that date">
    Select **Keep Plan** on the card, or **Reactivate Subscription** once it has ended, and you are back on **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancelling does not lock you out: "your patient records, clinical notes, and history stay fully accessible. You're never locked out."

## The rule to read twice

<Info>
  **Anything other than active or trialing counts as Free.** A failed renewal, a lapsed cancellation or a **Past Due** card collapses every paid feature to the Free floor for the whole workspace until billing is fixed — even though the plan name still appears on the card. If features vanished and nobody changed a setting, look at the subscription status first. See [Plans and what they include](/platform/plans/overview).
</Info>

## Check it worked

- The workspace card shows **Active** (or **Trial Active**) and the new plan name.
- Inside the workspace, the banner reads **Active Essential Plan**, or whichever plan you chose, with **Next billing** and a date.
- The feature you upgraded for is now usable rather than showing an upgrade prompt.
- The charge appears under **Billing** → **Payment History**. See [Your subscription invoices and receipts](/platform/plans/invoices-and-receipts).
- For a scheduled downgrade, the banner shows the amber **Scheduled:** line with the date, and nothing new appears in **Payment History** until then.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment Cancelled">
    You closed the provider's page, or the card was refused there. Nothing was charged and your plan choice is kept. Select **Try Again**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I paid but the workspace is still on the old plan">
    Select **Refresh** on the **Billing** page. Confirmation from the payment provider can take a minute. If it has not moved after five, [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) with the time of the payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="That plan belongs to a different edition of ClinikEHR">
    You move up and down within your edition's ladder; you never move between editions. See [Plans and what they include](/platform/plans/overview) and [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) to move a workspace to another edition.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Essential allows 5 people. Deactivate or re-role … clinicians first.">
    The plan you chose has a ceiling on how many people can be on the team, and the owner counts. Deactivate people who have left, or give someone a role that does not use a seat (supervisors, and billers or schedulers without a clinical role), then choose the plan again. See [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your last payment did not go through. Update your card…">
    A workspace whose last payment failed cannot move to new pricing until it is paid. Select **Update card** in the panel, fix the card, and try again. See [Payment methods](/platform/plans/payment-methods).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We could not confirm the price for this plan change">
    Nothing was changed and nothing was charged. The price for that plan could not be confirmed on our side, so we do not show a figure we cannot stand behind. [Contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) and we will sort it out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Add a card as your default payment method before changing plan">
    A yearly plan with extra clinicians bills those clinicians monthly on a separate subscription, which needs a default card. Add one from **Update card** on the **Billing** page, then choose the plan again. Nothing was changed or charged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="That plan is no longer sold">
    Starter cannot be chosen from another plan, because it is no longer offered. Choose Essential or Team, or [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A plan change is already scheduled for this subscription">
    Only one change can wait at a time. Select **Withdraw change** on the banner first, then pick the plan you actually want.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This subscription is set to cancel at the end of the period">
    A cancelling subscription cannot change plan — the change would quietly undo the cancellation. Select **Keep Plan** on the workspace's card first, then change plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trial Not Available — this clinic has already used its introductory trial, or has had a paid subscription before.">
    The \$29.90/₦29,900 trial is one per workspace, and a workspace that has ever paid for a subscription is not offered it again. Creating a second workspace to get another trial gives you an empty workspace you then have to maintain — pick a paid plan on this one instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The card says Past Due">
    The renewal failed. Paid features are already off. Fix the payment before anything else — see [Payment methods](/platform/plans/payment-methods).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancel Subscription is greyed out">
    You have no paid subscription to cancel: "No paid subscriptions found 🎉". If you are not enrolled in the paid trial at all, there is nothing to cancel — a workspace simply stays on Free until you choose a plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This subscription is billed in a way we cannot change automatically">
    A small number of older workspaces are billed through an arrangement the plan switcher cannot adjust on its own. Rather than take a payment that would leave you paying for two subscriptions at once, the change is refused. [Contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) and we will move you across without charging you twice — you keep everything you have already paid for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This clinic already has an active paid subscription">
    You reached the checkout page for a workspace that is already paying. Starting a second subscription would bill you twice, so it is refused. Go to **Billing**, choose the plan you want on the workspace's own card, and the change is prorated against what you already pay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I own several workspaces and changed the wrong one">
    Every workspace has its own subscription and its own card. Check the name on the card before you confirm. See [Work across more than one workspace](/platform/account/switching-workspaces).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
