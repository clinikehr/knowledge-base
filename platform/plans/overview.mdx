Your plan decides **which features are unlocked and how much capacity you get**
inside your workspace. It does not decide which modules exist — that is your
edition — and it does not decide who may do what — that is roles and
permissions. See [Who can see and do what](/start/roles-and-access).

## The ladders

Each edition has its own ladder, and you move up and down within it. You never
move between editions. See [The three editions](/start/editions).

| Edition | Plan ladder, cheapest to richest |
|---|---|
| Clinic & Hospital | Free → Starter → Essential → Team → Enterprise |
| Pharmacy | Starter → Professional → Business → Enterprise |
| Diagnostics | Starter → Professional → Business → Enterprise |

Only Clinic & Hospital has a **Free** plan. Pharmacy and Diagnostics start at
**Starter**.

**Enterprise is priced individually.** It is the plan for hospitals, multi-branch
pharmacy groups and multi-site labs, and its cost depends on your size and
structure. Ask us rather than looking for a price on the card.

## What your plan actually controls

Four different things, and it helps to keep them apart.

| What the plan sets | What you notice |
|---|---|
| **Which features are unlocked** | Locked cards and upgrade prompts instead of the feature |
| **How much capacity you have** | Caps on records, contacts in the CRM, products in the store, published AI agents |
| **How many staff seats you may have** | The invite is blocked, or charged, at the cap. See [Staff seats](/platform/plans/seats) |
| **Your monthly AI credit allowance** | How many agent runs you get before you top up. See [AI credits](/platform/plans/credits) |

On Clinic & Hospital, the plan also decides the **shape of your navigation**:
solo and team plans get one flat list and call the people you serve **clients**,
while the enterprise plan gets a hospital layout grouped by service line and
calls them **patients**.

## AI credits by plan

Clinic & Hospital workspaces are granted these every month:

| Plan | Monthly agent credits |
|---|---|
| Free | 50 |
| Starter | 1,000 |
| Essential | 2,500 |
| Team | 5,000 |

Refill packs are bought separately and never expire. See
[AI credits](/platform/plans/credits).

## The free trial

A workspace that is on Free and has never trialled gets 7 days of full access,
and it takes **no card**.

There are two ways in, and both are normal:

- **From the plan page.** The **Try ClinikEHR Free for 7 Days** section offers
  **Start 7-Day Free Trial**. You get "Your 7-day free trial has started! Let's
  finish setup..."
- **From the first-run page.** Many new workspaces land on "Let's get your clinic
  set up" — "You've got full access for 7 days — no card needed." — with a single
  **Start for free** button, **No credit card required**, and **Or compare plans**
  underneath if you would rather pick a paid plan now.

One trial per workspace. If it has already been used you get **Trial Already
Used** — "This clinic has already used its free trial period." — and the plan
comparison instead.

When the 7 days end, a Clinic & Hospital workspace moves to Free. Your records,
notes and history stay fully accessible; the premium features stop.

## The rule that surprises people

<Warning>
  **A subscription that is not active or trialing is treated as Free.** A failed
  card, a cancellation that has run out, an unpaid renewal — any of these drop
  every paid feature to the Free floor immediately, on every screen, for
  everybody in the workspace. Nothing is deleted and nobody is locked out of
  their records, but the paid capabilities are gone until the billing is fixed.
</Warning>

This is why a workspace can appear to "lose" telehealth, insurance or the store
overnight with no setting having changed. Check the subscription before you
check anything else: under **Billing**, a clinic card reads **Active**, **Trial
Active**, **Past Due**, **Cancelled** or **No Subscription**. Anything other
than **Active** or **Trial Active** explains the missing features on its own.

## Where to see your plan

<Path steps={['Personal home', 'Billing', 'Plan Subscriptions']} />

Each workspace you own gets a card showing its plan, its status, its **Current
Plan** features, its patient or customer usage against the cap, and either
**Trial ends** or **Next billing**. Inside a workspace, the same detail is on
**Dashboard** → **Subscription**.
