A new Clinic Drive folder can be opened by everyone in the clinic. Limit it when it holds something private, such as contracts or staff records.

## Limit a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder's menu">
    Open the menu on the folder and select **Folder access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can open it">
    Choose **Everyone in the clinic**, **Certain roles**, **Certain people** or **Owners and managers only**. For roles or people, choose which ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Folders and files inside follow the same setting unless you change them. Owners and managers can always open every folder.

## What each choice means

| Choice | Who can open the folder |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone in the clinic** | Every member who can use Drive |
| **Certain roles** | People with those roles, plus owners and managers |
| **Certain people** | The people you choose, plus owners and managers |
| **Owners and managers only** | Owners and managers |

A file shared with a colleague by name is still visible to that colleague when the folder around it is limited. See [Share a file or folder inside the clinic](/platform/drive/share-files).

People whose role is technical, such as a developer or IT administrator, see a Clinic Drive file only when it is shared with them by name. They have no access to patients' files in Drive.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot widen a folder">
    A folder inside an owners-and-managers-only folder cannot be opened up on its own. The message says "This folder sits inside an owners-and-managers-only folder. Change that folder first."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no Folder access option">
    Setting who can open a folder is for owners and managers by default. Folders that ClinikEHR manages, such as **Ozni documents** and **Team chat**, cannot be changed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
