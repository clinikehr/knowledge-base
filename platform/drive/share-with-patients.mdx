## Share a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Share sheet">
    Open the patient's file, select **Share**, then **Patient portal**. You can do the same from the chart's **Files** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show it">
    Turn on **Show in the patient's portal**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turn the switch off to take it back. The patient no longer sees it.

The chart's **Files** tab and Drive use the same setting, so they always agree.

## What cannot be shared

- A file marked **sensitive**. Clear the mark first if it should not be.
- A file still being checked for viruses, or one that has not been checked. Upload it again to have it checked.
- A file marked entered in error.
- A file protected by the substance use disorder confidentiality rules (42 CFR Part 2).

## What the patient sees

**My documents** in the portal lists everything the clinic has shared with them: files you showed, superbills and statements. They can filter by type and download what they need. A patient with a representative sees only what the representative may see.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The switch is off and greyed out">
    The line under it says why: the file is sensitive, still being checked, or marked entered in error.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The patient cannot find the file">
    Ask them to open **My documents** in the portal. The file shows there once you turn the switch on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
