Your workspace has one storage limit for everything in Drive. The **Storage** page shows how much is used and where the space goes.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Drive', 'Storage']} />

## The limit for each plan

| Plan | Drive storage (files and ClinikMail) |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5 GB |
| Essential | 50 GB |
| Team | 250 GB |
| Enterprise | 1 TB |

The limit is one amount per workspace, however many people work in it. Extra storage you bought is added to it (see [Buy more storage](#buy-more-storage) below), and so is any ClinikForms storage you bought before packs moved to Drive. To change your plan, see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).

## What counts toward the limit

Everything your workspace stores counts toward the one amount: files you upload, patient files, Team chat files, ClinikForms files and signatures, lab reports, imaging, video visit and ambient recordings, whiteboards, all of your ClinikMail (the messages and their attachments), patient portal message attachments, Medi-Cal files, store downloads, number porting invoices, and the profile photos and branding images you have added. The **Storage** page shows where the space goes. A study's key images or a whiteboard's pages are one item, and every picture in it counts toward the amount once.

Some of these are counted but never listed in Drive: mail messages (shown on the Storage page as **Mail messages**). A message's size includes its attachments, and the copy of an attachment saved as a file, which you can see in Clinic Drive, is counted separately because both are stored, profile photos, branding, store and form pictures, and the files that belong to ClinikForms. They are shown on the Storage page under **Photos and branding** and **Forms**, and you open them where you work with them.

Not counted: exports that expire after you download them, and files ClinikEHR keeps for its own running. Fax and billing documents are shown in a patient's files as links to the original; their space is not added to the Drive figure yet.

Mail has no storage limit of its own: when Drive is full, incoming mail is still delivered and messages without attachments still send; only a new message with attachments is refused until you free space or add storage (see [Mail storage](/practice/work/mail-storage)).

Files that belong to another feature (a lab report, a recording, a mail attachment) open in that feature, not in Drive's own viewer, and cannot be renamed, moved, deleted or shared from Drive. An owner can place a **legal hold** on one: while it is held, the feature's automatic clean-up skips it.

## How many files you can add at once

To keep an account from being flooded, each person can add up to **100 files in 10 minutes** and **1,000 files a day**, counting files uploaded in Drive and files added to a patient's chart together. If you reach it, the upload stops with a message saying how long to wait, and any files still waiting in that batch are marked as not added so you can send them again later. Patients' uploads from the client portal have their own, separate limit.

## Buy more storage

On a paid plan you can add space without changing plan, with a monthly **storage pack**. Each pack adds to the limit for as long as it is active.

| Pack | Adds | Price in US dollars | Price in naira |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **+50 GB** | 50 GB | $10 a month | ₦15,000 a month |
| **+200 GB** | 200 GB | $30 a month | ₦45,000 a month |
| **+1 TB** | 1 TB | $100 a month | ₦150,000 a month |

You pay in your workspace's billing currency: naira if your workspace bills in naira, US dollars otherwise. Bettar Platforms, Inc. is the seller on the receipt.

- **Who can buy.** Only the clinic's **owner** can buy or cancel a pack. A manager sees the packs and is told to ask the owner.
- **Free plan.** Free has no packs. Upgrade your plan for more space.
- **One of each size.** You can hold a +50 GB, a +200 GB and a +1 TB pack together, but not two of the same size. For more than that, choose a larger plan.
- **Cancel any time.** A cancelled pack keeps working until the end of the month you paid for, and the Storage page shows **Ends on** with the date. After that your limit goes back down. Nothing is deleted, but if you are then over the limit, new uploads are refused until you free space or add storage.
- **If a renewal payment fails,** the pack keeps working for a short grace period and then ends. You can still select **Cancel pack** during that grace period.
- **A pack renews every month until it is cancelled.** If your workspace leaves its paid plan, each pack is cancelled for you and ends when the month you paid for does; you will not be charged for another month. While the workspace is on the Free plan the pack does not add space, it is not refunded, and it counts again if you return to a paid plan before it ends. An owner can also select **Cancel pack** on the Free plan to end it sooner than that cancellation would.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Storage">
    Open **Drive** and select **Storage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a pack">
    Under **Extra storage**, select **Buy** beside the pack you want. You pay on the secure payment page that opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Storage">
    You see **Waiting for the payment to be confirmed**, then the pack shows **Active** and your limit goes up. This usually takes under a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To cancel, select **Cancel pack** beside it and confirm.

## What counts

Everything stored counts:

- files in **Clinic Drive** and **My Drive**, and their earlier versions;
- patients' files, including documents patients upload themselves and files that arrive by fax;
- the documents you gave Ozni, which are counted once (when you stop sharing a file with Ozni, its copy is removed and is no longer stored);
- billing documents (superbills and kept copies of statements), faxes you have received and files you attached in a conversation with Ozni (counted, never listed in Drive);
- Team chat attachments and ClinikForms uploads.

A file that fails the virus check does not count. Reports and exports that expire on their own do not count.

## Notices

You are told when the workspace passes **80%** and **95%** of its limit, and when it is full: a notice in your bell, an email to the owner, and a banner above Drive ("Drive storage is filling up", "almost full", "full").

The **Storage** page also holds two lists for owners and managers. **Links** shows every secure link made in the clinic, with a **Stop** button and an export; see [Send a file outside the clinic](/platform/drive/secure-links). **Former staff** lists people who left with files still in My Drive. When someone leaves after this change with files in My Drive, owners and managers get a notification that opens **Former staff**, so the files can be moved to a colleague, to Clinic Drive or to Trash. Nothing is deleted.

## When it is full

- **Files someone chooses to upload are refused**, wherever they are added: in Drive, on a patient's chart, a file attached to a message in the client portal, an eligibility document, a saved whiteboard, a Medi-Cal file and a store download. The message reads "Your clinic's Drive storage is full, so this file was not added." and names who can free up space or change the plan. Nothing is stored when a file is refused.
- **A patient is never shown your storage or plan.** If a patient attaches a file to a portal message while your workspace is full, they read "This clinic can't receive files right now. Please contact the clinic." and their words stay in the box so they can send the message without the file.
- **Records your workspace must keep are still saved:** signed forms and consents, files attached to forms and to signing requests, and the insurance card and ID a patient adds on their Insurance Profile. They are counted, so your total can pass the limit.
- **Nothing is ever deleted to make room.**

To make space, move files you no longer need to Trash. A file in Trash keeps counting until it is removed for good after 30 days, so space is freed only then. Or [buy more storage](#buy-more-storage) (the owner) or change your plan.

<Note>
Patient files marked entered in error are kept for the full records-retention period and keep counting toward your storage.
</Note>

## Check it worked

The **Storage** page's **Storage used** bar moves after you remove files, buy a pack or change your plan, and the banner above Drive goes away. The **Subscription** tab of the Dashboard shows the same figures on its **Storage** row, including the space your packs add.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see the Storage page">
    The **Storage** page is for owners and managers by default. Ask one of them, or ask the owner to give you the manage-storage permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Uploads are refused but the page says there is space">
    The figure on the page can lag by a minute after a large upload or a plan change. Wait a moment and try again. If a file is larger than the limit allows for its type, the message says so.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I paid for a pack but it does not show">
    The pack appears once the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute: select **Check again** on the Storage page. If it still does not show after a few minutes, contact support with the date of the payment. A pack is only added for the owner of a workspace on a paid plan. If a payment cannot be turned into a pack (for example a pack of that size was already active), the subscription is cancelled and the payment is refunded in full.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I do not see Buy beside the packs">
    Only the owner sees **Buy** and **Cancel pack**. A manager sees the packs and is told to ask the owner, and a workspace on the Free plan is shown an upgrade instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We were over the limit on the day Drive started">
    Limits apply from the day Drive starts. Existing files stay and nothing is deleted, but new uploads are refused until the workspace is back under its limit. Owners are told beforehand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
