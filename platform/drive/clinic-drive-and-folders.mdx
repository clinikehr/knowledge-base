**Clinic Drive** is the shared shelf: everything the team needs to find. **My Drive** is your own desk: drafts and notes nobody else needs. Both use folders.

## Make a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Select **Clinic Drive** or **My Drive**. To make a folder inside another, open that folder first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select New folder">
    The **New folder** sheet opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a name under 120 characters in **Folder name**. A name that already exists in the same place is refused with "Something with that name already exists here."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    The folder appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload files

Select **Upload files**, or drop files onto the list. Each file is checked for viruses first. The **Uploads** panel shows each file's progress, and a file that fails the check is not added. If the workspace's storage is full the upload is refused; see [Storage and limits](/platform/drive/storage-and-limits).

To replace a file with a newer copy, open its menu and select **Upload a new version**. Earlier versions stay under **Earlier versions** in the file's details and count toward your storage.

## Find, sort and tidy

- **Search Drive** finds a file by name in the section you are in.
- **Filters** narrows by **Type**, **Label**, who uploaded it and the date added. **Sort by** orders the list by **Name**, **Modified**, **Size** or **Type**, and the view can be a list or a grid.
- **Star** a file or a folder to find it again under **Starred**. A star is yours alone. A folder you open from **Starred** opens where it lives, in **Clinic Drive**, **My Drive** or **Shared with me**.
- **Label** gives a file or a folder a colour label for yourself. Open the item's menu, select **Label**, then pick one of your labels or make a new one. The label shows on the item, and **Filters** > **Label** lists the files and folders that carry it. Select **Remove the label** to take it off.
- **Rename** and **Move** reorganise files and folders. In **Clinic Drive** these are for the people who may manage folders (owners and managers by default) and for the person who uploaded the file.
- **Details** shows the type, size, who uploaded it, the virus check result and earlier versions.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who by default | Controlled by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Drive and Clinic Drive | Everyone | Role |
| Upload, make folders in **My Drive** | Everyone | The upload permission |
| Make, rename, move **Clinic Drive** folders | Owners and managers | The manage-folders permission |
| Remove a **Clinic Drive** file | The person who uploaded it, owners and managers | Role |
| Place a legal hold | The owner | The legal-hold permission |

Permissions are set per person under **Settings**. A person the owner has not restricted can do whatever their role allows.

## Check it worked

The folder or file shows in the list, and you can reopen it after a refresh.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no New folder button in Clinic Drive">
    Creating Clinic Drive folders is for owners and managers by default. Ask one of them, or make the folder in **My Drive**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My file shows a virus-check message">
    A file that did not pass the check cannot be opened. Upload a clean copy. A file that shows the check could not finish can be uploaded again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't rename or move a file">
    In **Clinic Drive** only the person who uploaded a file, owners and managers can change it. In **Patients**, a file's name is fixed; change its type or visit instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
