Drive is the one place to find a file. It holds the files your whole team shares, the files only you can see, and every document filed to a patient. Open it from the sidebar, directly above **Forms**. In a hospital, that is in the **Records** group; if your hospital does not use the Records area, **Drive** is at the end of the first group instead.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Drive']} />

## What you see

A list of sections down the left (on a phone, a menu at the top) and the files of the section you chose on the right.

| Section | What it holds | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clinic Drive** | Files and folders the whole workspace shares: policies, contracts, templates | Everyone who works in the workspace. An owner or manager can see all of it |
| **My Drive** | Your own files and drafts | Only you. Owners and managers cannot browse another person's **My Drive** |
| **Patients** | Each patient's documents, grouped by what they are | The same people who can open that patient's record. See [File a patient's documents](/platform/drive/patient-files) |
| **Starred** | Files you starred | You |
| **Recent** | Files you opened lately | You |
| **Trash** | Files you or a colleague removed, kept for 30 days | See [Entered in error and Trash](/platform/drive/entered-in-error-and-trash) |
| **Storage** | How much space the workspace has used and where it goes | Owners and managers by default. See [Storage and limits](/platform/drive/storage-and-limits) |

Use **Search Drive** above the list to find a file by name. **Filters** narrows the list by type, label, who uploaded it and the date it was added.

<Note>
Drive never shows you a file you could not open somewhere else. A patient document you are not allowed to see in the patient's record does not appear in Drive, in a search, in **Starred** or in **Recent**.
</Note>

## What is in it on day one

Everything your workspace already stored is there: documents on patients' charts, files patients uploaded, and the documents you gave Ozni. Nothing was moved or copied; Drive lists what is already stored and counts it.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see Drive in the sidebar">
    Drive opens for every role. If the row is missing, your workspace's owner may have turned off your access to it; ask them, or contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file I expect is not in the list">
    Check the section first (a patient's file is under **Patients**, not **Clinic Drive**), then **Filters**: a file marked entered in error is hidden until you switch on **Show entered in error**. If you are not allowed to see the patient's record, their files do not appear either.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
