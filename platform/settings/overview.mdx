Settings is one page with a rail of tabs down the left — a dropdown at the top on a phone. Which tabs are in that rail is not the same for every workspace, so this page is the map: every tab that exists, who gets it, and what it controls.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings']} />

## The rule that explains a missing tab

Four things decide whether a tab is in your rail:

1. **Your edition** — Clinic & Hospital, Pharmacy or Diagnostics. See [The three editions](/start/editions).
2. **Your plan** — some tabs only exist on the enterprise plan, and a couple only on solo and team.
3. **Your [care areas](/platform/team/care-areas)** — on a hospital, switching a care area off removes its settings tab along with its navigation group.
4. **Your role** — owners can change everything on a tab; managers and staff see most tabs read-only or not at all. See [Roles and access](/start/roles-and-access).

<Note>
  **A link to a tab your workspace does not have opens General instead of an error.** So if you followed a link and landed somewhere unexpected, the tab does not exist for you — nothing is broken. Work down the four points above to find out which one is responsible.
</Note>

## The full catalogue

In the order they appear in the rail.

| Tab | Who sees it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **General** | Every workspace | Your name, address, phone, email, website, description, logo and timezone. See [Your business profile](/platform/settings/business-profile). |
| **Staff** | Every workspace | Add, edit and remove staff, and track pending invitations. On Clinic & Hospital it also lists invites waiting for seat payment. See [Invite staff](/platform/team/invite-staff). |
| **Role Navigation** | Clinic & Hospital, enterprise plan | Which modules each role is allowed to open. See [Roles](/platform/team/roles). |
| **Facility** | Clinic & Hospital, enterprise plan | Which departments this hospital runs — the care areas that control what appears in navigation. See [Care areas](/platform/team/care-areas). |
| **Theatre** | Clinic & Hospital, enterprise plan, with the theatre care area on | Your theatres, the surgical safety checklist, and whether an incomplete checklist blocks a case or only warns. |
| **Wards** | Clinic & Hospital, enterprise plan | Wards and the beds in them. |
| **Departments** | Clinic & Hospital, enterprise plan | The clinical and administrative departments staff are assigned to. |
| **Billing** | Every workspace | Free Clinic Mode, currency, tax rate and flat rate fee. See [Currency, tax and free-clinic mode](/platform/settings/currency). |
| **Online Payments** | Every workspace | Connect Stripe or Paystack so you can take money from patients or customers. See [Payments](/clinic/revenue/payments). |
| **Payment Details** | Every workspace | Bank transfer, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal and similar channels, printed on your invoices. |
| **Auto Pay** | Clinic & Hospital | Charging a card on file automatically. The feature inside the tab needs Essential or above. |
| **Loyalty** | Pharmacy | Reward repeat customers with automatic rebates. |
| **Inventory** | Any workspace with the inventory care area on | Expiry alerts, dispensing enforcement and inventory data controls. |
| **Insurance** | Clinic & Hospital, solo and team plans | Electronic claims setup. See [Insurance](/clinic/insurance/overview). |
| **Security** | Every workspace | Require two-factor authentication, manage passkeys, and turn on security alerts. See [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa). |
| **Notifications** | Every workspace | Which in-app notices and emails your workspace receives. See [Notification and email preferences](/platform/settings/notifications). |
| **Client Portal** | Clinic & Hospital, Diagnostics | Turning the portal on, its features and its branding. See [Enable the portal](/portal/setup/enable). |
| **Sharable Documents** | Clinic & Hospital, Diagnostics | Which documents are automatically sent to clients during appointments. |
| **Lab Workflow** | Diagnostics | Accessioning and result rules, plus **Report Branding** for the signature block on lab reports. |
| **Calendar Integrations** | Clinic & Hospital | Connected Google and Outlook accounts, and read-only calendar subscriptions. See [Connect an external calendar](/platform/integrations/calendar). |
| **Data Export** | Every workspace | Export workspace data as CSV or ZIP. Large exports run in the background. See [Export your data](/platform/security/data-export). |
| **Appointment Services** | Clinic & Hospital | The services patients can be booked for. See [Services](/clinic/revenue/services). |
| **Booking Page Designer** | Clinic & Hospital | The look, content and forms of your public booking page. See [Your booking page](/clinic/front-desk/booking-page). |
| **Contact Form** | Clinic & Hospital | Your public enquiry form, its fields and its website embed. See [Contacts](/clinic/front-desk/contacts). |
| **Communications** | Clinic & Hospital | Which channels are enabled, messaging and voice, and connecting a Gmail or Outlook mailbox. |
| **AI Settings** | Clinic & Hospital | External tool connectors for your assistants, and the agent webhook trigger. See [AI agents](/platform/ai/agents). |
| **Membership Page** | Clinic & Hospital | Your public membership signup page. See [Memberships](/clinic/revenue/memberships). |
| **Store Page** | Clinic & Hospital | Your public storefront. See [Your store](/clinic/revenue/store). |
| **Booking Settings** | Clinic & Hospital | Availability, schedules, special dates, resources and per-provider booking policies. |

## Tabs most people are looking for

| You want to change | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| The name and address on your invoices | **General** |
| The currency prices are shown in | **Billing** |
| Who can open which screen | **Staff**, then **Role Navigation** on a hospital |
| Whether staff must use two-factor | **Security** |
| Emails your workspace sends and receives | **Notifications** |
| The look of a page patients or customers see | The tab for that page — see [Branding your public pages](/platform/settings/branding) |
| Serving a page on your own web address | The same tab, under **Custom domain** — see [Use your own domain](/platform/settings/custom-domain) |
