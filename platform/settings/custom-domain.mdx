Five of your public pages can be served on a domain you own, so a patient books at `book.yourclinic.com` rather than at a ClinikEHR address. You add two DNS records, and the security certificate is issued for you.

## What can have a domain

| Surface | Example | Set it up on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking page | `book.yourclinic.com` | <Path steps={['Settings', 'Booking Page Designer', 'Design']} /> |
| Store | `shop.yourclinic.com` | <Path steps={['Settings', 'Store Page']} /> |
| Membership page | `membership.yourclinic.com` | <Path steps={['Settings', 'Membership Page']} /> |
| Contact form | `inquire.yourclinic.com` | <Path steps={['Settings', 'Contact Form']} /> |
| Forms | `forms.yourclinic.com` | Open any form, select **Share**, then **Use your own domain** |
| Published AI assistant | `assistant.yourclinic.com` | <Path steps={['Agents', 'Public UIs']} /> |

One domain per surface, and each surface has its own **Custom domain** card. Forms are the one surface with many pages: a forms domain serves every published form at its own address — `forms.yourclinic.com/` followed by the part of the form's link after `/f/` — and **Link on your own domain** appears in **Share** once the domain is active. A forms domain only ever shows your own clinic's forms. Four separate subdomains for four pages is normal and expected.

<Note>
  **The patient portal does not support custom domains.** It is always served on the ClinikEHR portal address, and there is no setting to change that — so there is nothing to hunt for on the **Client Portal** tab. Everything else about the portal, including its branding, is yours to set: see [Portal branding](/portal/setup/branding).
</Note>

## Connect a domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide the subdomain">
    Use a subdomain — `book.yourclinic.com`, not `yourclinic.com`. Most registrars cannot point a bare domain at anything but their own hosting, and the card says as much.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the surface's settings and find Custom domain">
    The card is headed **Custom domain** — "Serve this page on a domain you own". Enter your hostname and select **Connect domain**. **Set up DNS for** your domain opens straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the ownership record at your DNS provider">
    The list shows three lines: **Ownership**, **Routing** and **Security certificate**. Add **Ownership** first. Each record has a **Type**, a **Host**, a **Full name** and a **Value**, each with its own copy button — most registrars want the **Host** (the part before your domain), a few want the **Full name**. Paste the **Value** exactly. This proves the domain is yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the routing record">
    Add **Routing**, again copying **Host** and **Value**. This is what points visitors at your page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Verify now, or just wait">
    Changes can take from a few minutes up to 48 hours to appear. You do not have to keep the page open: we check on our own every few minutes while the domain is being connected, and add a notice to your bell when it is live. Each line shows **Verified**, **Not found** (nothing published there yet), **Wrong value** (something is published, and the list shows what we found instead) or **Not checked yet**, so you can see exactly which record is missing. The sheet also says when it was last checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the certificate">
    **Security certificate** has nothing for you to add. It reads **Not checked yet** until both records above are right, then moves to **Verified** once the certificate is issued — usually minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it reads Live">
    When the badge says **Live**, an **Open** button appears beside the hostname. Select it, and your page loads on your own address over HTTPS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seeing the records again

Select **DNS records** on the card at any time — while the domain is being connected, once it is **Live**, and after it has been suspended. If you move to a different registrar, open it there and add the records again. A live domain is re-checked every hour to make sure it keeps working; if a record is removed you get a notice, and the domain comes back by itself once the records are right again.

## The status ladder

The badge on the card tells you exactly where you are.

| Badge | Means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting for ownership record** | The ownership record is not visible yet | Add it from **DNS records**, then **Verify now** |
| **Waiting for routing record** | Ownership is proved; routing is not in place | Add the routing record, then **Verify now** |
| **Issuing certificate** | Both records are good; the certificate is being issued | Wait. Usually minutes |
| **Live** | Your page is being served on your domain | Nothing |
| **Needs attention** | Setup failed. The reason is shown under the badge | Read the reason, fix the record, **Verify now**. Automatic checks are paused until you do |
| **Suspended** | A record was removed or the plan changed, so the domain stopped serving. The reason is shown, and you are told in the bell and by email | Restore the record from **DNS records**; it comes back by itself within the hour, or press **Verify now** |

**Nothing is served on your domain until the badge reads Live.** Until then your ClinikEHR address is the working link, and it keeps working afterwards too.

## Removing a domain

Select **Remove** on the card. The confirmation says what happens: the page stops opening on that domain straight away, its ClinikEHR address keeps working, the DNS records at your registrar are not changed (delete them there if you no longer need them), and for 30 days no other account can connect that domain. The card goes back to **Connect domain** immediately, and anywhere else that offered the link on your domain — the **Share** panel for forms — stops offering it. If someone else has the same screen open, it updates when they next return to it.

## Check it worked

- The badge on the **Custom domain** card reads **Live**.
- **Open** loads your page on your own address, with a padlock in the browser.
- The page looks like your ClinikEHR page — same branding, same content.
- Your original ClinikEHR link still works. It always does.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I added the records and it still says Waiting">
    DNS changes take time to spread — usually minutes, occasionally a few hours, and up to 48 in the worst case. Select **Verify now** occasionally rather than removing and re-adding the domain, which restarts the clock.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify now keeps failing after several hours">
    Almost always a typo or a duplicated hostname. Check three things at your DNS provider: the record **name** matches what the card shows exactly; the **value** was pasted with **Copy value** and not retyped; and your provider has not silently appended your domain to a name that already contained it — `book.yourclinic.com.yourclinic.com` is the classic version of this.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Custom domain card on the tab">
    Custom domains are part of the **Team** plan and above. On a lower plan the card shows an upgrade prompt instead of the form. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to use my bare domain, without a subdomain">
    Use a subdomain. Most registrars cannot point a bare domain at an external service, and doing it usually breaks your main website's mail records as a side effect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The browser warns the connection is not private">
    The certificate is not issued yet — the badge will still read **Issuing certificate**. Wait for **Live** before sharing the address with anyone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It says Needs attention">
    The reason is printed under the badge in red. Fix the record it names and select **Verify now**. If the reason is not something you can act on, [contact support](/platform/help/contact-support) with the hostname.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I point two surfaces at the same hostname?">
    No — one domain per surface, and a hostname is claimed once. Give each page its own subdomain.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
