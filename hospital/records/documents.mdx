**eSignatures** is your hospital's full signature ledger: everything sent out for signature and what it is doing now, newest first, in one list. Where [Records](/hospital/records/overview) shows only what needs a person today, this shows all of it.

It is a tab of [Forms](/hospital/records/forms). It used to be two places — a **Signed documents** tab, and before that a **Documents** entry in the sidebar. Both are part of **eSignatures** now, the links patients hold still work, and old links to either open this tab.

## What you're looking at

Each row is marked with what it is: **Signature request** — prepared with the signing builder, and selecting it opens its tracking page (see [Prepare a document for signature](/practice/forms/prepare-a-document-for-signature)) — or **Document**, a consent form or agreement sent from one of your templates with the earlier signing links.

Every row has the same status pill:

| Status | What it means for a document |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Sent, not yet opened |
| **In progress** | The patient has opened it but not signed |
| **Completed** | Signed — the signed copy is on their record |
| **Declined** | The patient refused to sign |
| **Voided** | You withdrew it |
| **Expired** | The link ran out before they signed |

**Empty state.** A hospital that has sent nothing shows "Nothing has been sent for signature yet." with **Send a document for signature**.

## Before you start

Two prerequisites, and both stop you dead rather than failing quietly:

- **An active template must exist.** Templates are kept in **Settings → Patient Experience → Sharable Documents**.
- **For a document from one of your templates, the patient must have an email address on file.** The chart warns "⚠️ This patient does not have an email address. Please update their profile before sending documents.", and sending is refused with "Selected patient does not have an email address". A form or a signature request can still reach a patient with no address — through their portal, or in person on this device.

## Send a document for signature

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the patient">
    Open [Patient records](/hospital/records/patient-records), find the person, and open the row's **⋯** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send to patient">
    The **Send intakes** sheet opens — the same one the patient's own record uses — on its first step, with the patient's name and the address it will go to. Check that address before going further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to send">
    One list, in groups: **Packs**, **Forms** (published forms that go to a patient or by private link) and **Signable documents** (ready to send, with a signer and at least one field) when your hospital has any, then **Consent documents**, **Clinical outcomes & measures**, **Questionnaires & intakes**, and **Clinical templates** when you have your own. Tick as many as you need. The templates you tick go in one email together; each form and each document to sign arrives as its own invitation. Anything the patient already has open is marked, so the same consent does not go out twice. A signature request that someone else signs as well — a witness, a clinician — asks on the next step who signs each part: **A specific staff member**, **A named person outside the clinic**, or **The appointment's provider** when you started from an appointment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context">
    **Continue to email** takes you to **Compose email**, where the subject and message are yours to change. Say why they are being asked to sign: a consent form arriving with no explanation is a consent form that does not get signed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    **Review & confirm**, then send. The patient gets one email with their own links, and each document appears on **eSignatures** at **Sent**. The confirmation names what went and what did not — an item that was skipped, and why, is said rather than counted as sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow it up

On **eSignatures**, a **Document** row's **⋯** menu offers:

<Steps>
  <Step title="View">
    What was sent, when, and where it has got to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend">
    The document opens with the patient and its content filled in; check them and select **Send Document**. It goes out again as a new document with its own link, and the earlier link keeps working until it expires or is revoked from the patient's record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    A PDF of the document, marked as not yet signed when it is not. Not offered on a cancelled document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **A template your hospital has converted to a form is sent as that form.** Once a template has been converted with **Convert to form** and the form published, it can no longer be sent as a document — not from **Resend**, the **Send intakes** sheet, an assistant or an appointment default — and you are told which form replaces it. Templates that have not been converted keep sending as before.
</Note>

## Who can do this

**Role** decides whether the ledger opens: owners, managers, doctors, nurses and receptionists pass, and anyone else is told they do not have permission. **Forms** needs the **Records** care area in **Settings → Facility**, and on Enterprise an owner controls which roles see the **Records** group under **Settings → Role Navigation**.

Inside, what each person may do is decided by the Forms permissions in their staff permissions, plus your plan:

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the ledger and read it | Owners, managers, doctors, nurses, receptionists | Role, and the **View forms** permission |
| **Send to patient** | The same people | The **Create and edit forms** permission, and your plan — the controls are inert on Free |
| Send, remind, correct or void a signature request | The same people | The **Send documents for signature** permission |
| **Resend** a document | The same people | Role, and your plan |
| **Download** | The same people | Role |
| Prepare a document for signature, or create or edit a template | The same people | The **Create and edit forms** permission, and your plan |

Enforcement is **opt-in per person** — a colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"** before it starts applying. A **denied action is recorded**; an allowed one is not. Hiding **Forms** from a role is a convenience, not a security boundary.

## Check it worked

- The document is on **eSignatures**, marked **Document**, at **Sent**.
- When the patient opens it the status becomes **In progress**; when they sign it becomes **Completed** and the signed copy is on their chart.
- A resent document is a new row at the top of the list.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Converted to the form — send the form instead">
    That template has been converted to a published form, so it is no longer sent as a document. Send the form instead. An appointment default still naming the template sends nothing until an owner or manager chooses **Use this form here** in **Settings → Patient Experience → Sharable Documents**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient does not have an email address">
    Documents from your templates are delivered by email. Add the address to their record, then send — see [Patient records](/hospital/records/patient-records). A form or a signature request can instead go **Portal only — no email** when the patient has portal access, or be completed **In person, on this device**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient says they never got the email">
    Ask them to check spam first. If it is genuinely missing, confirm the address on their record, revoke access to the outstanding document from their record and send a new one. A patient with portal access can also open it from the portal without the email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The list looks shorter than I expected">
    **eSignatures** shows ten rows a page, newest first. Use the page controls under the list, or show more rows per page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload a file I already have and send that for signature?">
    Yes. On **eSignatures**, select **Send a document for signature**, then **Upload a new document** — see [Prepare a document for signature](/practice/forms/prepare-a-document-for-signature).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does a signed copy go?">
    Onto the patient's record, in their files. Nothing needs filing by hand — that is what keeps the **Unfiled** queue on the records desk short.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send the same document to several patients at once?">
    Not from one action. Documents are sent per patient, because each one carries that patient's own signing link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a signing link last?">
    Long enough for a reasonable reply, then it expires — and your hospital's expiring-within threshold, set under **Settings → Facility**, decides when the records desk starts flagging it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a declined document need anything from me?">
    Not in the product. A refusal to sign is a clinical and administrative conversation, not a status to clear — decide with the clinician what happens next.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
