**Marketing** is where you build and send campaign emails — a newsletter, a recall reminder, a re-engagement email to lapsed patients — to a chosen group of people, with unsubscribe, consent and safe-sending handled for you.

## What you're looking at

Five tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaigns** | Every campaign you have created, with its status and a quick funnel |
| **Templates** | Ready-made starter designs, and any you have saved yourself |
| **Segments** | Saved groups of contacts, with a live count of who currently matches |
| **Journeys** | Automated email sequences that run on their own once turned on |
| **Analytics** | Results across your campaigns |

**New campaign** sits at the top of the **Campaigns** tab. Every campaign carries a status: **Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Paused**, **Cancelled** or **Failed** — a paused campaign always names why (for example, a bounce or complaint rate that tripped a safety pause), so **Paused** is never a mystery.

**This page keeps itself up to date.** While a campaign is scheduled, sending or paused, its status and its figures move on their own — including a campaign somebody else started, and a safety pause nobody chose. You do not need to reload to see a send progress, and you will not miss a pause because you were looking at the screen when it happened.

## Understand who you can email

Before anything else, Marketing asks who this campaign is for, because a hospital's marketing rules are not the same as an ordinary business's. There are three audiences, and the product treats them differently on purpose:

| Audience | Who it is | The rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Leads and contacts** | People in your CRM who are not patients — enquiries, prospects, your waitlist | Ordinary marketing consent: they opted in, and they can opt out |
| **Patients — health-care operations** | Your own patients, about the care you provide them — a flu-shot drive, a recall reminder, a new service | Allowed without a signed marketing form; opt-out is always honored |
| **Patients — authorized marketing** | True marketing to patients — a third-party product, anything you are paid to promote | Requires a signed marketing authorization on file for every recipient before you can send |

Pick the audience when you start a campaign, and it cannot be changed once the campaign has sent — it is part of the record of what you sent and why. Choosing **Patients — authorized marketing** without confirming the authorization blocks sending until you check the box confirming a signed form is on file for everyone in the audience.

Open tracking (whether the product records that someone opened your email) defaults **off** for both patient audiences and **on** for leads — because whether a patient opened an email is itself the kind of thing a hospital should not collect without thinking about it first. Click tracking always stays on for every audience, since a click is something someone chose to do.

No campaign is ever sent to someone who has unsubscribed or asked not to be contacted, regardless of which audience or segment you choose.

## Build a segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Segments">
    Select the **Segments** tab, then start a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions">
    Filter by field, stage, tag, source or owner. As you add and adjust conditions, a live recipient count updates beneath them — it recalculates as you type, and shows a loading shimmer rather than an old number while it does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Name the segment and save it. It is now reusable across any campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a condition cannot be evaluated by the sending system, you are told so directly rather than being shown a narrower count than what would actually be emailed — a segment that cannot be fully applied will not silently send to more people than the count on screen.

## Create and send a campaign

The composer is a checklist. Each section shows a green check once it is complete, or exactly what is missing — and the **Send** action only lights up once every row is green.

<Steps>
  <Step title="To — choose the audience">
    Pick a saved segment. The recipient count appears here too, and a campaign is blocked from sending if nobody in the segment can currently be emailed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From — pick your sending address">
    Choose a verified sending identity for this hospital.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subject">
    Write a subject line, or turn on A/B testing (Essential plan and above) to try two or more subject lines against a portion of the audience before the rest goes out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Content">
    Add blocks — heading, text, image, button, divider, spacer — or start from a template. A preview shows exactly what will send, on both desktop and mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Send now, or pick a date and time in the future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    A review screen states plainly what is about to happen — who receives it, from which address, and roughly when it will finish — before you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Before **Send** unlocks, the checklist also runs a set of pre-send checks: an **Unsubscribe link** present in the email, a **Spam score**, that every **Link** in the email resolves and uses a secure address, that every **Merge tag** (like a first name) will actually fill in for the audience, a required **Test send** to your own address, a check of **Volume vs sending history** (a brand-new hospital workspace cannot blast its whole list on day one), and confirmation that **Automatic safety pause** is armed. Every row explains itself and tells you how to fix it if it is not green yet.

## Templates

**Starter templates** are ready to use and never count against your plan — duplicate one and edit it. Your own saved designs sit alongside them, with a running count like **3 of 5 saved**. At your plan's limit, saving offers to replace an existing template rather than leaving you stuck.

## Automations (Journeys)

A journey is a sequence that runs on its own once activated — an email, then a wait, then another email, optionally branching on what a contact does. From the **Journeys** tab you can see each one's live enrollment count, and **Activate** or pause it; a confirmation names exactly what activating it does before it starts. Someone who unsubscribes exits every journey they are in immediately, and a contact in several journeys at once still never receives more than the same overall sending limit applied to campaigns.

## Read your results

**Analytics**, and each campaign's own report, lead with **Opened (filtered)** and **Clicked (filtered)** — filtered out are opens caused by email apps automatically pre-loading images, and clicks from automated security scanners, neither of which reflect a real person. A **Show raw numbers** toggle reveals the unfiltered totals beside them, labeled honestly rather than presented as if they were the same thing. Reports also show unsubscribes, and — because the point of a campaign is what it leads to, not just who opened it — how many recipients went on to book an appointment.

## Who can do this

| Action | Who can do it by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View campaigns and reports | Staff granted access | Permission |
| Write and edit campaigns, templates and segments | Staff granted access | Permission |
| Send a campaign | Owner, manager, or staff explicitly granted it | Permission |
| Turn an automation on or off | Owner, manager, or staff explicitly granted it | Permission |

Writing a campaign and sending it are deliberately separate permissions — drafting is cheap and reversible, and firing a campaign at your whole patient list is not, so the product does not conflate the two.

## Check it worked

- A sent campaign moves from **Scheduled**/**Sending** to **Sent** in the **Campaigns** list, on its own, without reloading the page.
- Its report starts filling in with delivered, opened and clicked figures shortly after.
- A test send you asked for arrives at your own address, rendered exactly as the real campaign will look.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Send stays greyed out">
    Open the checklist — every red row names what is missing and how to fix it. **Send** never unlocks until all of them are green.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My segment shows fewer people than I expected">
    The count only includes people who can currently be emailed under this audience's consent rules — someone who has unsubscribed or opted out is excluded from every count, everywhere.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A campaign is Paused and I didn't pause it">
    An automatic safety pause tripped — usually a bounce or complaint rate above the safe threshold. The reason is shown on the campaign; review it before resuming.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't send to patients about a paid product">
    That needs the **Patients — authorized marketing** audience, which requires confirming a signed authorization is on file for every recipient before it will send.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New campaigns are refused for the month">
    You are at your plan's monthly send limit. Unused sends roll forward once, so check next month, or see [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will a campaign ever reach someone who unsubscribed?">
    No. The unsubscribe and do-not-contact rules are checked again at the moment of sending, not just when you build the segment — so someone who unsubscribes after you schedule a campaign still will not receive it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a campaign and Contacts' old Campaigns tab?">
    Marketing is the current, built-out campaign system — audience classes, the checklist, templates, journeys and full reporting. It is where new campaigns belong.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit the individual steps inside a journey?">
    Not yet — you can view a journey's steps, and activate or pause it, but building a new step sequence is not available from this screen yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does raising my plan change what happens to contacts I already have?">
    No. Contacts are never limited by plan, on any tier. Plan only changes how many sends, templates and active automations you have available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
