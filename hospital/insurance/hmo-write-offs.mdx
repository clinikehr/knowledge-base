Sometimes an HMO rejects a line for good. A **write-off** closes what it will not pay so the claim stops showing as owed. It is permanent, it needs a reason, and it is recorded against the person who made it.

## Write off a balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the claim's balance">
    On **HMO receivables**, select **View claims** for the insurer, then **Write off** beside the claim. You can also choose **Write off balance** from a claim's **Actions** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and the reason">
    **Amount to write off** starts at what is owed; you can write off less. **Reason** is required — say why it will not be collected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Write off**. You are told **Written off** and the balance is reduced at once — unless your hospital asks for a second person to approve it, below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You cannot write off more than the insurer owes. A claim with a write-off already waiting for approval cannot take a payment or another write-off until it is decided.

## Ask for a second approval above an amount

By default the person who may write off a balance does it on their own. To make larger ones wait for someone else, an owner or manager opens **Approving write-offs** on **HMO receivables**, switches on **Require a second person for larger write-offs** and sets **Needs approval above**. A write-off up to that amount still applies at once; above it, the button reads **Send for approval**.

A waiting write-off **changes nothing** on the claim. It appears under **Write-offs** as **Waiting for approval**. A person who may approve it, and who is **not the person who asked**, sees **Approve** and **Refuse**:

- **Approve** applies the write-off. It cannot be undone. If the claim's balance has changed since the request, you are told and the request must be refused and asked again.
- **Refuse** leaves the claim as it was.

You can add a note to either.

## Who can do this

| Action | Who by default | The permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write off, or ask to write off | Owners, managers | **Write off HMO balances** |
| Approve or refuse | Owners, managers | **Approve HMO write-offs** |
| Turn second approval on or off, set the amount | Owners, managers | — |

Both permissions are off for everyone else until an owner switches them on for a person in their [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions) sheet. The person who asks can never be the person who approves.

## Check it worked

- The claim reads **Paid** or **Part paid** with the written-off amount no longer owed — or **Written off** if nothing was paid.
- Under **Write-offs**, the request reads **Written off**, with your reason.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Write off is not offered">
    You may not have the permission, or the claim is not submitted, is denied or is already settled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I asked for a write-off and nothing changed">
    It is waiting for a second person to approve it. It will show **Waiting for approval** under **Write-offs**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Approve is missing on my own request">
    A different person must approve it. Ask another owner, manager or person who has been given **Approve HMO write-offs**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
