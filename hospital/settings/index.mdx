Settings is where the hospital's shape is decided — which units exist, who may open what, how money is taken and what patients see. This page is the map: the five sections, who can open them, and the tabs that exist only on Enterprise.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings']} />

## If a tab you were sent is not there

Settings deep-links carry the tab in the address, like `?tab=facility`. If your workspace does not have that tab, the page opens on **General Settings** instead of showing an error. So a link that "does nothing" has almost always landed on General, and the tab genuinely does not exist for this hospital — check the plan, the edition and the care area before you go looking for a bug.

## Getting in, and who can

**Settings** sits in the footer of the main sidebar, below the modules. Selecting it opens the settings area with its own left rail titled **Clinic Settings**. On a phone or a narrow window that rail collapses into a dropdown at the top of the page; the tabs and their order are identical.

You need to be an **owner**, a **manager** or an **accountant**. Anyone else gets: "You don't have permission to access the settings module. Please contact your clinic administrator."

Getting in is not the whole story. Some controls are **owner-only** even for a manager — **Currency**, **Tax Rate** and **Free Clinic Mode** among them. **Facility**, **Theatre** and **Role Navigation** need owner or manager to change anything; an accountant can open them but not edit. Where a card is read-only for you it says so at the bottom: "Only the clinic owner or a manager can change these."

## The five sections

The rail is banded into five sections, and the band headings are the same words on every screen.

| Section | Covers | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PRACTICE** | The hospital profile, its care areas, units, wards, theatres, staff, inventory rules and notifications | [Facility](/hospital/settings/facility) · [Departments, wards and theatres](/hospital/settings/departments-wards-theatre) · [Staff and roles](/hospital/settings/team-and-roles) |
| **BILLING** | Subscription and plan, currency and tax, payment processing, memberships, services and the store | [Billing and payments](/hospital/settings/billing-and-payments) |
| **PATIENT EXPERIENCE** | Scheduling, the booking page, the contact form, the patient portal and sharable documents | [Booking, portal and documents](/hospital/settings/patient-experience) |
| **AUTOMATION & AI** | Messaging channels, AI agents and credits | [Communications and AI](/hospital/settings/automation-and-ai) |
| **SYSTEM** | Security rules and data export | [Security, data export and inventory](/hospital/settings/system) |

The first band is headed **PRACTICE** even for a hospital — the heading follows the edition rather than the plan, so a 300-bed site and a two-room practice both read the same word there. Nothing behind it is scaled down.

## What Enterprise has that Solo and Team do not

This is the part worth reading before you go hunting. An Enterprise hospital gets **five tabs that no other plan has**, four of them in a rail group the sidebar calls **Departments settings**.

| Tab | Sidebar wording | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Facility** | "Care areas, shifts, nursing & records bands" | Which units this hospital runs, plus the observation, shift, waiting and chase thresholds |
| **Theatre** | "Theatres, safety checklist & enforcement" | Your theatres and the surgical safety checklist rule |
| **Ward Management** | "Inpatient and ward setup" | Wards, sub-wards and bed spaces |
| **Departments** | "Clinic service departments" | Departments, sub-units and consultation rates |
| **Role Navigation** | "What each role sees in the sidebar" | Which modules each role may open, and where they land |

**Theatre needs one more thing.** It only appears once the **Surgical theatre** care area is on, and that area ships **off**. If four of the five are showing and Theatre is not, that is why — turn the area on in [Facility](/hospital/settings/facility) first.

### And one tab Enterprise loses

**Insurance Settings** exists on **Solo and Team only**. The two plans run different insurance systems, and a hospital's payers, coverage, pre-authorisations and claims live in their own module rather than in a settings tab — see [Insurance](/hospital/insurance/overview). Nothing is missing; it moved.

Two other tabs never appear here either, and neither is a fault: **Loyalty** belongs to the Pharmacy edition, and **Lab Workflow** to the Diagnostics edition.

## The rail, top to bottom

**PRACTICE** — *Profile*: **General Settings**. *Departments settings*: **Facility**, **Theatre**, **Ward Management**, **Departments**. *Team*: **Staff & Teams**, **Role Navigation**. *Inventory*: **Inventory**. *Practice notifications*: **Notifications**.

**BILLING** — *Practice Billing*: **Billing & Plans**. *Payment processing*: **Online Payments**, **Payment Details**, **Auto Pay**, **Memberships**. *Services and products*: **Appointment Services**, **Store Page**.

**PATIENT EXPERIENCE** — *Scheduling*: **Calendar Integrations**, **Booking Settings**, **Booking Page Designer**, **Contact Form**. *Patient portal*: **Patient Portal**. *Documents*: **Sharable Documents**.

**AUTOMATION & AI** — **Communications**, **AI Settings**.

**SYSTEM** — **Security**, **Data Export**.

Two entries here are conditional. **Inventory** shows only while the **Inventory** care area is on, and **Calendar Integrations** shows only for owners and managers.
