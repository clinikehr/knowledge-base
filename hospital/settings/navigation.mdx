**Navigation** lets you tidy the menu for everyone in your clinic: turn off sections you never use and move the ones you use most to the top. Names stay exactly as they are — you change only what shows and in what order.

## What this tab controls

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section switch | Shows or hides that section in the sidebar and in the phone menu | All on, except **Outreach Events**, which starts off (Enterprise plan) |
| Page switches under a section | Shows or hides a single page inside it, such as **Triage List**. When every page under a section is off, the section is left out too | All on |
| **Up** and **Down** buttons | Move a section one place within its group. Sections stay inside their own group | The standard order |
| **Menu preview** | Shows the menu as it will look before you save, before role access is applied | — |
| **Save** | Saves your choices. Disabled until you change something | — |
| **Reset to standard navigation** | Brings every section back, in the standard order, after you confirm. **Outreach Events** goes back off | — |

### Patients and Patient Records (optional)

On an Enterprise plan the top of this tab has a card, **Patients and Patient Records**, with a switch, **Separate Patients from Patient Records**. It is **off** until you turn it on, and nothing about your menu or your pages changes while it is off. Turning it on gives the front desk and the clinical team two pages:

- **Patients** keeps names, contact details, insurance, forms and consents, the patient's team chat, and a simple **Has allergies** or **No allergies on file** tag. It no longer shows the medical profile, clinical documents, recent activity, blood type or the Analytics tab.
- **Patient Records** holds the medical profile, clinical documents, recent activity and analytics, and gets its own box in [Role navigation](/hospital/settings/role-navigation).

By default doctors, nurses, records officers and lab and radiology staff can open Patient Records; the receptionist and the biller cannot. Owners and managers always can. A role whose navigation you had already customised keeps its choices, with Patient Records added for the clinical roles. Turning the switch off puts the two back on one page.

<Warning>
  **What this does and does not protect today.** Separating the pages, the menu and the Patient Records page (which checks who may open it) are in place. Restricting the underlying clinical records inside the system follows in a later update, so treat this as a working separation, not a complete lock. It also only narrows who sees what once **Role-based navigation** is on in [Role navigation](/hospital/settings/role-navigation); with it off, every role can still open both pages. The card tells you this and, if you are the owner, offers to switch it on.
</Warning>

### Show under: Front desk or Records

When the **Front desk** and **Records** departments are both on in [Facility](/hospital/settings/facility), a few rows get a **Show under** menu: **Patients**, **Patient Records**, **Forms**, **Contacts**, **Front Desk** and **Records**. Choose **Show under Front desk** or **Show under Records** to move that row into the other group, then select **Save**. No other section can be moved. If either department is off, the menu is not offered and your choice, once made, comes back when both are on again. **Reset to standard navigation** puts every row back where it started.

Moving a row only changes the heading it sits under in the menu and in Role navigation. It never changes who may open the page.

### Departments

Departments you switch off in **Facility** (for example theatre or in-patient care) hide all of their menu items. Those items still appear on this tab, greyed, with a **Turn on in Facility settings** link. This tab only fine-tunes items inside departments that are on, and it never changes Facility. If you turn a department back on, the choices you made for its items come back with it.

### Outreach Events starts off

**Outreach Events** is an Enterprise feature that the standard menu keeps off. It sits under **Front Desk** in the list, with its switch off and the note **Off until you turn it on**. Switch it on and select **Save** to add it to the menu for everyone; switch it off, or use **Reset to standard navigation**, to take it away again. It needs the **Front desk** care area. See [Outreach events](/hospital/front-desk/outreach-events).

Some entries are locked and show **Always shown**: Dashboard. They are where people land, so they cannot be turned off.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Team**, then **Navigation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off what you do not use">
    Switch a section off. To hide just one page, switch off that page under its section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the busiest sections first">
    Use **Up** and **Down** beside a section. Check the **Menu preview** as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. The sidebar and the phone menu update straight away for you, and for everyone else the next time their page loads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

- **Everyone in the clinic sees the same menu.** This is a clinic-wide choice, not a personal one.
- **Hidden means hidden from the menu only.** A section you turn off can still be opened from a link or a bookmark, so nobody loses work they have open or a link someone sent them.
- **Moving a row is not permission.** Placing Patient Records under Front Desk does not give the front desk access to it, and hiding or moving a row never takes access away.
- **Who may open what is unchanged.** Role navigation still decides which roles can open which pages, and each role still lands where it did.
- **Departments stay in charge.** A department switched off in Facility hides its items whatever you choose here.
- **Names do not change.** You cannot rename sections here.
- **Get Started** appears at the top while your setup is incomplete, whatever you choose.
- **Settings** and the **Audit Log** are never affected.

## Check it worked

Look at the sidebar: the sections you turned off are gone and the rest follow your order. On a phone, open the menu and compare. Open a section you hid by its link — it still opens.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see this tab">
    Only the clinic owner or a manager sees **Navigation**. Ask one of them to make the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A section I turned off still opens">
    That is by design. Turning a section off hides it from the menu; it does not block the page. To stop someone opening a page, use their access settings instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I turned off every page under a section and the section disappeared">
    A section with nothing under it would be an empty row, so it is left out. Turn one page back on to bring it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A switch is greyed out">
    Either the entry is locked (**Always shown**), or its department is switched off in Facility settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Save is greyed out">
    You have not changed anything yet, or your role cannot change the menu.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
