This is where your hospital's staff rota lives: who is working, when, and what happens when someone needs cover. It sits inside Settings rather than its own page, next to **Staff & Teams**.

<Path steps={['Settings', 'Team', 'Shifts']} />

## What this tab controls

| Section | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rota** | The week or month grid, by person or by department or ward. Click a slot to assign a shift, or a shift to edit it. On a phone this becomes a day-by-day list instead of a squeezed grid. | Everyone (edit needs a permission — see below) |
| **Shift types** | Reusable templates like "Day 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM", including overnight shifts. Repeating patterns generate real shifts from a template on a schedule you set (e.g. every Monday, Wednesday and Friday). | Managing needs a permission |
| **Cover & swaps** | Requests to swap or cover a shift: a colleague accepts, then a manager approves before the shift actually changes hands. | Everyone with a stake in the request; approving needs a permission |
| **Coverage rules** | A minimum headcount for a department, ward or role during a time window — the rota flags any window that falls short, and highlights a person booked onto two overlapping shifts. | Managing needs a permission |

A staff member who cannot manage the rota sees a simple **My shifts** view instead: their own upcoming shifts, a **Request swap or cover** button on each, and the requests they are part of.

## Assign a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Rota tab">
    Choose **Week** or **Month**, and **By person** or **By department or ward**. The filter next to it lists your departments and your wards in separate groups. Use the arrows to move between periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a slot or a shift type">
    Click an empty slot for that day and person, or **New shift** to pick anyone. Choosing a shift type fills in the hours for you; you can still adjust them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and save">
    Pick who it belongs to — or leave it **Open (unassigned)** for someone to claim later — and save. The shift appears on the rota immediately, and the person gets a notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Every shift belongs to a department or ward

A shift names its **Department or ward** — chosen from your hospital's active departments under [Departments](/hospital/settings/departments), never typed. The list has two groups, **Departments** and **Wards** (a ward is a department marked **This is a ward / inpatient department**), and a ward is shown with **(Ward)** after its name on the rota. A shift on a ward is stored exactly like one on a department. When it applies, a shift may also narrow to one **Sub-unit** (leave it as **Whole department**, or **Whole ward** for a ward, to cover all of it). A new shift cannot be saved without a department; shifts made before this change can be fixed from the banner described below, one click for all shifts that share the same typed department. If your hospital has more than one active [location](/hospital/settings/locations), a **Location** picker appears too; with one location it is hidden and every shift uses it.

Because a shift points at the department itself, **renaming a department or a location never breaks a shift**. The triage handover, the Nursing Station and every assign-staff list read the rota by department and by the shift's time, so someone on shift for **Outpatient Department** shows under that department whatever it is called later. The handover's **No rota for this department** means no shift is set for that department at that time, and it links straight to the rota filtered to that department (**Open the rota for this department**).

**Times read in 12-hour.** Start and end are chosen on the same time picker as the appointment form — hour, minute and **AM** / **PM**, with quick buttons for common times — so a shift reads **7:00 PM – 7:00 AM**. If the end is at or before the start the panel says **This shift ends the next day**, and the rota shows **(next day)** beside the hours.

**Nursing shift times.** When a shift is for a ward department (one an owner or manager marked **This is a ward / inpatient department** under [Departments](/hospital/settings/departments)) and your hospital runs inpatient care, the shift panel offers the times from **Settings → Facility → Nursing shifts** — for example **Day 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM** — as buttons that fill the start and end for you. You can still change them; a ward shift whose times do not line up with the nursing shifts is allowed and marked **Outside the nursing pattern**. Changing the nursing shift pattern never rewrites a shift: after you save it you are told how many upcoming ward shifts no longer line up.

### Shifts that still need a department

Shifts created before this change carried a typed department. Where the typed text was exactly one department's name or code (ignoring capitals and extra spaces) it was linked for you; nothing is matched any more loosely than that. The rest stay on the rota marked **Department not set**, and a banner at the top of the **Rota** tab counts them with a one-click fix: **Choose the department** gives every shift that shares the same typed text its department in one step (**Assign a department** does the same for upcoming shifts with none). Owners and managers only, and recorded in the audit log.

**Switching off a department or location that upcoming shifts use** asks first and says how many upcoming shifts use it. Those shifts stay on the rota, marked **Inactive department** or **Inactive location**, so cover never silently disappears; **Move those shifts** in the banner reassigns them when you are ready. Shifts that have already happened are never changed.

## Build a repeating pattern

Instead of assigning the same shift week after week, create a shift type once, then a pattern that says which days it repeats on and how far into the future to generate it. The tab shows how many shifts a pattern will add before you confirm. Running it again later only adds what's missing — it never duplicates a shift already on the rota.

**Copy week** duplicates a whole week's rota to another week in one step, for the whole team or one department.

## Request and approve cover

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for cover">
    From your own shift, or a colleague's if you manage the rota, select **Request swap or cover** and add a reason, then **Ask for cover**. The panel closes and **Request sent** appears; the request is under **Cover & swaps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A colleague accepts">
    Anyone the request was sent to — or anyone at all, for an open request — can accept it from **Cover & swaps**. Accepting does not move the shift yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A manager approves">
    Only once a manager approves does the shift actually reassign. Approving or declining notifies both people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Coverage rules

Set a minimum headcount for a department or role during a time window — for example, at least two people on Front Desk between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM on weekdays. The Rota tab flags any window that falls below the minimum, and separately flags a person booked onto two overlapping shifts.

<Note>
  **A shift fills in a day that has NO working hours set at all — it does not add to a day that already has some.** If a person has no [Availability](/hospital/settings/booking-settings) schedule covering a given weekday, a shift assigned that date makes them bookable for exactly the shift's hours. On a day they already have a working-hours schedule — even a narrow one — an extra shift on that same day changes nothing about what can be booked; their regular hours are what's offered. To make someone bookable for LONGER on a day they already work, widen their Availability schedule for that day instead. A service line that never uses shifts sees no change.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Cancelling a shift removes it from the rota permanently**, along with any open swap or cover request for it. This cannot be undone — assign a replacement shift afterward if the work still needs covering.
</Warning>

## On shift now

Wherever you choose a person to assign a patient to (the front desk **Check in** sheet, a triage nurse, the appointment form's provider, a ward patient's care team), the people whose shift covers that department and place right now are listed first under **On shift now**. Everyone else is listed below under **Everyone else** and can still be chosen. The **Nursing Station** also shows who is on shift now. If nobody has a matching shift, the list looks exactly as before.
