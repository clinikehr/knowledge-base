## Security

Described in the rail as "Clinic-wide security rules". Three things live here, and the first applies to everyone.

### Require Two-Factor Authentication

Switching this on makes a second factor mandatory for every member of staff in this hospital. Nobody reaches a patient record without one — a staff member who has not set theirs up is prompted to at their next sign-in.

Turn it on when your staff are at a desk rather than mid-shift, and warn them first. Someone who signs in at 6am to start a ward round and meets a setup prompt they were not expecting is going to call you, not their phone. The mechanics of enrolling are on [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa).

### Passkeys

A passkey lets someone sign in with the fingerprint, face or PIN their device already uses instead of typing a password. It is a convenience for the person signing in, and it does not by itself satisfy a two-factor requirement — if two-factor is required here, a passkey user still completes their second factor.

### Security alerts

Tells you about sign-in events worth knowing about, such as a sign-in from an unfamiliar device. Leave these on; they are how a compromised account is noticed before it is used.

## Data Export

Described as "Export clinic data as CSV or ZIP". Choose what to export and in which format — a **CSV** for a single set of records, a **ZIP** when the export spans several.

A large export does not tie up your screen. It runs in the background and you carry on working; you are told when it is ready to download. Do not re-request it because nothing appeared to happen — that just queues a second copy.

An export is a HIPAA-relevant action: it takes patient data out of the product and puts it somewhere the product no longer protects. Handle the file accordingly, and delete it once whatever needed it is done. See [Data export](/platform/security/data-export).

## Inventory

Described as "Alerts, dispensing rules & reset", under **PRACTICE › Inventory**. The tab exists only while the **Inventory** care area is on in [Facility](/hospital/settings/facility).

Three things sit here:

- **Expiry alerts** — how far ahead of expiry stock is flagged, so a batch is used or written off before it is wasted rather than after.
- **Dispensing enforcement** — how strictly the product holds a dispense to available stock. Tighter is more accurate and slower; looser keeps the counter moving and lets records drift. Pick deliberately.
- **Danger Zone** — the inventory reset.

<Warning>
  The **Danger Zone** reset clears this hospital's inventory and cannot be undone. It asks you to type `RESET` to confirm, because a confirm button on its own is too easy to select by accident. Run a **Data Export** of your stock first, and only reset a workspace you are genuinely starting over — never a live one to "clean up" a count.
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- With two-factor required, sign in as a test staff member and confirm the second factor is demanded. An owner is not exempt.
- Add a passkey on one device and confirm it signs you in, and that any required second factor still applies.
- Request a small export and confirm the file arrives in the format you chose and opens correctly.
- Request a large one and confirm you can keep working while it runs, and that you are told when it is ready.
- Set an expiry alert window, then check the inventory list flags a batch inside that window and nothing outside it.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Staff are locked out after I required two-factor">
    They are not locked out — they are being asked to enrol. Walk them through it on [Two-factor authentication](/platform/security/mfa). Switching the requirement back off is a decision for an owner, not a quick fix for one person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A passkey signs someone in but they are still asked for a code">
    That is correct where two-factor is required for this hospital. A passkey replaces the password, not the second factor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My export never arrived">
    Large exports run in the background and can take a while. Wait for the completion message rather than re-requesting, which only queues another copy. If nothing arrives at all, contact support with the time you requested it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Inventory tab">
    It appears only while the **Inventory** care area is on. Turn it on in [Facility](/hospital/settings/facility). The Store is a separate surface and is not affected by that area.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dispensing is refusing stock we physically have">
    The count in the product does not match the shelf. Correct the count rather than loosening the rule — a looser rule hides the discrepancy instead of fixing it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone reset our inventory">
    The reset cannot be undone. Restore what you can from your most recent stock export and recount. Then review who holds owner and manager access under [Staff and roles](/hospital/settings/team-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
