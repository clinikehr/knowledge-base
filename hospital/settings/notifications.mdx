## What this tab controls

Every switch saves on its own, straight away. There is no **Save** button.

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** | "Master switch for all clinic emails. Turn off to pause non-essential email." | On |
| **Appointment** | "New bookings, cancellations, and reminders" | On |
| **Patient** | "New patient registrations and updates" — also gates the provider-arrival email when someone is checked in | On |
| **Staff** | "Staff invitations accepted and role changes" | On |
| **Billing** | "Payment received, invoices, and overdue alerts" | On |
| **Subscription** | "Plan changes, trial expiry, and payment status" | On |
| **Document** | "Document signed, viewed, or expired" | On |
| **Insurance** | "Claim status changes and approvals" | On |
| **System** | "System updates and maintenance notices" | On, **and cannot be switched off** |
| **Achievement emails** | "Milestone badges, tier-ups, and your clinic's trophy case" | On |
| **Daily operational digests** | "Morning owner summaries for inventory, pharmacy, and lab" | On |

Two notes on that list. The **System** switch is drawn but fixed, and the line under the card explains why: "System notifications cannot be disabled as they contain important updates about your clinic." And the category list adapts to your edition, so you are only offered categories your hospital can actually produce.

## Your notification bell, and what is yours alone

The bell in the header keeps **your own** copy of every notice, in three views: **Unread**, **All** and **Archived**. What you do with a notice is yours: a notice sent to the whole clinic that a colleague has read stays unread for you. On each notice you can **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Archive** or **Restore**; **Mark all read** clears everything unread that does not need you. A notice you have read is archived automatically 30 days later, and an archived notice is kept for 90 days.

**Notices that need you.** A patient assigned to you, a countersignature waiting for you, a note returned to you and a change of supervisor are marked **Needs action** and carry an **Acknowledge** button. They stay in **Unread** — reading them is not enough — until you acknowledge them or the thing they ask of you is done (the visit is checked out, the note is countersigned), when they clear themselves. **Mark all read** leaves them where they are and tells you how many. A notice that needs you cannot be archived until you have acknowledged it. Acknowledging is recorded against your name.

**Pop-ups.** When you are signed in, a new notice also appears as a card in the corner of the screen, with **Acknowledge** or **Open** where they apply. Notices that were already waiting when you opened the page do not pop up. While the bell is open nothing pops up over it, because a new notice joins the list you are looking at (an emergency still does).

**Clinical activity.** The people whose work a record starts are told when it is created: the nurses on shift when a patient joins the triage queue (**New triage**), the lab when a lab order is placed (**New lab order**), and the pharmacy when a prescription is written (**New prescription**). Each says only what happened and opens the queue, never who the patient is. If your rota names who is on shift now, those people are told; if not, everyone in that role is. You are never told about a record you made yourself. Several arriving together show as one pop-up, such as **3 new lab orders**. A clinic that has muted the **Patient** category does not send these three.

**Emergency.** When a patient is marked as an emergency, **Emergency in triage** appears in red for the emergency responders: doctors, psychiatrists, nurses, nurse practitioners, midwives, paramedics, psychiatric nurses, respiratory therapists, senior clinical orthopedic officers, owners and managers. Those on shift in the department are told; if none of the clinical team is on shift there, every responder in the clinic is. If the patient is currently admitted to a ward, the responders whose shift right now is on that ward are told as well (and those on an in-patient unit shift that names no ward), whatever department the emergency was raised in; a ward shift never adds someone who is not an emergency responder, and the person who raised the emergency is never told of their own. The nurse of record is told only if they hold one of those roles. Front desk, billing, accounts, pharmacy, laboratory, records and stores staff never receive an emergency notice, pop-up or sound, and an emergency raised from the **Emergency** button in triage now sends one notice, not a second one to the whole clinic. The notice and its **Open triage** button take you straight to that patient's triage chart. If you are a responder it always reaches you, always pops up, stays on screen until you dismiss it, and waits in your bell as **Needs action** until you acknowledge it or the patient is seen. An emergency recorded for a patient on a ward always alerts, even though the patient is already admitted, and stays open until it is switched off, the patient is discharged, or an outcome is recorded — being admitted does not count as being seen. Neither you nor the clinic can switch it off, and not even **No pop-up for notices that need me** silences it.

**Your own choices.** Under **Settings → Notifications**, the **My notifications** card has three groups, saved the moment you change a switch (you get **Notification settings saved**):

- **Activity**: **Show in my bell** and **Pop up** for **New triage**, **New lab order**, **New prescription**, **New patient added** and, if you are an owner, manager or billing role, **Payment received**.
- **Sounds**: **Play a sound for emergencies** (on by default, shown only to emergency responders) plays a short two-tone alert of about one second when an emergency arrives, and once more ten seconds later if you have not acknowledged it; it never repeats beyond that. **Play a sound for other new notices** (off by default) adds a soft chime for everything else. Your browser allows sound only after you have clicked or pressed a key on the page; until then the emergency pop-up shows **Sound blocked — click to enable**. Each has **Play test sound**, so you can hear the tone and check your speakers; that click also lets your browser play sound. A change takes effect at once, in every open window, without reloading. Sounds still play when the window is in the background or minimised, including in the desktop app. The tones are made on your device, so they need no download and work without a connection, but a new notice reaches you only once you are back online.
- **Assigned to you**: **No pop-up for notices that need me**, and **Email me when a patient or shift is assigned to me**. The email says that something was assigned and names no patient; it follows the clinic's email switches and your account's **Product & account emails** switch. A changed or cancelled shift still emails you so your calendar stays right.
- **Everything else**: **Show in my bell** and **Pop up** for each category.

**Payment received.** When a patient's invoice payment is recorded, whether at the desk, online or by a payment link, the owner, managers and the billing roles (cashiers, billers, accountants and receptionists) are told in the bell with **Payment received**. Clinicians are not. Only payments from patients count: money received from an insurer or other payer does not raise it. Two payments on the same invoice within a few minutes show as one entry in your bell. It says only that a payment was recorded and opens the invoice; it never names the patient or the amount. You are never told about a payment you recorded yourself, and an Auto Pay charge keeps its own **Auto Pay charged** notice instead. It does not pop up unless you switch **Pop up** on under **My notifications → Activity → Payment received**, and a clinic that has muted **Billing** does not send it.

**The payment email.** Separately from the bell, an online or card payment also emails the owner and the account's contact address, and the email says who paid and for what. Its first line names the patient and the amount, for example "Jane Doe paid $120.00 online — invoice INV-0042.", and its details list the **Client**, the **Invoice** or **Invoices**, the payment method and the reference. If the patient is restricted to their assigned team, the email shows their secured label, for example **Secured patient 7** instead of a name, and if the patient cannot be identified it says **A patient**. The subject line shows only the amount, never a name, so it is safe on a lock screen. **View invoice** opens the invoice when the payment covered one invoice; otherwise the button opens the billing dashboard. A failed payment email carries the same **Client** and **Invoice** details. A clinic that has muted **Billing** does not send it.

**New patient added.** Whenever a patient is registered, by any route (the front desk, online booking, the patient portal, an import of one, a CRM contact becoming a patient, a lab specimen transfer, or an integration), everyone on your team with a sign-in is told in the bell, except the person who registered them. The notice reads **New patient added** and says how, by whom and what kind of registration it was, for example "Registered at the front desk by Ada Obi · child"; a patient who registers themselves online reads "Registered online". It never shows the patient's name or Patient ID, and it opens the patient's record, which stays behind your usual access rules. The owner, managers, receptionists and other administrative and front-desk roles get a pop-up by default; everyone else gets the entry in the bell without one, and can switch the pop-up on under **My notifications → Activity → New patient added**, which says which default applies to your role.

The pop-up shows where the patient came from as a small label (**Front desk**, **Online**, **API**, **CRM**, **Lab transfer** or **AI assistant**), who registered them and whether they are an adult, a child or a newborn, with two buttons: **Open patient** and **Patient list**. Several arriving together fold into one pop-up such as **3 new patients added**, whose **View** button opens the list.

**Importing many patients.** A file import does not announce each patient. When it finishes, one notice such as "42 patients imported" goes to everyone except the person who ran it, with a single **Patient list** button.

These sit inside your clinic's switches above: a category your clinic has muted reaches nobody. Your choices are kept per clinic, because your role differs from clinic to clinic. You can also set them from **Profile and Settings → Notifications**, under **My notifications in a clinic**, with a clinic picker if you belong to more than one.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    From the sidebar footer select **Settings**, then **Practice notifications** → **Notifications**. The rail says "Practice" even for a hospital; the switches are the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on email as a whole">
    Leave **Email notifications** on unless you want to pause hospital email entirely. Turning it off confirms with "All non-essential clinic emails are paused. Receipts, security, and billing alerts still send."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mute the categories nobody acts on">
    Flip a category switch off. You get "Notifications muted" and the name of what you turned off — for example "Appointment notifications turned off."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal with the two extras">
    **Achievement emails** and **Daily operational digests** sit below the categories, separated by a line. The digests are a morning summary for the owner covering inventory, pharmacy and lab; turn them off if nobody reads them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

There is no save — each switch takes effect as you flip it, and the toast tells you it landed.

| What you changed | What you'll notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** off | Non-essential hospital email stops. **Transactional email keeps sending** — receipts, security alerts, password resets and billing alerts are never suppressed by this switch. In-app notifications are unaffected. |
| A category off | Both the in-app notice **and** the email for that category stop. The bell stops showing them, and nothing about that category reaches the hospital inbox. |
| A category back on | Notifications resume from that moment. Nothing suppressed while it was off is delivered late. |
| **Achievement emails** off | Badge and tier-up emails stop. Your trophy case is still there to look at. |
| **Daily operational digests** off | The morning inventory, pharmacy and lab summary stops arriving. |

**This store governs email directed at the hospital.** It is not your own account settings, and it is not what a patient has asked for. If you are trying to stop a specific message, work out which store owns it first:

| Store | Where you set it | What it governs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email notifications** master switch | This tab | Hospital-directed email, on or off, as a whole |
| The category switches | This tab | Hospital email **and** in-app notices, per category |
| A person's own account settings | [Account notifications](/platform/account/notifications) | Email addressed to someone as a person — account, security and marketing |
| The individual patient's record | The patient's intake sheet | That patient's own appointment reminders and confirmations |

So: a patient complaining about reminders is fixed on their record, not here. An owner tired of milestone emails turns off **Achievement emails** here. Someone who does not want marketing email changes it on their own account.

## Rebooking reminders

A second card on this tab, **Rebooking reminders**, is about your **patients**. With **Suggest the next visit** on, a patient whose usual time until their next visit has come round — and who has nothing booked with you since — gets one email suggesting they book that service again, with a button that opens your booking page with the service already chosen. The same suggestion appears as a **Book your next visit** card in their patient portal.

- **Off by default, and only the owner can turn it on.**
- **A service needs a usual gap.** Set **Usual time until the next visit** on the service in [Appointment services](/hospital/settings/appointment-services); a service without one never sends.
- **One email per visit, at most**, and none if the patient has booked anything since. It names only the service and the hospital.
- **Patients stay in control.** A patient whose record has appointment emails turned off gets none, and every email carries **Stop these reminders**, which also removes the portal card. Appointment confirmations and reminders are not affected.
- **Your booking page must be published** and the service bookable online by existing patients.

## Check it worked

Flip a switch and watch for the toast — "Notifications muted" or "Notifications enabled", naming the category. Reload the tab; the switch should hold its new position. Then trigger something in that category — book a test appointment, say — and confirm nothing arrives in the hospital inbox and nothing appears under the bell.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The System switch will not move">
    It is deliberately fixed on. System notices carry service and maintenance information every hospital needs, so there is no way to mute them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I turned everything off and email still arrives">
    Essential email is out of scope for these switches. Receipts, security alerts, password resets and billing alerts always send. If what you are getting is a reminder to a *patient*, that is set on the patient's record, not here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A category I was told about is not in my list">
    The list adapts to your edition and only offers categories your hospital can produce. A missing switch means that category never fires for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Failed to update preferences"'>
    The switch snaps back to where it was and nothing was saved. Check your connection and flip it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A colleague still gets emails I muted">
    Muting here stops hospital-directed email. Email addressed to them as a person — account and security notices, marketing — is governed by their own [account notifications](/platform/account/notifications).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can see the switches but they will not move">
    Opening Settings needs owner, manager or accountant. If you have that and a switch still will not move, it is mid-save — each one disables for a moment while it lands.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
