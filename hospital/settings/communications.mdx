Communications is where the hospital gets a dedicated number, watches its message credit balance, and turns on the channels agents and staff reach patients over — texts, calls, fax and email. Nobody types a provider's numbers or connection details here: the platform buys and registers the number, and the database — not this form — is what proves a number belongs to this hospital.

## What this tab controls

| Card | Control | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your number** | **Get a number** | Opens checkout for a dedicated number ($15/month plus a one-time $25 registration fee). The number appears once payment is confirmed |
| | **Release number** | Stops the monthly charge and releases the number. Reminders fall back to email until you get a new one |
| **Fax Inbox** | **Open Fax Inbox** | Faxes the hospital has received, waiting to be filed into a patient's documents |
| **Communication credits** | Balance, this month's usage | One shared balance for texts, calls and fax pages |
| | **Buy a pack** | A one-time top-up, billed to the card on file |
| | **Auto top-up** | Automatically buys a pack when the balance runs out. Off by default |
| **Channels** | **SMS**, **Voice**, **Email** switches | Turn each on or off |
| | **Fax** | Always available once the hospital has a fax-capable number — sent from any signed document, no switch needed |
| | **WhatsApp** | Not available yet |
| **Email** | **Send from (verified address)** | The address agent email is sent from, e.g. `clinic@yourdomain.com` |
| | **Reply-to (optional)** | Where replies land if that is a different address, e.g. `frontdesk@yourdomain.com` |
| | **Send from your own mailbox** → **Google** / **Outlook** | Not available yet. The **Connect** buttons are visible but inactive |

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a number">
    In **Your number**, select **Get a number**. You're taken to checkout for the recurring number fee plus a one-time registration fee. Once payment is confirmed, you land back on this tab and the number appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the channels you use">
    In **Channels**, switch on **SMS**, **Voice** and/or **Email**. Fax needs no switch — see [Faxing](#faxing-a-document) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the email address">
    Put a verified hospital address in **Send from**. Use **Reply-to** when replies should reach a monitored inbox rather than the sending address — a front-desk mailbox rather than a no-reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the credit balance">
    Texts, calls and fax pages all draw from one balance in **Communication credits**. Buy a pack, or turn on **Auto top-up** so it never runs dry mid-month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Faxing a document

Fax is not a switch — it is gated by having a number and by the **Send Fax** permission (owners and managers always have it; anyone else needs it granted in Settings → Team → Permissions). To send one, open any signed document — a clinical note, a lab or radiology report, an invoice, or a signed patient document — and choose **Send by fax**. You'll see the page count and credit cost before confirming.

Faxes the hospital **receives** land in the **Fax Inbox**, reachable from this tab or from the sidebar. Nothing is matched to a patient automatically — a fax tells you who sent it, never who it's about — so a person reviews each one, opens it to see what it says, and either files it into a patient's documents or marks it junk. An unfiled fax is removed automatically 30 days after it arrives, and the inbox flags anything close to that date.

## What changes once you save

Turning on a channel means an agent step or automated message that already sends over it starts actually reaching patients — from the number and address on this tab. Until a channel is on, whatever depends on it **stays silent rather than failing**: no error appears, the message simply never leaves. A published agent whose step sends a text simply cannot when SMS is off.

Two things worth knowing. This tab governs communications the hospital sends — including agents — not the hospital's own internal notification emails; appointment reminders, invoice notices and staff alerts follow their own preference switches, not these. And patient contact preferences still apply on top: a channel being enabled here never overrides a patient who has opted out.

Getting a number is a real, recurring charge from the moment checkout confirms — releasing it stops that charge immediately. Running out of communication credits does not fail silently either: sends fall back to email so nothing is ever lost, just delivered a different way.

## Check it worked

**Getting a number:** after checkout, return to this tab — the number should read "Registered and active" (a fresh number briefly shows a provisioning status first).

**A channel:** save, reload the tab — the switches should be as you left them. Run one agent or automated message on a channel you enabled, to a colleague rather than a patient, and confirm it arrives from the number or address you configured.

**Fax:** send a document to a real fax number you control, or check the Fax Inbox after asking someone to fax the hospital's number.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An upgrade panel covers the whole tab">
    "Get a number for texts and calls, and let your clinic send appointment reminders and messages. Available on any paid plan." See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only clinic owners and managers can configure communications">
    Accountants can open settings but not this tab's controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Get a number does nothing, or names a payment problem">
    Select it again — it should open a payment page for the number's monthly fee and one-time registration fee. If it says numbers aren't available for the hospital's currency yet, that currency isn't priced yet (today: US and Canada only) — reminders still send by email in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A channel is on but nothing sends">
    Confirm the hospital has a number in **Your number** — a channel switch with no number behind it has nowhere to send from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Fax Inbox is empty">
    That's expected until someone actually faxes the hospital's number — nothing is seeded or simulated. Once a fax arrives, it appears here for filing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replies are going to a mailbox nobody reads">
    Set **Reply-to** to a monitored address. It overrides the sending address for replies only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Connect buttons for Google and Outlook do nothing">
    Mailbox sending is not available yet. Agents send from the verified address above in the meantime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reminders still go out after I turned Email off">
    Ordinary hospital notifications are governed by the notification preferences, not by these channels. Change them there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
