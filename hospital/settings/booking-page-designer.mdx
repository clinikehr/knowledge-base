The booking page is the hospital's public front door for appointments. This tab designs it, chooses what it offers, decides whether money is taken at booking, and publishes it — with a live preview beside every change.

## What this tab controls

Above the tabs sit **Preview**, **Save Changes** and **Publish** / **Unpublish**, a **Booking Page URL** card with **Copy** and **Open**, and a **Live** or **Draft** badge.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Design** | Template, **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Accent** and **Background Color**, quick presets, **Font Family**, **Page Slug**, and a **Custom domain** card (Team and above) |
| **Content** | The words on the page — headings, description, what the hospital wants a visitor to read before choosing |
| **Services** | **Services Management** — "Select services from your clinic to display on the booking page", with search, a category filter, a featured flag, and a **Services on Your Booking Page** list |
| **Forms** | The questions a patient answers while booking |
| **Settings** | Booking rules and payment, below |

### The Settings tab

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Allow Online Booking** | "Enable direct booking from the page" | Off |
| **Clinic Timezone** | "All appointment times will be calculated based on this timezone." | UTC |
| **Advance Booking Days** | "How many days in advance patients can book appointments" | 30 |
| **Booking Confirmation Message** | "Message shown to patients after they submit a booking request" | Blank |
| **Show "Powered by ClinikEHR"** | A small line at the foot of your booking page, in every template, and on the confirm, cancel, reschedule and payment-return pages, linking to clinikehr.com. It stays on for Free, Essential and Starter practices; **Team** and **Enterprise** can turn it off. If a Team practice later moves to a lower plan the line comes back, and your choice is remembered if you upgrade again | On |

And the **After a client books** card:

| Control | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Redirect after booking** | A switch. Off: clients stay on their confirmation. On: they see their confirmation for 5 seconds, then go to the page you choose. **Stay here** cancels it | Off |
| **My website** | Takes clients to the **Website** in **Settings → General**, the one website for your whole practice. Unavailable, with the reason shown, until you have entered one there | — |
| **Another page** | Takes clients to an `https://` address you type, for example a thank-you page. An address that is not a plain `https://` web address is refused when you save | — |

Under the switch, one line says what clients will experience, for example "After booking, clients see their confirmation for 5 seconds, then go to yourclinic.com." Turning the switch off keeps your choice for later. With it on, saving without a usable page is refused.
| **Accept Payments** | "Allow patients to pay through the booking page". Reveals everything below | Off |
| **Payment Methods** | Card, Cash, Bank Transfer, Insurance | None |
| **Require Upfront Payment** | "Require payment before appointment" | Off |
| **Payment Provider** | Stripe or Paystack, whichever your currency uses | Auto-detected |
| **Payment Instructions** · **Refund Policy** | Text shown to the patient | Blank |
| **Card on file** | **Off**, **Optional** or **Required** — whether clients save a card while they book. Only selectable when a Stripe account is connected and ready; not offered to hospitals that bill in Naira | Off |

With **Require Upfront Payment** on, patients pay the full fee when booking. There is no deposit setting here: to take a deposit instead, or to charge for late cancellations and missed visits, set it on each service — see [Deposits and cancellation fees](/hospital/settings/appointment-services#deposits-and-cancellation-fees).

### Booking policy

The **Booking policy** card holds your cancellation and deposit policy in your own words. Patients read it, with each service's rules, and tick **I have read and agree to this booking policy** before they book a service that takes a deposit, is paid in full when booking, or has a cancellation or missed appointment fee. Select **Save policy** to save it — separately from **Save Changes** — and each change starts a new version. Only the owner or a manager can change it.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Design the page">
    On **Design**, pick a template and set the four colours — or take a **Quick Preset** — then a **Font Family**. Set the **Page Slug**, which becomes the page's public address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    On **Content**, say who the page is for and what happens after a booking. Keep it short; a booking page is read standing up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the services">
    On **Services**, search your catalogue and add the ones patients may book themselves. The badge shows how many are on the page, and **Services on Your Booking Page** lists them — "These services will be displayed on your public booking page." Flag one as **Featured** if it should lead. To let a patient book more than one service in the same appointment, turn on **Clients can book several services in one visit** in the **Several services in one visit** card at the top and choose **Most services in one booking**; it saves straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the questions">
    On **Forms**, decide what a patient must answer while booking. Ask for what the clinic genuinely needs at the desk and no more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on and set the window">
    On **Settings**, switch **Allow Online Booking** on, confirm the **Clinic Timezone**, and set **Advance Booking Days** to how far ahead you want the diary opened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about money">
    Turn on **Accept Payments** only if you want to collect at booking. Then choose **Payment Methods**, and use **Require Upfront Payment** to take the full fee at booking. For a deposit rather than the full fee, set it on the service and write your **Booking policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about a card on file">
    In **Card on file**, choose **Optional** or **Required** if the hospital keeps a card for patients who book online. The card goes on the patient's record, where **Edit client** and Auto Pay use it; nobody is charged when it is saved. It is saved only for a new patient or one who confirmed their email with a code — never onto an existing record matched by a typed email alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, then Publish">
    Select **Save Changes** — "Your booking page has been successfully updated" — then **Publish**. The badge flips from **Draft** to **Live**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

Saving and publishing are two different actions, and both are needed.

- **Save Changes** stores the design and settings. Until you save, the amber banner reads "You have unsaved changes. Don't forget to save your work!" and the public page still shows the old version.
- **Publish** puts the page online: "Your booking page is now live and accessible to patients." **Unpublish** takes it offline again — "Your booking page has been taken offline."

**Two conditions must both hold before anyone can book online:** **Allow Online Booking** is on, *and* at least one service is visible on the **Services** tab. A published page with neither renders and offers nothing, which reads to a visitor as a broken site.

**Opening hours are not on this tab.** Times come from schedules in [Booking settings](/hospital/settings/booking-settings), minus existing bookings and any blocking calendar events. That is precisely why a page can be published, correct and still show a patient no available times — the page is fine and the schedule is missing.

Once payment is on, **Require Upfront Payment** changes the booking flow: the patient pays before the appointment is created — the full fee, or the deposit a service sets — and a booking that tries to skip the payment is refused, on the booking page and in the client portal. Money lands in the hospital's own account connected in [Online payments](/hospital/settings/online-payments), chosen by your billing currency — never anywhere else. If it is not connected and ready, this tab shows "Stripe not connected" (or "Paystack not connected") with a link back there rather than a provider picker, and patients book without paying until it is.

Every booking is checked again when it is made: the page must be published, **Allow Online Booking** on, the service still added on **Services**, the time still free, and the date within **Advance Booking Days**. A patient who has already paid is not refused for a window that closed while they paid.

A service with a **deposit**, **Pay in full when booking** or a cancellation or missed appointment fee changes what the patient sees before booking: what is paid now, the rules, your **Booking policy**, and **I have read and agree to this booking policy**, which they must tick — on the booking page and when booking from the patient portal alike. The deposit shows as paid on the appointment's invoice. A patient who cancels in time is refunded automatically. A late cancellation or a no-show is never charged automatically: the appointment shows the fee to review, and an owner, manager or accountant chooses **Charge** (with the amount), which charges the patient's card on file after a confirmation, or **Waive fee**.

With **Several services in one visit** on, a patient can tick extra services under **Add to this visit**. The visit is one appointment with one provider who offers every chosen service; the times and prices are added together, and only start times long enough for the whole visit are offered. The diary, invoice, emails and portal show every service, for example **Ultrasound + Consultation**.

Options and add-ons set on a service are offered on the page: the patient chooses an option before times appear, ticks add-ons, and sees the total time and price — for example **Facial — Long + Add-on: Mask**. **Not bookable online** and **Add-on only** services are not listed; **New clients only** services are refused for a patient who confirmed their email as an **Existing client**; **Existing clients only** services ask the patient to confirm with the email code first. Offered times respect each service's **Minimum notice**, **Book up to (days ahead)** and **Buffer after (minutes)**, and the same limits are checked again when the patient books.

A booking made here creates a real appointment in the hospital diary, so the front desk sees it alongside everything booked by phone.

Patients can also use a promo code, a gift card or a session from their package on the booking page and in the patient portal. The **Promo code** and **Gift card** fields appear only while you have an active promo code or gift card, and **Use a session from your package** only for a patient who confirmed their email with the code. See [Used when a patient books online](/hospital/revenue/packages-and-gift-cards#used-when-a-patient-books-online).

## The QR code

The **QR code** card gives you a code patients scan with a phone camera to open your booking page. A booking is counted as **Booked from the code** when it is made, including a booking that is paid for first: it is counted once, when the payment completes it.

- **It is in your booking page's own colour, with your logo in the middle.** If that colour is too light for a camera to read reliably, the code is drawn in near-black and the card says so. If your logo cannot be loaded, the code is drawn without it and the card says that too.
- **Only a code that has been checked is offered.** Before **Download PNG** or **Print** appear, the finished picture is read back on your device and must open exactly the address printed under it. If that check fails, the card says the code could not be checked and offers nothing to print.
- **It always uses the saved address.** Change the address or the look, save, and the code is drawn again. Anything you have typed but not saved is never printed.
- **If the page is switched off**, the code still works the moment you turn it on; until then anyone who scans it sees that the page is unavailable, and the card warns you.

### Poster and card

Select **Poster and card** to open **Print your code**. Choose **Poster (A4)** for a wall or window, or **Reception card (A6)** for the front desk, then **Download PDF** or **Download PNG**. Both carry your booking page name and logo in your colour, the line "Scan to book an appointment", and the short address for people without a camera. Everything is in words, so it reads the same on a black-and-white printer. The file is made on your device; nothing is uploaded.

### One code for each location

With more than one active location, a **One code for each location** section lists a code for every one, each with its own **Download PNG**, **Print** and **Poster and card**. Scanning a location's code opens the booking page with that location already chosen, so the **Choose a location** step is skipped.

### How many people used it

Owners and managers see **Last 30 days** on the card: **Opened from the code** and **Booked from the code**. These are counts only. No name, device or address is kept with them. The counts are approximate, and refreshing the page in the same tab counts once.

## The confirmation PDF

After someone books, they can keep or print a confirmation. **Download confirmation** appears on the page they land on after booking, on the page they return to after paying, and on the page behind the **Confirm** link in their email; a **Download confirmation (PDF)** link in the booking email is coming and is not there yet. It is a one-page PDF with your letterhead, their first name only, the date and time in your time zone (and theirs when it differs), the service or services, the location with its address or "Online video visit", their booking reference, what they paid and what is still due in your currency, and how to confirm, cancel or reschedule. If your client portal is on and has an address, it also carries a QR code with "Scan to manage your appointments"; for a visit at one of your locations it is that location's code.

The link is personal to the booking: it only works with the booking reference that came with the booking, and someone without it, or with a wrong one, gets the same "could not be found" answer as for an appointment that does not exist.

## Check it worked

Select **Open** on the URL card and view the page in a private browser window. You should see your branding, your services, and — critically — **real times to choose from**. Book one test appointment through to the end: it should appear on the hospital calendar with the answers the patient gave, and your **Booking Confirmation Message** should be what they saw.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The page is published but shows no available times">
    The page is not the problem. Availability comes from [Booking settings](/hospital/settings/booking-settings) — check there is a schedule covering those days and that the provider or venue matches.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page is published but nothing can be booked">
    Check both conditions: **Allow Online Booking** on the **Settings** tab, and at least one service showing under **Services on Your Booking Page**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My changes are not on the live page">
    You published without saving, or saved after publishing and stopped. Select **Save Changes** whenever the amber unsaved-changes banner is showing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stripe not connected under Payment Provider">
    "You need to connect a Stripe account to collect upfront payments for your current currency." Finish it in [Online payments](/hospital/settings/online-payments), then come back and pick the provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new service is not on the page">
    Being active in the catalogue only makes it bookable internally. Add it here on the **Services** tab as well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Times are an hour out">
    **Clinic Timezone** on the **Settings** tab is wrong. Every offered time is calculated from it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
