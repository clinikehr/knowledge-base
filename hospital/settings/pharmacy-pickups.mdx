These settings decide what your pharmacy asks when someone collects medicine: whether a signature is required, whether prescriptions carry a pickup code, how often the privacy notice is acknowledged, the notice itself, your own yes-or-no questions, and whether a patient may sign on their own phone. Each card saves on its own.

**Where to find them.** On the Enterprise plan they are in **Settings**, then **Facility**, under **Pharmacy pickups**, and appear only while **In-house pharmacy** is switched on. On the other plans they are in **Settings**, then **Pharmacy**, then **Pickups**. They are the same controls in both places.

## What this tab controls

| Card | Control | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pickup rules** | **Require a signature at pickup** | On (the default): a pickup cannot be recorded until the patient, or the person collecting, signs. Off: the signature is still offered, but **Record pickup** works without it, and the record says **Not signed** |
| | **Prescription pickup codes (Rx barcode)** | On (the default): the dispensing slip prints each pickup code as a barcode, the **Prescription List** has **Scan code**, and the patient's ready-to-collect message and portal show the code. Off: none of them show a code |
| **Privacy notice acknowledgment** | **Once, and again when the notice changes** | The default. The patient acknowledges at their first pickup, and again after you publish a new version |
| | **At every pickup** | "The patient is asked each time medicine is collected." |
| **Questions at pickup** | The default questions | Counselling offered, and who is collecting. Marked **Always asked**; they can't be removed |
| | **Add a question** | Adds your own yes-or-no question. Turn on **Must be answered** to make it required |
| | **Edit**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Remove** | Change the wording, the order, or remove one of your questions |
| **Remote signing** | **Allow signing links** | Lets staff send the patient a link to sign on their own phone |
| | **A link works for** | **1 hour**, **24 hours**, **3 days** or **7 days** |
| **Your privacy notice** | **Publish your privacy notice** / **Publish a new version** | Write the notice, give a link to your website copy, or both |

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide the pickup rules">
    Under **Pickup rules**, turn **Require a signature at pickup** and **Prescription pickup codes (Rx barcode)** on or off. Each saves the moment you flip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your privacy notice">
    Under **Your privacy notice**, select **Publish your privacy notice**. Enter the **Notice text**, a **Link to the notice on your website (optional)**, or both, and select **Publish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often it is acknowledged">
    Under **Privacy notice acknowledgment**, choose **Once, and again when the notice changes** or **At every pickup**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own questions">
    Under **Questions at pickup**, select **Add a question**, enter the **Question**, turn on **Must be answered** if it is required, and select **Add question**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on signing links">
    Under **Remote signing**, turn **Allow signing links** on or off, and choose how long a link works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes once you save

- **With the signature switched off,** the pickup sheet still shows the signature box, marked **(optional)**, and **Record pickup** no longer waits for it. A pickup recorded without one is stored as **Not signed**, and the downloaded record says so; it is never left blank. Pickups already recorded keep the signature they have.
- **The privacy notice acknowledgment is still asked** whenever it is due. With no signature, ticking that the notice was read records it, with who recorded it and when.
- **With pickup codes switched off,** the dispensing slip prints no code or barcode, **Scan code** is hidden from the **Prescription List**, and the ready-to-collect message and the patient's portal no longer show a code; the message asks the patient to bring the dispensing slip or ID. A pickup is still recorded from **Record pickup** in the row's actions menu. A slip printed earlier still scans with a handheld scanner, and earlier pickup records keep their code.
- **The pickup sheet** on the **Prescription List** asks the new questions, in the order you set, from the next pickup someone opens. See [Record a prescription pickup](/hospital/pharmacy/pickups).
- **Publishing a notice** creates a new version. With **Once, and again when the notice changes**, every patient is asked again at their next pickup. Until any notice is published, nobody is asked to acknowledge anything.
- **Earlier records do not change.** A pickup keeps the exact wording of each question and the notice version that was shown when it was signed. Editing or removing a question never rewrites an earlier answer.
- **Removing a question** only stops it being asked from now on.

## Check it worked

Open **Record pickup** on a dispensed prescription. Your questions appear under **Questions from this clinic**, and the **Privacy notice** section appears when the patient is due to acknowledge.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find these settings">
    They appear for an owner or manager of a clinic with the In-house pharmacy care area switched on. On the Enterprise plan they are under **Facility**, then **Pharmacy pickups**; on the other plans, under **Pharmacy**, then **Pickups**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Record pickup says a signature is required">
    Your clinic requires a signature at pickup. An owner or manager can make it optional with **Require a signature at pickup**. The check is made when you record the pickup, so it applies even to a sheet that was already open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Publish refuses my link">
    "The link must start with http:// or https://." Copy the full address from your browser's address bar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Patients aren't asked about the privacy notice">
    No notice is published yet, or the patient already acknowledged the current version and you chose **Once, and again when the notice changes**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
