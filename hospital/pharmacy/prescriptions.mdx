**Prescription List** is the pharmacy unit's working queue: every script written across the hospital within your **Pharmacy** queue window, waiting for someone at the dispensary to pick it up. That includes scripts a pharmacist wrote in the pharmacy station, and scripts for admitted patients. It is where a pharmacy shift starts.

## What you're looking at

The page opens with a greeting and the line **Here's a list of all prescriptions for today!** How far back the list looks is the **Pharmacy** queue window — an owner or manager sets it under **Settings → Facility → Queue windows** (24 hours up to 30 days). The same window decides how long a script stays open to dispense: a script in this list always opens. Anything older is in [Prescription history](/hospital/pharmacy/prescription-history).

Above the table:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search for prescription** | Filters the list as you type |
| **New Prescription** | Opens the prescription form on the [pharmacy station](/hospital/pharmacy/station) |
| **Refresh** | Re-reads the queue — useful when a clinician is writing scripts while you work |
| **View Columns** | A **Toggle columns** list for showing and hiding columns |

The table itself carries eight columns, five of them on by default:

| Column | Shows | On by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Patient ID** | The hospital number you quote back to the ward | Yes |
| **Patient Name** | Who the script is for | Yes |
| **Status** | **Pending** or **Dispensed** | Yes |
| **Prescribed By** | The clinician who wrote it | Yes |
| **Dispensed By** | Who filled it, once it is filled | Yes |
| **Patient Type** | Outpatient, or **Ward Patient** | No |
| **Date** | When it was written | No |
| **Mode** | How the medication is taken | No |

A **Ward Patient** badge on a row means the script belongs to an admitted patient, and the row's actions change wording to match — **View Ward Prescription**, **Dispense Ward Prescription**. A script a pharmacist wrote with no visit behind it shows **Pharmacy Rx**, and everything one prescribing session wrote for that patient opens together.

The footer sets how many rows you see — **5**, **10**, **20** or **50** **per page** — and tells you how many there are in total.

**Empty states.** A hospital with nothing written today shows **No prescriptions found.** If you have typed in the search box and nothing matches, you get **No prescriptions match your search criteria.** instead — clear the search before concluding the queue is empty.

## Open a script

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find it">
    Enter the patient's name or hospital number in **Search for prescription**. Selecting anywhere on the row opens it, so you rarely need the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    Select **⋯** at the end of the row. **View Details** is always offered, to everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it in view mode">
    **View Details** opens the script read-only: the medications, quantities, dosage, frequency, duration and any instructions the prescriber left. Use this when a ward rings to ask what was written.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dispense from the queue

The dispensing actions appear only for staff signed in as a pharmacist. Everyone else sees **View Details** and nothing more.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a Pending script">
    Only a script at **Pending** offers **Dispense**. One already at **Dispensed** offers **Update** instead, for correcting what was recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Dispense">
    The script opens in dispensing mode with the prescribed lines in front of you, each showing exactly what is on record — a line still awaiting dispensing reads **Pending**, not **Dispensed**, until you set it. If the whole script is going out today, **Mark all pending as Dispensed** sets every still-pending line in one click; you still enter each quantity before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set any line that isn't going out today">
    Each medication has its own **Dispensing Status** — **Dispensed**, **Pending**, **Cancelled** or **Out of Stock** — and its own quantity. Change only the lines that need it: a script with three items can leave two at **Pending** and set the third to **Out of Stock** in the same save. Only a line you set to **Dispensed** asks for a quantity; **Pending**, **Cancelled** and **Out of Stock** save with no quantity at all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw the stock against it">
    Recording that medicine physically left the shelf happens in the **Fill Rx** tab of [Inventory](/hospital/supply/inventory) — that is the step that moves stock, links it to the patient, and takes a witness for a controlled item. See [the pharmacy station](/hospital/pharmacy/station) for the full flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each line shows its amount as you set the dispensed quantity, and the card totals everything at the top. A medication with no price configured shows **"No price set"** rather than a $0, and a row whose dispensed medicines have no price shows no payment badge — there is nothing to pay. None of this appears for a clinic marked **free** in Settings.

## After you dispense

Select **Dispense Prescriptions** and the success message appears, then you are back on the **Prescription List**. The row now reads **Dispensed**, and beside it a badge says **Payment pending**, **Part paid** or **Paid**. Nothing is charged by dispensing: payment is taken only when you choose **Collect payment** from the row, below.

- If a dispensed line did not draw from stock — a medicine that is not linked to a stock item, a line with no quantity, or not enough usable stock — a **Stock did not move** message says so in plain words. Each is also listed under Inventory → **Reconciliation** so the shelf can be put right.
- To hand the patient their slip, open the row's **⋯** menu and choose **Print dispensing slip** (see [Print from the list](#print-from-the-list)).
- A clinic marked **free** in Settings sees no payment badge and no **Collect payment**, because there is nothing to collect.

## Collect payment from the list

**Collect payment** in a row's **⋯** menu opens a panel beside the list — you do not leave the page, and nothing is recorded until you select **Collect**. It is greyed out, with "Your role can't take payments at this clinic. Ask an owner or manager." beneath it, if your role cannot take payments or you have not been given the **Collect payments** permission.

1. The medicines on that row that were dispensed and are still unpaid are **ticked**. The patient's other unpaid items are listed below, unticked, so you can add them.
2. A medicine with no price shows **No price set** and cannot be ticked; the line says who can set its price in Inventory.
3. To take part of a line, change **Pay now**. Choose the **Payment method** (Insurance appears only for a patient with insurance on file) and, if you like, add a note. The panel shows the subtotal, any tax or additional charge, and **To collect** in your clinic's currency.
4. Select **Collect**. **Payment recorded** appears with the reference, and **Print receipt** prints the receipt. If anything goes wrong you see the reason and nothing is marked paid.

In a clinic marked **free** the item does not appear at all. Payment needs a connection: with no internet the panel says **Payment needs a connection** and does not send anything. The row's menu then offers **Print payment receipt** instead of **Collect payment**. The same payment is also listed under [Payments](/hospital/revenue/payments).

## Print from the list

The **⋯** menu on a row also prints. **Print dispensing slip** and **Download slip PDF** appear once a line has been dispensed. **Print payment receipt** and **Download receipt PDF** appear once a dispensed line has been paid, and **Collect payment** appears while one is still unpaid. Anyone who can open the script can print it, and each print is recorded in the audit log.

Each medication also offers **Download PDF** — a server-generated prescription document (the patient's name, ID, date of birth, age and gender; medication, dosage, frequency, duration and the dosing schedule; quantity; the prescriber's name and licence, and the dispenser's once it is dispensed). A prescription not yet dispensed is stamped **"Not yet dispensed"**, so a partly-filled script can never be mistaken for the final record.

The prescriber's saved signature (**Account → My signature**) prints above their name and licence; without one, the document reads "Electronically signed by" and their name. A prescription written in a visit or on a ward shows no price — it is charged through the patient's bill. A walk-in prescription at the pharmacy shows a **Payment** section instead: the amount, what has been paid, the balance and the payment status, in your clinic's currency.

Beside **Download PDF**, **Send** emails the patient a private link instead of the file itself — see [Send a report securely](/platform/security/send-a-report) for what the recipient sees and how it is logged.

## Who can do this

Two different gates decide what you get here.

**Role** decides whether the module opens at all. The **Pharmacy** group needs the **In-house pharmacy** care area, and on Enterprise an owner can hide the group from a role entirely under **Settings → Role Navigation**.

**Permission** decides what you may do once inside.

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the list and **View Details** | Any staff member the role navigation lets in | Role → route |
| **Dispense** / **Update** a script | Owners, managers and pharmacists by default | **Settings → Facility → Clinical access → Dispense a prescription** — an owner can widen this to other roles; anyone outside the chosen list is refused |
| Write a new script | Prescribers and pharmacists | Role → route |
| Draw the stock in **Fill Rx** | Pharmacy and stock staff | Role → route |

Two behaviours surprise people. Enforcement is **opt-in per person** — a colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, which is why the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**. And a **denied action is recorded** while an allowed one is not; hiding the Pharmacy group from a role is a tidiness measure, not a security boundary.

## Check it worked

- A dispensed script shows **Dispensed** in the **Status** column, with your name under **Dispensed By**.
- The row leaves the pending part of the queue and can be found in [Prescription history](/hospital/pharmacy/prescription-history).
- If you drew stock in **Fill Rx**, the item's on-hand figure in [Inventory](/hospital/supply/inventory) has dropped by what you gave out.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The list says No prescriptions match your search criteria">
    There is text in **Search for prescription**. Clear it. The other empty state — **No prescriptions found.** — means the queue genuinely has nothing in it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A script a doctor just wrote is not here">
    The queue does not poll continuously. Select **Refresh**. If it still does not appear, check the prescriber saved it rather than leaving the form open.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I only see View Details, with no Dispense">
    The dispensing actions default to owners, managers and pharmacists. Ask an owner to check your role, or check **Settings → Facility → Clinical access → Dispense a prescription** for what this clinic has chosen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can see the row but the columns I need are missing">
    **Patient Type**, **Date** and **Mode** are hidden by default. Switch them on under **View Columns**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Pharmacy group in my sidebar">
    It needs the Enterprise plan and the **In-house pharmacy** care area in **Settings → Facility**. See [Care areas](/hospital/get-started/care-areas).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same list as Prescription History?">
    No. This is the working queue for your Pharmacy queue window. History is the cross-day record of everything ever dispensed, and it is the one to search when a patient asks what they were given last month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do some rows say Ward Prescription?">
    They belong to an admitted patient, written on the ward rather than in an outpatient clinic. They dispense the same way; only the wording changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I correct a script I have already dispensed?">
    A pharmacist gets **Update** on a dispensed row. The original record is not erased — the change is recorded against your name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does dispensing here take the medicine off the shelf?">
    Not on its own. Stock moves when you draw it in the **Fill Rx** tab of Inventory. Keeping the two steps distinct is what makes the stock figure trustworthy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I mark one item Out of Stock while dispensing the rest of the same script?">
    Yes. Each medication on the script has its own **Dispensing Status**, so you can leave two lines at **Dispensed** and set a third to **Out of Stock**, **Pending** or **Cancelled** in the same save — only the lines you dispense need a quantity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell a script's items at the counter instead?">
    Ordinary items, yes — see [the pharmacy counter](/hospital/pharmacy/pos). Anything needing a witnessed dispense is refused at the till by design.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
