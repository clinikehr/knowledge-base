**Inventory** is one stock record for the whole hospital — "Stock, lots, and expiry across your rooms and devices." The dispensary, the wards, theatre and the online store all draw on it, which is what stops two departments holding two different answers to "how many do we have".

## What you're looking at

Eight buttons run along the header:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Receive** | Books stock in against an item |
| **Dispense** | Takes stock out |
| **Transfer** | Moves stock between locations |
| **Reports** | Valuation and movement reporting |
| **Import** | Brings a catalogue in from a file |
| **Opening stock** | Sets your starting position when you first go live |
| **Export** | Takes the catalogue or stock out again |
| **Add item** | Creates one item by hand |
| **Link medicines** | Shown to owners, managers and anyone the clinic lets dispense. Matches medicines that are not linked to a stock item, and unlinks one that is — see [Link medicines to stock](#link-medicines-to-stock) |
| **Catalogue medicines** | Owners and managers. Switches medicines on or off for the prescriber's picker — see [Choose what doctors can prescribe](#choose-what-doctors-can-prescribe) |

Below them, the tabs. A hospital normally sees eleven, with two more appearing when they apply:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | Every item you stock |
| **Stock** | What is on hand, by lot and location |
| **Ledger** | Every movement — received, dispensed, transferred |
| **Counts** | Cycle counts, for reconciling the shelf against the record |
| **Fill Rx** | Prescriptions waiting to be drawn from stock |
| **Purchasing** | Suppliers and purchase orders |
| **Vouchers** | Goods received vouchers |
| **Recalls** | Vendor and regulator recalls, and who was exposed |
| **Locations** | Your rooms, stores and devices |
| **Alerts (n)** | Low stock, expiry and other open alerts, plus a **Ledger reconciliation** card listing completed tests, dispensed prescriptions and the like whose stock was never taken off the shelf. Open a row to take it off, return it to stock, or mark it reconciled with a reason — the row leaves when it is done |
| **Reconciliation** | Dispensing enforcement and reconciliation. A dispense that did not draw from stock is listed here, with **Link to stock** for a medicine that is not linked |
| **Vaccines** | Appears where vaccine stock exists |
| **Ward MAR** | Appears where you run wards |

Where an alert is open, a line above the tabs says so: "\{n\} open inventory alerts — see the Alerts tab." Empty states are per tab — **Stock** reads "No stock on hand. Receive a lot to get started."

## The two tabs that make this a hospital screen

Most of Inventory works the same anywhere. Two tabs exist because the hospital has departments hanging off the stock record:

**Fill Rx** is the bridge to the pharmacy unit. A prescription written anywhere in the hospital waits here until someone draws stock against it, with a witness where the item is controlled. See [the pharmacy station](/hospital/pharmacy/station). Filling from stock follows the same **Settings → Facility → Clinical access → Dispense a prescription** choice as the pharmacy counter — with one difference: until an owner saves a role on that row, filling from stock stays open to everyone on the team, while the counter's own dispense screen follows its **Pharmacist** default from the start.

**Ward MAR** is the bridge to the wards, and appears only when your hospital runs them — "Each dose given to an admitted patient depletes stock and is logged here." That is the connection people miss: a nurse signing a dose on the drug chart is a stock movement, not just a clinical note. See [the nursing station](/hospital/inpatient/nursing-station).

## Link medicines to stock

A prescription can only draw stock when its medicine is linked to a stock item. A medicine that is not linked is still dispensed, and shows under **Reconciliation** as **Not linked to inventory**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Link medicines">
    Select **Link medicines** in the header, or **Link to stock** on a **Not linked to inventory** row under **Reconciliation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the matching item">
    Each unlinked medicine shows up to three stock items with similar names. An exact name match is marked **Exact name**. Select the item that is really the same medicine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the row">
    Select **Link to this item**. Linking changes nothing else: no stock moves, no item is created, and a dispense that already happened is not drawn again. If the medicine had no price, it takes the stock item's selling price; a price already set is kept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Link all exact matches** is the only bulk action. It links only medicines whose name matches exactly one stock item that no other medicine uses, and asks you to confirm first. A similar name is always confirmed row by row.

### Unlink a medicine

The sheet has two tabs. **Not linked** is where you link. **Linked** lists every medicine that draws stock, with the stock item it draws from.

- A medicine that shares its stock item with another medicine is marked **Shares this item with 1 other medicine**, and these are listed first. Choose **Sharing a stock item** in the filter to see only them. This usually means an older medicine from your starter list was linked to an item your own medicine already uses.
- **Different name** marks a medicine whose name is not the stock item's name.

Select **Unlink** on the row and confirm. Only that medicine loses its link. Any other medicine on the same stock item stays linked, and the stock item, its stock and its price do not change. From then on, a prescription for the unlinked medicine does not draw stock until you link it again.

If the list changed while you had it open (someone else linked or unlinked the same medicine), the unlink is refused with a message rather than applied to the new link. Close the sheet and open it again to see the current links.

### Delete or archive a medicine you no longer want

After you unlink a medicine, an owner or manager is asked what to do with it:

- **Delete medicine** is offered when the medicine has never been prescribed, sold or dispensed. It removes the medicine for good.
- **Archive medicine** is offered when it has prescriptions or sales, because those records still need it. Archiving takes it off every prescriber's list; its past prescriptions and sales stay as they are. You can switch it back on in **Catalogue medicines**.

**Keep it** leaves the medicine unlinked and still prescribable. You can come back to it later: on the **Not linked** tab, owners and managers have **Delete or archive** on every row.

**Link to stock** on a **Reconciliation** row links the medicine first and marks the entry reconciled only once the link has been saved. **Mark reconciled** on its own records that you have dealt with the entry; it does not link the medicine.

## Choose what doctors can prescribe

The prescriber's medicine list shows a badge beside each medicine — **In stock 24**, **Out of stock** or **Not stocked** — and lists stocked medicines first. Nothing is hidden because of quantity, so a medicine that is out of stock can still be prescribed.

To remove medicines nobody stocks, an owner or manager selects **Catalogue medicines**, filters to **Not linked to stock**, selects the medicines and chooses **Switch off**. They stop appearing when a doctor prescribes; prescriptions already written are not changed, and you can switch them back on.

## Receive a delivery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Receive">
    From the header, or from the row of the item you are booking in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the lot and its expiry">
    Record the lot number and its expiry date. This is the step everything else depends on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the location and save">
    Say which room, store or device the stock is going to — picking later only offers what that location holds. The on-hand figure rises and a **Ledger** entry records who booked it in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Picking is earliest-expiry-first, and an undated lot is invisible to it.** A lot received with no expiry date will not be picked ahead of anything, so short-dated stock sits at the back of the shelf until it expires. Switch on **Require expiry date on new lots** under [Inventory settings](/hospital/settings/inventory) and the problem stops arising.
</Note>

## Who can do this

**Role** decides whether the screen opens. **Inventory** needs the **Inventory** care area in **Settings → Facility**, and on Enterprise an owner controls which roles reach the **Supply Chain** group under **Settings → Role Navigation**.

**Permission** decides what you may do inside. The acts a stock screen distinguishes, in the product's own words:

| Action | Permission | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book a delivery in | *No permission* | Reaching **Inventory** is the gate |
| Correct a count or write off damage | *No permission* | Reaching **Inventory** is the gate |
| Move stock between sites | *No permission* | Reaching **Inventory** is the gate |
| Raise and approve orders | **Manage suppliers and purchase orders** | "Add suppliers, raise and approve purchase orders" |
| Countersign a controlled dispense | **Witness a controlled-drug dispense** | "Countersign the register when a controlled drug leaves the shelf" |
| See what stock cost | **View cost prices and margin** | "See what stock cost and what each sale makes" |

Two things to know. First, that is the **Pharmacy edition's** permission catalogue: a hospital's own staff sheet offers the clinical and billing toggles instead, so on Enterprise these stock acts are open to anyone whose role reaches the screen. Restrict access at the **Role Navigation** layer, not by hunting for these toggles.

Second, that withholding is a Pharmacy-edition behaviour too. A standalone pharmacy that denies **View cost prices and margin** sees "Hidden — needs authorisation from your clinic owner." in place of the figure, never a **0**. A hospital has no such switch, so everyone who can open Inventory sees every cost and valuation on it.

Enforcement generally is **opt-in per person**: a colleague never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**. A **denied action is recorded**; an allowed one is not.

## Archive, restore or delete an item, and write off a lot

An item you no longer sell does not have to disappear. **Archive** takes it out of sale and dispensing and out of new stock movements, but keeps everything recorded against it. Archived items **stay in the Catalog** with an **Archived** badge and muted text, so you can still find them and bring them back.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item's menu">
    On **Catalog**, select **Item actions** on the item's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to do">
    **Archive** (on an active item) or **Restore** (on an archived one). A box says what will happen; select **Archive** or **Restore** to confirm. An archived item shows its quantity on hand, and **Receive** and **Dispense** are not offered until you restore it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete an item that was never used">
    **Delete** appears in the menu only for an item that has never been stocked, sold, ordered or used anywhere. The box says it is permanent; select **Delete item** to confirm. Anything with history cannot be deleted and has to be archived instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An item that still has stock on hand cannot be archived. The error names the quantity: move it with **Transfer**, or remove it with **Waste**, then archive.

To clear a whole lot, open **Stock**, select **Stock actions** on one of the lot's rows and choose **Write off lot**. Enter a **Reason**, select **Continue**, then select **Write off lot** to confirm. The remaining units are removed through the stock record, so the **Ledger** shows what was written off and why, and the lot is archived. A lot is never deleted.

If an action is refused, the message under the toast says why. Archive, restore, delete and write-off need the **Manage the inventory catalogue** permission; owners and managers always have it.

## Filter the Catalog and Stock tabs

Both tabs have a filter bar above the list. Every filter is applied to your whole inventory, not just the page on screen, so the count above the list — "12 items", or "3 stock lines match your filters" — is the true total and the page buttons follow it. Search still works and narrows within whatever filters you have set.

**On a computer** the main filters sit in a row: **Type**, **Category**, **Stock level**, **Status** and **Expiry** on **Catalog**; **Location**, **Lot status**, **Expiry**, **Quantity** and **Type** on **Stock**. **More filters** opens the rest. **On a phone or tablet** a single **Filters** button, with a badge showing how many filters are on, opens them all in a panel. Choices apply as you make them; **Done** closes the panel.

**Catalog** can be filtered by:

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | One or more item types, such as medication, supply or vaccine |
| **Category** | One or more categories, including **Uncategorised** |
| **Stock level** | **In stock**, **Low (at or below par)** and **Out of stock**. Low means you hold some, and no more than the par level set on the item |
| **Status** | **All items** (the default), **Active items** or **Archived**. Archived items carry an **Archived** badge and a **Restore** action |
| **Expiry** | Items with expired stock, or stock expiring within 30, 60 or 90 days |
| **Properties** | **Controlled**, **Refrigerated**, **Hazardous** and **Sold at the till**. Pick several and an item must have all of them |
| **Barcode** | **Has a barcode** or **No barcode** — handy for finding what a scanner cannot read |
| **Medicine index** | Items **Linked to the medicine index** or **Not linked** |
| **Manufacturer**, **Default supplier** | Items from the makers and suppliers you choose |
| **Has stock at** | Items you hold in the locations you choose |
| **Sale price**, **Cost** | A minimum and a maximum. **Cost** appears only if you are allowed to see cost prices |

**Stock** can be filtered by **Location**, **Lot status** (active, quarantined, recalled, expired, depleted), **Expiry** (**Expired**, **Within 30 days**, **Within 60 days**, **Within 90 days**, **No expiry date**), an **Expires between** date range, **Quantity** (**Zero quantity**, **Has reserved units**, **Fully reserved**, **Item below par**), **Type**, **Category**, **Funding source** and **Item**. **Item** searches as you type, by name, barcode or SKU, so it finds any product however large your catalogue is.

Each filter you set appears as a chip under the bar. Select a chip to take that one off, or **Clear all** to start again (on **Catalog**, back to **All items**). **Sort** orders the list — by name, on hand, price or recently updated on **Catalog**; by item, expiry, quantity or location on **Stock**.

Your filters are kept in the page address, so a refresh keeps them, the back button returns to them, and you can send the link to a colleague — it opens the same tab with the same filters. If the filters hide everything, the list says **No items match these filters.** (or **No stock lines match these filters.**) with a **Clear filters** button.

<Note>
  Cost filters follow the same permission as cost figures. If you are not allowed to see cost prices, there is no **Cost** filter or cost sort, and the list never uses cost to narrow what you see.
</Note>

## Check it worked

- The item's on-hand figure on **Stock** matches the shelf.
- A **Ledger** entry names you, the movement and the time.
- A dispensed script has left **Fill Rx**, and ward doses appear under **Ward MAR** having reduced the stock they came from.
- **Alerts (n)** falls on its own as low-stock and expiry warnings stop being true — receive stock above the threshold, correct an expiry date, use a lot up or archive the item, and the alert leaves the tab without a refresh.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Some of this stock is past its expiry date and cannot be transferred.'">
    The shelf holds the quantity you asked for, but part of it has passed its expiry date, and **Transfer** only moves stock that can still be used. Transfer a smaller quantity, or remove the expired stock first with **Waste** or **Return to vendor**. Your count is not wrong — expired stock is also written off automatically overnight.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transfer says some of this stock is close to expiry">
    This is a warning, not a refusal — **Transfer** never blocks stock that is merely close to its date, only stock already past it (see above). Once you enter a quantity, the form lists which lot it would come from, how much, and how many days remain, soonest first, right under **Quantity**. If the whole quantity would come from short-dated stock it says so plainly. The **Transfer** button stays enabled and nothing extra needs confirming — moving short-dated stock to a busier location is often the right call. "Close to expiry" is your facility's own expiry-alert horizon under **Settings** › **Inventory** › **Expiry alert horizons**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stock is right on the shelf but wrong on screen">
    Run a cycle count from the **Counts** tab rather than adjusting item by item — a count reconciles and records why; an adjustment only changes the number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A short-dated lot is not being picked first">
    It has no expiry date, so earliest-expiry-first cannot rank it. Add the date, and switch on **Require expiry date on new lots**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Ward MAR tab is missing">
    It appears only where wards exist — see [Wards and beds](/hospital/get-started/wards-and-beds).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A prescription is not in Fill Rx">
    Either it is already filled, or it was never saved. Check the [prescription list](/hospital/pharmacy/prescriptions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Valuation reads Hidden — needs authorisation">
    Cost and margin figures are withheld from your account. The screen still works; the money column does not show.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inventory is missing from the sidebar">
    It needs the Enterprise plan and the **Inventory** care area in **Settings → Facility**. See [Care areas](/hospital/get-started/care-areas).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the pharmacy unit have its own separate stock?">
    No — one hospital, one stock record. The pharmacy station's **Inventory** tab is this same screen, which is why a counter sale and a ward issue move the same figure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Opening stock for?">
    Setting your starting position when you first go live, so day one's figures are your real shelf rather than zero. Use it once, not as a way to correct a count later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What appears on the Recalls tab?">
    Vendor or regulator recalls. Opening one quarantines the affected lots and lists the patients who received them — the part you cannot reconstruct by hand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the online store take stock from here?">
    Yes. A store product links to an inventory item, and its stock and cost are read-only in the store — see [the online store](/hospital/revenue/store).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I stock-take with a handheld scanner?">
    Yes — see [Stock counts](/pharmacy/inventory/stock-counts) and [CipherLab counting](/pharmacy/inventory/cipherlab).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
