**Front Desk** is reception's home screen. It answers one question — who is in the building and who is still waiting — and puts the four things a receptionist does all day within one screen of that answer.

The whole group needs the **Front desk** care area switched on in **Settings → Facility**. See [Care areas](/hospital/get-started/care-areas).

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Front Desk', 'Front Desk']} />

## What is in the Front Desk group

The group holds four entries, and they stand or fall together: switching the care area off removes all four from the sidebar at once.

| Entry | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Front Desk** | The live check-in board on this page |
| **Appointments** | The appointment book, services and the booking page |
| **Patients** | The patient list, registration and the self-registration waitlist |
| **Contacts** | Enquiries, follow-ups and campaigns |

**Patients** also appears under **Records** as **Patient Records**. Both entries open the same list and both highlight at once — that is expected, not a fault.

## Reading the board

Three tiles run across the top: **Waiting now**, **Long waits** and **Seen today**. Each shows an em dash while it loads rather than a zero, so a figure on screen is always a real count.

Below the tiles, four buttons cover the whole of reception — **Check in**, **Book appointment**, **New patient** and **New contact**. Each opens a sheet over the board, so you never lose the queue you were looking at. Registering someone with **New patient** flows straight into checking them in, which is the actual walk-in motion.

The queue itself is filtered by three tabs — **Waiting**, **Checked out** and **All**, each carrying its own count — and searched by reason, room or staff member with **Search reason, room or staff…**. The columns are:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Checked in** | The time they arrived |
| **Waiting** | How long they have been waiting |
| **For** | The visit items — a service, a lab test or a custom label |
| **With** | The staff member they are assigned to, or an em dash |
| **Location** | The room, if one was recorded |
| **Status** | A coloured badge for the wait band |

Select any row to reopen that visit. Each row also carries **View** and, until the person leaves, **Check out**. Checking someone out takes effect immediately with no confirmation — it is reversible by checking them back in, and a confirmation on every one would slow a busy desk to a crawl.

When nothing is queued the board reads **Nobody waiting**.

## The long-wait threshold

A visit is flagged as a long wait after a number of minutes your clinic sets, and the board prints the current figure under the table. Change it in **Settings → Facility** under **Front desk waiting**. The tile, the badge and the row colour all follow the same number, so there is only ever one definition of "too long" in the building.
