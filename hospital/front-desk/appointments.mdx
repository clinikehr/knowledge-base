**Appointments** is the hospital's book. In a solo practice patients mostly book themselves; at a hospital front desk it is usually the other way round — reception books on the patient's behalf, onto a named provider and service, while the patient is at the counter or on the phone.

## What is in the workspace

The appointments sidebar has four entries. **Overview** is the calendar itself; the other three open the matching page in clinic **Settings**, so do not be surprised when the screen changes around you.

| Entry | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The calendar — the book itself |
| **Services** | **Appointment Services**, the bookable things a patient can be given a slot for |
| **Booking Page** | **Booking Page Designer**, the public page patients book on |
| **Settings** | **Appointment Settings** — availability, rules, resources, dates and communication |

## Read the calendar

**Overview** is a full-screen calendar, not a dashboard — there are no stat tiles, because at a front desk the day itself is the summary.

- Move with **Today** and the arrows either side of the month.
- Switch how the day is arranged: **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Providers** or **Resources**. **Providers** is the hospital view — one lane per clinician, which is how you spot the consultant with a hole in their morning.
- Colour the book **By Status** or **By Service**, and narrow it with **Search patients, services...** or **More filters**.

## Book an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Create">
    Or select the empty slot you want on the calendar — both open the same **Initial Entry** sheet, on the **Appointment** tab. The other two tabs, **Event** and **Task**, put something in the day that is not a patient booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the patient">
    Search under **Patient**. If they are not registered, select **Create patient** without leaving the sheet — or register them properly first in [Register and find patients](/hospital/front-desk/patients).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the service">
    **Services** offers the bookable list — "Select clinic service". This is what the visit is for, and what it is charged at.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule, provider and place">
    **Schedule** is the date and time, **Select Provider** the staff member, and **Location** the room or address. A resource attached to the service shows as **Auto-reserved for this service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what the clinician needs">
    **Symptoms Description**, **Patient Notes** and **Clinic Notes**. Set **Notification** so the patient is told, then read the **Appointment Summary** back before you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **A booking is made against a provider, not a department.** There is no department field on this sheet — the department comes later, at [triage](/hospital/outpatient/triage), where the patient is routed into a consultation queue. Booking the right clinician is what matters here.
</Note>

## Set your availability

<Path steps={['Appointments', 'Settings']} />

**Appointment Settings** — "Configure availability, special dates, and communication preferences" — has five tabs:

| Tab | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Availability** | **Clinic Availability**, your operating hours and slots. **Add Schedule** for each pattern you keep |
| **Booking Rules** | How far ahead people may book, and how late a change is accepted |
| **Resources** | Rooms and equipment a booking claims, so two clinics cannot take the same one |
| **Special Dates** | Holidays, off-days and special hours. **Add Special Date** |
| **Communication** | **Confirmation Settings**, **Reminder Settings** and **Message Templates** |

Enter special dates in advance and in bulk. Every holiday you forget is a patient who arrives to a locked door.

Reminders need a channel as well as a schedule. The channels — SMS, voice, fax and email — live under **Settings → Communications**, and a reminder with no enabled channel reaches nobody.

## Charge the card automatically

<Path steps={['Settings', 'Auto Pay']} />

**Auto Pay** — "Automatically charge a patient's saved card on file when their appointment is confirmed or completed — no manual invoicing." Switch on **Enable Auto Pay**, then pick the trigger under **Charge when the appointment is…**: **Confirmed**, **Completed**, or both. Each invoice is only ever charged once, and **Email a receipt to the patient** sends a confirmation each time.

It needs the **Essential** plan or above and a connected Stripe account — without one you get "Connect Stripe to use Auto Pay". Only the owner can change it.

<Warning>
  Auto Pay carries a **1.9% platform fee per charge**, on top of your normal card processing, and it charges a real card with nobody at the desk pressing a button. Confirm your patients have consented to a card on file before switching it on, and check the first day's charges by hand.
</Warning>

Patients without a saved card are skipped and left for manual collection, and a card that is declined or needs 3-D Secure is never force-charged — you are notified to collect it instead.

## Check it worked

- The appointment is on the calendar for the right day, and the **Providers** view shows it in that clinician's lane.
- The patient's confirmation reached them on the channel you expect.
- A slot outside a provider's schedule is not offered, and a special date is closed off entirely.
- With Auto Pay on, confirming an appointment records a payment against it without anyone taking one.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Appointments entry in my sidebar">
    The **Front desk** care area is off — see [Turn on your care areas](/hospital/get-started/care-areas).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find a department field on the booking">
    There is not one. Appointments are booked onto a provider; the department is chosen at triage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A provider has no bookable times">
    They have no schedule under **Availability**, or a special date covers the period. Check the schedule first — an empty one offers nothing rather than everything.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Auto Pay is not charging">
    Three things must all hold: the **Essential** plan or above, a connected Stripe account, and a card saved for that patient. Any one missing and the appointment stays unpaid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reminders are not going out">
    Check the channel under **Settings → Communications** before you touch the reminder schedule. See [Notifications](/platform/settings/notifications).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two clinics were booked into the same room">
    The room was not added under **Resources**. A resource is what makes the book refuse a double booking.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
