Money reaches a hospital two ways: somebody hands it over at a desk and you record it, or you send a link and they pay by card. **Payments Management** covers both, and shows what is still outstanding.

## What you're looking at

The heading reads **Payments Management** — "Process and track payments from patients for services, medications, and more". **Refresh** and **New Payment** sit beside it.

Four tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Total Collected**, **Pending Balance**, **Recent Payments** and **Top Method**, with a method breakdown and recent activity |
| **Payments** | Every payment recorded, searchable and filterable |
| **Pending** | Patients with an outstanding balance |
| **Total income** | Billing and the Pharmacy till together, per day and by payment method, for the dates you pick. See [See your total income in one place](#see-your-total-income-in-one-place) |

The **Payments** table shows **Date**, **Patient**, **Invoice**, **Amount**, **Method**, **Type**, **Reference** and **Received By**. **Filter by Method** narrows it to **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank Transfer**, **Mobile Money** or **Insurance**. Each row's **Actions** menu offers **View Details**, **Download Receipt**, **Void Payment**, **Copy Payment ID** and **Copy Payment Info**.

The **Pending** table lists **Patient Name**, **Patient ID**, **Items**, **Oldest Item** and **Total Pending**, with **Process Payment** on the row. **Oldest Item** is what to sort a chase list by.

**Process Payment** (and **View Items** on the row) opens **New Payment** already on that patient, with their unpaid items ready to tick; you are not asked to find them again.

A patient is listed only when something is genuinely payable. A consultation counts at the amount recorded on it, so a visit opened without a price, or at no charge, adds nothing and no longer shows a balance with no items behind it. A lab test or imaging study is payable from the moment it is ordered, whether it comes from a consultation or a ward stay, and a **cancelled** test or study is never offered for payment.

**Empty states.** With nothing outstanding, **Pending** reads **No Pending Payments** and "All patients have cleared their pending payments. Great job!" A search that matches nobody says "No patients found matching your search with pending payments." instead.

## See your total income in one place

Billing and the Pharmacy till used to be two separate screens, so working out what actually came in meant adding them up yourself. The **Total income** tab does it for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    On **Payments**, select **Total income**. It opens on the last 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the dates">
    Choose a range with the date picker above the figures. Everything on the tab follows it: the four figures, the chart and both tables. One click on a day means that single day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it">
    **Total income** is **Billing** plus the **Pharmacy POS** takings after refunds. **Refunded at the till** shows what was handed back. **Income by day** splits each day by where the money came in, **By payment method** lays Billing beside the Pharmacy till for cash, card, bank transfer and the rest, and **Day by day** lists every day that had money in or out, newest first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What counts, so the figure matches your own books:

- **Billing.** Every payment recorded against an invoice or taken at the desk, on the day it was recorded. A **voided** payment is left out.
- **Insurers.** The part of a bill an insurer still owes is **not** income yet. It counts on the day you record the insurer's payment, from [HMO receivables](/hospital/insurance/hmo-payments), and not again when you share that payment across claims.
- **Pharmacy POS.** What customers paid at the till, and any later instalment on a part-paid sale on the day it was paid. A cancelled sale is left out. A customer paying from their account balance counts when the balance is spent on a sale, not when it was topped up.
- **Refunds.** Taken off on the day of the refund, under the method the sale was paid with. A refund never reduces income by more than the sale actually took.
- **Days.** Each day is a day at your clinic, in your clinic's time zone, so a sale at 11:30 pm is on the day it happened.

When nothing came in for the dates you picked, the tab says so — "No income was received for ..." — and shows zeros, rather than leaving you to wonder whether it loaded.

<Note>
  **Total Collected** on **Overview** counts Billing payments only and covers all time, so it will not match **Total income** for the dates you pick. Use **Total income** for the whole picture.
</Note>

## Record a payment against an invoice

This is the usual route, because it settles a specific balance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the invoice">
    Under **Billing → Invoices**, open **Invoice Actions** and select **Record Payment**. The dialog names the invoice and its balance due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the amount">
    **Amount** is prefilled with the balance due. Overtype it for a part payment — the invoice then reads **Partially paid** and keeps the remainder outstanding. Zero is refused: "Enter an amount greater than zero".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the method">
    **Payment Method** defaults to **Cash**, with **Card**, **Bank Transfer**, **Mobile Money**, **Insurance** and **Other** alongside. Record what actually happened — the **Overview** tab breaks takings down by method, and a transfer logged as cash quietly ruins that. If your hospital bills HMOs (Settings → General → Insurance set to **Outside the US**), **Insurance** is not a method: use [Bill HMO](/hospital/insurance/bill-hmo) for the insurer's share and take the patient's share with any method here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reference and save">
    **Reference (optional)** takes the transfer reference, cheque number or terminal receipt number. It is what makes a payment findable when a patient queries it months later. Select **Record Payment**: you get **Payment recorded** and the balance drops immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **You cannot record more than the invoice still owes.** An amount above the balance is refused with "That is more than this invoice still owes. Check whether the payment has already been recorded, then confirm it if you still want to record it." It is almost always the same payment entered twice — an online payment records itself. If the patient really did pay extra, record it from the patient's record with **Record payment**, which asks you to confirm it first. An online payment that arrives for more than an invoice still owed is recorded, never turned away, and you are told in-app: **Payment received on a settled invoice**.
</Note>

## Take a payment with no invoice behind it

**New Payment** opens a fuller form for money taken at a desk against services rather than a bill.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the patient">
    **Patient Details** searches with "Search for a patient...". Any consultation or flat-rate credits they hold are shown here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what is being paid for">
    **Payment Items** — "Select what is being paid for" — has two inner tabs: **Direct Payment** for services and consultation rates, and **Pay Invoice** to settle an existing bill. Each item takes a **Pay:** amount, so a part payment is badged **Partial Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the method and amount">
    **Payment Method** offers six tiles — **Cash**, **Card**, **Insurance**, **Mobile Money**, **Bank Transfer** and **Other** — then **Amount to Pay**, **Reference (Optional)** and **Notes (Optional)**. Hospitals set to **Outside the US** see five tiles — there is no **Insurance** tile — and a **Bill HMO** button for a patient with an insurer on file; see [Bill an HMO at the till](/hospital/insurance/bill-hmo).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process it">
    A **Payment Successful** dialog shows the reference, date and method, and offers **Print Receipt**. Part-paid items are called out: "⚠️ This includes partial payments. Some items have remaining balances."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reduce what a patient pays (subsidy)

When the clinic subsidises or reduces a charge for a patient, record the amount the patient will actually pay, so the item reads **Paid** instead of **Partially paid** or **Pending**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the item and choose Reduced amount">
    Under **Payment Items**, tick the item. Next to its **Pay:** box, choose **Reduced amount**. The button shows only to owners, managers and anyone an owner has given **Apply reduced amounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter what the patient will pay, and why">
    **Amount the patient will pay** is the total for that item after the subsidy. **Reason (required)** cannot be left empty. The panel shows **Patient pays now** and **Subsidy (not collected)** before you apply it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process the payment">
    The item now takes exactly that amount, shows **Reduced to … · Subsidy …**, and after the payment it reads **Paid** and no longer appears under **Pending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **The amount must be more than zero, less than the charge, and no less than anything already paid on the item.** Waiving an item completely is a write-off, not a reduced amount, and is not recorded here. You can reduce one item and charge another in full in the same payment.
- **Not for an item billed to an HMO.** The HMO and the patient already share its price, so **Reduced amount** is not offered for it.
- **A subsidy is not money received.** The payment total, **Total income** and the revenue figures count only what was paid. Reductions are listed on their own under **Payments** in **Reduced amounts**, with the date, item and who gave it, and voided payments are left out. The reason is shown to owners, managers and anyone who holds **Apply reduced amounts**.
- **The receipt says so.** The item is marked "Subsidy … — paid in full" and the receipt has a **Subsidy** line. It is never counted as paid and never leaves a balance due.
- **Not with Insurance.** A reduced amount cannot be combined with an insurance payment. Choosing **Insurance** removes any reduction you set.
- **To change it, untick the item.** Unticking removes the reduction and the item goes back to its full balance.

## Send a payment link instead

If the patient is not in front of you, send a link and let them pay by card.

<Note>
  **This is reached from an appointment, not from the patient's record.** Open
  the appointment in the calendar and take a payment from there. The **Payments**
  page and the patient's record record money that has already been handed over;
  neither of them sends a link.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the appointment and start a payment">
    Select **Send payment link** — "Collect payment via a secure link sent to contact".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it covers">
    Pick the invoices the link should settle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it goes to">
    **Send to** offers the same three choices as sending an invoice: the client's
    email on file, **A different email**, or **Both**. Choose a different email
    when someone else is paying — an employer, an insurer contact, a family
    member — and enter up to three addresses separated by commas.

    If the client has no email on file, the on-file choices say so and are not
    selectable; add one to their record, or send the link to a different email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send Link**. You get "Payment link sent to \{email\}" naming every
    recipient, and the invoices it covers are marked as sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Everyone listed gets the link, but the card receipt goes to one address** —
  the client when the client is a recipient, otherwise the first address you
  entered. That is the address the payment page is prefilled with.
</Note>

<Note>
  **A payment link expires 24 hours after it is sent** — the email says so, and offers a fresh one on request. Links also need a connected payment account under **Settings → Online Payments**. A hospital collecting through Paystack cannot use Stripe links: "This clinic collects payments via Paystack — Stripe payment links are not available." Send your bank details on the invoice instead.
</Note>

## Void a payment taken in error

<Warning>
  Voiding is the only way back, and only these roles can do it: "Only a clinic owner, manager or accountant can void a payment." The dialog explains what happens — the payment "will be marked voided and the invoice balance restored. The record is kept for your books and audit trail." Give a **Reason (optional)** anyway; a voided payment with no reason is a question somebody will ask later.
</Warning>

Nothing is deleted. The voided row stays visible, struck through and badged **Voided**, and the invoice returns to its outstanding balance.

## Who can do this

**Role** decides whether **Payments** opens; an owner sets that per role under **Settings → Role Navigation**.

Once Payments opens, almost nothing narrows further:

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record a payment | Anyone who can open Payments | The **Collect payments** permission, for anyone you have saved in the permissions sheet — owners and managers always can |
| Record an insurer's settlement | Anyone who can open Payments | Nothing |
| See the money figures on **Overview** | Anyone who can open Payments | Nothing |
| Open the payment reports | Anyone who can open Payments | Nothing |
| See **Total income** | Anyone who can open Payments | Nothing |
| Reduce what a patient pays (**Reduced amount**) | Owners and managers | The **Apply reduced amounts** permission. It is off for everyone else, even staff you have not saved in the permissions sheet, until the owner switches it on for a person |
| Void money already taken | Owners, managers, accountants | A server check nobody bypasses — "Only a clinic owner, manager or accountant can void a payment." |

Your hospital's staff sheet does carry a **Billing** section of per-person toggles — **Create billing documents**, **Add client payments**, **Add insurance payments**, **Edit service fees**, **View Payment Reports**, **View financial dashboard**, **Refund payments** — and they are recorded against each person. No billing screen reads them, so they change nothing today. Restrict billing at the **Role Navigation** layer instead.

Someone without **Collect payments** sees **Process Patient Payment** and **Record Payment** greyed out with "Your role can't take payments at this clinic. Ask an owner or manager." Enforcement is **opt-in per person** — someone never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**. A **denied action is recorded**; an allowed one is not. No figure on this page is withheld by permission — if you can open Payments you see all of it, **Total income** included.

## Check it worked

- The payment is under **Payments** with your name in **Received By**, the method and your reference.
- The invoice reads **Paid**, or **Partially paid** with the correct remaining balance.
- The **Pending** tab no longer lists that balance.
- A voided payment is struck through and badged **Voided**, and the invoice balance is back.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The prefilled amount is not what they paid">
    Overtype it. The prefill is the balance due, not a fixed value.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I recorded a payment against the wrong invoice">
    Void it with a reason, then record it correctly. You cannot delete an invoice once it has a payment, and voiding is limited to owners, managers and accountants.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send payment link is not offered, or is refused">
    Either no account is connected under **Settings → Online Payments**, or your hospital collects through Paystack and Stripe links are unavailable. There is also a small minimum amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient says the link no longer works">
    Links last 24 hours. Send a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="They paid online but the invoice still says Pending">
    Online payments record themselves, but not always instantly. Select **Refresh** before recording it by hand — recording it manually as well would double-count the money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Figures on Overview show as dashes">
    You may open the screen but not see the financial figures. Viewing the dashboard and viewing payment reports are separate permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A lab test or imaging study never showed up under Pending Items">
    Fixed — a test or study ordered against a ward admission (rather than an outpatient visit) could be missing from **Pending Items** entirely, so its cost could never be collected here. It now appears alongside everything else.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A dispensed prescription is not under Pending">
    A prescription is listed once it is **Dispensed** and unpaid, whether a doctor wrote it in a visit, it belongs to an admitted patient, or a pharmacist wrote it in the pharmacy station. After a dispense, **Collect payment** on the dispensing screen opens this page with the patient selected. A medicine with no price set is still listed, marked **No price set**; set its selling price under Inventory and it can then be paid.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a direct payment and an invoice payment?">
    A direct payment is money taken against a service with no bill raised — common at a hospital front desk. An invoice payment settles a specific document. Both appear under **Payments**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does voiding refund the patient's card?">
    No. It corrects your record and restores the invoice balance. Returning money charged to a card is done with your payment provider.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do pharmacy counter takings appear?">
    In [Pharmacy sales](/hospital/pharmacy/sales), not here. A counter sale is settled on the spot and is not an invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I print a receipt later?">
    Yes — **Download Receipt** on the payment's row, or **Print Receipt** from the payment view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
