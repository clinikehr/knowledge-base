The **Lab Test List** is the bench's inbox: **Here's a list of all lab requests for today!** From here you pick up a request, run it, and record what the analyser or the microscope said.

## Before you start

**Laboratory** and **Radiology** come from one switch — the **Diagnostics** care area in **Settings → Facility**. If the group is missing from your sidebar entirely, that is why; see [Care areas](/hospital/get-started/care-areas).

Requests reach this list from a [consultation](/hospital/outpatient/consultation), a ward chart, or **New Test Report**, which opens the ordering form on [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports). Everything you request together for one patient from **New Test Report** is **one row**, however many tests it holds: five tests for Mary Jones are Mary Jones once, with all five inside it, exactly like a doctor's request from a consultation. Select the row and every test opens together for result entry, and **Download results** saves them as one report. An order with no visit behind it reads **Direct lab order** in the patient type, and one a midwife raised from the antenatal profile reads **Maternity / ANC** (for someone you chose **Walk-in** for, it reads **Walk-in client**, with their walk-in number in the ID column — see [Walk-in lab and imaging clients](/hospital/diagnostics/walk-in-clients)). The row shows its payment status — for example **Payment pending** — beside **Status** (for a test billed to the patient's insurer it reads **Paid · HMO pending** once the patient has paid their own share and the insurer's part is still outstanding), and opens when you choose it: a registered patient's order on this page with all its tests together, a walk-in client's on [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports).

## What you're looking at

Above the table sit five controls: **Search for patient**, **New Test Report**, **Refresh**, and, side by side (on a phone they share a row below the other controls), the date range (**Filter by date range**) and **View Columns** → **Toggle columns**, which is how you switch the optional columns on.

With no dates chosen the list shows your **Laboratory** queue window (see [Facility settings](/hospital/settings/facility)). To look further back, select **Filter by date range** and pick the first and last day; the list then shows the tests created on those days, newest first. **Clear dates** returns to the queue window. A range can be up to 12 months — a wider one is refused with **Choose a range of 12 months or less.** A list shows at most 500 requests at a time; if more match, a line under the table says **Showing the newest 500 of \{n\}** and asks you to narrow the dates.

| Column | Shows | On by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Patient ID** | The hospital number | Yes |
| **Patient Name** | Who the sample belongs to | Yes |
| **Status** | **Pending** or **Completed** | Yes |
| **Conducted By** | The scientist who ran it, or **Not conducted yet** | Yes |
| **Requested By** | The clinician who ordered it | Yes |
| **Date** · **Patient Type** · **Test Name** · **Result** | Extra detail | No |

A quiet day, or a hospital that has not ordered anything yet, reads **No recent lab test data available for this clinic.** That is the state a brand-new workspace lands on, and it is correct rather than broken.

If a client's record is restricted to their care team and you are not on it, their waiting tests do not appear in the table. A **Secured clients** block above it shows **Secured patient** and a number with how many lab orders are waiting — no name, test, result or note. Select **Request emergency access** on it if you need the record to provide care now; once it is granted the tests appear in the table like any other. See [Client security](/practice/work/client-security).

<Note>
  This list shows a test as **Pending** or **Completed** only. The richer view — **In Progress** and **Cancelled**, the **Critical** badge, and payment status — lives on [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports), which is also where ordering and printing happen.
</Note>

## Run a test and record the result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    From the row's action menu choose **Conduct Lab Test** on a pending request, or **Update Lab Test** on one already completed. **View Lab Results** opens it read-only, which is what a non-lab role gets. **Cancel test** (for lab scientists, owners and managers) stops a request that has not been run; it asks for a reason. If the row holds several tests, the sheet first asks you to pick one under **Test to cancel**; leaving it empty says **Choose the test to cancel.** A paid test is cancelled and credited by an owner or manager instead (**Cancel and credit patient**). See [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports). **Ask the ordering clinician** opens a team chat conversation with the clinician who ordered the test, linked to the order and its patient (see [Ask about an order](/hospital/pharmacy/prescriptions#ask-about-an-order)). A walk-in client has no patient record, so their orders do not offer it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check you have the right patient">
    The form opens on **Patient Information**, badged **Ward Patient** or **Outpatient**, with the patient ID, name, gender, age and weight. The heading underneath says which — "Ward Patient laboratory testing" or "Outpatient laboratory testing" — because the two go to different places afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a value for each parameter">
    Under **Laboratory Tests** each test lists its **Test Parameters**. Type into **Enter \{parameter\}**, or pick from **Select value** where the parameter is a choice. The reference range sits under the field as **Reference: \{range\}**, so you are never converting units in your head.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Select **Submit Lab Results**, or **Update Lab Results** if you are correcting one. You get a confirmation naming the patient type — "Ward lab test results have been submitted successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the request is open, **Sticky Notes** and **Status Notes** sit as two tabs on the right edge, each with a count, as on the consultation and triage charts. They hold the patient's notes across every visit, so a note left at the front desk or by the doctor ("hard to draw blood, use the left arm") is in front of you before you take the sample. Drag a tab up or down if it is in the way. See [Sticky notes and status notes](/practice/work/client-chart#sticky-notes-and-status-notes).

Reference ranges come from the test definition, not from this form. A test defined without ranges shows none here and prints none on the report, for every patient.

Each test also shows what it bills — the panel's own price from your [Lab station](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-station) catalogue, or, when only specific parameters were requested and every one of those has its own price set, the sum of just those. An item with no price set yet shows **"No price set"** rather than a $0, and the card totals every test at the top. None of this appears for a clinic marked **free** in Settings — no prices, no total, nothing to collect.

**Download requisition**, beside the number of tests, downloads one PDF with **every** test ordered in that visit or ward stay — each test with the parameters that were asked for, the clinical information from the visit, the patient's latest weight, and the requesting clinician's name and licence. It is marked **"Requisition only — no results are recorded on this document"** and now also carries a large, pale diagonal **REQUISITION — NOT A RESULT** stamp across every page, so it can never be mistaken for a completed report even if the banner is scrolled past.

The requisition shows no prices: tests ordered in a visit or on a ward are charged through that patient's bill. It carries the requesting clinician's signature when they have saved one under **Account → My signature**, and otherwise reads "Electronically signed by" and their name.

Once at least one test on the list has a value entered, a second button — **Download results** — sits beside the requisition. It downloads a single PDF covering every test in that visit or ward stay that has something recorded, organized one block per test with each parameter's value, unit, reference range and an **Abnormal**/**Critical** flag on anything out of range — the same layout the [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports) screen produces for one test at a time. A test with nothing entered yet is left off this PDF; one with values but not yet **Completed** still appears, its block and the whole document stamped **PRELIMINARY** so it is never read as final. Because different tests on the same visit can be run by different scientists, this report credits each test's own **Conducted by**, rather than a single signature for the whole document.

Beside **Download results**, **Send** emails the patient a private link instead of the file itself — see [Send a report securely](/platform/security/send-a-report) for what the recipient sees and how it is logged.

## Sign-out is the step that matters

Entering numbers and *releasing* them are two different acts. A test is only a result other clinicians will act on once it moves to **Completed** — that is the clinical authority step, and it is why **Conduct Lab Test** and **Update Lab Test** are restricted to lab scientists while everyone else gets **View Lab Results**.

<Warning>
  A result that has not been signed out is preliminary. Never hand it to a patient, a ward, an employer or a referring doctor as though it were final — complete it first, or send nothing.
</Warning>

## Who can do this

Your **role** decides whether the **Laboratory** group opens at all, and whether an owner has hidden it from that role under [Role navigation](/hospital/settings/role-navigation). What you may do inside it is decided separately:

| Action | Who by default | How it is governed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open the list and read results | Anyone on the team | Nothing — this page has no role check of its own |
| **Conduct Lab Test** / **Update Lab Test** | Owners, managers and lab scientists by default | **Settings → Facility → Clinical access → Enter lab results** — an owner can widen this to other roles; anyone outside the chosen list is told **Only lab scientists can conduct or update lab tests** |
| Order a new test | Clinicians and lab staff | Role |
| See the money on a request | Anyone who can open the list | Nothing — a hospital workspace has no permission that hides money |

Enforcement of the per-person permissions is **opt-in**: someone never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**. A **denied action is written to the audit log**; an allowed one is not. Hiding a module from a role's sidebar is a convenience, not a security boundary. See [Staff permissions](/platform/team/permissions).

## Check it worked

- The row's **Status** moves from **Pending** to **Completed**.
- **Conducted By** shows your name in place of **Not conducted yet**.
- Switching on the **Result** column shows the value you entered.
- The result appears on the ordering [consultation](/hospital/outpatient/consultation) or ward chart, under **Results**.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Only lab scientists can conduct or update lab tests">
    Result entry defaults to owners, managers and lab scientists — an owner can widen it under **Settings → Facility → Clinical access → Enter lab results**. Ask an owner to set your role correctly, or have a lab scientist enter the values — you can still open the request with **View Lab Results**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Please select a lab test first.">
    You reached the results form without a request attached — usually from a stale link. Go back to the list and open the row from its action menu.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No recent lab test data available for this clinic.">
    Nothing has been ordered, or the search box still has text in it. Clear **Search for patient** and select **Refresh**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The parameters I expected are not on the form">
    The form is built from the test's parameter list. Add the missing parameters to the test in your catalogue — see [Lab station](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-station) — and raise the request again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Laboratory group in my sidebar">
    Either the **Diagnostics** [care area](/hospital/get-started/care-areas) is off, or the hospital is not on the Enterprise plan. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I correct a result I already completed?">
    Yes — **Update Lab Test** reopens it for a lab scientist. The change is on the record with your name and the time, which is the point: a corrected result is a new clinical statement, not a quiet edit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between this and Lab Reports?">
    This list is the day's requests, for the bench. [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports) is the full order-to-report surface: ordering, payment, printing, and the richer status and critical-value badges.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do ward and outpatient tests look different?">
    They are filed against different records — a ward request belongs to an admission, an outpatient one to a consultation. The form badges which it is so the result lands in the right chart.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does this list include yesterday's requests?">
    It is built for today's work, within your queue window. Select **Filter by date range** above the table to look further back (up to 12 months), or use [Lab reports](/hospital/diagnostics/lab-reports) with its period filter, or **Lab Test History**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Lab Test History show?">
    Two tabs. **By Patient** (the default) groups every test into one row per patient — how many, their last test date, and the most recent status — with a search box and a date-range filter; leave the range blank to see a patient's whole history, or narrow it to a specific period. Selecting a patient opens their tests for that range as cards (test name, result, status, and whether it was a visit, a ward stay, a direct order or a Maternity / ANC order). When only one parameter of a test was ordered, its card names that **Parameter** with its unit and shows its **Reference range**; a parameter later removed from your lab's catalogue shows as —. **All Records** is the one-row-per-request table, for finding a single request fast; tests ordered together at the lab desk or from Maternity are one row.

    Each row's menu (and, on a phone, each card's menu) offers **Download report**, which saves the lab report as a PDF — one report for a whole visit, ward stay or direct order (every test requested together), or for the single test of a walk-in client. It shows **Preparing report...** while the file is built and confirms with **Report downloaded**. A test with nothing recorded yet has nothing to put in a report, and you are told so.

    Select **View Details** to open a past request and you can download it from there too: **Download requisition** saves the order as a PDF, **Download results** saves the results (shown once the request has results recorded), and **Send** shares the report securely by email, exactly as on the live form. The same buttons are on the details of a direct order and of a walk-in client's test. If a file cannot be made you see **Download failed**; try again, and contact support if it keeps happening.

    Above the tabs, **All**, **Visits**, **Ward**, **Direct orders** and **Maternity / ANC** narrow All Records and a patient's list to one kind of test. The choice stays in the page address, so a refresh or a shared link opens the same view. Patient totals always count every kind.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a direct order, and where does it appear?">
    A direct order is one booked straight at the lab for a registered patient, with no consultation and no ward stay behind it. It has its own row in **All Records**, marked **Direct lab order** (or **Maternity / ANC** when a midwife ordered the antenatal profile) — select **View Details** to open the order, with every test in it and its result — and it counts in the patient's total and appears in their list under **By Patient**. It is never called a walk-in: a registered patient is not one. Orders placed before this change keep the wording they were saved with, but those requested together are now shown together too; the ones a midwife placed earlier read **Direct lab order**, because nothing recorded where they came from. A sensitive test's result stays hidden unless you are on the client's care team or the owner.

    Someone you test **without registering them** is different: a [walk-in client](/hospital/diagnostics/walk-in-clients). They are not a patient, so they appear under the **Walk-in clients** tab of Lab History instead, with a number such as **W-000123**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Secured patient block above Lab Test History?">
    A client whose record is restricted to their care team, and whom you are not allowed to see, shows there as **Secured patient** and a number with how many lab orders exist — nothing else. Select **Request emergency access** to open their tests for the time you choose; the owner and the client's assigned staff are told, and the request is recorded. See [Client security](/practice/work/client-security).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who sees that I opened a patient's results?">
    Owners and managers, under [Audit log](/platform/security/audit-log). Every read of clinical data is recorded with your name and the time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
