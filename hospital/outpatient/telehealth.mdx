A telehealth session runs in the browser. You schedule a room, the patient gets a link by email, and you host the call from ClinikEHR with your notes open beside it — useful for follow-ups, results conversations and specialist reviews that do not need the patient in the building.

## Before you start

**Telehealth** has no care-area switch — it is in the sidebar on every plan, and every plan includes telehealth links each month (Free 10, Essential 250, Team and Enterprise unlimited). Links reset on the 1st of each month, and a link for a visit that is cancelled before the call starts does not count. When the month's links are used, the appointment says so and offers an **Upgrade** button. Recording is included on every plan; the in-call AI note taker needs Essential and above.

The patient needs an email address on their record for ClinikEHR to send the invite. Without one you can still create the room and send the link yourself.

## What you're looking at

The dashboard is headed **Telehealth** — "Manage video consultations with your patients" — with **New Call** at the top right and four tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Rooms scheduled but not yet held |
| **Recent** | Sessions that have finished |
| **Recordings** | Finished recordings, once they have been assembled |
| **AI Notes** | Drafts the AI note taker produced, waiting to be filed |

Each row's **⋯** menu offers **Send Invite**, **Copy Link**, **Mark as No Show** and **Cancel**.

## Schedule a room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the scheduler">
    Select **New Call** to open **Schedule Telehealth Call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the patient">
    Use **Select Patient** ("Search patients...") or **New Patient** if they are not registered yet.

    Someone with no email address cannot be invited by ClinikEHR: "This patient doesn't have an email address. Please add one to send the invite, or disable 'Send Invite Email' below."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time and type">
    Pick the **Date**, the time and a **Duration**, then the session type — Consultation, Group Therapy, Team Meeting or Webinar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the room can do">
    Toggle **Waiting Room**, **Recording**, **Screen Sharing**, **Chat** and **Send Invite Email**, and add **Room Notes** if the room needs context.

    The right-hand rail summarises what you have set up under **Patient Information**, **Session Summary** and **Enabled Features** — read it before you commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create Room**. You will see "Telehealth Room Created — Invite email sent to patient", and the session appears under **Upcoming**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Host the call

Select **Join** on the session. The pre-join screen shows the room code and your **Host Profile**, with camera and microphone toggles (it says **Camera is off** when yours is), background effects and device **Settings**. When you are happy, select **Join Meeting Now**.

Inside the call the top bar carries the room code, a **REC** indicator when recording, the participants, **Notes**, a connection quality indicator, **Copy Link** and Settings. Along the bottom sit mute, camera, **Background** effects, **Screen Share**, noise suppression, reactions, **Chat & Participants** ("Write a message...") and **Leave call** for everyone. **Leave call** drops only you: the call stays open and you can rejoin with the same link, from the **Rejoin call** button on the screen you land on. Beside it, the arrow opens a menu with **End call**, which only the host sees (not a co-host, and not the patient). Choosing it asks **End this call for everyone?** first; **End call** then removes everyone right away, the link stops working and a recording that is running stops. Nobody can rejoin after that. **Leave call** keeps the call open; **End call** finishes it for everyone. If everyone has left and nobody comes back within 30 minutes, the call finishes on its own, as if the host had ended it, and the link stops working.

If the room has a waiting room you are told how many people are waiting, with **Admit** or **Deny** per person and **Admit everyone**. On a hospital clinic list that is what stops the next patient walking into the previous consultation.

## Record the session

Select the record button to open **Start Recording?**. It tells you exactly what is captured: "Records BOTH sides of the call — every participant's video and audio, plus any shared screen, composited into one file. Everyone in the call is asked to agree first, and recording begins only when they have."

The sheet carries the **AI Note Taker** switch, which starts **off** for every recording because the recording goes to an outside AI service, and ends with the consent line: "**Consent:** each person in the call sees a request to agree to being recorded. If anyone says no, nothing is recorded. Each answer is saved with the recording."

Confirm with **Start Recording**. Recording does not begin straight away. Each other person in the call sees **Record this session?** with **Yes, I agree** and **No, do not record**, and cannot carry on without choosing. You see who is still to answer ("Waiting for … to accept the recording"), and if the recording does not start you are told whether someone said no or did not answer in time. A short loss of your own connection while recording does not stop it; if you are not back within about 15 seconds it does. When everyone has agreed, recording starts and everyone sees the red **REC** badge. If anyone says no, or does not answer within about 90 seconds, nothing is recorded and the call carries on. Someone who joins while a recording is running sees the badge at once and is asked the same question; until they answer they are kept out of it (their video and voice are left out of the file) until they answer; if they say no, or do not answer, the recording stops, the part already recorded is kept and can be played, and no AI notes are made from it. In **Recordings** that recording carries the note **Stopped — a participant declined recording**. If you leave the call while you are the one recording, the recording stops (leaving while someone else is recording does not stop it), the part already recorded is kept and can be played, and **Recordings** says **Stopped — the host left the call**.

If your clinic has turned off **Require explicit consent to record** in **Settings → Client Portal**, people are still told with the **REC** badge, but they are not asked to agree first. Leave that setting on unless your clinic's policy says otherwise.

**Stop Recording?** ends it and begins processing; the file lands on **Recordings** once that finishes. Each person's answer, with the time and the wording they saw, is kept with the recording and is included in your data export as **Recording Consents**.

<Warning>
  **Say what you are recording and why before you press Start.** A telehealth recording captures every participant's video and audio and becomes part of the patient's clinical record. ClinikEHR asks each person to agree and keeps their answer, but a clear word from you first is still the right way to start. If anyone objects, do not record.
</Warning>

## Write the note

Two ways, and you can use both.

**The Notes panel** is host-only and opens beside the call. It has a template picker and saves continuously as you type. A patient switcher at the top means a group call produces one note per patient; until you pick someone it reads "No client linked yet".

**The AI Note Taker** is switched on, for that recording only, in the **Start Recording** sheet along with a **Note template**. It is off by default, and a recording nobody agreed to cannot be sent for notes. After you stop, "AI will automatically generate your session notes." Drafts land on **AI Notes**, where **Save to Notes** files one to a chart — choose the **Patient** and **What to save**. Read it before you save; it is a draft, not a record.

## Who can do this

| Action | Who by default | Governed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open **Telehealth** and schedule a room | Any clinician the Outpatient group is shown to | Role Navigation |
| Host a call, admit from the waiting room | The clinician the room belongs to | Room host. Once you save a person's permissions: **Host video visits** |
| Start and stop recording | The host | Room settings — recording must be enabled on the room. Once you save a person's permissions: **Record video visits** |
| Play, download or list recordings | Any clinician | **View video visit recordings** |
| Delete a recording | Owners and managers | **Delete video visit recordings** — off for everyone else until you switch it on for a person |
| Run the in-call AI note taker and read its notes | The host | Plan — it needs Essential and above; below that the toggle is disabled. Once you save a person's permissions: **AI notes from video visits** |
| File an AI draft to a patient's chart | Clinicians | The same rules as writing a [clinic note](/hospital/outpatient/notes) |
| Cancel a session or mark a no-show | Anyone who can open the module | Role |

Two behaviours worth knowing. Permission enforcement is **opt-in per person**: someone never saved in the permissions sheet is unrestricted, and the sheet warns **"Saving starts enforcing"**. And a denied action is recorded in the audit log while an allowed one is not — hiding **Telehealth** in [Role Navigation](/hospital/settings/role-navigation) is a convenience, not a security boundary.

A telehealth call needs a live connection throughout, so nothing on this page works offline. See [Working offline](/platform/apps/offline) for what does.

## Check it worked

- The session has moved from **Upcoming** to **Recent**.
- A recording appears on **Recordings** a few minutes after the call ends.
- An AI draft is on **AI Notes**, and what you typed in the Notes panel is on the patient's chart in [Clinic Notes](/hospital/outpatient/notes).
- What the patient sees at their end is described in [Join a video visit](/portal/patients/telehealth).

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="A call is still shown as in progress after everyone left">
    A call nobody ended is closed automatically once it has been idle for 24 hours. It moves to **Completed** and anyone still shown in it is marked as having left. This is done quietly: no follow-up message or workflow is triggered by it, and the client's join link stops working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The invite email was not sent">
    The patient has no email address on file. Add one to their record, then use **⋯** → **Send Invite**, or **Copy Link** and send it yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient cannot get in">
    Join links are personal and work for six hours from the scheduled start, so an old email will not work — resend with **Send Invite**. The booking confirmation and the appointment reminder both carry the client's own **Join video visit** link once the visit has a room. If the room has a waiting room, they are waiting for you to **Admit** them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nobody can see or hear me">
    Check the camera and microphone toggles on the pre-join screen and in **Settings** — the pre-join screen says **Camera is off** when it is. Close any other application using the camera, then rejoin.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording is not on the Recordings tab">
    A recording is assembled after the call ends, so give it a few minutes. Check the **REC** pill was showing during the call — if it never appeared, recording never started.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="This browser can't play this recording's format">
    Safari cannot play the video format most session recordings use. Select **Download recording** to watch it in another video player, or open **Recordings** in Chrome, Firefox or Edge instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording has expired / is no longer available">
    Recordings are kept for 90 days. Past that, the badge reads **Expired**, and the row shows "This recording expired on [date] and is no longer available" — Play and Download are gone, but you can still delete the row. If a recording that should still be within its 90 days shows "This recording's file is no longer available" instead, its file was lost; deleting the row is the only remaining action.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient never showed up">
    Use **⋯** → **Mark as No Show** so the session is closed honestly rather than left open on **Upcoming**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The call keeps dropping">
    The connection quality indicator in the top bar shows which end is struggling. Turn off background effects and screen sharing first — both are demanding — and ask the patient to move closer to their router.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a telehealth session create a consultation?">
    No. The room is separate from the outpatient queue. Document the visit in the in-call Notes panel or as a [clinic note](/hospital/outpatient/notes), or open the patient's [consultation](/hospital/outpatient/consultation) alongside the call if they were triaged into your department.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can more than one patient be in a call?">
    Yes — the session types include Group Therapy, Team Meeting and Webinar. The Notes panel has a patient switcher so a group call produces one note per patient rather than one shared note.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can see a recording?">
    Staff who can open **Telehealth** in your hospital. A recording is part of the patient's clinical record, and opening one is logged like any other access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn recording off for the whole hospital?">
    Yes — recording is a per-room switch you set when scheduling, and it can also be prevented at the patient's end through their portal settings. A room created with recording off has no record button.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the patient need an account?">
    No. They join from the link in their invite. A patient who also uses the portal can join from there instead — see [Join a video visit](/portal/patients/telehealth).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
