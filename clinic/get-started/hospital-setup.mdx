A hospital workspace looks different from a solo practice from the first screen:
the people you serve are called **Patients**, and the sidebar is grouped by
service line — **Front Desk**, **Outpatient**, **Inpatient**, **Theatre**,
**Diagnostics**, **Pharmacy**, **Revenue**, **Insurance**, **Executive**,
**Supply Chain**, **Records** — instead of one flat list.

This page is the order to do things in. Each stage links to the page that covers
it properly.

  before="An enterprise workspace, owner access, and a decision about which units you actually run"

## Why order matters

A group only appears in the sidebar when its **care area** is switched on — a
hospital with no operating theatre never sees a **Theatre** group. Navigation
follows the care areas, so decide those first, rather than inviting staff into a
hospital whose shape you then change under them.

## Stage 1 — Decide your care areas

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Facility']} />

The **Facility** card — "Which departments this hospital runs. These control
what appears in navigation." — has a switch per area:

| Switch | Turns on |
|---|---|
| **Front desk** | Check-in board, appointments, registration and contacts |
| **In-patient care** | Ward admissions, discharges and bed management |
| **Surgical theatre** | Theatre scheduling and perioperative records |
| **Diagnostics** | Laboratory and radiology ordering, stations and reports |
| **In-house pharmacy** | Dispensing, prescriptions and the pharmacy station |
| **Insurance** | Claims, pre-authorisation and the insurance dashboard |
| **Inventory** | Stock, purchase orders and consumables |
| **Records** | The records officer's queues, documents and patient records |

Turn on only what you run today. Switching one off later is safe — "It does not
delete anything, and existing records stay reachable by direct link." Full
detail: [Care areas](/platform/team/care-areas).

## Stage 2 — Build the structure inside each area

<Steps>
  <Step title="Departments and sub-units">
    **Settings › Departments** — "Manage your clinic departments, sub-units, and
    consultation rates." Select **Add Department**, then open it to add
    **Sub-Units** and their **Consultation Rates**. A department is the unit a
    patient is routed to and the unit a consultation is billed under, so name
    them the way your staff say them out loud.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wards, sub-wards and beds">
    **Settings › Ward Management**, if **In-patient care** is on. Add each ward,
    then its sub-wards, then its **Bed Spaces**. A bed has to exist before
    anyone can be admitted to it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shift pattern and theatres">
    The **Facility** card also holds the shift pattern the nursing station,
    shift notes and fluid charts split on — set it to your real handover times.
    **Settings › Theatre** covers theatres and the safety checklist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stage 3 — Staff it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite people with the right specialty and access level">
    Follow [Invite your team](/clinic/get-started/invite-team) for each person.
    The **Specialty** picker is narrower on enterprise than on a solo plan, and
    the administrative roles — **Clinic Manager**, **Clinic Biller**, **Clinic
    Scheduler** — are what most non-clinical hospital staff need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scope what each role can open">
    **Settings › Role Navigation** — "Which parts of the clinic each role sees
    and can open." This is what stops a lab scientist opening the ward round and
    a receptionist opening the theatre list. Owners and managers are not
    affected by it.

    It governs pages, not actions — what someone may *do* on a page they can
    open is set by their permissions. See [Roles](/platform/team/roles) and
    [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Set role navigation before your first live day, not after. Staff learn where
  things are in their first week, and moving the furniture afterwards costs more
  than getting it right once.
</Note>

## Stage 4 — Services, billing and insurance

- **Consultation rates** per sub-unit, from **Settings › Departments**.
- **Appointment Services** for anything bookable — see
  [Add the services you offer](/clinic/get-started/services).
- **Currency** and billing settings, from **Settings › Billing & Plans**.
- **Insurance**, if the care area is on — payers, pre-authorisation and claims.

## Stage 5 — Go live

Walk one patient end to end before you announce the switch: register at the
front desk, triage, consult, order a lab test, dispense, bill. Every hand-off
you find missing is a care area, a department or a permission — cheap to fix on
a quiet afternoon, expensive at 9am on a Monday.

## Check it worked

- The sidebar shows exactly the groups you expect, and no group for a unit you
  do not run.
- A staff member from each unit signs in and sees their own queues — and not
  each other's.
- **Front Desk › Patients** can register someone, and that person can be
  admitted to a real bed.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A group is missing from the sidebar">
    Its care area is off. **Settings › Facility**. The **Theatre** group in
    particular is hidden entirely until **Surgical theatre** is on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A staff member cannot see a page they should">
    Check **Role Navigation** for their role first — that is the page-level gate
    — before touching their individual permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot admit a patient — there is nowhere to put them">
    Beds are the last level of ward setup. Ward, then sub-ward, then **Bed
    Spaces**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The sidebar says Clients, not Patients">
    That is the solo/team layout, which means the workspace is not on the
    enterprise plan. See [Choose a plan](/clinic/get-started/choose-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nursing notes are landing in the wrong handover">
    The shift pattern on **Settings › Facility** does not match your real shift
    times. Correcting it changes how future notes are filed; notes already
    written keep the shift they were saved under.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
