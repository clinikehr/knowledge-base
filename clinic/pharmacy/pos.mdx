A hospital pharmacy unit sells over the counter exactly the way a retail
pharmacy does. The till in your **Pharmacy** group is the same point of sale
that ships as the Pharmacy edition, drawing on the same stock, batches and
receipts — so the how-to lives in one place and this page only covers what a
hospital needs to know on top.

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Pharmacy', 'POS']} />

## What is different inside a hospital

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stock is shared" icon="/images/icons/boxes-stacked.svg">
    The counter sells from the same inventory the wards and the dispensary draw
    on. A sale at the till moves the same on-hand figure a ward issue does.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Controlled items are refused" icon="/images/icons/shield-halved.svg">
    An item needing a witnessed dispense cannot be rung up. The till says so —
    "requires a witnessed dispense — use the inventory Fill Rx flow instead of
    POS." Take it to [dispensing](/clinic/pharmacy/dispensing).
  </Card>
  <Card title="A sale is not an invoice" icon="/images/icons/receipt.svg">
    Counter sales appear under **Sales** in the Pharmacy group. Patient billing
    — consultations, procedures, admissions — is separate and lives in
    [Billing](/clinic/revenue/billing).
  </Card>
  <Card title="The buyer is a customer here" icon="/images/icons/user.svg">
    At the till the person paying is whoever is at the counter, which is not
    always the patient on the script.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  Offline behaviour is the same here as in a standalone pharmacy: the till keeps
  selling and replays the sales when the connection returns, but any figure it
  shows from before the outage is labelled as stale. Trust the label — see
  [Selling offline](/pharmacy/pos/offline).
</Note>

## If the POS screen is missing

- The **Pharmacy** group appears only when the pharmacy
  [care area](/platform/team/care-areas) is switched on, and only on the
  Enterprise plan. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
- If the group is there but **POS** is not on your rail, your role does not
  include the till. Ask an owner or manager to check
  [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
