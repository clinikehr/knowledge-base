A hospital pharmacy unit does two separate jobs: a clinician writes the
prescription, and pharmacy staff fill it from stock. This page covers both, and
the batch and witness controls that sit between them.

## Where everything lives

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Prescription List** | Scripts waiting to be filled |
| **Prescription History** | Everything already dispensed |
| **Pharmacy Station** | The dispensing bench |
| **POS** | The retail counter — see [Sell at the pharmacy counter](/clinic/pharmacy/pos) |
| **Sales** | Counter takings |

## Write the prescription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Either open the [consultation](/clinic/clinical/consultation) and use the
    **Orders** tab, or open **Pharmacy Station** and select **New Prescription**.
    Prescribing from the consultation keeps the script attached to the encounter
    that justified it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the patient and date">
    Fill **Select Patient** and **Prescription Date**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add each medication">
    Use **Search medications...** to add a line, then complete **Mode ***,
    **Quantity ***, **Dosage ***, **Frequency ***, **Duration *** and, if it
    helps whoever dispenses, **Instructions (Optional)**. Repeat for every drug
    on the script.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and save">
    **Additional Notes** carries anything the pharmacist needs to know. The
    **Prescription Summary** panel shows an **Estimated Total** so nobody is
    surprised at the counter. Save, and the **Prescription Created** dialog
    appears with a **Reference:** — quote that number if you need to find the
    script again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fill it from stock

Filling happens against inventory, so the stock that leaves the shelf is the
stock the system knows about.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fill Rx">
    In the pharmacy unit's inventory area, open the **Fill Rx** tab. It lists
    prescriptions waiting to be dispensed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the script">
    Select the row. The **Fill prescription** sheet opens — "Draw from stock
    against this script — FEFO, patient-linked, audited."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the stock and quantity">
    Choose the **Stock item** and the **Location** it comes from, then enter the
    **Quantity**. Stock is drawn oldest-expiry-first, so you are not choosing a
    batch by hand and short-dated stock does not sit at the back of the shelf.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a witness for a controlled item">
    If the item is a controlled drug the sheet shows **Witness (controlled
    item)** in red and will not let you continue without one — "DEA requires a
    witness to dispense a controlled substance." Choose the colleague who
    actually watched, not whoever is nearest.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dispense">
    Select **Dispense**. Stock comes off, the script is marked filled, and the
    dispense is recorded against the patient with your name and the witness's.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A controlled item cannot be sold at the till. If you try, the counter refuses
  it — "requires a witnessed dispense — use the inventory Fill Rx flow instead
  of POS." Take the script to **Fill Rx** rather than looking for a way past
  the message.
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- The script no longer appears under **Fill Rx**, and shows as dispensed in
  **Prescription History**.
- The stock item's on-hand figure has dropped by the quantity you dispensed —
  see [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index).
- A controlled dispense shows both your name and the witness's.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The medication is not in the stock item picker">
    **Fill Rx** draws from inventory, so a drug that has never been received
    into stock cannot be dispensed from it. Receive the stock first, then fill
    the script.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The quantity I need is more than is on hand">
    Dispense what you have and leave the rest outstanding, or move stock in from
    another location — the **Location** picker only offers what that location
    holds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The witness field will not accept my colleague">
    It only lists staff in this workspace who are permitted to witness. If the
    person is standing with you but not in the list, they need a staff account —
    see [Invite staff](/platform/team/invite-staff).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The till refuses the item">
    That is the controlled-substance rule, not a fault. Controlled items are
    dispensed through **Fill Rx** with a witness, never rung up at the counter.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Pharmacy group in my sidebar">
    Either the pharmacy [care area](/platform/team/care-areas) is off, or your
    workspace is not on the Enterprise plan. See
    [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
