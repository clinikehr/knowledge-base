Your service list is the price list for the whole clinic. Invoices are built from it, your [booking page](/clinic/front-desk/booking-page) offers what is on it, and reporting groups revenue by it. Get it right once and everything downstream gets easier.

## The Services screen

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | How your catalogue is shaped and what it earns |
| **Services** | Every service, its price and whether it is active |
| **Categories** | The groups you sort services into |

**New Service** and **New Category** are in the header.

## Add a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sheet">
    Select **New Service**. The **New service** sheet opens — "Add a new service to your clinic's catalogue."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and price it">
    **Service Name**\* is what appears on the invoice and on your booking page, so write it the way a patient would recognise it, not as an internal code. **Price**\* is in your clinic's currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the service type">
    **Service Type**\* — "The category this service falls under." The picker is grouped: General, Medical Services, Diagnostic Services, Therapy Services, Alternative Medicine, Mental Health and Wellness Services. Pick the closest; it is what reporting uses to tell a consultation apart from a lab test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a category and a code">
    **Service Category** is your own grouping, and you can create one from inside this sheet if it does not exist yet. **Procedure Code** takes the CPT/HCPCS code if you bill insurance — it carries through onto invoice lines and claims.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it and save">
    **Description** is the text a patient reads when booking. Leave **Active** on — "Toggle whether this service is currently available." — and select **Add Service**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a category

Select **New Category**, enter a **Category Name**\*, an optional **Description**, and leave **Active** on. Categories exist so that a list of sixty services is navigable: group by what a patient came in for ("Antenatal", "Occupational health"), not by which staff member performs it.

## Retiring a service

Switch **Active** off rather than deleting. An inactive service stops being offered for booking and stops appearing in the invoice picker, but every invoice that already used it keeps its line, its price and its history intact. Deleting a service that has been billed is how a revenue report ends up with a hole in it.

## Check it worked

- The service appears under the **Services** tab with its price in your clinic's currency.
- "Search services to add…" on a [new invoice](/clinic/revenue/invoices) finds it.
- If you offer it publicly, it appears on your [booking page](/clinic/front-desk/booking-page).

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The price shows in the wrong currency">
    Currency is a workspace-wide setting under **Settings → Billing**, not a per-service one, and only an owner can change it. See [Currency](/platform/settings/currency).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot change a price">
    Editing service fees is granted separately from creating services, so that the people who maintain the catalogue are not automatically the people who can reprice it. See [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The service does not appear on my booking page">
    Check it is **Active**, then check it is offered on the booking page itself — a service can exist in your catalogue without being published for online booking. See [Your booking page](/clinic/front-desk/booking-page).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Prices need to differ per insurer">
    Payer-specific pricing is not set here. On the Enterprise plan, set negotiated rates under **Insurance** → **Pricing** — see [Insurance in ClinikEHR](/clinic/insurance/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Every price shows as zero or charges are disabled">
    **Free Clinic Mode** is switched on under **Settings → Billing** — "Enable this if your clinic provides free services. All charges will be disabled." Your clinic carries a **Free Clinic** badge while it is on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
