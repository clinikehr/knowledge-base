A membership is a recurring plan a patient subscribes to — a wellness
programme, a chronic-care package, a retainer. ClinikEHR collects the payment
each period and settles it to your own payout account, so you are not chasing
the same people every month.

## Connect payouts first

This is not optional and it is not something you can defer. Until your payout
account is fully connected and able to accept charges, the Memberships screen
shows **Connect payouts to sell memberships** and every create button is
disabled.

Which provider you use is decided by your clinic's currency, not by preference:

| Your currency | Provider | Where to connect it |
|---|---|---|
| NGN | Paystack | **Settings** → **Online Payments** → **Paystack Connect** |
| Anything else | Stripe | **Settings** → **Online Payments** → **Stripe Connect** |

A half-finished Stripe connection is treated as no connection — the screen says
"Finish Stripe onboarding (charges not yet enabled) to sell memberships." Go
back and complete it.

## The Memberships screen

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Plans** | Your recurring plans, with a count in the tab |
| **Add-ons** | Extras a member can buy on top of a plan |
| **Members** | Who is subscribed, and to what |
| **Transactions** | Every collection, successful or not |
| **Overview** | How the programme is performing |

## Create a plan and enrol someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a template">
    Select the option to create a plan and pick one of the supplied templates,
    or start from scratch. The plan form says what it is for: "Define a
    recurring plan your patients can subscribe to. Billing settles to your
    connected payout account."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the price and period">
    Price the plan in your clinic's currency and choose how often it renews.
    This is what the patient's card is charged, unchanged, each period.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add add-ons if you sell them">
    Use **Sell add-on** for extras on top of a plan. Add-ons need the **Team**
    plan — "Add-ons require the Team plan." Everything else on this page works
    on **Essential**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enrol a member">
    Select **Enroll member** — "Subscribe a patient to a membership plan.
    They'll be billed automatically to your connected payout account." Use this
    for people who sign up at the desk. Everyone else signs themselves up on
    your public page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Enrolling a member starts a recurring charge against their card. Confirm the
  plan and the price with them before you enrol — the first collection is not a
  preview.
</Warning>

## Publish the public signup page

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Membership Page']} />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the page address">
    Enter a **Page address**. Availability is checked as you type, so you know
    immediately whether the address is free.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the content">
    **Headline**, **Subheadline**, **Intro (optional)** and **Footer note
    (optional)** are what a prospective member reads. **Success title** and
    **Success message** are what they see after they have joined — use them to
    say what happens next, not just "thanks".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match your branding">
    Apply your clinic's branding so the page does not look like it belongs to
    somebody else. See [Branding](/platform/settings/branding).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take it live">
    Flip the switch from **Off** to **Live** and save. To put the signup form on
    your own website instead, copy the widget or iframe snippet from the same
    page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it worked

- The **Plans** tab shows your plan with a count, and the create buttons are
  enabled rather than greyed out.
- Your public page loads at the address you chose and shows the plan and its
  price.
- After the first enrolment, the member appears under **Members** and the
  collection appears under **Transactions**.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Everything is greyed out and I cannot create a plan">
    Your payout account is not connected, or not yet able to accept charges. The
    banner names the exact reason. Fix it under **Settings → Online Payments**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Memberships are not in my sidebar at all">
    "Memberships require the Essential plan or higher. Upgrade your plan to sell
    memberships." See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot add an add-on">
    "Add-ons require the Team plan." Plans themselves work on Essential.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A staff member cannot open Memberships">
    "Only clinic owners and managers can manage memberships." This one is not
    adjustable through per-person permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My page address is refused">
    Addresses are checked for format, for reserved words, and for uniqueness
    across every ClinikEHR workspace — so a plausible address may already be
    taken by somebody else. Try a more specific one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A member's payment failed">
    Failed collections appear under **Transactions**. Use the collections manager
    to retry or to contact the member; a card that has expired needs the member
    to update it, not a retry.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
