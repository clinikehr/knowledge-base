Money arrives two ways: somebody hands it over at the desk and you record it, or
you send them a link and they pay online. This page covers both, and how to see
what is still outstanding.

## The Payments screen

Three tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The money picture — what came in, how, and when |
| **Payments** | Every payment recorded, newest first |
| **Pending** | What is still owed |

**New Payment** in the header records a payment that is not tied to a specific
invoice you are already looking at.

## Record a payment against an invoice

This is the usual route, because it settles a specific balance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice's actions">
    Find the invoice under **Billing** → **Invoices** and select **Record
    Payment**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the amount">
    **Amount** is prefilled with the balance due. Overtype it for a part
    payment — the invoice then shows as **Partially paid** and keeps the
    remainder outstanding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the method">
    **Payment Method** defaults to cash. Set it to what actually happened; the
    **Overview** tab breaks takings down by method, and a bank transfer logged
    as cash quietly ruins that.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a reference">
    **Reference (optional)** is where the transfer reference, cheque number or
    terminal receipt number goes. It is what makes a payment findable when a
    patient queries it three months later. **Notes (optional)** carries anything
    else.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    You get a "Payment recorded" confirmation, and the invoice's balance drops
    immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once a payment is recorded against an invoice, that invoice can no longer be
  deleted — only cancelled. Record payments against the right invoice; correcting
  one afterwards is a deliberate, recorded reversal, not a quiet edit.
</Warning>

## Send a payment link instead

If the patient is not standing in front of you, send them a link and let them
pay by card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the patient's record">
    From the client record, select **Send payment link**. The **Send secure
    payment link** panel opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what it covers">
    Pick the invoices the link should settle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send it">
    The email tells the patient the link expires in **24 hours**. Sending a link
    also marks the invoices it covers as sent, so you do not need to email the
    invoice separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Payment links need a connected payment account under **Settings → Online
  Payments**. Clinics collecting through Paystack cannot use Stripe payment
  links — the product says so: "This clinic collects payments via Paystack —
  Stripe payment links are not available." Send your bank details on the invoice
  instead, using **Settings → Payment Details**.
</Note>

## Check it worked

- The payment is listed under **Payments** with your name, the method and the
  reference you entered.
- The invoice shows **Paid**, or **Partially paid** with the correct remaining
  balance.
- The **Pending** tab no longer lists that balance.
- For a payment link: the patient received the email, and the invoices it covers
  show as sent.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The amount prefilled is not what they paid">
    Overtype it. The prefill is the balance due, not a fixed value, and a part
    payment is a normal thing to record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I recorded a payment against the wrong invoice">
    Do not delete the invoice — you cannot once it has a payment on it. Reverse
    the payment and record it correctly. Reversing is a separate permission from
    adding, so you may need an owner or manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send payment link is not offered">
    Either no payment account is connected under **Settings → Online Payments**,
    or your clinic collects through Paystack and the link type in question is
    Stripe-only. Connect an account, or send the invoice with your bank details
    on it instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient says the link no longer works">
    Links expire 24 hours after they are sent. Send a fresh one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The patient paid online but the invoice still says Pending">
    Online payments record themselves, but not always instantly. Refresh the
    **Billing** page before recording it by hand — recording it manually as well
    would double-count the money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Figures on the Overview tab show as dashes">
    You are permitted to open the screen but not to see the financial figures.
    Viewing the financial dashboard and viewing payment reports are separate
    permissions — see [Permissions](/platform/team/permissions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
