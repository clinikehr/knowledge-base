The store sells what your inventory already holds — supplements, devices, dressings, anything you stock — at a public address your patients can reach without signing in. Stock and cost stay in inventory; the store is the shop window.

## The Store screen

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Products** | Everything you have listed |
| **Orders** | What customers have bought |
| **Overview** | **Revenue (paid)**, **Orders**, **Avg. order value** and **Active products** |

## List something for sale

There are two routes in, and the fast one is usually right.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sell from inventory">
    Select **Sell from inventory**, search with "Search inventory by name or SKU…", and select **Sell** on the item. That publishes it as a product with one variant, linked to the inventory item it came from. Use this for anything you already stock and are not fussy about presenting.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Build the product">
    Open the product editor — "Details, variants, and photos. Stock \+ cost come from the linked inventory item." Use this when the product needs a description, photographs, or more than one size.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Building a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Give it a **Title** and a **Description**, assign a collection, and set the tax rate and label if this product is taxed differently from your default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save once to create it">
    The product is created on the first save. Photos and extra variants attach to a product that exists, so save before you try to add them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variants">
    Under **Variants**, select **Add variant** and name it — "Variant name (e.g. 100ml)". The variant you start with is named **Default**; rename it if you are selling more than one size. Link each variant to the inventory item it draws from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save each variant">
    Variants save one at a time, not in a batch. Save each one before moving to the next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Stock and cost are not editable in the store.** They come from the linked inventory item, so there is one number for how many you have, wherever you look. Change stock in [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index), not here.
</Note>

## Publish the storefront

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Settings', 'Store Page']} />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Claim your store address">
    Enter a **Store address**. It is checked for format, for reserved words, and for uniqueness across every ClinikEHR workspace, so a common name may already be gone. Your shop then lives at that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the shopfront">
    **Announcement bar (optional)**, **Headline**, **Subheadline**, **Intro (optional)** and **Footer note (optional)** are the page copy. **Success title** and **Success message** are what a customer sees after they order — say what happens next and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it">
    Set an **Accent color** and **Theme**, choose a **Layout** and **Products per row**, and pick a **Featured collection** and **Featured products** for the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set tax">
    Set your default tax rate and its label here, so new products start correct rather than needing fixing one by one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Flip the switch from **Off** to **Live** and select **Save**. The page is not public until you do both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your plan allows

| Plan | Active products | Also |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | No storefront at all | — |
| Starter | 100 | — |
| Essential | 1,000 | — |
| Team | Unlimited | Digital products, embeddable storefront, abandoned-cart recovery |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Same as Team |

Two limits people meet early:

- "Digital products are available on the Team plan. Upgrade to sell downloads."
- "Embedding your storefront is a Team feature. Upgrade to add it to your website."

A custom domain such as `shop.yourclinic.example` is available on Team and Enterprise — see [Custom domains](/platform/settings/custom-domain).

<Warning>
  A subscription that lapses drops you to Free, and Free has no storefront. Your products are not deleted, but the public page stops selling until the plan is active again. See [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- The product appears under **Products** and counts toward **Active products** on the **Overview** tab.
- Your store address loads in a private browser window, with the product and its price visible.
- A test order appears under **Orders**, and the linked inventory item's on-hand figure has dropped.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot edit the stock level or the cost">
    That is deliberate. Both come from the linked inventory item — change them in [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My variant did not save">
    Variants save individually, and only after the product itself exists. Save the product first, then save each variant in turn.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The store address is refused">
    It is checked for format, reserved words, and uniqueness across all ClinikEHR workspaces. Choose something more specific to your clinic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The page is not public">
    The switch must read **Live** _and_ you must select **Save**. Setting one without the other leaves the store off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have hit the product limit">
    Starter allows 100 active products and Essential 1,000. Deactivate what you are not selling, or move up a plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An order came in for something out of stock">
    The store sells against inventory, so this means the inventory figure was wrong. Reconcile with a stock count — see [Inventory](/pharmacy/inventory/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
