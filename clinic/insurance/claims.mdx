A claim asks an insurer to pay for care you have already delivered. Which
screens you use depends entirely on your plan, because ClinikEHR has
[two separate insurance systems](/clinic/insurance/overview) — pick the tab that
matches what your sidebar shows.

<Warning>
  On the electronic system, claim submissions and eligibility checks are charged
  per use, and **Create and submit claims** sends them to the payer immediately
  with no second confirmation. Use **Create claims** while you are learning, and
  review before you submit. See [Plans](/platform/plans/overview).

## Prepare the claim

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Insurance', 'Claims']} />

Claims here are prepared by you and tracked by you. Nothing is transmitted
electronically — you send the claim to the insurer the way you already do, and
this is the record of it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the claim">
    Select **New**. The claim form opens as a set of tabs you work through in
    order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Basic Info">
    The patient, the encounter and the dates. This is what ties the claim to the
    care that was actually delivered.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Items & Services">
    Every service, medicine and consumable you are billing for. Prices come from
    the payer-specific rates on the **Pricing** tab of the Insurance Dashboard,
    not from your standard [service list](/clinic/revenue/services).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insurance & Diagnosis">
    The insurer, the patient's cover, and the diagnosis codes that justify every
    item on the claim. An item with no supporting diagnosis is the denial you
    will spend next month arguing about.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review & Submit">
    Read the whole claim, then submit it. If you obtained a
    [pre-authorization](/clinic/insurance/pre-authorization), quote it — an
    approved request is the fastest route through an insurer's review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track it

The Claims screen has **Claims**, **Analytics** and **Activity**. Work the
**Claims** list by status; **Analytics** is where you notice a payer whose
denial rate is climbing, and **Activity** is the history of who did what to a
claim.

## Create claims from unbilled work

<Path steps={['Sidebar', 'Insurance']} />

This system starts from your calendar rather than a blank form: it lists
appointments you have not billed, and turns them into electronic claims.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish the setup first">
    Under **Settings** → **Insurance**, complete **Insurance Profile**, add your
    payers on **Payers**, and enrol each provider on **Enrollment**. Claims to a
    payer you are not enrolled with will not be accepted, however correct they
    are.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record the patient's coverage">
    On the client record — "Add the client's insurance coverage to enable
    eligibility checks and claims." Run the eligibility check and read the badge:
    **Eligible** or **Ineligible**. Checking before the visit is what stops you
    working for nothing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Unbilled appointments">
    The **Unbilled appointments** tab lists completed appointments with no claim
    against them. Select the ones you are billing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the claims">
    Use the split button. **Create claims** produces drafts you can check;
    **Create and submit claims** creates them and sends them to the payer in one
    action. After either, the view jumps to the **Claims** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and follow up">
    From **Claims**, submit any drafts and watch the status. The stats bar counts
    **Draft**, **In Progress**, **Paid** and **Denied**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reconcile the money

The **Payments** tab is where remittances land. Record what the payer actually
sent against the claims it covers, choosing the method — **Check**, **EFT /
ACH**, **Card** or **Other**. A claim marked **Paid** with nothing reconciled
against it is a claim you have not actually been paid for.

**Analytics** shows how the whole pipeline is performing — submission volume,
denial rate, and how long payers take.

## Adding and removing payers

Add payers from the search sheet — "Search by payer name, ID, or alias (e.g.
Aetna, Blue Cross, 62308)..." — then switch them active or remove them. A payer
that already has claims is never deleted: "This payer has existing claims and
will be deactivated (not deleted)." That is deliberate, so your claim history
does not lose the name of who you billed.

## Check it worked

- The claim appears in the **Claims** list with a status and the payer's name.
- Nothing you meant to bill is still sitting on **Unbilled appointments** (on
  the electronic system) or unclaimed (on the hospital one).
- When money arrives, the claim moves to paid *and* has a payment reconciled
  against it.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My old claims have disappeared">
    You are almost certainly looking at the other system. The hospital register
    and the electronic workflow share no data, and a plan change moves you
    between them. See [Insurance in ClinikEHR](/clinic/insurance/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The payer rejected the claim immediately">
    On the electronic system that usually means enrollment, not coding: the
    payer does not recognise the provider for that transaction type. Check
    **Settings** → **Insurance** → **Enrollment**, submit the enrollment, and
    sync its status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The eligibility check says Ineligible">
    Confirm the member number, the payer and the date of birth on the client's
    coverage — a single wrong digit reads as no cover. If the details are right,
    the patient's cover has genuinely lapsed and they are self-paying; raise an
    [invoice](/clinic/revenue/invoices) instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need to remove a payer we no longer bill">
    Remove it. If it has claims it is deactivated rather than deleted, which
    keeps your history readable. It stops appearing on new claims either way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The claim price is not what we negotiated">
    On the hospital system, payer-specific prices live under **Insurance** →
    **Pricing**. Fix the rate there rather than overtyping each claim.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Insurance is blocked on my plan">
    The electronic system is not available on the Free plan; the upgrade prompt
    names **Starter**. The hospital system needs Enterprise plus the insurance
    [care area](/platform/team/care-areas). See
    [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
