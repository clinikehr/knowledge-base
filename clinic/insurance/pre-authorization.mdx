A pre-authorization asks an insurer to agree, in advance, that they will pay for something. Get it before you treat and a claim is a formality; skip it and a claim for a procedure that needed approval is a denial waiting to happen.

<Note>

</Note>

## Raise the request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new request">
    From **Pre-Authorization**, start a new request. The form opens on **Patient & Request Information**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the patient and the request">
    Choose the \*\*Patient \*\*\* — the picker says "Select patient..." — then the \*\*Request Type \*\*\* and a **Priority Level**. Priority is what tells your own team which requests cannot wait, so set it honestly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the deadlines">
    **Submission Deadline** is when you must have sent it; **Authorization Deadline** is when you need an answer by. Both drive your follow-up, so fill them in even when the payer has not stated one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the items">
    Add a line for each thing you want approved. Each line takes an \*\*Item Type \*\*\*, the item itself, a \*\*Description \*\*\*, a \*\*Quantity \*\*\* and a **Unit Cost**. **Tariff Code**, **Tariff ID** and **Diagnosis** carry the payer's own reference where you have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the codes">
    Under **ICD 10/CPT CODE**, search with "Search ICD-10, CPT, or HCPCS codes..." and attach the codes that justify the request. This is the field payers read first: a request with a procedure code and no diagnosis code supporting it is the most common avoidable denial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Add anything else under **Additional Notes**, then use **Review & Submit**. Read the summary before you send — you are asking a payer to commit money against what is on that screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Prices on the request come from the payer-specific rates you set under **Insurance** → **Pricing**, not from your standard [service list](/clinic/revenue/services). If a figure looks wrong, the negotiated price is where to fix it.
</Note>

## Track the answer

The **Pre-Authorization Requests** list is where you chase. Search across "Search requests, patients, insurance...", or filter by status and by type. The stats above it show **Total Requests**, **Pending**, **Approved** and your **Approval Rate** — a falling approval rate is usually a coding problem, not bad luck.

When an insurer responds, record their decision on the request. An approval is what you quote on the [claim](/clinic/insurance/claims) later.

## Check it worked

- The request appears in **Pre-Authorization Requests** with a status, the patient and the insurer.
- The **Pending** count on the stats row has gone up by one.
- Every item you expect to deliver is on the request — an item that is not on it is not approved, even if the rest of the request is.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The patient's insurer is not in the picker">
    Add the insurer on the **Companies** tab of the Insurance Dashboard first, then record the patient's cover on **Coverage**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find the right code">
    "Search ICD-10, CPT, or HCPCS codes..." matches on name, code and description, so try the clinical term rather than the number. If nothing fits, describe it precisely under \*\*Description \*\*\* — a payer will query a vague description, and a query costs more time than the search did.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The unit cost is not what we agreed with this payer">
    Payer-specific prices live under **Insurance** → **Pricing**. Fix the negotiated price there so every future request is right, rather than overtyping it on each one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The request was denied">
    Read the payer's reason against the codes you submitted. Most denials at this stage are a missing supporting diagnosis, a quantity beyond what the plan allows, or an item the patient's cover excludes. Correct it and raise a new request rather than arguing the old one.
  </Accordion>

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
