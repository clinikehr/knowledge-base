The **Nursing Station** is the shift's worklist: "Observations due across the
ward. Recording happens on the patient." It tells you who needs attention next;
the charting itself happens against the individual patient.

## Narrow the worklist

Four controls sit above the list:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ward filter | Defaults to **All wards** |
| **Search patient or bed…** | Finds one person without scrolling |
| **Bed order** | Sorts the way you physically walk the ward |
| **Priority** | Sorts by what is most overdue |

Use **Bed order** while you are walking and **Priority** when you are catching
up.

## Read the handover

A **Shift handover** block sits above the worklist. Add to it with **Add to the
handover…** as the shift goes on rather than writing it all at the end — this is
what the next shift reads first. Until anything is written it shows **Nothing
written this shift yet.**

## The worklist columns

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Patient** | Who, and their bed |
| **Ward** | Which ward and sub-ward |
| **Last obs** | When observations were last recorded, or **No obs yet** |
| **Last note** | When anyone last wrote |
| **Doses** | What is due on the drug chart |
| **Fluids** | Intake and output balance |
| **Status** | The observation state |

When there is nothing outstanding the list reads **Nothing waiting**.

## Chart from a row

Each row carries two actions:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the pen to chart">
    It offers a **Nursing note** or a **Round note**, and opens the charting
    sheet without leaving the worklist.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the tab you need">
    The sheet has **Observations & fluids** and **Drug chart**. It always opens
    on **Observations & fluids**, because that is the question the board asked.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record, and return to the list">
    Saving confirms with **Nursing note recorded** or **Round note recorded**,
    and the row's **Last note** updates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Open for anything bigger">
    **Open** goes to the patient's full inpatient chart — the whole stay, all
    tabs. See [Admit, chart and discharge on the ward](/clinic/inpatient/wards).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What needs a connection

Writing a note and loading the drug chart both need a live connection, and the
product says so rather than failing quietly: **Writing a note needs a
connection** and **The drug chart needs a connection**. A stale drug chart is a
claim about a patient's medicines, so it is never shown from cache. See
[Working offline](/platform/apps/offline).

## Check it worked

- The row you charted shows a fresh **Last obs** or **Last note**.
- A patient with nothing outstanding drops off the default view.
- Your handover text is visible in the **Shift handover** block to the next
  person who opens the station.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing waiting, but I know observations are due">
    Check the ward filter is on **All wards** and that no search text is left in
    the box. Then switch to **Priority** to bring the most overdue to the top.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The pen does nothing">
    Writing a note needs a connection. Reconnect and try again — nothing is lost
    by waiting, and nothing half-written was saved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Drug chart tab will not load">
    Same reason. The drug chart is never served from cache, because a stale one
    would be a false statement about a patient's medicines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nursing Station is missing from the sidebar">
    It needs the enterprise plan and the **In-patient care** area in
    **Settings → Facility**. See [Care areas](/platform/team/care-areas).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A dose will not record from here">
    Doses are recorded on the patient's drug chart, and the round may be claimed
    by another device. See the drug round section of
    [Admit, chart and discharge on the ward](/clinic/inpatient/wards).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
