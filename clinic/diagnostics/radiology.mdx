Imaging works like the lab: a clinician requests the study, the modality
performs it, a radiologist reports it, and the signed report goes back to the
requester. This page walks that loop inside a hospital workspace.

## Where everything lives

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Radiology List** | Requests waiting, in progress and done |
| **Radiology History** | Everything already ordered |
| **Radiology Station** | The reading view |
| **Radiology Reports** | Finished reports, ready to print or download |
| **Modalities** | Your connected imaging equipment |

**Modalities** appears only when the imaging bridge is switched on for your
workspace. Without it the other four screens still work — you simply attach
images by hand rather than receiving them from the equipment.

## Request a study

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order from the consultation">
    Open the [consultation](/clinic/clinical/consultation), go to the **Orders**
    tab and select the **Imaging** sub-tab. Ordering here keeps the request tied
    to the encounter and the clinical question behind it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or order from the station">
    Open **Radiology Station** and raise the request there. Use this for walk-in
    and referred work that never had a consultation in your system.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Say why">
    Write the clinical indication into the request. The radiologist reads that
    before they read the images, and a request with no question attached comes
    back as a description rather than an answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Report the study

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the study">
    From the imaging list, select **Create Report** on the row. If images are
    attached the report screen says "{'{n}'} images attached" and offers
    **Open full viewer**, **Download study** and **Archive to cloud**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the images">
    **Open full viewer** launches the full diagnostic viewer in a new tab.
    **Download study** takes a copy for offline reading. **Archive to cloud**
    moves the study into long-term storage — do that once reporting is finished,
    not before.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write findings and impression">
    Enter the **Findings** — what you can see — and then the **Impression**,
    which is the answer to the clinical question. Referring clinicians read the
    impression first and often only that, so put the conclusion there rather
    than leaving it implied by the findings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the report">
    Sign it. Signing is what makes the report a clinical document rather than a
    working draft, and it is what releases the report to the requester.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  An unsigned report is stamped as preliminary and must never leave the building
  looking final. If a ward needs an urgent read before you can sign, speak to
  them — do not print the draft.
</Warning>

## Check it worked

- The study shows as reported in **Radiology History**, with your name on it.
- The report appears under **Radiology Reports** and prints without a
  preliminary stamp.
- The requesting clinician sees the report on the patient's
  [chart](/clinic/clinical/patient-chart).

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There is no Radiology group in my sidebar">
    Either the diagnostics [care area](/platform/team/care-areas) is off, or your
    workspace is not on the Enterprise plan. See
    [Change your plan](/platform/plans/change-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Modalities is missing">
    **Modalities** only appears when the imaging bridge is enabled for your
    workspace. Everything else on this page works without it — you attach and
    read images manually instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The study says no images are attached">
    The equipment has not delivered them yet, or the study was performed on a
    modality that is not connected. Check the study on the equipment before you
    report from memory, and ask the radiographer to re-send it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Open full viewer does nothing">
    The viewer opens in a new tab. If nothing appears, your browser blocked the
    pop-up — allow pop-ups for ClinikEHR and select it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I signed a report with an error in it">
    Speak to an owner or manager. Correcting a signed report is a deliberate,
    recorded act rather than a quiet edit, because the first version has already
    been read and acted on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
