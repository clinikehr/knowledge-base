Checking a patient in puts them on the waiting list, records what they came for
and who they are waiting to see. Checking them out closes the visit and lets you
issue a visit record.

The **Check-in** button sits on the patient chart's **Overview** and on the
consultation header. From the front desk board you can start the same sheet with
**Check in** and search for the person first.

## Check someone in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Check In Patient sheet">
    Select **Check-in**. The sheet opens with the current time already chosen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the arrival time">
    Under **Pick check-in date & time**, change it if the patient arrived
    earlier than you got to the screen. This is the time the waiting clock runs
    from.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach the appointment, if there is one">
    Choose it under **Appointment (optional)**. Leave it on **No appointment**
    for a walk-in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Say what the visit is for">
    **Visit items — services, labs, custom** takes a service, a lab test, or a
    custom label typed in yourself. Add as many as the visit needs; they are
    what the **For** column on the board shows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign the patient">
    Pick the clinician or team member under **Assign to**, or leave it on
    **Unassigned** and assign later. Enter a **Location / room** if your clinic
    tracks rooms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the reason for visit">
    Free text in **Reason for visit**, plus anything the care team should know
    under **Notes (optional)**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Check in">
    The patient appears on the front desk board as waiting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The sheet refuses to save an empty visit: **Add a reason, appointment, or at
  least one item**. A check-in with none of the three tells the clinician
  nothing, so at least one is required.
</Note>

## After the check-in

Reopening the visit gives you **Edit Check-In**, where you can:

- **Save changes** — update the items, assignment, room or reason.
- **Check out** — close the visit. It also appears inline on each board row.
- **Cancel check-in** — remove a visit recorded in error.

## Issue the visit record

The same sheet has **Download PDF** and **Email patient**. Emailing confirms
with **Visit record sent** and names the address it went to.

<AuditNote action="Opening a patient's check-in" />

## Why the buttons are greyed out on the consultation chart

On the consultation chart, **Intakes**, **Upload** and **Check-in** stay
disabled until the patient record has finished loading. That is deliberate — a
check-in recorded before the chart knows whose it is would be attached to
nobody. Wait a moment and they enable themselves.

## Check it worked

- The patient is on the front desk board under **Waiting**, with a running wait
  time.
- The **For** column shows the visit items you attached and **With** shows the
  assigned staff member.
- After checking out, they move to the **Checked out** tab and **Seen today**
  goes up by one.
- **Email patient** reports **Visit record sent**.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check in is greyed out">
    On the consultation chart it stays disabled until the patient record
    resolves. Give it a moment. If it never enables, reopen the chart from the
    patient list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The sheet will not save">
    You have no reason, no appointment and no visit item. Add any one of the
    three.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The wait time is wrong">
    The clock runs from the time under **Pick check-in date & time**, not from
    when you saved. Reopen the visit and correct it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Checked out by mistake">
    Check the patient back in. There is no confirmation on check-out precisely
    because it is reversible this way.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A visit is flagged as a long wait too soon">
    The threshold is set for the whole clinic in **Settings → Facility** under
    **Front desk waiting**. The board prints the current figure beneath the
    table.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
