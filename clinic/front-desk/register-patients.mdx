The patient list is the single place a person is created, found and edited. Every
other module — appointments, consultation, billing, wards — reads from it.

Your plan decides the word. On the enterprise plan the list is **Patients**; on
solo and team plans it is **Clients**, and every label on the screen follows. On
enterprise it is reachable from two places — **Front Desk → Patients** and
**Records → Patient Records** — and both highlight together. That is one list
under two doors, not a bug.

## What the list gives you

Stat cards run across the top, then two tabs:

- **All Patients** (or **All Clients**) — the list itself, with sorting by first
  name, last name or date added, and filters for status, billing type and
  **Waitlist**.
- **Analytics** — distribution charts for gender, age, country and chronic
  conditions across your whole list.

The toolbar carries **Refresh**, **Import**, **Export**, **New Patient** and
**Columns**, which chooses what the table shows.

A clinic with no records yet gets an empty state offering three ways forward:
explore a sandbox of sample data, import a list, or add someone. The sandbox is
sample data for you to click around in — nothing in it is a real person and
nothing you do there reaches a real record.

## Add one patient

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select New Patient">
    The registration sheet opens over the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter who they are">
    Name, date of birth and contact details. Enter what you have — the record is
    editable forever and a booking does not wait on a complete profile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set how they pay">
    Choose the billing type — self pay, insurance or free. Insurance details can
    be added to the record afterwards.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    The patient appears at the top of the list. From the front desk board,
    registering someone this way flows straight into checking them in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open or edit a record

Select the **⋯** menu at the end of a row:

- **View details** opens the full chart — visits, documents, billing, files.
- **Edit** reopens the record for changes.

## Work the self-registration waitlist

If patients can register themselves, their requests arrive as a waitlist rather
than as live records. Filter the list to **Waitlist** to see them, then use the
row menu:

- **Approve request** creates the patient record.
- **Decline request** rejects it.

The approval dialog carries an **Enable client portal access** checkbox.

<Note>
  **Approving does not give anyone portal access unless you tick that box.** It
  is off by default. Ticking it lets the patient sign in by magic link to see
  their records, book appointments and message your team — see
  [Turn on the patient portal](/portal/setup/enable).
</Note>

## Import and export a list

**Import** brings an existing list in from a file; **Export** takes yours out.
Import in one pass rather than in fragments — a half-imported list is harder to
reconcile than a missing one.

## Check it worked

- The patient appears in **All Patients** and can be found by searching their
  name.
- Opening **View details** shows the details you entered.
- They can be selected when booking an appointment and when checking someone in.
- An approved waitlist request is gone from the **Waitlist** filter and present
  in the main list.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A patient I just created is not in the list">
    Select **Refresh**, and check whether a status or **Waitlist** filter is
    still applied from earlier.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An approved patient cannot sign in to the portal">
    The **Enable client portal access** checkbox was not ticked at approval.
    Open the record and grant access from there — see
    [Turn on the patient portal](/portal/setup/enable).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The screen says Clients, not Patients">
    You are on a solo or team plan. The word follows the plan, and the screen is
    otherwise the same. See [The three editions](/start/editions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Patients highlights in two places in the sidebar">
    Correct on the enterprise plan. **Front Desk → Patients** and
    **Records → Patient Records** are the same list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sample patients appeared that nobody created">
    That is the sandbox. It is sample data for exploring the screen and is
    separate from your real list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
