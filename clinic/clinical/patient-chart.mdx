The chart is the one screen that holds everything recorded about a single
person — their details, their visits, what they were prescribed, what was
ordered, what they owe and what they have signed. Every clinical module writes
into it, so it is the right place to start when you need context before you act.

The Clinic & Hospital edition ships two versions of it. Solo and team plans open
a **client** chart built around the relationship; the enterprise plan opens a
**patient** record built around the demographics a hospital has to hold. Both
describe the same person, and both are reached the same way.

## What you get on each plan

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Solo and team — the client chart">
    The header carries the name, client type and status badges, date of birth
    and age, email and phone, with **Edit profile** beside it. Under that sits a
    row of actions — **Intakes**, **Upload**, **Message**, and a **⋯** menu whose
    **Change status** option moves the person between **Active**, **Inactive**,
    **Prospective** and **Archived**.

    | Tab | What is on it |
    |---|---|
    | **Overview** | Balance, invoices, consults and next appointment; the current check-in state (**Not checked in** until someone checks them in); **Current Medications** and **Recent Diagnoses** |
    | **Billing** | Invoices and payments for this person |
    | **Measures** | Recorded measurements over time |
    | **History** | The visit and activity trail |
    | **Medications** | Prescriptions and e-prescribing — see [Prescribe and e-prescribe](/clinic/clinical/prescriptions) |
    | **Labs** | Test results filed against this person |
    | **Files** | The health-records vault and shared forms |

    A right-hand sidebar stays with you across tabs: **Billing** (Balance, Total
    Paid, Billing Type), **Upcoming** appointments, **Client Info** and
    **Contacts**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Enterprise — the patient record">
    The enterprise record opens as a demographic form rather than a summary,
    because a hospital is required to hold more about each person: Title, names,
    Gender, DOB, Marital Status, National ID, Occupation, Employment,
    Nationality, Country of Residence, email and phone numbers, Residential
    Address, **Next of Kin**, Blood Type, Genotype and Current Medications.

    Alongside it are **Insurance** and **Recent Activity** tabs. Clinical
    activity lives in the modules that produced it — [triage](/clinic/clinical/triage),
    [consultation](/clinic/clinical/consultation), [the lab](/clinic/diagnostics/lab),
    [radiology](/clinic/diagnostics/radiology) and
    [wards](/clinic/inpatient/wards) — and is summarised on **Recent Activity**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sticky notes and status notes

A floating dock sits on the edge of the chart: two draggable vertical tabs,
**Sticky Notes** in amber and **Status Notes** in blue, each with a count badge.
Pinned sticky notes also render as dismissible cards on the chart itself.

To add one, open **Sticky Notes** and fill in **Title**, **Note**, a **Colour**
and a **Priority**, then decide whether to pin it — the toggle reads "Keep this
note above the others" — and select **Add note**.

<Note>
  **A sticky note belongs to the person, not the visit.** It follows them into
  every future visit and every module, so it is the right place for "always
  needs a chaperone" or "prefers afternoon appointments" — and the wrong place
  for something that only matters today. Anything clinical belongs in a
  [clinical note](/clinic/clinical/notes).
</Note>

## Check a person in from the chart

Select **Check-in** to open the **Check In Patient** sheet. Pick the check-in
date and time, choose an appointment (or leave it on **No appointment**), add
**Visit items** — a service, a lab test or a custom label — set **Assign to**
(a staff member, or **Unassigned**) and enter a **Reason for visit**. Select
**Check in** to finish.

The sheet refuses to save an empty visit: "Add a reason, appointment, or at
least one item". Once checked in you can **Save changes**, check the person out,
or cancel the visit, and the visit record can be downloaded or emailed — you
will see "Visit record sent" when it goes.

The full front-desk workflow, including the queue view, is in
[Check patients in](/clinic/front-desk/check-in).
