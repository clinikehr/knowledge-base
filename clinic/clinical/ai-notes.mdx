The **AI Note Taker** listens to a consultation, or reads a document you upload,
and writes a structured note in the format you choose. You then read it, correct
it and sign it. It drafts; you remain the author.

## Generate the note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Note Taker">
    The sheet is headed **AI Note Taker** — "Generate structured clinical notes
    from recordings or documents". It walks you through in order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a template">
    "Select a Template — Choose the note format for your documentation." This
    decides the shape of what comes back, so pick the format you would have
    written by hand.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customise the output">
    Under "Customize Output":

    | Setting | Options |
    |---|---|
    | **Note Tone** | Professional, Empathetic, Concise, Detailed |
    | **Speaker Mode** | Multi-Party for a conversation, Single Speaker for you dictating alone |
    | Custom instructions | Free text, for example "Focus on medication changes and mental status" |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record, or upload a file">
    **Live Recording** — "Speak naturally — all participants will be captured".
    You can pause, resume, stop and re-record.

    **Upload File** — MP3, MP4, PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD, up to 100 MB. Use this for
    a recording made elsewhere, or a referral letter you want summarised into
    the note.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for processing, then review">
    The session processes, then shows you the drafted note. Read it against what
    actually happened before you go any further.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply it to the note form">
    Applying the draft drops the text into the note form, where you edit, add
    what the recording missed, and sign as normal — see
    [Write a clinical note](/clinic/clinical/notes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Read every line before you sign.** A generated note is a draft written from
  audio, and audio mishears drug names, doses and negations. A clinician must
  review and approve it — your signature says the words are yours, not the
  model's. Nothing is filed to a chart until you save it.
</Warning>

## Manage your AI sessions

**AI Notes** in the left rail opens the manager sheet over the page. Filter by
**Completed**, **Processing**, **Failed** or **Incomplete**, and act on each
session:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Regenerate | Runs the same recording against a different template. You will see "Note regenerated" |
| **Stop** | Ends a session that is still running |
| **Delete** | Removes the session |
| **Open in Notes** | Takes you to the note it produced |

A session that has stopped making progress shows an amber **Stalled** badge —
stop it and record again rather than waiting.

## In a video consultation

Telehealth has its own note taker built into the call, which can start
automatically when you begin recording. That one needs the Team or Enterprise
plan. See [Run a video consultation](/clinic/clinical/telehealth).

## Check it worked

The session shows as **Completed** in the **AI Notes** manager, the drafted text
is in your note form under the template you chose, and the note is attached to
the right person. Nothing reaches the chart until you save or sign it.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I am asked to manage add-ons">
    The AI Note Taker is an add-on rather than a plan feature. Team and
    Enterprise clinic owners get it bundled; every other seat needs its own.
    Select **Manage Add-ons** in the prompt, or see
    [Plans and add-ons](/platform/plans/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No client selected">
    Select the person in the **Quick Note** card before opening the sheet. The
    note taker files its output against a chart, so it needs to know whose.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The session is stuck on Processing">
    Look for the amber **Stalled** badge in the **AI Notes** manager. If it is
    there, select **Stop** and run it again — a long recording on a weak
    connection is the usual cause.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My upload was rejected">
    Only MP3, MP4, PDF, DOCX, TXT and MD are accepted, up to 100 MB. A long
    recording in a lossless format will be over the limit — export it as MP3.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The note came back in the wrong format">
    Regenerate it from the **AI Notes** manager against a different template.
    The recording is reused, so you do not have to see the patient again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recording missed a second speaker">
    Set **Speaker Mode** to Multi-Party for a consultation. Single Speaker is
    for dictating on your own after the patient has left.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
